# CONJECTURE MODE — CONJECTURE-GENERAL REGISTER

Under Charter §2A, a CONJECTURE-GENERAL entry needs all four fields:
- G1: supporting cases;
- G2: the bold generalization;
- G3: its current scope;
- G4: a breaking test.

An entry may guide what gets tested next. It can never earn PREDICTED, DERIVED or VALIDATED status, and never a class-level exclusion. A failed G4 test is banked as CONJECTURE-GENERAL-REFUTED.
