## P-06 (R-06): The CM/non-CM boundary of the memory kernel

**Question.** What property of a conservative microscopic parent's memory kernel makes its Laplace transform completely monotone, and where exactly does the S5-1 parent sit relative to that boundary?

**Existing result tested.** S5-1 = MARKOV-LIMIT-OTHER-CLASS: the Level-0 G-D generator (first-order, completely monotone kernel) is NOT derived by the conservative parent; native g=1 damping has a branch cut with t^{-3/2} tail. K-L0 FAILS.

**New ingredient.** The question "is CM a stable or unstable boundary of this parent class?" — i.e. is K-L0's failure generic or fine-tuned?

**Hypothesis.** The CM boundary is a finite-distance surface in deformation space: a family of controlled deformations of the S5-1 kernel crosses it at a computable point.

**Null.** The S5-1 kernel is CM-isolated: no neighborhood of deformations yields CM. That would be a genuine no-go upgrade for the whole class.

**Success criterion.** Either: (a) an explicit deformation family with a computed crossing point (CM restored at one side, lost at the other, analytically verified), or (b) a proof that the neighborhood is CM-free.

**Failure criterion.** The deformation family is uncontrolled (no limit theorem bounds the reduction) — then this is parked, not failed.

**Scope.** The S5-1 parent and its analytic continuation only. No new microscopic model.

**Forbidden reinterpretations.** No redefinition of "completely monotone"; no redefinition of "generator"; no claim that a CM point elsewhere in deformation space discharges S5-0.

**Expected information gain.** Either a boundary theorem (serves the unselected S-7 directly) or a structural no-go strengthening S5-0.

## P-08 (R-08): The faithful-representation audit

**Question.** Does requiring a lift to be a faithful representation of the full observable algebra eliminate any of the surviving candidates?

**Existing result tested.** L0 LIFT SELECTION = IRREDUCIBLE/SUPPLIED, with surviving earned selectors D-1, D-4(narrowed), D-5(narrowed). The faithful-representation audit is the owner's PRESERVED option (constraint, not uniqueness).

**New ingredient.** None (the audit is pre-authorized in kind; this is its scout execution).

**Hypothesis.** Faithfulness prunes at least one of D-1/D-4n/D-5n.

**Null.** All three survive faithfulness — that is itself informative: the constraint is empty on the survivor set.

**Success criterion.** A survivor count change (any) with the mathematical reason stated.

**Failure criterion.** Ambiguity in "the full observable algebra" for the classical substrate such that faithfulness cannot be stated precisely — then BLOCKED, reported as such.

**Scope.** The surviving lift set only; no new lifts.

**Forbidden reinterpretations.** Cannot alter IRREDUCIBLE/SUPPLIED; no new selector invented here; faithfulness failure of a lift is a constraint, not a disqualification of the whole lift program.

**Expected information gain.** Directly executes a preserved option the canonical record named as the next candidate test.

## P-09 (R-09): Locality-of-representation as an unpriced selector

**Question.** Does demanding that the lift map local classical observables to local quantum operators (in some declared sense of "local") prune the survivor set?

**Existing result tested.** Lift selection closed at frozen selectors D-1..D-7. Spatial locality of the lift was NOT priced.

**New ingredient.** NEW ASSUMPTION: "locality of lift" (declared precisely below before testing).

**Hypothesis.** At least one surviving lift fails locality, shrinking the set.

**Null.** All survivors are local in the declared sense.

**Success / failure criteria.** Same structure as P-08: any survivor-set change counts; ambiguity in the locality definition → BLOCKED.

**Scope.** Surviving lifts only, after P-08's result.

**Forbidden reinterpretations.** Cannot declare locality post hoc to kill a survivor; the definition is frozen before testing.

**Expected information gain.** Tests whether physical locality — the one thing the substrate genuinely has (E-2) — reaches the lift layer at all.

## P-17 (R-17): The Caldeira–Leggett bath as a C-B candidate

**Question.** Does a standard Ohmic oscillator bath reproduce the S2 C-B reduced mean-response structure?

**Existing result tested.** S2-HB = UNRESTRICTED-REALIZATION-ONLY, NO-PHYSICAL-BATH-CANDIDATE (the O-6 conservative chain parent and S5-1's limit both failed the P-4/P-6 checks).

**New ingredient.** A different declared bath family (Ohmic CL bath), which the record never tested.

**Hypothesis.** The CL bath's reduced dynamics on the quartic drift carries the C-B discriminator signature (noise-generated spread feeding drift curvature).

**Null.** The CL bath reduces to the S-1 linear-Gaussian equivalence class (discriminator invisible), which would REPRODUCE the known S2-HB obstruction with a different parent — a strong negative.

**Success criterion.** The reduced dynamics is computed and compared to C-B's O(t³) identity; any definite verdict (match or mismatch) counts.

**Failure criterion.** The reduction is not controllable at the coupling needed — parked.

**Scope.** One bath model, one observable (the retained mean), one coupling family.

**Forbidden reinterpretations.** No redefinition of the C-B class; no moving the goalposts on "physical bath" (energy-conserving, local, autonomous).

**Expected information gain.** Tests the largest open seam with the most tractable honest candidate.

## P-18 (R-18): The ergodic fast-variable null

**Question.** Does an ergodic fast variable (deterministic, chaotic, mixing) produce a martingale-noise limit that matches C-B?

**Existing result tested.** Same as P-17; this is the *clean-limit* version and the designed null.

**New ingredient.** Averaging-theorem construction: no path encoding, only initial-condition uncertainty over the fast variable.

**Hypothesis.** The averaged drift is the original drift plus O(ε) corrections; the limit noise is NOT a true martingale unless the fast invariant measure satisfies a detailed-balance-like condition — locating precisely where the S2-HB obstruction lives.

**Null.** The limit is a deterministic ODE (no noise at all) — that itself would sharpen S2-HB's "unrestricted-only" reading.

**Success criterion.** A theorem-grade statement (any of: martingale fails, martingale holds with conditions, no noise survives averaging) with proof.

**Scope.** Fast-slow systems with a quartic slow drift; no simulation.

**Expected information gain.** Either a mechanism for the noise or a sharpened obstruction; the discriminator between P-17's candidates.

## P-01 (R-01): Floor-edge portability across coupling families

**Question.** Do the E-1 edges (gap→memory, passivity→positivity) survive replacing the K-type coupling with a Laplacian (graph) coupling?

**Existing result tested.** E-1 LOAD-BEARING on the declared C1-a class.

**New ingredient.** A different coupling family (nearest-neighbor Laplacian; random-regular graph).

**Hypothesis.** gap→memory transfers; passivity→positivity transfers only for symmetric Laplacians (asymmetry breaks it — predicting a NEW class-split).

**Null.** Neither edge transfers — the floor's dependency map is coupling-family-specific.

**Success criterion.** Both edges evaluated on both families with explicit spectral arguments.

**Forbidden reinterpretations.** No redefinition of P_memory/P_positivity; transfer or non-transfer reported as-is.

**Expected information gain.** First test of whether the certified dependency map is a property of the substrate or of one coupling family.

## P-02 (R-02): Sector formation without fermions

**Question.** Does the SF-1 mechanism (conserved number scaling sector → inequivalent IR law classes) require fermionic statistics, or just an exact conserved quantity?

**Existing result tested.** SF-1 ACCEPTED for the free-fermion parent; SFG-0: mechanism is Tier-C, does not migrate to earned core.

**New ingredient.** A free-boson ladder with conserved number.

**Hypothesis.** The same scaling-sector split occurs (mechanism = conserved quantity, not statistics).

**Null.** Bosonic sectors give ONE law class (statistics matters, contra the minimal hypothesis).

**Success criterion.** IR classes computed for at least two scaling sectors; verdict stated.

**Scope.** Free boson ladder; the SF-1 density-response observable; same coarse-graining.

**Expected information gain.** Isolates which ingredient (conservation vs statistics) carries SF-1 — directly informs whether SFG-0's Tier-E wall is about statistics or about conservation.

## P-23 (R-23): Decoherence-rate scaling as an observable

**Question.** What decoherence-rate scaling Γ(m) does the CM/supplied structure predict for macroscopic matter waves, and does it differ from the Diósi–Penrose and CSL nulls?

**Existing result tested.** NO_GO entry 4: the energy-basis wedge is INVISIBLE-BY-SUPPRESSION as an *amplitude* observable.

**New ingredient.** The *rate-scaling* question (exponent, not amplitude).

**Hypothesis.** The CM generator fixes the exponent of Γ(m) independent of the noise amplitude, producing a parameter-free exponent prediction.

**Null.** The exponent is amplitude-dependent (then no parameter-free prediction exists, and the route dies).

**Success criterion.** An exponent computed from the certified structure with declared inputs; a comparison table vs DP/CSL exponents.

**Scope.** Symbolic scaling analysis only; no experiment design.

**Expected information gain.** The campaign's only shot at an empirical discriminator; exponent predictions are cheap to falsify.

## P-15 (R-15): Nonlinear branching and the Born question

**Question.** Can a structurally different branching model (nonlinear mean-field drift + noise, not the tested branch-preserving unitary class) produce unequal branch weights without postulating a measure?

**Existing result tested.** EXPERIMENT_P_FRONTIER = CLOSED_AT_TESTED_MODEL_CLASS; decoherence ≠ outcome selection in the tested class.

**New ingredient.** A structural premise change: nonlinear self-consistent drift (the S2 C-B machinery pointed at branching).

**Hypothesis.** Branch weights in such a model are determined by the drift's nonlinearity (not equal, not Born) — making the model a *candidate* mechanism whose mismatch with Born is itself the finding.

**Null.** Weights stay equal regardless of nonlinearity.

**Success criterion.** A worked toy model with computable branch weights; verdict vs Born stated.

**Scope.** One toy model, analytic; numeric check only for stability.

**Forbidden reinterpretations.** Any mismatch with Born is a NEGATIVE finding for the model, not evidence for GRUT; no claim that this "solves" the Born problem.

**Expected information gain.** Tests whether the S2 machinery generalizes to the quantum branch problem at all.
