# SCOUT_0 STANDING SATURATION CRITERIA (written into the repository 2026-10-01)

**Provenance.** The original scout campaign instruction contained a stopping rule that was never committed
to the repository. ZOOM_OUT_04 therefore substituted five weaker local criteria (S-a…S-e). The text
below is the **auditor-supplied restatement** of that rule (conversation of 2026-10-01). The original
wording is not available in the repository or in the scout's working context, so this restatement
**governs** from now on. ZOOM_OUT_04's S-a…S-e are superseded.

**The scout may be declared saturated only if every material criterion is MET:**

1. No remaining high-information probe under the current premise set.
2. Top candidate results have hostile review and independent derivation/checks.
3. Parent/model sensitivity has been tested rather than relying on one toy family.
4. Empirical routes have been pursued or shown non-identifiable/non-distinctive; at least one
   empirical family remains active unless ruled non-identifiable.
5. The access/lift frontier has produced either a constraint or a clear obstruction.
6. Physical-noise origin has a physical parent, a universality result, or a no-go/identifiability
   result.
7. Generator emergence has been tested beyond the original S5 route.
8. Outcome work changes model class beyond Experiment P and cleanly separates
   decoherence/selection/weights.
9. Cross-layer compression has been actively attacked rather than merely observed.
10. Prediction candidates have been tested against hidden supplied/free parameters.
11. Imported standard physics has been separated from GRUT-earned derivation.
12. The campaign states what concrete evidence would change its conclusions.
13. No obvious new probe with materially different premises remains inside the authorized playground
    scope.

**Verdict vocabulary:**
- SCIENTIFICALLY SATURATED AT CURRENT PREMISES
- PROVISIONALLY SATURATED — OWED CHECKS ONLY
- NOT SATURATED — SPECIFIC HIGH-VALUE ROUTE REMAINS

Do not declare SATURATED if a material criterion is NOT MET.
