## 1. Repeated mathematical structure — one principle behind both zoom-outs

ZOOM_OUT_01 found **descent invariance** at the lift interface: every earned criterion factors
through the descended one-particle data, so it cannot separate lifts. P-17/P-18 found the same shape
at the noise interface: every reduced observable factors through the effective forcing law, so it
cannot separate realizations.

> **Quotient principle (structural observation).** Earned structure and observations live on a
> quotient: the one-particle contraction, or the effective law. Supplied layers are coordinates
> along the fibres: lift, statistics, bath ontology, realization. Anything that factors through the
> quotient is constant on fibres.

This gives SYN-0's "nine mutually non-selecting supplied layers" (E-20) a mechanism. The layers fail to
select **because** the earned map forgets fibre coordinates. Each layer forgets them for its own
reason, but the arrow is the same: ontology → effective law → observables, and the second arrow
destroys information about the first.

## 2. Assumptions that keep reappearing

- The **"same state space"** qualifier: it carries all of S2-1's content.
- **Preparation independence (P)**: the forcing law must not depend on the system's initial state;
  otherwise it is absorbed as a deterministic slip.
- **Linear back-reaction** (harmonic bath), or **none** (skew product). Genuine nonlinear back-reaction
  is the one untested escape.
- **Chosen sector assignment** (`p_tt`): the only reason L11 is conditional.

## 7. Where to search next (wide-net rule)

The target is now precise: **find a mechanism that restricts the reduced law itself.** Candidates in
reach of the playground:

- **Law-class formation from conservation (P-02).** SF-1 showed a conserved sector selecting
  inequivalent IR law classes. That is a restriction on the law, not on the ontology. P-02 (bosons)
  tests whether the restriction comes from conservation or from statistics. **Promoted: run next.**
- **Floor-edge portability (P-01).** Tests whether the earned predicates (L1–L3) are
  substrate-general or coupling-family-specific. If they port, they are standard; if one fails to
  port, the failure is a law-level discriminator between coupling families. Run after P-02.
- **Lock census (new, spawned: P-24).** A record-wide search for every relation between two
  observables in which a supplied amplitude cancels (L11-type). It is cheap (read-only), and it is the
  most direct hunt for "GRUT restricts the law". Queue after P-01/P-02.
- **Genuine nonlinear back-reaction baths.** The one escape from P-17's class. Parked: known
  homogenization limits return Markov diffusions, so expected value is low.
