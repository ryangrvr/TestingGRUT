## 0. Verdict

> **TC-4's strong slogan ("dynamics cannot choose its own operator content") is REFUTED at the operator level.**
> 1. **An allowed-but-absent relevant operator is generated by coarse-graining.** The microscopic current is
>    `λ₃u³` only (`λ₂,bare = 0`). When Z₂ is broken only through the noise, the measured coarse current–density
>    relation has a quadratic coefficient **`λ₂,eff = c₂ = +0.274`**.
> 2. **A forbidden operator is not generated.**
>    - Z₂ control: `c₂ = +0.002` (≈ 0).
>    - With no nonlinear current (λ₃ = 0): `j(u₀) ≡ 0`. Generation needs an existing interaction to feed it.
> 3. **Operator content is also emergent relative to the sector or state.**
>    - **Background shift:** the same microscopic theory has `λ₂,eff(u₀) = 3c₃u₀` about a nonzero conserved sector,
>      with `c₃ = +0.30` (renormalized from the bare 0.5).
>    - **SSB:** a Z₂-symmetric action in its broken state has odd effective operators (2D Ising, block skewness ≈ ∓1.85
>      at T = 2 vs ≈ 0.02 at T = 3).
> 4. **Anomalies fix coefficients.** π⁰→γγ: the coefficient `N_c(Q_u² − Q_d²) = 1` gives Γ = 7.78 eV (≈ 7.8 eV
>    measured, secondary). There is no free Wilson coefficient, but the representation content (`N_c`, charges) is
>    supplied.
>
> **Refined TC-4 (candidate):** dynamics / RG / state selection generate and select operators **within the
> symmetry-allowed closure** `O_allowed(Φ, d, S)` of the effective state. They cannot by themselves choose the
> **upstream field / symmetry / representation content**, or the **sector / state data** (u₀, vacuum) that lower the
> effective symmetry.
