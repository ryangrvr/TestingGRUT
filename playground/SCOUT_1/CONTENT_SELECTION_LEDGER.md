## Hierarchy (candidate)

    coupling values (C1)            -> erased by RG (survive only as scales)
    operator closure (C2)           -> generated / selected within the symmetry-allowed closure of the state
    symmetry class (C3)             -> UV constraints preserved; IR may be ENLARGED (W5-ES open); lowered by sector / vacuum selection

    dimension / composition (C5)    -> supplied (D3 would test whether consistency can choose it)

**Refined TC-4 (candidate):** dynamics and RG climb from C1 to C2 automatically. They do not climb to C3, C4 or C5.

plus the sector / state data that select among symmetry-equivalent realizations.
