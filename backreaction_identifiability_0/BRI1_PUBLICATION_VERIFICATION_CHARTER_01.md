# BRI1 PUBLICATION VERIFICATION — CHARTER 01

## 1. Frozen scientific result

The object under verification is the existing theorem:

> For the frozen reciprocal finite Duffing bath X1, there exists a small-time
> interval such that, for every fixed time in that interval and sufficiently
> large finite bath size `N_B`, the interventional reduced-force family lies
> outside the shared causal signed-affine exogenous class `E2±`.

The witness is a reflection-safe standardized third-cumulant / skewness shape
change with

`κ3(F) = K/N_B + O(N_B^-2)`

and the witness vanishes in the reservoir limit.

The existing scope remains binding:

- internally proved;
- not externally reviewed;
- not a GRUT-specific law;
- not a GRUT empirical prediction;
- not primitive randomness;
- not a unique microscopic ontology;
- not an escape from `E_univ`;
- frozen-τ PF4Q coefficients remain evidence only and are not load-bearing.

## 2. Publication question

The sole question is:

> Is BRI1-X1 a mathematically correct, independently reproducible,
> clearly novel-or-distinctly-positioned statistical/open-systems result that
> can support a short paper without claim inflation?

original proof.

A valid reproduction must independently recover the chain:

1. frozen X1 Duffing bath and clamp equations;
2. exact i.i.d. force-sum structure;

4. parity of the reference protocol;
5. first-order response equation;

9. positive variances;
10. conversion to a reflection-safe `E2±` orbit violation;
11. `O(1/N_B)` disappearance in the reservoir limit.

Do NOT use the frozen-τ numerical values to prove the theorem.

Classify each proof step as:

- reproduced independently;
- reproduced but dependent on an imported standard theorem;
- not reproduced;
- discrepancy found.

If any load-bearing step is not reproduced, publication verification fails
until resolved.

### V2 — Hostile novelty / positioning audit

- nonlinear generalized Langevin equations;
- anharmonic oscillator baths;

> anharmonic baths generate non-Gaussian noise.

That is known.

> a finite reciprocal anharmonic environment can be distinguished from every
> single shared causal affine exogenous forcing model across an

> invariant, with the escape scaling as `O(1/N_B)` and disappearing in the
> reservoir limit.

For every close paper, record:

1. citation;
2. model class;
3. reciprocal or one-way coupling;
4. finite or infinite bath;
5. intervention family or single protocol;
6. observable used;
7. competitor model class;
8. whether an identifiability theorem is proved;
9. whether an explicit finite-size rate is given;
10. exact overlap with BRI1-X1;

## 4. Frozen claim firewall

The paper may claim, if verified:

> finite reciprocal anharmonic back-reaction can leave an interventional
> reduced-force signature that is not removable by one shared causal affine
> modulation of a protocol-independent exogenous process.

The paper may also claim:

> the distinguishing non-affine contribution is mesoscopic and decays as
> `O(1/N_B)` in the stated perturbative/theorem regime.

The paper may NOT claim:

- GRUT experimentally confirmed;
- new fundamental force;
- new quantum mechanics;
- primitive randomness;
- unique environment ontology;
- universal identifiability against arbitrary stochastic processes;
- escape from `E_univ`;
- universal finite-bath theorem beyond the frozen X1 model/class;
- a certified finite-time experimental threshold unless separately proved.

## 5. Publication-grade theorem statement

The verification must produce a proposed theorem statement suitable for a
paper, with every quantifier explicit:

- what is fixed;
- what is existential;
- what holds for all sufficiently large finite `N_B`;
- what depends on `t`;
- exact competitor class;
- exact intervention family;
- exact reservoir-limit scope.

No phrase such as "all nonlinear baths" is permitted.
