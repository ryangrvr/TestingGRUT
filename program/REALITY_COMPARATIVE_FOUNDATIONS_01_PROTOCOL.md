## 0. Purpose

The boundary map established that within the branch-preserving unitary class
$\mathcal{C}_{\rm bp}$, decoherence is derived but outcome selection is algebraically
impossible without breaking a class premise. Four doors exist. This experiment asks:

$$
\boxed{\text{Which candidate framework adds the least new physical structure
while explaining the most unexplained phenomena?}}
$$

The object under test is **not GRUT** and **not the instrument** — it is the set of
competing outcome ontologies, judged blind and symmetrically.

**No preferred framework. No GRUT-specific success criterion. If none survives, that
is the result.**

## 1. Frozen candidate set (no substitution, no reordering after seeing results)

1. **Standard QM/QFT + decoherence** (no additional structure)
2. **GRW / CSL / DP** (objective collapse — Door 1)
3. **Bohmian mechanics** (additional beables — Door 2)
4. **Everett / decoherent histories** (outcome-semantics revision — Door 3)
5. **Relational QM / QBism / operational** (probability-concept revision — Door 4)

Additional gravity-linked proposals (gravitational collapse, Penrose) may be added
**only** as a preregistered sixth case if recorded in this protocol before execution.
Decision: **INCLUDED as Case 6** (objective collapse driven by gravity), since it is a
Door-1 variant with distinct empirical content.

## 2. Frozen twelve-question audit (identical for every candidate)

1. What is physically real on this account?
2. What are its primitive variables?
3. What dynamics are fundamental?
4. Where do probabilities come from?
5. Does it produce definite outcomes — and how?
6. Does it recover ordinary QM (unitary sector)?
7. Does it recover classicality?
8. What additional inputs does it require beyond $\mathcal{C}_{\rm bp}$?
9. Which parameters are empirical inputs?
10. What genuinely novel predictions does it make (prediction-test discipline: fixed
    before comparison, not fitted, no hidden adjustable parameters)?
11. What observations could falsify it (with current experimental bounds where they exist)?
12. What unexplained remainder does it leave?

## 3. Frozen scoring axes

For each candidate, compute:

| Axis | Definition |
|---|---|
| **EXPLANATORY_GAIN** | previously-unexplained phenomena it accounts for (definite outcomes, Born weights, classicality, measurement record) |
| **STRUCTURE_COST** | number/kind of new primitives, dynamics, or postulates beyond standard QM |
| **COMPRESSION** | EXPLANATORY_GAIN per unit STRUCTURE_COST |
| **EMPIRICAL_STATUS** | testable / tested / empirically empty by construction (doors 3–4) / contradicted |
| **STATUS_LEXICON** | DERIVED / ASSUMED / EFFECTIVE / EMPIRICAL_INPUT / NON_IDENTIFIABLE / UNRESOLVED / PREDICTIVE / CONSTRAINT / POSTULATE / CONTESTED / REPRESENTATIONAL (RC-05 vocabulary, frozen) |

**Compression is the ranking metric, not elegance, popularity, or philosophical appeal.**

### Operational definition of compression (frozen)

$$
\text{COMPRESSION} = \frac{\text{itemized EXPLANATORY_GAIN}}{\text{itemized STRUCTURE_COST}}
$$

Both numerator and denominator are independently itemized before execution:

**Explanatory targets (numerator):**
- outcome definiteness supplied
- outcome probability weights supplied
- classicality recovered
- measurement record explained
- novel empirical prediction (only if prediction-test criteria are met)

**Structural commitments (denominator):**
- altered dynamics
- added ontology / beables
- new state or probability postulate
- free parameters
- preferred basis / foliation / collapse noise
- observer-relative or revised semantics
- additional axioms beyond $\mathcal{C}_{\rm bp}$

The score is a **preregistered comparative heuristic**, not a theorem. The two
itemized lists are not assumed objectively commensurable; the score is reported
with both itemizations visible, never as a bare number alone. Ties and
incommensurable cases are reported as such rather than forced to a ranking.

## 4. Anti-bias rules (frozen)

* **No symmetry seeking**: candidates may receive very different scores.
* **No skepticism bias**: a theory is not penalized for having empirical inputs (RC-05 G).
* **No credulity bias**: "consistent with", "interprets", "post-dicts" never become
  DERIVED or PREDICTIVE (RC-05 H).
* **CONTESTED is preserved**: where the literature genuinely disputes a derivation
  (e.g. Everett Born-rule), the dispute is preserved with its assumptions — no forced
  verdict.
* **Doors 3–4 classification**: may score high on explanatory gain only if the gain is
  real under its own semantics; the emptiness-by-construction of empirical
  discrimination must be recorded alongside, not hidden.
* **Source discipline**: primary sources before classification; the collapsed
  boundary theorem is used only to classify *what premise a candidate alters*, never
  as evidence that the candidate is wrong.
* The instrument may not be modified during execution; protocol failures are recorded
  as `PROTOCOL_LIMITATION`.

## 6. Pre-registered hypotheses (to be tested, not assumed)

* H1: Door-1 (collapse) frameworks achieve outcome selection at measurable structure
  cost, with genuine (declining but open) empirical parameter space.
* H2: Door-2 (Bohm) reproduces all standard predictions with added beables; its
  compression is dominated by the added ontology + measure postulate.
* H3: Door-3 (Everett) removes outcome-selection cost by semantic revision; Born-rule
  status remains CONTESTED on the record.
* H4: Door-4 frameworks are empirically empty by construction and can claim no
  discriminating gain.
* H5: Standard QM + decoherence has zero structure cost and zero outcome gain — the
  baseline against which all compression is measured.

All five may fail. If they fail, the failure is the result.

## 7. Success condition

The experiment succeeds if it produces a **defensible compression ranking** with
preserved disputes, correct RC-05-style classifications, and explicit unexplained
remainders per candidate — regardless of which candidate ranks first, including
"none ranks above baseline."
