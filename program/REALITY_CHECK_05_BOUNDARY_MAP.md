## 3. Escape map

To obtain genuine dynamical outcome selection, a theory must **alter at least one
premise of $\mathcal{C}_{\rm bp}$**, or alter what "outcome/weight" means.
No bigger/louder/longer-memory environment works — that entire route is algebraically closed.

| Door | Premise violated | Frameworks | What is added | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Modify dynamics | unitarity | GRW, CSL, DP | stochastic non-unitary collapse ($\lambda$, $r_0$) | **POSTULATED** — outcome selection by construction |
| 2. Modify state ontology | Hilbert-space-only | Bohmian, hidden variables | additional beables (positions) + $\|\psi\|^2$ measure | **ADDITIONAL_INPUT** |
| 3. Modify outcome meaning | outcome = one realized result | Everett, decoherent/consistent histories | all branches real; indexical/relative outcome | **CONTESTED** — Born-rule derivations disputed (RC-05 Case 03) |
| 4. Modify probability concept | probabilities as physical frequencies | relational QM, QBism | observer-relative belief | **REFRAMED** — moves rather than solves |

**Closed routes (all algebraically excluded by the theorem):** larger environment, chaos,
long memory, non-Markovianity, stronger coupling, alternative pointer observables,
alternative partitions, initially entangled baths.
