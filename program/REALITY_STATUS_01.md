## C. What survives after GRUT-the-explanation is removed

These are *physics questions*, not GRUT claims. Each is the surviving core of an
original GRUT intuition, tested and stripped of failed mechanisms:

- **P-A.** Reality can be represented as a closed quantum system; all apparent
  openness (memory, dissipation, noise) is effective. *Consistent with standard
  theory; not uniquely GRUT.*
- **P-B.** Open-system memory is emergent from coarse-graining. *Standard
  Mori–Zwanzig machinery; GRUT adds no distinct claim beyond the corpus record.*
- **P-C.** Is there a deeper relation between state space, entropy, coarse-graining,
  and the emergence of classical structure — including whether the arrow of time
  is characterizable within a closed microscopic description, or requires
  additional boundary/state information? *Genuinely open.*
- **P-D.** Is complex structure (i) physically fundamental or representational?
  Real-symplectic representation of C^N is observationally equivalent in the
  tested cases; any physical distinction remains unresolved. *Open.*
- **P-E.** Is there a fundamental ontology beneath Hilbert-space/QFT descriptions?
  *Wide open; no candidate on the record closes it.*

"Unity" hypotheses (one thing / one space / one state / one field / one algebra /
one substance) must be kept **distinct** — HISA-01 demonstrated that collapsing
distinct referents is the program's characteristic failure mode.
