## 1. What DP is

The Diósi–Penrose model (DP) proposes that quantum state reduction is a real physical
effect caused by gravity: a spatial superposition of two mass distributions has a
gravitational self-energy difference `E_G`, and the superposition decays on a
timescale `τ ~ ħ/E_G` (Penrose's heuristic formula [S4]). Diósi's line [S1,S2,S3]
supplies an actual stochastic collapse dynamics realizing that heuristic; in its
modern form it is Markovian white-noise collapse dynamics structurally close to CSL [S5,S6].

Primary lineage (each arrow a model modification, not a status upgrade):

| Year | Source | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1984 | Diósi, Phys. Lett. A 105, 199 (1984) | First gravitational localization model |
| 1987 | Diósi, Phys. Lett. A 120, 377 (1987) | Universal master equation with gravity-violated unitarity |
| 1989 | Diósi, Phys. Rev. A 40, 1165 (1989) | Dynamical collapse model, gravity-set timescale |
| 1996 | Penrose, Gen. Rel. Grav. 28, 581 (1996) | Heuristic collapse formula `τ ~ ħ/E_G` |

| 2007 | Markovian white-noise form | Bypasses the original energy non-conservation defect, at the cost of added structure |

| 2021 | [Donadi et al., Nat. Phys. 17, 74–78 (2021), arXiv:2111.13490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.13490) | Gran Sasso underground radiation test: lower bound on the effective size of the nucleon mass density ~3 orders above previous bounds; "rules out the natural parameter-free version of the Diósi-Penrose model" |

## 2. Model reconstruction

- Primitive ontology: mass-density field; definite mass distributions.
- Dynamics: stochastic localization (Markovian white noise in the 2007 form).
- Gravity coupling: collapse timescale set by `E_G` (heuristic, not derived from any
  gravitational field equation).
- Outcomes: definite localized mass distributions, supplied by the postulated dynamics.
- Probability measure: operationally supplied by the same postulated noise framework
  (linear/QND structure); it is model-internal, not derived from unitary QM.

## 3. Relation to the C_bp boundary theorem

DP escapes the tested branch-preserving unitary class by **altering the dynamics (Door 1)**.
The boundary theorem is used here only as a structural classifier, not as disconfirmation:
DP is *outside* `C_bp`, which is exactly why it is not touched by the P replication result.

**POSTULATED COLLAPSE ≠ DERIVED OUTCOME SELECTION.** DP supplies outcome definiteness
and Born weights by postulate. The deeper question — why that stochastic law, why that
measure — is relocated, not answered.

## 5. Verdict

- DP is **not derived**; its collapse dynamics are postulated.
- DP is **predictive** in the frozen-protocol sense: falsifiable parameter space,
  already exercised once — the natural version is empirically excluded.
- DP is a legitimate **Door-1** alternative that escapes `C_bp` by changing dynamics,
  and is therefore immune to the Experiment-P class obstruction.
- Its special standing in the comparative matrix (best compression score + genuine
  empirical leverage) survives **only for modified-parameter variants**; the
  parameter-free version is dead on current data.
- DP does **not** answer outcome selection at the derived level: it postulates the
  mechanism that other frameworks lack.
