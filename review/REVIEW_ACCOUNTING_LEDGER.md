## B. Audit of each residual component

**D_dyn: was anything derived, or moved into the choice of dynamics?**
- **Derived from D** (conditional on D being given):
  - graph / spectral / walk dimensions;
  - causal-order statistics;
  - the invariant measure under rigid D;
  - structural attractors mod gauge.
- **Moved into D** (RELOCATION): explicit targets b, a and the transition entries; ordered-reference existence under all-to-all coupling.
- **Not derived:** D itself (the Hamiltonian, connectivity, propagation rule). → D_dyn is a genuine residual.

**Σ ⊗ H_corr: was a state compressed, or only defined relative to a factorization?**
- **Only defined relative to Σ.**
  - Low correlation, freshness and productness are frame-relative (D8, ΣH-0 Prop. 1).
  - Compatibility must be checked at every grouping (IR-05).
- The special-moment boundary cannot be stated without Σ, and Σ is not selected without preferences, scales or state
  information.
- The H_state → D compressions in H2 are real, but they live in dissipative D, paid for by an environment preparation.
  That preparation is itself an H_corr|Σ-type datum.
- → The coupling is confirmed. No compression of the pair was achieved.
