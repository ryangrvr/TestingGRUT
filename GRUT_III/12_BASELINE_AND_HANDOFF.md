## 2. What a sixth postulate (Stage-3 Card 1) must satisfy — constraints the baseline now makes concrete

A distinctive GRUT claim is, by `07` T2, a Δρ_f ≠ 0 or a non-KMS N_f for a *stated* observable, state and preparation, with a
mechanism and no refitting. The baseline turns that into a checklist. A Card 1 that fails any line is dead before a builder
spends budget:

1. **It cannot break the vacuum's KMS.** In Minkowski and exact de Sitter the restricted vacuum is KMS by
   Bisognano–Wichmann / Gibbons–Hawking — a theorem from Lorentz (de Sitter) invariance and positivity, not an assumption of
   GRUT. So no card can give a Lorentz-invariant vacuum a viscosity or a memory (w = 0 is the sharp form of this; `03`).
   KMS can only be broken where it already fails conventionally: in a non-equilibrium state or a time-dependent background.
2. **In those regimes it must beat a computed conventional answer, not silence.** The baseline fixes the conventional
   non-KMS structure the card competes with: O(ε²) with f₂(x) and D₂(ω) in the conformal sector (`v11`); the shape function
   r(ω) and D = (H³/4π²)[1 − (1 + 2 ln 2)ε] in the massless sector (`v14`); the exact ΛCDM kernel today (`v11` D). A card's Δρ
   must be a definite function that differs from these, computed in the same convention (fixed mean time, ω ≠ 0).
3. **No Gibbs or FDT premise** (charter CV-7/relocation): a card that assumes a temperature and derives N from ρ has
   assumed the conclusion; the baseline shows that is exactly how GRUT I/II "classified" without selecting.
4. **Any new field pays the fifth-force price** (`03`: Cassini γ − 1 = (2.1 ± 2.3)×10⁻⁵; Eöt-Wash λ < 38.6 µm at α = 1), and
   a mechanism that is an added operator in P0's content is conventional physics with a price, not GRUT.
5. **No free function and no free scale.** `v13` showed what a scale does: a coarse-graining cutoff moves the O(ε)
   coefficient anywhere. The card's Δρ must be invariant under such choices or it is not a prediction.
6. **It must name its observable platform out of equilibrium**, at the temperature of a non-equilibrium reference
   (the charter's T_mono), and stay compact.
7. **Where it touches the massless sector it meets data.** The first-order massless-sector structure is what the slow-roll
   corrections to the primordial spectra are made of; the cleanest point of contact is the single-field tensor consistency
   relation n_t = −r/8, fixed by the free tensor sector's first-order structure. A card that modifies that sector at O(ε)
   must either reproduce the relation or predict a definite deviation (a genuine falsifier once r is measured; a pointer,
   not a result).

## 3. The candidate space, as the baseline leaves it

There are exactly three ways to get Δρ ≠ 0 for a fixed observable, and the baseline prices each:

- **Change the state** (same operators). This is conventional non-equilibrium QFT unless the card supplies a *selection
  rule* for the state that P0–P5 do not — and that rule must be parameter-free and must not be "the state is thermal at X"
  (constraint 3). No such rule has been proposed on this branch. In FRW the comoving frame is a natural preferred frame,
  so a state-selection rule is not forbidden by symmetry; it is simply absent.
- **Change the operator content** (new field or coupling). Conventional, priced (constraint 4), and the price list is
  already written; the one historical attempt (CP-05's light field) answered a bounded physical question and did not
  select a hidden sector.
- **Change the identification** (replace P1's "the bath is the causal complement, read by its modular flow"). Then the
  theory is no longer GRUT III; it is a different theory that must be stated from scratch and priced from scratch.

I do not have a candidate that passes all seven lines. That is the honest position to hand to the charter: the constraints
are concrete, the baseline is in place, and the next physics step is a *principle* that selects a non-equilibrium state
without a hidden temperature — not another computation inside P0–P5.
