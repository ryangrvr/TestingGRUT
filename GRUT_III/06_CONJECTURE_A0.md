# 06 — Conjecture: the low-acceleration scale of the responsive horizon

**Grade: CONJECTURE.** Not a prediction of GRUT III; not derived; carried with its discriminating test.
If it fails, nothing in 01–05 changes.

## The structural fact

Jacobson's local-equilibrium derivation (D3) requires, at each point, a local Rindler horizon — at distance
c²/a from an observer of acceleration a — small enough to be "local", in practice a → ∞. In a universe with a
cosmological horizon at c/H, an observer with a < cH has a Rindler horizon *outside* the cosmological one; the
thermal state they see is not Unruh's but the de Sitter–Unruh state with

    T = (ħ / 2π k_B c) √(a² + c²H²)        (Narnhofer–Peter–Thirring 1996; Deser–Levin 1997),

and the Clausius construction as written does not go through. The equation of state therefore has a built-in
acceleration scale, cH, below which the derivation of Einstein's equation is *not licensed by P2*. GRUT III as
formulated says nothing about what happens there; it only says the usual argument stops.

## The empirical coincidence (numbers from `calc/v6`)

| quantity | value (m s⁻²) | ratio to a₀ |
|---|---|---|
| a₀ (radial acceleration relation; McGaugh–Lelli–Schombert 2016) | 1.20 ± 0.02 (±0.24 sys) × 10⁻¹⁰ | 1 |
| cH₀, H₀ = 67.4 / 73.0 | 6.55 / 7.09 × 10⁻¹⁰ | 5.5 / 5.9 |
| cH₀/2π | 1.04 / 1.13 × 10⁻¹⁰ | 0.87 / 0.94 |
| cH₀/6 (Verlinde 2016) | 1.09 / 1.18 × 10⁻¹⁰ | 0.91 / 0.99 |
| c√(Λ/3)/2π | 0.86 / 0.93 × 10⁻¹⁰ | 0.72 / 0.78 |

Milgrom (1999) proposed MOND as a vacuum effect from exactly this temperature; his heuristic gives a₀ ∼ cH₀ to
within an order of magnitude but not the coefficient. Verlinde (2016) obtains cH₀/6 from a volume-law entropy
contribution of the de Sitter medium. Neither is a derivation within GRUT III, and this document does not supply
one.

## The discriminating prediction

If the scale is set by the apparent-horizon temperature, a₀(z) ∝ H(z): a₀(z = 1)/a₀(0) = 1.79, a₀(z = 2)/a₀(0) = 3.03
(flat ΛCDM, Ω_m = 0.315). If by the Λ-horizon, a₀ is constant. Standard MOND assumes constant a₀; ΛCDM has no
a₀ at all. High-redshift rotation curves and the redshift evolution of the baryonic Tully–Fisher relation decide.

## What the conjecture must survive before it can be promoted

CMB acoustic peaks (ΛCDM's third-peak fit requires non-baryonic gravitating matter at z ∼ 1100); cluster masses and
the Bullet Cluster; weak-lensing tests of emergent gravity (Brouwer et al. 2017 consistent; later analyses in
tension); the small intrinsic scatter of the RAR; big-bang nucleosynthesis (untouched). Verlinde's version
currently fails several of these. A GRUT III version would have to do better, and the only honest way to find out
is the computation in `05` O6.
