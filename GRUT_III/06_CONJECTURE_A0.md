# 06 — Appendix: a numerical coincidence, not adopted (a₀ ∼ cH₀)

**Grade: CONJECTURE — Milgrom's, not GRUT's.** Revision 1 argued that Jacobson's local-equilibrium derivation
"is not licensed" for observers with a ≲ cH. That argument was wrong (R1): the local Rindler horizon is an auxiliary
boost horizon through an arbitrary point, its normalisation κ cancels between the heat flux and the temperature
(visible in `v2` §2.1), and no observer's physical acceleration enters. **Retracted.** What survives is the
following, none of it GRUT's.

## The physics that does involve a physical acceleration

A detector with proper acceleration a in de Sitter sees T = (ħ/2πk_B c)√(a² + c²H²) (Narnhofer–Peter–Thirring 1996;
Deser–Levin 1997). Milgrom (1999, "The modified dynamics as a vacuum effect") built MOND inertia on exactly this and
obtained a₀ ∼ cH₀ up to an undetermined coefficient; Verlinde (2016) obtains cH₀/6 from a volume-law entropy
contribution. GRUT III contains no mechanism for either.

## The discriminator (already proposed in the literature)

If the scale is set by the apparent-horizon temperature, a₀(z) ∝ H(z): a₀(z = 1)/a₀(0) = 1.79, a₀(2)/a₀(0) = 3.03
(flat ΛCDM, Ω_m = 0.315); if by the Λ-horizon, a₀ is constant; standard MOND assumes constant a₀. This test has
been discussed before (Milgrom and others); it is recorded here as context, not as a GRUT prediction.
