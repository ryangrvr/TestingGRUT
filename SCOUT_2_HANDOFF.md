## D. System individuation (S2-1, S2-1b)

- **H alone does not define subsystems.** A Hilbert space plus a spectrum individuates nothing.
- **Locality criteria can constrain a TPS.** Under 2-local qubit locality the TPS is non-unique for n ≤ 7 and locally
  unique for n ≥ 8. This is CPR-type, given the criterion.
- The **local dimension, the locality class and the objective remain priced** (and genericity claims are
  Lebesgue-priced).
- **Competing natural locality criteria disagree** (S2-1b):
  - minimal k is degenerate without a supplied number of factors;
  - qubit vs ququart: MDL / graph prefer qubits, Lieb–Robinson / autonomy prefer ququarts;
  - within a fixed d, a Clifford frame splits MDL / loc2 from graph / LR / autonomy;
  - stability is measure-priced.
- **CPR-type uniqueness is scoped to a supplied locality framework** (n, d, k). CPR's equivalence already quotients
  H-preserving unitaries.

## F. Probability / convexity (S2-3, S2-3b, S2-4)

- **Uniquely ergodic dynamics can fix an invariant measure** in special cases: time averages are universal from every
  start. A horocycle flow on a compact quotient does this, but it fails on the non-compact X₂.
- **Mixing does not erase fine-grained preparation information.** Pullback contrast stays 1.0000 up to t = 500. Only
  coarse readouts forget.
- **Operational probability remains access-sensitive**: partition, readout and preparation class.
- **Deterministic hidden dynamics can produce an affine mixing law**, provided the tester is independent (0.3000 = 0.3000).
- **The numerical mixture weights remain access / partition-priced** (the threshold λ; Y-02).

## G. Tomography / shared reference (S2-5, S2-6, H2-7)

- **One real-QM J⊗J tomography obstruction** can be activated by a correlated shared reference: the local difference
  goes 0 → 2.000.
- **Full multipartite local tomography was NOT derived** (Y-05).
- **The reference's existence and state are H. Holding / access to it is A.**
- Ordered reference coherence is an asymptotic statement, not "LT iff ordered phase" (Y-06).
- **Ordered reference coherence can be selected by special dissipative D only under priced conditions.**
  - Works: all-to-all alignment (200 / 200 aligned mod Z₂).
  - Fails with local coupling: ring twisted states (21 / 30 starts); 2D frozen stripes (44%).
  - Noise / disorder turns it into a parameter regime.

- **Multiple local dimensions remain on the fronts**: 5 – 10 types in the 28 main runs, and 3 – 10 across the scale runs.

  rank), normalization / transforms (1 – 5), scales (τ, δ, fragments, ε) and the state.

  supported, full theorem not reproduced (Y-09).

## J. H / arrow results (S2-H2, S2-D-arrow)

- **Contractive / dissipative laws can make system-state basins irrelevant**, with no measure needed.
- **Explicit target parameters are relocation into D**, not elimination.
- **Structural attractors give true, scoped H compression** (mod gauge; tuning / non-locality / arrow-priced).
- **Exact global unitary dynamics preserves information**: global trace distance is constant to 12 digits, and
  bijective contractions are exactly invertible.
- **Finite closed unitary systems admit no non-constant continuous every-state arrow over any fixed positive horizon.**
  This is D0: recurrence, Theorem 1, and Corollary 1′ by concatenating U_{kT}-shifted windows (Y-11).
- **States fixed covariantly by H alone are stationary** (Proposition 2).
- **Typicality gives equilibrium appearance, not a temporal direction**: P(increase) = 0.499 ± 0.013 on an energy shell.
- **Low environment entropy is neither necessary nor sufficient** (§B).
- **Arrows require a special correlation / freshness structure relative to Σ.** A reused bath re-purifies the system;
  correlated ancillas break Markovianity.
- **Records reverse under the reversed boundary state** (redundancy 6 → 0).
- **Orientation remains unselected** (Janus symmetry, exact).

## L. Joint ΣH result (S2-ΣH; with REPAIR 06)

- **Every pure state admits many product-making factorizations.** For a fixed product target ray the frames form a coset
  of dimension (N−1)² + 1. The **full product-making family** F_ψ has dimension (N−1)² + 1 + 2Σ_i(d_i − 1) and
  codimension 2N − 2 − 2Σ_i(d_i − 1) in U(N) (Y-13).
- **State independence alone selects nothing.**
- **Compatible H / ψ pairs can reveal a native frame**: one LU class, robust to priority orders and tolerance sweeps.
  But **the compatibility is the supplied boundary datum**, which is non-generic.
- **Generic incompatible pairs give Pareto nonuniqueness.** No tolerance winner is shared. Ranking correlation first
  returns the trivial ψ-product frame.
- **The local dimension / grouping remains unselected even in compatible cases**: 32×2, 16×4 and 8×8 are tied. Only a
  "min k" priority or MDL picks qubits.
- **C_min gives a time-translation-covariant TPS with a relative boundary epoch** (0 → −2.25). A fixed-epoch C₀ loses
  dominance under the shift.
- **Averaging and typical-time prescriptions fail the positive control.**
- **Orientation remains unselected**: correlation is exactly symmetric about the special moment.
- **MDL depends on the description language and the relative coding price**: 3 – 6 winners over 45 settings.
