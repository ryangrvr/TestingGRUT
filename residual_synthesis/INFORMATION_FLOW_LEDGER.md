## Scope note on SCOUT "TRUE DERIVATION (scoped)"

SCOUT-2 (reviewed) graded several results **TRUE DERIVATION (scoped)**:
- uniquely ergodic time averages;
- the affine law;
- H2 structural attractors (tuned / non-local);
- the D0 no-go;
- ΣH-0 Proposition 1.

These derive operational C5 structure from (H, ψ) inside the finite envelope. **They are the reductions that defined the
residual; they do not eliminate a residual component**, and none of them appears as an elimination in any later campaign.

They are therefore **not** counted in class E. This preserves both scopes per the hard rule: SCOUT's derivation
vocabulary and the Bridge / QFT / Gravity TRUE COMPRESSION count are different ledgers.

## Central distinction (stated once)

    forbidden class (A)  ≠  conditional reconstruction (C)  ≠  information eliminated (E)

- **A** shrinks a class.
- **C** moves supplied information upstream into a seed + rule.
- **E** would remove supplied information.

Every positive-looking result across S, B, Q and G is in A, B, C or D. **None is in E.**
