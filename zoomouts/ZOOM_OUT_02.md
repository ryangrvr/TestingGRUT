## The ten questions

1. **Derived or renamed?** Each C5 component has been **re-expressed**, either:
   - as dynamical-structure conditions (D): locality, continuous reversibility, unique ergodicity,
     incommensurability; or
   - as basin / state data (H): the shared frame's ordered phase, the preparation class.

   Nothing is derived from nothing.
2. **Where did the measure enter?**
   - Only in genericity claims, MaxEnt, basin weights and mixing relaxation.
   - The unique-ergodicity route is the single measure-free route, and it does not forget preparations.
3. **Where did access enter?**
   - Coarse partitions (S2-3);
   - hidden coins (S2-4);
   - who holds which reference (S2-5).
4. **Is the subsystem decomposition unique?** Given a locality criterion, and only for large systems.
5. **Is composition unique?** Up to the field, given continuous reversible interaction. The field reduces to H
   (S2-5/S2-6).
6. **Did a basin sneak in?** Yes, and it now carries the most weight:
   - ℝ vs ℂ;
   - the preparation class;
   - basin weights.
7. **Is an observer assumed?** As a second, *independent* subsystem (S2-4) holding a *shared* reference (S2-5).
   The observer is individuated subsystem structure plus independence plus a shared state.
8. **Did information decrease?** No primitive was eliminated. Information **moved downward**: from operational
   axioms (C5) to properties of the primitive dynamics (D) and of the universe's state (H).
9. **Has the C5 boundary moved?** **Yes.** The irreducible remainder is now:

   > **D:** locality, continuous reversible interaction, unique ergodicity / incommensurability of the primitive
   > dynamics
   >
   > **+ H:** the basin / state of the universe (shared ordered frames, preparation class, measure)

   **C5 as an operational layer is not irreducible.** It is a **dynamical + state layer** in disguise.
10. **The single hostile example that would destroy this:** a C5 component that cannot be re-expressed as D + H.
    Candidates:
    - the *locality criterion itself* (S2-1b);
    - the *local dimension* (C5-G).

    Either could be an irreducible kinematic choice outside D + H.

## Theorem recognition — candidate T2-1 (C5 = D ⊕ H)

> **Candidate.** In the toy universes tested, each C5 component is equivalent, as a supplied-information item, to
> one of:
> - (D) a structural property of the primitive dynamics: locality of H, continuous reversible interaction, unique
>   ergodicity / incommensurability; or
> - (H) a state / basin / measure datum of the universe: a shared ordered reference field, a preparation class, a
>   reference measure.
>
> **Status:** classification over 6 probes; not a theorem. The **measure / basin layer H** is the one no mechanism
> has touched.
