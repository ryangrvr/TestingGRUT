## The ten questions

1. **Derived or renamed?**
   - **Partly derived:**
     - TPS uniqueness given locality (S2-1);
     - exclusion of polytope composites given continuous interaction (S2-2);
     - measure-free frequencies under unique ergodicity (S2-3);
     - mixture weights (S2-4).
   - **Renamed:**
     - individuation becomes a locality criterion;
     - composition becomes continuous reversible dynamics;
     - randomness becomes access + independence.
2. **Where did the measure enter?** Only where "generic / typical / a.e." was invoked (S2-1 spectra; S2-3 mixing;
   S2-4 rational independence). The **uniquely ergodic** route is the one place a measure is genuinely earned.
3. **Where did access enter?** S2-3 (coarse partition, records), S2-4 (hidden coin). Not in S2-1/S2-2.
4. **Is the subsystem decomposition unique?** Only above a size threshold and **given** a locality criterion.
5. **Is composition unique?** Up to the field, given continuous reversible interaction.
6. **Did a basin sneak in?** In S2-3: the preparation class (a.c. vs singular) *is* basin / measure data.
7. **Is an observer assumed?** S2-4 makes it explicit: the tester is a second subsystem, and its **independence** is
   the price. This links C5-D/E back to C5-A.
8. **Did information decrease?**
   - Specification is **re-expressed** in dynamical primitives: a locality criterion; continuous reversible
     interaction; uniquely ergodic hidden subsystems; independence.
   - No primitive was **eliminated**.
9. **Has the C5 boundary moved?** Yes, by a level of description. C5 can be rewritten as **dynamical-structure
   conditions**:
   - locality of H;
   - continuous reversible interaction;
   - unique ergodicity / incommensurability;
   - access partition.

   **New candidate floor ("D-layer"):** *properties of the primitive dynamics and its decomposition into mutually
   independent subsystems.*
10. **The single hostile example that would destroy this:** a C5 structure derived from dynamics **without** any of
    locality criterion / continuity / unique ergodicity / access being chosen. The S2-3 hostile (horocycle: uniquely
    ergodic + mixing) is the nearest candidate to *strengthen* the synthesis, by removing the forgetting /
    earned-measure tension. It is queued as S2-3b.

## Emerging synthesis (candidate)

> **C5 ≈ D (dynamical-structure conditions) + access.**
> - Individuation, composition, convexity and probability can each be re-expressed as properties of the primitive
>   dynamics: locality, continuous reversibility, unique ergodicity / incommensurability.
> - Each also needs one **access / independence** structure.
> - No probe has removed the need to *choose* those dynamical properties.
