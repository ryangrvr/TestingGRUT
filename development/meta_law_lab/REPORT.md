- A two-bit register produces a preparation/probe response matrix I₄,
  rank four. The factorization H=AB proves rank(H)≤N for any common
  classical hidden-state model with at most N states. Thus it excludes all

  Quantum dimension bounds are different. A finite-data enclosure is

- A standard Pólya urn and a fixed beta-distributed latent parameter have

  two stated causal mechanisms. A fixed **count-state** urn reproduces
