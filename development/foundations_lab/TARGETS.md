# Targets and strategic ranking

**DRAFT — external review pending.** Rankings are judgment about achievable
scientific discrimination, not evidence probabilities, GRUT scores, or
candidate selection. There is no complete theory of reality in this audit.
A dynamical law can be useful without uniquely determining its initial state
or every emergent object. Requiring an explanation of literally everything
before accepting one new restriction would prevent ordinary scientific progress.

## Rank eight possible scientific targets

The ranking favors a clear baseline, identifiable evidence and a finite
failure condition. It does not order the philosophical importance of the
questions. No candidate law or draft card is specified.

| Rank | Target | Why it earns this position | Required output / kill boundary | Empirical handle / physics to recover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Records, interventions and model discrimination | Fits GRUT's proved operational strengths; controlled systems and conventional comparators exist | A predeclared observable distinguishes dynamics rather than interface drift. Kill a claimed fundamental advance if standard dynamics already forces it. | Controlled record/response trajectories; recover ordinary QM/open-system/thermodynamic controls in their scope. |
| 2 | Bootstrap / consistency as conditional selectors | Existing examples really remove allowed physical possibilities | Explicit baseline, exact constraint and excluded admissible model. Kill claims of assumption-free selection if gaps/spectrum/representation inputs do the selecting. | Spectrum/scattering or critical-point data; recover the relevant QFT/EFT consistency conditions, not every cosmological parameter at once. |
| 3 | Emergent spacetime / gravity | Large genuine missing-physics target and several concrete experimental channels | Quantitative low-energy matching plus a distinct observable. Kill an operational route when its signal is indistinguishable from mediator/noise alternatives at feasible precision. | Gravity mediator, clock or GW observable; recover GR, appropriate quantum matter and tested causal/Lorentz limits. |
| 4 | Quantum structure / composition | Precise finite countermodels and reconstruction theorems; measurable operational content | Necessary versus sufficient assumptions and a separating task. Kill a claim of unique QM from an axiom subset by an admissible GPT counterexample. | Independent-source network/composition tasks; recover tested quantum probabilities and composition before claiming new gravity. |
| 5 | GRUT joint generation | Exposes supplied partitions and responses; worth preserving as a focused question | Representation-covariant operational identity and response restriction beyond standard coarse graining. Kill promotion if uniqueness or extra freedom loss is absent. | Held-out driven kernels under certified access; recover known persistence/response and R1 carrier semantics. Feasibility awaits a distinct restriction. |
| 6 | Cosmological arrow / boundary selection | Important open question, but ordinary dynamics and special boundaries must be distinguished | A boundary account with extra observable content. Kill a claim of explanation if it merely renames a low-entropy initial premise. | Cosmological correlation/entropy-history predictions; recover statistical mechanics and viable cosmology. Near-term discrimination uncertain. |
| 7 | Information geometry as a fundamental selector | Powerful established inference geometry but broad physical underdetermination | Need an independent bridge fixing dynamics/geometry, not QFI renamed as gravity. C06 kills uniqueness from contractivity alone. | Tomography/metrology can test model metrics; new-law discrimination absent without a further relation. Recover QM geometry and any claimed GR limit. |
| 8 | Numerical constants / all field content | Potentially decisive, currently highly vulnerable to hidden target insertion | Parameter-free or externally calibrated dimensionless relation, no inverse-fit or post hoc mode filter. Kill if the desired values determine operator coefficients or selected modes. | Independent precision constants/particle data after fixed RG mapping; recover interacting field content and known running. No feasible untuned selector demonstrated. |

Ranks 1 and 2 concern **how to discriminate or select**; they are not yet a
new theory. Rank 3 is the strongest route for genuinely new physical content
if the program pivots toward an established experimental community. The
ranking does not authorize Stage 3 or imply that three slots can cover all
eight targets. Sources and experimental limitations are detailed in
`FRAMEWORKS.md` and `EXPERIMENTS.md`.
