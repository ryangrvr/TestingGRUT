a bounded primary-source sweep, not an exhaustive limits compilation. “Null”
means no claimed signal for the tested model in that analysis; it does not
mean all alternatives are excluded. None of these platforms is certified

The gravity-entanglement interpretation changed enough that a 2017-only
survey would be misleading. Likewise, the latest DESI update makes it unsafe
to quote an older tension as settled new dark-energy physics. The review
therefore favors operational tests with explicit alternative likelihoods,
not opportunities chosen because a headline sounds foundational.

## Which platforms plausibly help now?

**Most immediate for a GRUT operational test:** a controlled quantum/thermal
record experiment with independently calibrated readout and intervention
channels. It can expose interface drift, state-preparation dependence and
ordinary nonequilibrium response. It is not a lock until a proposed
restriction, comparator and feasibility certificate are supplied.

**Most promising pivot for new physical content:** matter-wave/levitated
tests of specified departures from QM, and gravitational mediator tests
with explicit alternative dynamics. These already have meaningful controls
and exclusion outcomes. Apparatus access and quantitative noise forecasts
were not certified here; this audit does not promise that a new experiment
can be built within the three-card budget.

**Least ready as a first discriminator:** cosmological fractions, exact
constants inferred from adjustable spectral filters, and conscious
experience as a putative collapse variable. They currently lack the fixed
intervention/measurement semantics needed for the claimed GRUT target.

No unexplained anomaly in this finite sweep was shown to force GRUT or QIH.
This is **NOT FOUND in the inspected sources**, not a proof that all physics
is explained or that no future anomaly exists.
