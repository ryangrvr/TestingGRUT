# What established frameworks select, and what they still supply

**DRAFT — external review pending.** This is a bounded comparison, not an
exhaustive survey or a claim that one listed approach is confirmed quantum
gravity. “Success” separates experimentally tested parent theory from
conditional mathematical success. Source IDs resolve in `SOURCES.json`.
No approach is scored as a GRUT mechanism and no Stage-3 candidate is written.

## Selection mechanisms: genuine restrictions versus parameterization

“Removes freedom” always refers to a specified admissible set and supplied
assumptions. It is not a claim that those assumptions were generated.

| Mechanism / sources | Restriction actually obtained | Required background | What remains free / dimensionless coupling status | Relation to observations and GRUT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anomaly cancellation [F17] | Some otherwise allowed quantum gauge theories/charge assignments are inconsistent; C11 reduces charge ratios to one normalization | Gauge group, chiral representations, Yukawa assumptions | Overall gauge coupling, representation choice, generations and masses | Conditional quantum consistency, not fitting only. This is a model of a nondefinitional restriction. |
| Soft gauge/gravity consistency [F18] | Conservation of charges and universal coupling of a soft massless spin-2 field under stated S-matrix assumptions | Lorentz-covariant scattering, particles, factorization and soft limit | Spectrum, coupling magnitudes, higher interactions and backgrounds | Very strong standard restrictions; a renamed universal-coupling statement is not new GRUT physics. |
| Crossing, analyticity, unitarity and positivity [F12] | Exclude EFT coefficient signs/regions under dispersion assumptions | Suitable causal S-matrix, UV behavior and controlled IR subtractions | Many coefficients, scales, massless/IR caveats | Some measurable inequalities. Must define baseline including these before claiming extra restriction. |
| Conformal bootstrap / islands [F14,F15] | Crossing plus unitarity, symmetry and gaps can bound/isolate CFT data | Dimension, global symmetry and operator/gap assumptions | Which CFT describes nature; relevant deformations and scales | Precision Ising universality success is conditional; an island is not an assumption-free unique universe. |
| RG fixed points / universality [F22,F37] | Irrelevant microscopic differences can vanish at long distances; finite relevant directions can reduce parameter freedom | Dynamics/field class, RG transformation and a controlled fixed point | Relevant couplings and UV completion | Explains why different microphysics shares effective behavior; not proof of one microscopic identity selector. |
| Topological quantization [F38] | Integer invariant restricts robust response, e.g. Hall conductance in appropriate gapped systems | Hamiltonian, gap, topology, charges and boundary/control assumptions | Microscopic kinetic details, dimensional scales and phase selection | Clear measurable quantization. A good pattern for future restrictions, not a new candidate here. |
| Symmetry / variational principle [F03,F24,F26] | Invariance restricts admissible terms; an action supplies equations | Symmetry, variables, action or functional to extremize | Many invariant terms and coefficients; action itself is often the premise | Useful selection only after the allowed family is explicit. “Stationarity” without a functional is not an equation. |
| Information causality / local tomography / purification [F07,F09] | Exclude particular operational alternatives | Composition, allowed information tasks and a full axiom package | Dynamics, particles, constants; alternative axiom packages | C08–C09 are hostile alternatives. These constraints do not uniquely select all physics alone. |
| Complete passivity / KMS [F39] | Completely passive states are ground or nonnegative-temperature KMS states under the theorem's setting | Algebra and time evolution already specified; unrestricted tensor copies/cyclic controls | Hamiltonian, temperature and bath coupling | Derives equilibrium form conditionally, not kinetic activity; no Gibbs premise should be smuggled into a selector. |
| Swampland constraints [F23] | Proposed restrictions on EFTs admitting quantum-gravity completion | Particular quantum-gravity evidence and conjectural criteria | Multiple EFTs/vacua; status of individual conjectures | Conjecture must not be scored as theorem or experimentally established law. |
| Minimality / self-consistency alone [F36] | No general unique-world result established by these words | A specified language/class and consistency conditions | Typically many consistent descriptions | Equivalent descriptions may be equally physical. A selector must name the independently admissible possibilities it excludes. |

**TC-1 rescaling/relabeling control:** dimensionful values cannot be selected
by changing units or renaming coordinates. Anomaly ratios in C11 remove
relative charge freedom while leaving normalization/coupling freedom visible.
QIH's phase/frequency identifications and prime labels must likewise survive
permitted coordinate and label changes; a supplied reference scale, basis or
mode filter is an input. Dimensionless target matching after fitting the
operator is not rescued by choosing natural units.

The real-versus-complex quantum comparison has an actual empirical route:
independent-source network tests exclude the specified natural real-composite
model under their source-independence assumptions. Arbitrary multipartite
real embeddings with an extra shared resource are a different admissible
class, so the result is not a metaphysical proof that real numbers cannot
describe any quantum calculation. C09 is only the finite local-tomography
control, not a reproduction of those network experiments. [F41,E19]

## Is GRUT's joint identity/response demand fundamental?

The strongest case **for** the target is that equilibrium/persistence summaries
miss kinetic geometry. A controlled microscopic account must specify both
what remains robust and what happens under interventions. GRUT's demand
exposes a common error: adding a response equation after claiming to have
generated the system. Relational structure, robust local algebras and accessible
record channels may be useful bridges between microscopic dynamics and
operational observers. [G01–G06,F16,F27,F35]

The strongest case **against making it the primary missing law** is that a
fully specified ordinary microscopic dynamics already supplies persistence
and counterfactual transitions. The kinetic theorems prove underdetermination
by restricted summaries, not underdetermination by complete physics. C07
shows why an absolute unique factorization cannot be demanded from a bare
Hilbert space. Duality, gauge invariance, universality and multiple realization
further suggest that physically meaningful identity can be approximate and
relational, with equivalent microscopic descriptions. An exact investigator-
independent partition may be the wrong invariant to demand. This is an
inference from the comparison, not a general no-go theorem. [F04,F06,F16,F37]

Operational causality has the same boundary: unobserved mechanisms cannot be
identified without restrictions on hidden variables, intervention semantics
and readout changes. R1's protocol-dependent-readout construction is an exact
counterexample within its declared class. Holding those semantics fixed is an
operational assumption to certify, not a new influence law. Predictive-state
minimality can identify records adequate for a task without proving which
latent ontology is unique. [G04,F35]

**A discriminating challenge to fundamentality:** fix a standard microscopic
model and local operational algebra independently, derive its robust records
and driven kernels, and compare them with GRUT's proposed invariants. If all
GRUT observables and identities follow by standard coarse graining and the
purported new relation follows from existing constraints, fundamentality has
not been demonstrated. Conversely, a restriction that excludes an explicit
otherwise admissible conventional comparator, survives equivalent
representations, and is experimentally measurable would justify reopening
the fundamental claim. This states the needed distinction; it supplies no
candidate restriction and consumes no card.
