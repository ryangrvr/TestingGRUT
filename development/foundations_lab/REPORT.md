## 3. Where GRUT is probably overreaching

The inference **“summaries leave kinetics free, therefore fundamental physics
needs a new joint generator”** is not established. Standard microscopic
physics supplies transition geometry once its couplings, state and
interventions are specified. A generator may still explain why those inputs
take their values, but that is a stronger selection problem than the kinetic
counterexample proves.

Likewise, a single exact, observer-independent partition may be an inappropriate
target across gauge descriptions, dualities, effective theories and multiple
realizations. A physically meaningful local algebra, accessible channel and
robust operational identity can be relational and approximate. These facts
do not prove GRUT impossible; they show why its demanded invariant must be
specified before demanding a universal selector. [F04–F06,F16,F27,F35,F37]

Recovering QM, thermodynamics, gravity, constants, cosmic initial conditions
and phenomenal consciousness from one first card is not a finite first
milestone. A law need not select every initial condition to be a law. Keep
the broad destination, but require one precise extra operational restriction
before promoting the fundamental claim. Consciousness currently supplies no
verified physical selector; record formation, functional agents and subjective
experience must stay distinct. [N01–N08; `OBSERVERS.md`]

## 7. Decision

**CONTINUE GRUT BUT BROADEN/REFRAME.** Make operational discrimination the
near-term criterion; keep joint generativity as a hypothesis whose
fundamentality must be earned. Reframing is recommended, not implemented as
a canonical ruling in this lane.

Subjective recommendation weights, not measured likelihoods or truth
probabilities: **70% reframe, 25% pause/pivot, 4% continue as primary, 1%
abandon**. Confidence that a new fundamental identity-response degree of
freedom has *not yet been demonstrated*: **95%**. This is distinct from a
probability that GRUT can never work.

**Strongest counterargument:** ordinary Hamiltonians solve kinetics only
after the Hamiltonian and local algebra are supplied. A deeper principle
could constrain them jointly across sectors, and GRUT's accounting may be
precisely what prevents hidden premises. Reframing could dilute that ambition.
The reply is empirical: retain the ambition, but require an excluded
conventional possibility and an independently measurable restriction before
claiming its fundamentality. No such new restriction was established here.
