# Records, observers, and consciousness are different empirical targets

**DRAFT — external review pending.** No consciousness variable is introduced
as a physical selector. Sources N01–N06 and F27–F35 are in `SOURCES.json`.

| Level | Operational meaning | What conventional mechanisms can explain | What has not thereby been established |
|---|---|---|---|
| Detector record | Stable, accessible correlation after an interaction | Amplification, metastability, environmental decoherence | Awareness or a unique outcome interpretation from decoherence alone |
| Memory | Retention of a usable record across a declared interval/noise class | Dynamical storage, error correction, redundancy | An observer supplied by an assumption-free universal law |
| Causal agent | A persistent system selecting interventions that change its surroundings | Feedback and control under specified dynamics | Conscious intention or a new physical influence law |
| Predictive / self model | Records used to forecast outcomes, including the system's own actions and boundaries | Predictive states and learning in a specified environment | Unique latent ontology or phenomenal experience |
| Functional observerhood | Persistent organization plus accessible records and counterfactual transition abilities | Recurrent causal mechanisms, effective connectivity, memory and control | A new fundamental influence object, or experience causing collapse |
| Functional consciousness | Access, report, flexible integration and control capacities relevant to a stated theory | Competing neural mechanisms with testable contrasts | Identity with subjective experience by definition, or a fundamental physical consciousness field |
| Phenomenal consciousness | Subjective experience, distinguished from report or task performance | Several empirically investigated neural theories; no consensus explanation | A justified new quantum/gravity law or a measured universal consciousness field |

The supplied kinetic result is relevant to functional observerhood: the same
persistence summary can hide different transition mechanisms. It does not
show that conscious experience selects those mechanisms, or that ordinary
microscopic brain dynamics cannot determine them.

|---|---|---|

| Perturbational complexity [N06] | A measure of differentiated/integrated brain responses to stimulation, useful for state discrimination | A validated response index is not proof of an ontological theory of consciousness or a universal consciousness threshold. |
| 2025 adversarial collaboration and responses [N04,N07] | Preregistered IIT/GNWT comparisons, results challenging some predictions, with subsequent disagreement over interpretation | Report experimental contrasts and methodological dispute; neither a final winner nor evidence for QIH. |

The Padgett-associated 2013 neural case report concerns a person's
mathematical imagery. It provides no measurement of a holographic horizon,
microtubule quantum computation, or consciousness-driven collapse. [N01]
The QIH microtubule extensions supply qubits and coupling/threshold machinery
rather than deriving neural observers from their purported universal
operator. A detector can record an event while no conscious observer reads it.

**Conclusion:** keep phenomenal consciousness downstream of the physical
record and functional-observer problems. This is a program decision justified
by current evidential separation, not a theorem that consciousness can never
matter to fundamental physics. The audited sources provide no reproducible
evidence requiring subjective experience to modify quantum collapse.
