# SCOUT-2 CHARTER — the C5 Origin Campaign (owner charter, recorded)

**Fences:**
- GRUT-RAI, TestingGRUT, SCOUT-0 and SCOUT-1 are not modified. SCOUT-0/1 are frozen evidence (`provenance/`).
- The work is non-canonical. Promotion-worthy results are labelled CANONICAL-CANDIDATE only.

## Question

> **Can C5 itself — individuation, composition, locality / independence, states / effects, probability, tomography,
> dimension, and basin / preparation — emerge from a more primitive structure, or is it genuinely irreducible?**

## Strategy

- **Start BELOW C5.** Do not begin from reconstruction axioms, which start *at* C5.
- **Greenfield toy universes are allowed**, and need not be GRUT. Find the mechanism first. Ask about the GRUT bridge
  only after hostile survival (`GRUT_BRIDGE.md`, later).
- **Central test:** for every construction, separate *structure inserted* from *structure forced*. Mark any target
  that is defined into existence `DEFINITIONAL — NOT DERIVED`.
- **The measure problem is central.** Every use of "generic", "typical", "random", "almost surely", "natural" must
  name its measure (`ledgers/MEASURE_ORIGIN_LEDGER.md`).
- **Observer / access loop.** Never silently define systems by observer controls and observers by systems. Track
  interventions, measurement capability, readout, coarse-graining and records.

## Success and failure

**Strong success:** a chain
`primitive → unique subsystems → composition law → convex state space → affine probability → local tomography`
in which every arrow survives hostile alternatives. Partial success (for example, primitive causal independence ⇒ a
unique composition rule) also moves the boundary.

**Failure theorems are equally valuable.** Examples:
- "every operational reconstruction presupposes an intervention / measure / system-equivalence structure equivalent
  to C5";
- "subsystem decomposition is non-identifiable from closed dynamics alone";
- "probability cannot emerge from a deterministic ontology without a supplied measure or access rule".

## Prediction test

If a C5 structure is derived, immediately ask whether it **forces a new observable**: dimension, composition rule,
superselection, forbidden correlations, information capacity, an exact probability relation, or a deviation from
ordinary QM. "Complex QM emerges" alone is not a GRUT prediction.

## Zoom-out (every 3–4 probes): ten questions

1. Derived or renamed?
2. Where did the measure enter?
3. Where did access enter?
4. Is the subsystem decomposition unique?
5. Is composition unique?
6. Did a basin sneak in?
7. Is an observer assumed?
8. Did information decrease?
9. Has the C5 boundary moved?
10. Which single hostile example would destroy the synthesis?
