## The next bounded construction challenge

Choose one explicit geometry-protection architecture and supply its
primitive checks or microscopic interaction terms before another large
trajectory. In an independently defined candidate metric, show that an
off-metric transport event requires a virtual word whose effective
length grows with receiver distance, or prove a comparably strong
selection/interference theorem. Pay for the number of such words.

A code/constraint proposal must calculate its syndromes under **each**
primitive geometry move. In particular, exhibit how the activation-
transport-restoration sequence fails to return to the protected sector
in a constant number of operations. A mere energy penalty for the
temporary syndrome, or a large constant detuning, leaves the GC1
obstruction intact in that class.

Use the full Schur complement or a controlled connected expansion.
Distinguish a sufficient weighted-norm estimate from a necessary
condition: an extensive global norm can fail to converge while a valid
connected transport bound still exists. Do not prove locality by
discarding virtual geometry states, nor by using an arbitrary
nonlocal eigenbasis as the definition of events.

The formation calculation must then start from the original neutral
preparation with every added register and reservoir state specified.
Report the probabilities of all recording outcomes, preparation work,
probe injection/sector changes, and conditional source variance. A
constant spectral gap cannot populate a band absent in the initial
spectral weight under autonomous evolution. The old vacuum formation
identity does not supply a new phase or a dressed-probe preparation.

Finally include the original charged CP-12 excitations in the low-energy
accounting. The two new probe registers alone cannot establish universal
matter locality or a spin-2 gravitational continuum.
