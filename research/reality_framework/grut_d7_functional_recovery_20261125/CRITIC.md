# D7 adversarial audit

## The observable

The receiver is an actual qubit and the benchmark is the same Bell
projector for either b. The score verifies preservation of an
input channel on a fixed maximally entangled preparation. It
does not grade a layout or reward a shape associated with a
hidden label. A device that replaces every payload with one
preselected state gets fidelity 1/4 independently of that choice.
This catches false success on a known single-state test.

## Channels and causal order

At preparation the input and isolated reference share the Bell
pair. Neither carries the unknown source. The clock law is the
same for both source values. On the ordered path the latch G
is committed before the payload is loaded. The only precommitment
path from B to the primary decision is phase probe P to memory M.
The alternative passive coin is independent of B. The separate
direct port explicitly copies B and wins through a different
allowed interaction.

The payload necessarily experiences B at the disturbance link.
The compensation term gets no independent label of B. For the
Bell preparation, the payload marginal at that point is I/2
under either sign. Arbitrary joint processing of the exposed
programme and that payload is already included in the optimal
bound. Access to R, a second B query, pre-disturbance redundant
encoding, or an alternate physical path changes that bound's
hypotheses. R is outside all link supports. The archived probe,
mask and displaced record are also outside the correction term.
The no-assay arm removes information *before commitment* while
leaving the physical disturbance intact.

The device is reversible. Prefixes of the ordered map describe
the operations at sites, not a succession of measured clock
positions. The exact endpoint implements the full order, and
the unconditioned receiver calculation bounds incomplete
histories in the finite window. A different physical control
that accesses the earlier clock sectors or re-enters a source
station is a different experiment.

## Meaning of the two baselines

A scrambled pointer at the old calibrated angle is an
intervention on available information at fixed coupling. It
can perform worse than a properly optimized blind recovery.
The reported 4.1731-point shallow gain uses the latter as the
main comparator. This matters because even without a record the
shallow mixture retains some coherence. The deep mixture does
not, and the corresponding 0.50 ceiling holds for arbitrary
downstream processing of that one exposed payload.

The theorem gives coherent recovery its strongest allowed
inputs. The present classical pointer matches it. The bound
depends on the phase ensemble and accessible programme states,
so this calculation does not certify general performance with
a fixed pointer under different noise families.

## What was supplied

The geometry, movement schedule, near-terminal readout
window, prepared Bell resource, prior distribution and calibrated
actuator strengths were all specified. The optimization chooses a
fixed numerical strength from a known pair of possible dynamics.
The system acquires the sign but does not discover a noise model
or select its own probe or resources. Equal energy laws are
useful to check the comparison and do not by themselves enforce
equal access. Readout and reinitialization are outside the closed
episode. The physical reproduction is a second numerical
implementation in this packet. External experimental validation
