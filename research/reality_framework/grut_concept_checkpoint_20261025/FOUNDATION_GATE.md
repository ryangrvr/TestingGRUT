# Returning to the principle: content before a new construction

This is a program checkpoint, not an additional GRUT postulate. The owner
prefers taking stock before another interaction is added to the dark sector.
The current review's final verdict remains pending.

## Retain the null result

All baths being internal is compatible with the conventional models already
used. It has not selected a state, vacuum density, coupling, universal clock
scale or new observable in the tested campaigns. Compactness, finite state
capacity, a specific observer dictionary and vacuum equilibration are extra
assumptions; their adoption must not be represented as consequences of the
word "closed."

The concept can be stated consistently now, but its independent explanatory
content has not been demonstrated. A successful conventional scalar EFT
would establish that module's viability, not by itself an extra closure law.

## A proposal must name a restriction and its deciding comparison

For a claim that closure restricts the adopted framework, fix the operational
interface: preparations, accessible measurements, resources and conditions.
Let S_base be the possible operational distributions under the supplied
framework, and S_C those allowed after the proposed extra condition C.

The substantive claim is a **strict** restriction S_C subsetneq S_base,
with an explicit baseline model/state excluded by C. Merely rewriting the
same distributions using internal clocks or environment labels does not
establish strictness. Any claimed distinction must survive allowed nuisance
changes at that same interface. If a proposal changes the dynamics instead,
state that new dynamics explicitly and compare its predictions directly.

Before building another large implementation, write:

1. The exact additional postulate and which quantities it constrains.
2. Its compatibility with constraints, conservation and the observer's
   operational description in a specified regime.
3. A baseline counterexample that the postulate forbids, with a derivation
   rather than a label or desired final state.
4. One fixed discriminator and comparator, chosen before tuning parameters
   or inspecting a new held-out outcome.
5. The inputs that remain supplied and a clear failure condition.

This applies to a new restriction in a fixed adopted framework. It is not a
general claim that every possible new theory must be a subset of existing
physics.

## The three unfinished foundations

| Target | A concrete advance would supply | Already-tested shortcut to avoid repeating |
|---|---|---|
| Gravity/geometry | Physical propagation and universal matter response from stated microscopic dynamics, with a common operational metric | Calling a persistent graph or circulating structure a physical spatial metric without transport checks |
| Observers/time | A consistent state/observable dictionary and a consequence it fixes beyond a supplied state and clock | State restriction, trace matching or modular KMS presented as state selection |
| Vacuum | An action and state/preparation rule that select a late vacuum density, remain stable under stated corrections and yield a fixed test | Assigning a final equilibrium constant or rewriting the de Sitter entropy identity as a prediction |

No priority choice among unsolved constructions is presented as a new
scientific result. The immediate step back is this inventory and the
requirement for additional content. It does not initiate another scan or
declare a foundation solved.

## Genesis decision after the critic

Preserve the current numerical result under its late-number decay and
equilibrium assumptions. If a wider bound is established, archive it as a
worked negative with its domain. If it instead identifies a controlled
improving direction or a missing process, state its cost and decide on a
single bounded follow-up. Out-of-equilibrium CP-violating Y decay is a
possible different mechanism requiring its own CP source, rates, preparation
and debris accounting; it is not constructed or adopted by this checkpoint.
