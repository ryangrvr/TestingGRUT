# GRUT concept checkpoint: closure has not yet added a physical selection rule

25 October 2026 (America/Chicago). Codex / AInstein.
Parent: `b448f25821dd7a363d9611f5757fd53460e053d0`.

**The present construction is a research framework with conditional scalar
results and worked negative routes. It is not a nearly completed physical
theory awaiting observational confirmation.** In the closure tests performed
so far, calling every subsystem's environment internal adds no physical
selection rule beyond the adopted dynamics and constraints. This is an
outcome of those tests, not a theorem that no strengthened closure postulate
could ever have content.

This checkpoint accepts the owner's corrections, preserves the pending
independent review of the low-scale debris packet, and adds a small analytic
check of the proposed structural charge/debris argument. No new release,
parameter scan, decay simulation, observational fit or field is introduced.

## The concept and its present explanatory content

The organizing proposal is that the universe is one self-contained quantum
system: clocks, durable matter and records must be internal. Showing such
things can occur within specified conventional dynamics does not establish
an additional consequence of closure. The discriminating question is:

> What does closure require or forbid beyond the supplied laws, state,
> constraints and observational interface?

The closed-whole and vacuum campaigns have not supplied that additional
restriction. In particular, state restriction does not select a global or
local state; the Hamiltonian constraint does not set an observed additive
total energy to zero; and absence of an external boundary does not select a
vacuum pressure or the measured cosmological constant.

| Component | Result that can presently be retained | Obligation still missing |

The scalar sector is built using standard quantum-field and gravitational
tools; none of the extra fields has observational evidence in this program.
Its internal numerical checks are evidence about the calculation, not an
observation of those fields in nature.

## Empirical status belongs in the concept statement

The gravity-only benchmark has no established distinguishing signal in the
comparisons performed. This is not a theorem about every future experiment.
In the archived portal calculation, the particular Q=39944, v_phi=1 GeV
reference spectrum in xenon is closely matched by a 25.481 GeV point WIMP:
normalized reconstructed-bin total variation is 0.003874 under the fixed
response and halo assumptions. That reference is not a demonstrated allowed
cosmological model. These are earlier results, not a fresh detector recast.

If a viable genesis mechanism survives, the candidate discriminator remains
**a joint comparison of nuclear recoil shapes in multiple targets** using a
common object profile and population. Select the targets, response models,
statistic, WIMP comparator and nuisance treatment before tuning a successor
mechanism toward the comparison. A common WIMP fit must be allowed across
targets. Charge mixtures, halo uncertainty and backgrounds cannot be silently
fixed to favorable values. The comparison family is selected; a complete
experimental preregistration has not yet been made.

## The charge-source argument is promising but needs an extra step

Write the two complex interaction monomials as

\[
 K=\kappa Y^*X^3,\qquad L=\mu\Sigma^*Y^2.
\]

For the number convention used by the published release engine,
\(n_i=-2\operatorname{Im}(z_i^*D_tz_i)\), the exact homogeneous source
identities are

\[
\begin{aligned}
(\partial_t+3H)n_X&=-6\operatorname{Im}K,\\
(\partial_t+3H)n_Y&=2\operatorname{Im}K-4\operatorname{Im}L,\\
(\partial_t+3H)n_\Sigma&=2\operatorname{Im}L.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus the mu vertex is the sole source of the **combined light/mediator
charge** \(n_D=n_X+3n_Y\):

\[
(\partial_t+3H)n_D=-12\operatorname{Im}L,\qquad
(\partial_t+3H)(n_D+6n_\Sigma)=0.
\]

Once Y has disappeared into charge-conserving daughters, n_D is the relevant
surviving X-sector charge. There is never net gauge-charge creation.
Calling mu the direct source of n_X alone would miss the kappa transfer.

The exact necessary integral bound is

\[
|\Delta(a^3n_D)|\leq
12\int dt\,a^3|\mu|\,|\Sigma|\,|Y|^2.
\]

This constrains an integral of field amplitudes. By itself it does **not**
bound the final Y/Sigma particle occupations or the late decay deposit.
Nonzero fields, stored angular countercharge and residual radial/free-mode
excitations are different quantities.

For example, in the controlled heavy-Y matching regime there is a nonzero
forced response Y_*(X,Sigma). Eliminating it leaves the charge-violating
X^6 torque, even when the displacement Y-Y_* has zero instantaneous action.
Sigma can carry compensating angular charge around its nonzero vacuum
modulus without that charge being a radial Sigma particle population.
The explicit algebra and numerical checks are in [SOURCE_CHECK.md](SOURCE_CHECK.md).

This does not rescue a published release, demonstrate successful adiabatic
genesis, or establish a preparation with the required yield. The successful
charge releases may be forced outside the adiabatic regime by their yield,
time and phase requirements. That is precisely the additional dynamical
step the independent scaling review must establish before a structural
no-go follows. Finite torque alone requires nonzero amplitudes, not amplitudes
comparable to X.

There is also a quantitative cost to the quiet alternative. In the explicitly
assumed leading-EFT quartic/radiation envelope, the optimistic charge-yield
bound at initial |X|=.1 M_Y is only 1.22e-8 or 5.49e-8 of the required yield
for c_H=1 or 10. Even at |X|=M_Y it is only 3.86e-5 or 1.74e-4. Extrapolating
that bound to enough charge leaves the controlled matching regime. This is
a conditional analytic diagnostic, not a full-action no-go or a new surviving
release. It identifies the missing dynamical step without assuming it.

A successor's requirement should therefore be stated as: **generate the
required persistent charge while keeping all surviving debris below the
specified allowance through a consistent history.** An absolute prohibition
on a charge source involving heavy fields would be stronger than the current
argument warrants.
