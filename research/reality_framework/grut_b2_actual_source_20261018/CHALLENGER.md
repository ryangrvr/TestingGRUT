## Verdict supplied by the Challenger

source-verdict and B2-SB decomposition that changes their stated scoped

The min-coordinate T orientation, matrix Weyl equation, rank-one signs,
fuel fraction and off-diagonal reconstruction in ACTUAL_RESOLVENT.md
check. The following are concrete objections to premature closure.

1. T is not onto. Its adjoint kernel is two dimensional; a theorem or
   transfer matrix requiring T inverse is unavailable in this representation.
2. The fixed-point equation does not by itself select or bound the physical
   branch at real energy. The generic resolvent norm certifies contraction
   only for Im z greater than ||T||, far above the required late resolution.
   This defeats that bound, not every Weyl-function method.
3. At a regular point, rho_f=J^2 b/(pi|Delta|^2). If b>0, Delta cannot
   vanish there. Positivity almost everywhere still allows singular measure
   on exceptional null sets. Thresholds require their own estimates.
4. Scalar m_0 data do not give receiver capture. The matrix powers
   (MT*)^n M and their signed coherent spectral integrals must be controlled
   uniformly for n proportional to L and t proportional to L/J.
5. Finite-depth tail functions are M_n, not one fixed M. Finite exact
   Schur checks validate algebra and inherited compression only; they do
   not classify the half-line spectral measure or prove a continuous window.
6. The central packet's separated-charge fraction is already <=1/362
   by the banked graph norm. The unresolved loss is fuel occupation and
   spatial capture. Treating transverse loss as an arbitrary O(1) obstruction
   would overlook a usable existing estimate.
