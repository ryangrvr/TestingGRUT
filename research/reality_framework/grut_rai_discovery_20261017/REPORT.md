## Result and failure

- **Finite persistence target:** numerically refuted. At \(t=\pi\), expected
  pair number was 0.0016998777260556846, against the frozen minimum of 0.5.
  This sample lies inside the required interval and is sufficient to falsify
  the continuous-time target at ordinary floating-point diagnostic status.
- **Production:** the sampled peak was 0.983228168960443 expected pairs.
  A favorable conversion peak therefore does not establish persistence.
- **Exact isolated control:** with geometry, hopping and density absent,
  the resonant 2x2 block has \(p(t)=\sin^2(t)\). It returns all matter to
  fuel at \(t=\pi\). This reproduces the mechanism already anticipated in
  P1, rather than discovering a new universal obstruction.
- **Zero-resource seed:** matter and fuel vacuum stayed vacuum. Algebraically,
  conservation of \(F+N_+\) and \(F+N_-\) makes this exact for this law.
  The candidate does not create matter from a zero-resource seed.

The full result is not the isolated formula: it includes hopping, density,
geometry and receiver effects and leaves a small nonzero pair population.
There is no outward-rounded numerical certificate. The large threshold
separation and implementation controls support the finite diagnosis; they
do not substitute for independent mathematical review.

## Status and decision

The finite A1 persistence target is rejected at numerical diagnostic status.
The autonomous matter-bearing-world problem remains unresolved. The result
does not rule out other autonomous Hamiltonians, metastable sectors,
delocalized excitations, prepared clocks or paid reservoirs.

Stop this cycle without revision. A future separately frozen successor must
identify a mechanism that suppresses recombination while accounting for its
resources and retaining reverse processes. It must prove a useful lifetime
and prepare its own declared fuel/control state, rather than merely replay
P1's pulse schedule in a prepared clock.

externally powered preparation retain their exact prior scopes and pending
independent-review statuses. There is no new dimensional selection, gravity,
empirical prediction or physical validation. No external AI service or
human reviewer was contacted, and no unattended work continues.
