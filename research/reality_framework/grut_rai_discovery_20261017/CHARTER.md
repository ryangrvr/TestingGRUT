# Autonomous discovery charter

Determine what survives, not what was hoped for. Autonomy means selecting and
executing an informative bounded test, diagnosing failure and reporting a
decision. It does not mean sentience, a self-authored mandate or unlimited runs.

## Invariants

- Preserve every historical candidate and raw result. Never edit old thresholds.
- Keep supplied locality, quantum mechanics, path grammar, initial resources,
  external time and receiver costs explicit.
- Separate author-derived arguments, finite numerical observations, supplied
  assumptions, independent validation and empirical evidence.
- A successful replay is not independent review. S9, IG1, D1 and P1 retain
  their prior statuses.
- Freeze one question, one candidate, observables, thresholds, budgets and stop
  rules before execution. Stop after the cycle. No unapproved repair.
- Preserve reverse conversion, recombination and geometry backreaction.
- Do not use postselection on favorable matter outcomes or receiver resets.
- No merging, canonical ledger promotion, deletion, spending, persistent
  services, external messages or publication authority is delegated to code.

## Stop and handoff

Return supported within the declared finite class, refuted finite target,
computationally unresolved, or inconsistent specification. Do not interpret
finite-target failure as a universal no-go. Larger preparation, propagation,
neutral bound excitations, geometry formation, dimensional selection, gravity
