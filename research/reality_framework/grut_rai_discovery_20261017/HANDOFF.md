# Builder, Challenger and Integrator handoff

Use the frozen packet as a failed candidate, not as a target to repair in
place. The protocol, source and run IDs are immutable. A replay gets a new
run ID; a law or threshold change requires a separately named packet.

## Builder

Propose one explicit autonomous persistence mechanism before coding a larger
simulation. Specify its common Hilbert space, Hamiltonian, seed resources,
reverse transitions, energies, measurement protocol and useful lifetime.
Do not remove recombination or silently introduce a one-way rate. A prepared
clock implementing the old switches must be called supplied control.

## Challenger

Start from the proposed specification and target before reading the proof
where feasible. Test conserved charges, zero-resource invariance, recurrence,
finite-resource exhaustion and whether a long-lived state is populated with
nonvanishing unconditional probability. Seek an explicit counterexample.

Different language models can cross-check the work but are not automatically
independent scientific review. This session's roles shared model and context.
No Claude or separate ChatGPT execution is represented as having occurred.

## Integrator

Re-establish locality, formation compatibility, geometry persistence and
measurement composition for the whole successor. Shared names or registers
do not transfer P1 or S9 theorems. Positive electric energy and fixed flux
capacity remain part of the law unless a new candidate explicitly pays for
their changes.

## Recorder

Keep the old law, failed target, complete raw outputs, source hashes and
resource stops. Use the real provenance auditor, but never interpret its
discipline pass as a truth certificate. Do not promote a result into the
canonical register without the required review and owner decision.

The next campaign is not launched by this handoff. Its exact target and
resource limits must be frozen separately.
