# Exact controls and transfer boundaries

All arguments here are author-derived, independent review pending. They are
finite-model identities, not a theorem about nature.

## Resource conservation

Let \(F=\sum_e n_{f_e}\), and let \(N_+\) and \(N_-\) count the two species.
Each conversion monomial destroys one fuel and creates one particle of each
species. Its adjoint reverses precisely that transition, so it commutes with
\(F+N_+\) and \(F+N_-\). P1 geometry terms change neither matter nor fuel;
hopping and all diagonal terms preserve each number separately.

Consequently the always-on sum preserves both nonnegative charges.
Their joint zero eigenspace has fuel and matter vacuum. Thus the zero-resource
seed cannot create matter under this candidate. This obstruction is specific
to this Hamiltonian and seed, not a statement about all proposed cosmologies.

With one initial fuel the reachable sector has \(F+N_+=F+N_-=1\).
Expected pair number lies between zero and one, and mean fuel plus mean pair
number equals one. Initial supplied fuel energy is three in P1 units.

## Recombination, not irreversible production

Suppress hopping, density interaction and geometry motion only for this
separate diagnostic. On \(\{|f\rangle,|p\rangle\}\),

\[
H=\begin{pmatrix}3+\Delta&\lambda\\\lambda&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
p(t)=\frac{\lambda^2}{\lambda^2+\Delta^2/4}
\sin^2\!\left(t\sqrt{\lambda^2+\Delta^2/4}\right).
\]

At resonance and unit coupling, \(p(\pi/2)=1\) but \(p(\pi)=0\).
Conversion is genuine; persistent matter does not follow from a production
peak. Detuning decreases the peak and shifts recurrence. Switching conversion
off after a peak changes the law and cannot repair an always-on claim.

This exact control is already anticipated by P1's reverse-pulse diagnostic.
The new experiment tests the assembled always-on law with hopping, positive
electric energy, density interactions, receiver evolution and geometry motion.
Its numerical result must not be substituted into the isolated formula.

## Finite test logic

The persistence claim quantifies over every time in a fixed interval. A
single robustly resolved sample below threshold refutes it. Passing finitely
many samples cannot prove it, which is why the runner returns sampled support
only unless a continuous-time bound is supplied.

The finite diagnostic uses all sectors of the P1 physical kernel at its
declared N and two-mode receiver. This is not P1's physical large-N receiver,
not vacuum geometry formation, not a macroscopic buffer, and not a test of
large-N lifetime scaling. Norm, energy, conservation and an independently
implemented dense exponential are implementation controls only.
