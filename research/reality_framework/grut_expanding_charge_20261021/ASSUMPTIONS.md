What remains of the earlier baseline:

- The isolated finite objects have finite-time durability in their declared 3D test
  family. This packet does not extend that to all perturbations or cosmological life.
- The old canonical transverse amplification is not a validated internal-instability rate.
- The 100 GeV, r=1 preparation retains its **conditional** charge-floor exclusion,
  including vacuum-energy, curvature, entropy, classical-energy and averaging assumptions.
  This packet neither silently repairs that preparation nor excludes the action universally.
- A converged relic population, composite cosmological lifetime, and positive identification
  remain unestablished. No larger tally of integrity checks changes those statements.

No action extension is adopted. An inflaton coupling, charge source or ordinary detector
portal would be a separately versioned extension requiring an explicit owner decision
before it becomes the empirical target.
