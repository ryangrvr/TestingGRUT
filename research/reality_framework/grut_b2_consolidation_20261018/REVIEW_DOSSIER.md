## Falsification targets

Any one of the following warrants a precise correction or reopened proof
status: an omitted physical state; a wrong resolvent sign/Jacobian; an
uncontrolled threshold or spectral contribution; a false derivative or
global remainder bound; failure of continuous-time or all-length uniformity;
an invalid physical embedding; an incorrect joint probability composition;
or an allowed endpoint operation that invalidates the reference channel.

A sampled trajectory or ordinary replay cannot establish the theorem's
continuous-window, all-length quantifiers. Conversely, a failed sufficient
estimate is not automatically a physical violation. A physical rejection
needs an upper event bound below the frozen threshold at an allowed point.
