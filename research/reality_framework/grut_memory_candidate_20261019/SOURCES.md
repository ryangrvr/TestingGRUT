# Sources and actual scope

The Hamiltonian and candidate-specific calculations are specified in this
packet. The following sources establish context and prevent false priority
or inference claims; their models' theorems are not imported as M1 results.

| Primary source | Read scope and use |
|---|---|
| J. Maldacena, S. H. Shenker, D. Stanford, [A bound on chaos, arXiv:1503.01409](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.01409), JHEP 08 (2016) 106, DOI 10.1007/JHEP08(2016)106 | Regulated correlator definitions, the bound/assumption statements and section 3's Einstein/stringy examples. The bound is an upper bound under assumptions, not a universal equality. No independent proof of its full analytic argument is claimed. |
| S. H. Shenker, D. Stanford, [Stringy effects in scrambling, arXiv:1412.6087](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6087), JHEP 05 (2015) 132 | Abstract and near-horizon/finite-coupling discussion. Submaximal growth is not an unprecedented alternative to all established gravity. No string scattering calculation is reproduced here. |
| N. Pancotti, G. Giudice, J. I. Cirac, J. P. Garrahan, M. C. Bañuls, [Quantum East model: localization, non-thermal eigenstates and slow dynamics, arXiv:1910.06616](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.06616), Phys. Rev. X 10, 021051 (2020) | Abstract and publisher identification. Establishes substantial prior art for slow, constrained, disorder-free quantum dynamics. Its directional East constraint is not identified with the particular two-neighbor feedback register here. Its localization claims are not inherited. |
| A. Smith, J. Knolle, D. L. Kovrizhin, R. Moessner, [Disorder-Free Localization, arXiv:1701.04748](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.04748), Phys. Rev. Lett. 118, 266601 (2017) | Abstract and bibliography. Existing translation-invariant interacting models can generate localization-related behavior without quenched disorder. This is not a new possibility first supplied by GRUT. |
| Y. Huang, Y.-L. Zhang, X. Chen, [Out-of-time-ordered correlators in many-body localized systems, arXiv:1608.01091](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.01091) | Abstract. Local memory/transport and information spreading are distinct observables. No logarithmic light cone is inferred for M1. |
| T. Thiery, F. Huveneers, M. Müller, W. De Roeck, [Many-body delocalization as a quantum avalanche, arXiv:1706.09338](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.09338), Phys. Rev. Lett. 121, 140601 (2018) | Abstract and publisher identification. Motivates leaving thermodynamic robustness and thermal-inclusion attacks open; does not prove that the present finite model must or must not localize. |

The prior project P1 and closure-fork documents were checked to separate
observer restriction from a new dynamical memory postulate. No full audit
of unavailable project briefs, no independent experimental verification,
and no global absence-of-prior-art proof was performed. The new numerical
work was executed locally with the listed code and preserved protocols.
