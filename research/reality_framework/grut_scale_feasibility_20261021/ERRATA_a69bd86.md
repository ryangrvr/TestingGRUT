   |H-H0-W| by max(|H0|,|H|,|W|). We recomputed its maximum as 0.00118159082

   no tolerance is changed and neither is an empirical uncertainty.

   the time evolution and strengthens this qualification. It is not a new claim
   that our initial-time cross term predicted the final sign correctly.
