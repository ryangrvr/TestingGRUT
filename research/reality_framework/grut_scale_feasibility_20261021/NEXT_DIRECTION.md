## First exact obstruction: closure does not set an energy origin

On a fixed background, for any time-independent Hamiltonian H and real c,

\[
H_c=H+cI,\quad U_c(t)=e^{-ict}U(t),\quad
U_c\rho U_c^\dagger=U\rho U^\dagger,\quad [H_c,\rho]=[H,\rho].
\]

All matter measurement probabilities and reduced states are therefore identical
under this shift for the same instruments. Energy gaps are unchanged; a normalized
Gibbs state is also unchanged because its numerator and partition function acquire
the same factor. A proposed rule that uses only these shift-invariant closure
properties cannot select c. This is elementary standard physics, not a novel
GRUT theorem. The code includes an exact-rational two-state check of the generator
and gap invariance; the general argument is the displayed identity.

With a dynamical metric the action shift is a different operation:

\[
S_m\longrightarrow S_m-C\int\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x,
\qquad T_{\mu\nu}\longrightarrow T_{\mu\nu}-C g_{\mu\nu}.
\]

For the original Einstein action, holding its other parameters fixed, this
changes the physical vacuum density by C and changes curvature. Matter closure
on a fixed background cannot erase that gravitational response. The identity
above is **not** a no-go theorem for all quantum gravity, a closed gravitational
constraint system, nonlocal proposals, or new dynamics.

There is also a harmless renormalization relabelling:

\[
\rho_{m,\rm vac}\to\rho_{m,\rm vac}+C,\qquad
\rho_{\Lambda,\rm ct}\to\rho_{\Lambda,\rm ct}-C.
\]

It leaves their total unchanged. Predicting one unphysical split does not predict
the measurable total. Conversely, cancelling an added vacuum contribution by
retuning the counterterm to the already known expansion is not an explanation.
A mechanism that suppresses the matter vacuum source but leaves a free residual
integration constant has not yet predicted that residual's value.

## Success criterion for the next proposal

The ambitious GRUT objective is to derive at least one independently testable
dimensionless relation. A physical theory can have calibrated parameters; it need
not predict every constant. The stronger ambition here is to explain something
that the existing candidate simply supplies.

For a vacuum-energy proposal, define the target as the **total renormalized**
rho_vac/M_Pl^4 with the reduced-Planck convention explicit. Before a computation,
write the principle as equations and list every parameter, boundary condition,
state measure and cosmological input. Then answer:

1. Which extra structure makes the proposal constrain the gravitational vacuum
   term that the shift-invariant matter argument leaves free?
2. Does it actually fix a residual, including its sign and scale, or merely
   remove a large contribution while retaining an arbitrary constant?
3. Does the claimed value remain stable when a heavy threshold/loop contribution
   is included at the stated order, without readjusting a parameter to data?
4. What independent dynamical or observable consequence follows beyond fitting
   the cosmological constant, and which already-exposed data cannot be called
   held out?

H0 is not a substitute parameter-free target: even a known vacuum density leaves
matter/radiation densities and curvature in the Friedmann equation. State those
inputs and their independent calibration before claiming to predict H0.

Our prior knowledge of the measured expansion prevents a retrospective numerical
match from being blinded. An explanation can still be scientifically useful,
but a confirmatory empirical claim needs a genuinely separate test. Simply naming
an observed number in advance is not sufficient if the construction is adjusted
until it gives that number.

## What is decided and what remains missing

No more Candidate 1 production or wall runs are launched by this packet. The
bounded v-scaling calculation establishes a conditional possible interaction
channel, not an empirical result and not a complete v-map of every history.
There is no reason to spend a large numerical budget until the principle and
observable are specified.

The next deliverable must be a short mathematical proposal that passes the
energy-origin test or explicitly identifies the new assumption that avoids it.
It should be compared with the actual scope of Weinberg-type adjustment arguments
and existing vacuum-energy proposals before being called new. If no such
principle can be stated, the next honest result is an identified theoretical
obstruction, not another scalar field construction or an asserted theory of
everything. The two-system audit loop remains a means of finding errors, not a
guarantee that an untested claim is true.
