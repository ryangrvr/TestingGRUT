## Exact all-pairs result

Let \(|\varnothing\rangle\) be the empty strict partial order on fixed N labelled events; all N(N-1) directed one-relation posets \(|i\prec j\rangle\) are reachable from it by the CP-08 hopping Hamiltonian. Matter Hilbert space is (C²)^{tensor N}. For distinct i,j choose an impulse generated by \(B=X_i\) and a readout \(A=Z_j\), with the full Heisenberg evolution \(A(t)=e^{iHt}Ae^{-iHt}\). Set \(\hbar=1\) as in CP-08, \(P_\varnothing=|\varnothing\rangle\langle\varnothing|\otimes I\).

For any N>=2 and the same frozen \(U_{ij}=\exp(-i\gamma Z_iX_j)\) on addition \(i\prec j\),

\[
\boxed{
P_\varnothing\,i[A(t),B]\,P_\varnothing
=-2J^2 \sin(2\gamma)\,t^2
P_\varnothing\,Y_iY_j+O_N(t^3).
}
\]

This is an **operator identity on the empty-geometry matter sector**, and the coefficient is independent of N, the specific pair and the on-site \(\Omega\). With initially factorized matter states \(|Y+\rangle_i\otimes|Y-\rangle_j\) and arbitrary normalized states of the remaining spins, it yields \(R_{ji}(t)=+2J^2\sin(2\gamma)t^2+O_N(t^3)\). In particular the coefficient is 2 for the frozen J=1, gamma=pi/4. The \(O_N\) notation explicitly warns that this Taylor expansion need not be uniform in N.

**Proof.** At the empty geometry the only single-hop outputs are the one-edge orders, each with transition block \(H_{(i\prec j),\varnothing}=-J U_{ij}\) (the reverse-oriented move is also independently present). For \(A=Z_j\), the order-zero cross commutator with \(X_i\) vanishes. The first-order nested commutator projects to the empty-sector on-site matter Hamiltonian \(\Omega\sum_kX_k\), whose contribution commutes with \(X_i\) after acting on \(Z_j\) when i != j. At order \(t^2\), project the nested commutator \(i[A(t),X_i]\) and resolve contributions from the one-edge intermediate orders:
\[
Q^{(2)}_{ji}=iJ^2\sum_{k\ne l}[U_{kl}^\dagger Z_j U_{kl}, X_i].
\]
Terms proportional to the untransformed \(Z_j\) vanish because \([Z_j,X_i]=0\). The only term with a nonzero commutator is k=i,l=j. Indeed \(U_{ij}^\dagger Z_j U_{ij}=\cos(2\gamma)Z_j+\sin(2\gamma)Z_iY_j\), and \(i[Z_iY_j,X_i]=-2Y_iY_j\). All other one-edge contributions vanish, proving the formula.

This result is stronger than the N=2 control: **at fixed N, every ordered matter pair is accessible through the same leading global hopping mechanism, even starting from the empty order**. This follows from event-relabel covariance and all-pairs relation moves, not an emergent operational distance. No physical superluminal conclusion is licensed without a specified metric, clock and detector calibration.

## Exact final-geometry branch resolution

For each geometry C define the *joint* readout \(A_C=|C\rangle\langle C|\otimes Z_j\), measured at final external time. Its source derivative is \(R_{C,ji}(t)=\langle i[A_C(t),X_i]\rangle\). The same nested-commutator calculation gives, as operators on the initial empty sector,

\[
\boxed{
P_\varnothing\,i[A_C(t),X_i]\,P_\varnothing
=\begin{cases}
-2J^2\sin(2\gamma)t^2P_\varnothing Y_iY_j+O_N(t^3), & C=(i\prec j),\\
O_N(t^3), & \text{every other }C.
\end{cases}
}
\]

Thus **only the final one-edge geometry \(i\prec j\) carries the quadratic cross-influence**. If the final geometry is postselected as still empty, the unnormalized cross-response vanishes to this order. The conditional *normalized* detector response also lacks an O(t²) cross term since the empty survival probability has no first- or second-order source dependence. For the forward one-edge branch, its occurrence probability starts at \(J^2t^2\); a source intervention changes its conditional matter readout through \(U_{ij}\). It is therefore misleading to conclude from the unconditional \(2t^2\) response that matter signaled across a geometry which *remained empty throughout*. A relation-creating branch supplies the leading influence. An independently defined operational light cone and physical clock are still absent.

This signal does **not** require CP-08's non-Abelian route holonomy: it already arises for N=2, while the nontrivial composable-route Wilson loop needs at least three events. Causal influence from a dynamically created oriented edge and interference between different routes to one endpoint are different features of the same Hamiltonian.

\[
\boxed{
p_\varnothing(t)=1-J^2N(N-1)t^2+O_N(t^3).

This is an exact second-order coefficient for every N and every such initial matter state, independent of gamma and Omega. Its associated short-time scale is \(t\sim1/(J\sqrt{N(N-1)})\sim 1/(JN)\). **This is a perturbative crossover scale, not a derived physical lifetime, relaxation law or gravitational timescale.** Consequently, while the individual pair coefficient above is N-independent, the empty-geometry Taylor expansion has no demonstrated fixed-time uniform N limit.
