# B1 failures, exclusions and rejection conditions

## 1. The gap-only alternative does not by itself prepare matter

For the exact cell Hamiltonian H=[[0,lambda],[lambda,Delta]], initially
fuel, diagonalization gives

    p_pair(t)=4lambda^2/(Delta^2+4lambda^2)
              *sin^2(sqrt(Delta^2+4lambda^2)t/2).

Taking |Delta|/lambda large suppresses maximum production. It may suppress
return from a *supplied* pair, but does not solve fuel-to-matter preparation
in this cell. This familiar two-level fact is not a no-go for prethermal
many-body models, cascades, additional receivers, or the resonant B1 channel.
No numerical rescue of this alternative was attempted.

## 2. A1 remains failed and closed

A1 has lambda=1, adjacent-charge conversion at energy 13/4, and an extensive
matching of fuels. Its every-time retention gate fails. B1 changes the
conversion operator, prepared resource count, energy, contact strength and
degree-dependent diagonal law. It neither passes A1's gate nor proves that
A1 could have been saved by choosing a favorable later time. Its J=1/3840
enters a fresh complete Hamiltonian and time-domain proof; the old P1
conversion-off band was not exponentiated or compared directly with A1's pi.

## 3. B1 needs somewhere for the excitation to go

The frozen exact two-event control has matter probability 0.182092 at
theta=4. It violates the proposed operating threshold if one removes the
long-path hypothesis. This is an explicit counterexample to inferring
retention from the local conversion cell alone. In contrast the eight- and
sixteen-event controls spread over many matter states. The theorem uses
>=64 events to control far-end return analytically.

Every finite system has recurrence. No permanent locking, irreversible
matter creation, asymptotic exponential lifetime, or parametrically large
ratio tau_loss/tau_transport follows. The proven statement is one finite
use interval. A shorter record or a later observation is a different test.

## 4. Multi-cell coherence remains open

The two-fuel, eight-event full matrix keeps all 773 field-compatible states.
At theta=4 it gives p(two pairs)=0.751352 and mean pairs=1.735023. This is
not a tensor product of isolated one-resource channels. The exact adjacent
versus separated co-pair diagonal energy difference is 1/2, roughly 1920J.
Those collisions and exchange paths prohibit using the one-resource proof
at a density proportional to N. No collective survival or color-transport
bound at finite density has been derived. The finite diagnostic is not an
all-cell pass and is not an extensive autonomous-matter claim.

## 5. Supplied structure doing the work

* Occupied-interval interaction support is supplied. IG1's distant neutral
  observables remain counterexamples for the unrestricted constraint commutant.
* The grammar supplies a path class. Generated labels do not select dimension.
* Local neutral conversion is a new operator, not derived from charge or
  gauge invariance. It bypasses P1's poor adjacent-pair overlap with the low band.
* Fuel is prepared near the composite band. The declared interval is narrow:
  |delta Omega|,|delta lambda|<=J/1000. No theorem for arbitrary fuel mismatch,
  independent hopping disorder, color-dependent fields or removed degree
  counterterms is claimed.
* The degree counterterm flattens the leading endpoint energy shift. The
  uncorrected architecture has not passed this frozen test.
* Internal identity of the two color Hamiltonians is assumed. Local spin
  precession/dephasing or species-dependent forces require a new analysis.
* The recorded path, single fuel loading, source qubit and readout are supplied.
  Vacuum plus vacuum fuel still cannot spontaneously produce matter.
* Full moving-geometry use inherits the large receiver resource sequence;
  a fixed-path quantum simulator would not test that formation mechanism.

## 6. What would reject this result

An error in the exact block identity (1), residual (3), rational inequalities,
half-line arrival normalization, or the moving-prefix comparison invalidates
the corresponding theorem. The frozen finite checks inspect the first three,
not the all-size geometry proof. An independently implemented allowed
one-resource instance within the stated class violating (9) or (10) beyond
its numerical uncertainty is a direct counterexample. Do not repair the
Hamiltonian, decoder, time window or thresholds afterward.

Loss outside the stated domain, failure at two resources, or a very small
record does not contradict the theorem. It limits its usefulness. A theory
of nature, universal matter locality, gravity and an empirical discriminator
remain absent irrespective of a correct one-composite theorem.
