## The moving-prefix boundary account

This is the most review-dependent part of the integration. It is useful to
write the actual proof obligations rather than declare inheritance from A1.

The reference keeps the first ell recorded labels and their q bits fixed,
retains the actual receiver and all tail registers, and prevents matter or
fuel from crossing its right boundary. Tail assembly/pop still acts. A
static endpoint-degree correction makes the retained matter matrix exactly
the finite-path B1 matrix. Its difference from the physical degree term
is supported on the retained endpoint's co-pair occupation. Reference
outside matter/fuel and its connecting field remain vacuum. Thus its
retained matter dynamics factors from the genuine tail/receiver state.

Separate the full-minus-reference drive into two classes.

1. **Pure tail penetration.** From initial length m to the forbidden pop at
   ell requires a=m-ell prior word changes. Diagonal disorder/receiver
   phases do not change this count. The consistent empty-tail append plus
   adjoint norm is <=g: append alone has norm <=g/2 from
   sqrt[(N-m)m]/N<=1/2. Dyson in the length-preserving interaction picture
   bounds the boundary amplitude by tail_a(1.1t). Integrating the drive
   1.1 tail_a(1.1t) gives tail_(a+1)(1.1t).
2. **Resource-supported boundary terms.** Hopping across the cut, bare
   matter-dependent end motion, degree-counterterm discrepancy, and any
   altered empty-head gate vanish unless resource is on the last two
   retained events. Their combined norm on a vector is bounded by
   7 times the square root of its resource occupation there. This deliberately
   loose coefficient covers hopping <.06, the two bare end orientations
   (each <=1 using sqrt(N) target counting and n<=2), degree correction
   <.001, and up to two assisted contact/gate terms <=1.1 each. Fuel adds
   no crossing term: its conversion is on-event and inside the retained
   region. Diagonal electric/density cut terms annihilate the outside
   vacuum and zero connecting field. These contributions total <7.

The weighted resource estimate bounds the square root in class 2 by
sqrt(C) exp(-L/2) exp(vt/2). Integrating gives the second term of (11),
14sqrt(C)/v exp(-L/2)[exp(vt/2)-1]. If a proposed implementation does not
admit this common-space reference, empty-tail factorization, or boundary
norm account, its full-geometry claim remains unproved even if fixed-path
calculations pass. No total ||H||=O(N) smallness assumption is used.

The worst-case receiver norm remains finite on the physical n_receiver<=N-1
sector. The argument never resets it, picks an eigenmode, or deletes a
storage-error state on the physical side. Initial consistency follows from
the declared record, not from assuming all later physical states consistent.

frozen controls. At m=16, residual identity error is 2.92e-18, exact-K identity
error 1.09e-16, and actual nominal full-state error to Zwalk at theta=4 is

The two-cell eight-event result has p(two pairs)=0.997750 at theta=2 but
0.751352 at theta=4. This is not a failed frozen primary gate (none was
declared for density), nor proof of stable many-pair operation. It is a
concrete warning that the one-resource channel cannot be replicated without
an interaction analysis. The cycle stops at that boundary.
