# Primary-source comparison, not validation

Retrieved 2026-10-18. No source below validates GRUT-B1 or establishes its
priority. This packet's inequalities must stand on their own derivation.

* Abanin, De Roeck, Ho, Huveneers, **A rigorous theory of many-body
  prethermalization for periodically driven and closed quantum systems**,
  Communications in Mathematical Physics 354, 809-827 (2017),
  https://arxiv.org/html/1509.05386v3 and
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-017-2930-x.
  Its static theorem assumes an integer-valued dominant local number and
  controlled local interaction norms. It yields long-lived dressed quantities
  under stated scale-separation hypotheses. B1 does not assert these
  hypotheses for the complete dynamical geometry or inherit its exponential
  lifetime. A direct finite-time intertwiner is used instead.
* U. Fano, **Effects of Configuration Interaction on Intensities and Phase
  Shifts**, Physical Review 124, 1866 (1961),
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.124.1866.
  Discrete/continuum coupling and interference are established physics.
  B1's emission into an extended band is not presented as a new principle.
  Fano's paper concerns resonance spectra, not this GRUT lifetime theorem.
* E. Farhi, S. Gutmann, **Quantum computation and decision trees**,
  Physical Review A 58, 915 (1998),
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.58.915 and
  https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9706062.
  Continuous-time quantum walks are a conventional reference class.
  B1's half-line profile is derived directly by the sine transform/image
  method, not claimed as novel or as an empirical discriminator.
