## Primary literature checked for scope

* S. Bravyi, M. B. Hastings and F. Verstraete,
  "Lieb-Robinson bounds and the generation of correlations and
  topological quantum order", Phys. Rev. Lett. 97, 050401 (2006),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0603121 . Abstract checked for the
  established effective-light-cone context; this is not a claimed
  theorem for the present configuration law.
* T. Kuwahara and K. Saito, "Lieb-Robinson bound and almost-linear
  light-cone in interacting boson systems", Phys. Rev. Lett. 127,
  070403 (2021), https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11592 . Abstract checked
  for the established role of boson occupation/preparation constraints.
  Its hypotheses and specific light-cone are not imported into S4.
* S. Longhi, "Non-exponential decay via tunneling in tight-binding
  lattices and the optical Zeno effect", Phys. Rev. Lett. 97,
  110402 (2006), https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0611164 . Abstract
  checked for the standard boundary-emitter model underlying the
  symmetric S3 comparison, not for disordered-word formation.

All project-specific bounds used here are derived in MATHEMATICS.md.
Dyson expansion, Duhamel, weighted number moments, the spectral theorem,
permutation isometries and sparse unitary propagation are standard
mathematical tools. No novelty or empirical distinction is claimed
merely from using them. This is not an exhaustive priority review.
