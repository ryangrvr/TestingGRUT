# S4: what the prepared interior can access

These are in-class statements about the displayed quantum Hamiltonian.
They are not external validation or a prediction about nature. S3's
real-energy gap failure is retained. A finite-time access bound does
not turn the consistency sector into a low-energy spectral sector.

## 1. Law, components and disorder

Take S3 verbatim except for replacing delta sum q_i by sum delta_i q_i,
where delta_i=bar_delta+epsilon_i, |bar_delta|<=1/2 and
|epsilon_i|<=1/4. Common g remains in [0.9,1.1]. No transition support
changes. In particular there is no added event adjacency, drive or reset.
The receiver has L_N=64N^2 modes and at most M=N-1 bosons by the exact
charge sum q_i-n_R=1. Every reverse process remains.

Let P be the storage-consistent projector q_i=1 exactly for events in
the current word. Q=1-P. This is an operational consistency criterion,
not an isolated spectral band. In probe vacuum P is exactly invariant.
For the one-probe component put B=QHP. The unique source of each bare
one-mismatch destination gives

    ||B|| <= epsilon = 1/4.

Put the receiver Hamiltonian and all configuration-preserving diagonal
terms, including every independent delta_i, in D. All other interactions
form V. They obey the same S3 bounds, with every receiver occupation:

    ||V|| <= g_max+2epsilon+2J = nu = 2.1.

Neither bound uses a small extensive norm of H or a complement gap.
Therefore S3's metric word theorem, full-H distance front, nonzero
nearby response and positive-resolution Schur bound extend to every
pattern in this frozen disorder class. Its formation proof does not.

## 2. Exact distance to the first storage error

Fix a recorded consistent word w=(w_0,...,w_(m-1)). Put the probe at
w_b. Receiver motion costs zero in the following configuration count;
each assisted geometry, bare geometry or probe hop costs one. Then
the minimum count to first enter Q is exactly

    r_b = max(1,m-1-b).

For a source supported at positions at most b use this r_b as a common
lower bound. For the adjacent root pair (w_0,w_1),

    r = max(1,m-2).

Proof. The first bare transition is the first storage error. Before it,
every pop is storage-assisted and removes just the current last event;
every append extends the end. A bare pop requires the probe on the
current head or its parent. A bare append requires it on the current
head or the free event being appended. If the original probe event
has not been removed, advancing the probe along the immutable prefix
and retreating the head must together cover at least m-2-b steps
before the first bare pop. The final bare operation costs one. Bare
append at the head needs at least as many steps. If the probe event
has been removed, reaching that point required still more end pops.
Subsequent append/pop detours cannot reduce the count.

For b<=m-2 the bound is attained by hopping to the original head's
parent and bare-popping once. For b=m-1 a bare pop immediately attains
one. For m=1 a bare append attains one (N>=2). Receiver rearrangements
and coherent superpositions do not alter this support statement.

## 3. Complete feedback bound, not a copied spectral theorem

Write tau_r(x)=sum_(k>=r) x^k/k!, with tau_0(x)=exp(x).
The clipped Dyson tail is T_r(x)=min(1,tau_r(x)); T_0=1.
Let U(t)=exp(-itH), U_P(t)=exp(-itPHP) on P, and let X be the fixed
word/source subspace with arbitrary admissible receiver state.
The interaction-picture Dyson series has no Q contribution before r
insertions of V. More usefully B U_P(s) X has no contribution before
r-1 insertions. Hence

    ||B U_P(s) X|| <= epsilon T_(r-1)(nu|s|).

Compare with the block-diagonal unitary generated by PHP+QHQ only as a
mathematical reference. It is never the proposed physical Hamiltonian.
Exact Duhamel, with the complete H on the other side of the integral,
gives

    ||(U(t)-U_P(t)) X||
      <= epsilon integral_0^|t| T_(r-1)(nu s) ds
      <= (epsilon/nu) tau_r(nu|t|) =: D_r(t).

The last integration uses the un-clipped sum; r>=1. Cap the state-norm
difference by 2 and the leakage amplitude by 1. Consequently

    ||Q U(t) X|| <= min(1,D_r(t)),
    p_Q(t) <= min(1,D_r(t))^2.

These estimates include all visits and returns through Q, not merely
the leading bare amplitude or one selected virtual word.

For every mu>0, tau_r(x)<=exp(exp(mu)x-mu r). In particular,

    |t| <= r/(4 e nu)
      => D_r(t) <= (epsilon/nu) exp(-3r/4).

For every accepted macroscopic record, m>=max(2,ceil(N/2)), the
root-pair probe has r>=max(1,ceil(N/2)-2). This gives a conditional
observation time proportional to N with exponentially small storage
error and complement feedback. The formation window is proportional
to N^2. Their ratio shrinks; this is not an indefinitely persistent
phase. The guarantee concerns the declared interior protocol, not a
probe initially supported at the movable end.

## 4. Response and a precisely defined spectral driving measure

Use normalized columns x_0=|w,w_0;Phi_R> and x_1=|w,w_1;Phi_R>,
initial probe (x_0+x_1)/sqrt(2), and pulse exp(-i theta n_(w_0)).
The source variance is 1/4. Summing all final field/storage/geometry
records, chi_j(t)=Im <R_j U x_1,R_j U x_0>.

Let chi_j^P use U_P only as the comparison just bounded. Then

    |chi_j-chi_j^P| <= 2 D_r(t).

The contribution from inconsistent final records is bounded by

    |chi_j^Q| <= ||Q U x_1|| ||Q U x_0|| <= min(1,D_r(t))^2.

The second statement alone would miss Q-mediated returns into P;
the first bounds their entire net effect. The actual simulation uses H.
The existing first-order nearby response remains at least
Jt-[exp(2nu t)-1-2nu t], positive at t=0.005. A nonzero response at all
times proportional to N is not asserted by this lower bound.

For a Borel energy window I define F_Q(I)=1_I(QHQ), and define the
complement **driving** spectral measure at time s by

    mu_s(I) = ||F_Q(I) B U_P(s) psi||^2.

For every I, including any shrinking coupled-pole window,

    mu_s(I) <= epsilon^2 T_(r-1)(nu|s|)^2.

Also ||F_Q(I) Q U(t)psi||^2 <= min(1,D_r(t))^2. These are measures
of the drive and actual complement occupation. They are NOT the
prepared state's spectral measure for the full H. That latter measure
can have substantial weight in the same energy window as desired P
dynamics. No small total full-H energy-window weight, no per-pole
absence and no real-energy resolvent bound is inferred. The small
matrix spectral checks have no near-zero Q poles; they cannot test
the asymptotic pole theorem by extrapolation.

## 5. Explicit receiver access, including the formation period

Index receiver modes r=0,...,L-1 from the supplied contact. For mu>0 set

    F_mu = sum_r [exp(2mu r)-1] n_r.

Every geometry/storage/matter term commutes with F_mu. Assisted
emission changes only n_0, whose coefficient is zero. The mode hopping
commutator and Cauchy-Schwarz give

    d<F_mu>/dt <= 4 sinh(mu) (<F_mu>+M).

From the vacuum seed, <F_mu(t)> <= M[exp(4sinh(mu)|t|)-1]. Thus the
probability of any boson in modes r>=ell is at most

    M[exp(4sinh(mu)|t|)-1]/[exp(2mu ell)-1].

This is uniform over every independent detuning pattern. It uses a
receiver mode distance, explicitly supplied inside the receiver. It
is not an emergent event metric or an extra spacetime dimension.

The S3 explicit Weyl witness is entirely in the last half of the
receiver, beginning at ell=32N^2. For t<=8N^2, choosing mu=1 gives
an upper bound proportional to

    (N-1) exp[-(64-32sinh(1))N^2]
      = (N-1) exp[-26.39356... N^2].

Keep the displayed denominator for exact finite-N constants. An
accepted geometry ensemble with probability p_acc has this bound
divided by p_acc. Recording words and injecting the neutral probe
commute with F_mu, so for subsequent probe time tau use

    <F_mu>_accepted(tau)+M
      <= M exp[4sinh(mu)(t_form+|tau|)] / p_acc.

For symmetric S3, p_acc>=1/2 is known. Under independent disorder,
retain p_acc explicitly until formation is proved or measured.
This shows why the particular far-field Weyl witness is not freely
available in the actual preparation. It does NOT show that the entire
near-pole Q spectral subspace is inaccessible: projecting the witness
onto a Q energy window can change its receiver support. Section 4's
separate dynamical bound applies to every such window.

## 6. A controlled finite-mode computation of the physical receiver

Cut after ell retained modes, embed the cut-state evolution into the
physical longer receiver, and apply Duhamel to the one missing link.
On the embedded state the outside mode is vacuum, so the crossing
term has norm sqrt(<n_(ell-1)>). Integrating Section 5 gives the
explicit vacuum-preparation state-norm error

    ||U_L(t)psi0-U_ell(t)psi0||
      <= sqrt(M)/(2sinh(mu))
         * [exp(2sinh(mu)|t|)-1]
         / sqrt[exp(2mu(ell-1))-1].

The error in any probability is at most twice this number. The cut
includes every occupation allowed by charge; no photon-number cutoff
is added. It is an analytically controlled calculation of L_N, not a
silent replacement by a convenient short receiver. Ordinary numerical
roundoff is additional and is not certified by this analytic bound.

Use ell=128 for N=2 and ell=224 for N=3 throughout [4N^2,8N^2]. At
the largest time the N=3 state-norm bound is 2.67247e-24. Separate
two-mode controls have no useful physical-L_N error guarantee at those
times and are explicitly labeled different finite models.

## 7. The symmetric reduction really fails

Let G_1 be the uniform one-attachment word vector with its receiver
quantum at contact, and let Pi_rad project onto vectors uniform over
words at each length and fixed field occupation. Independent detuning
acts diagonally with values delta_i, i not the root. Thus exactly

    ||(1-Pi_rad) H G_1|| = stddev(delta_i : i != root).

It is nonzero for a nonconstant assignment, even though all primitive
emission coefficients remain equal. The binomial law is not inherited.
Nor can one keep only the attached subset: for i!=j, the seed's H^3
amplitudes at words (root,i,j) and (root,j,i), both photons at contact,
differ by

    sqrt(2) (g/N)^2 (delta_i-delta_j).

The two orderings have the same attached subset. Forgetting their
order loses coherent dynamics.

## 8. What exact reduction survives

Partition non-root labels into equal-detuning classes a with values
delta_a and sizes n_a. A type word u=(a_1,...,a_k) retains the entire
sequence. Let k_a(u) count its occurrences of class a. Its normalized
label-symmetric vector sums

    D_u = product_a (n_a)!/[n_a-k_a(u)]!

distinct actual words. From the bondless seed, the span of these
vectors tensored with the entire receiver Fock sector is exactly
invariant. Appending class a has matrix element

    -(g/N) sqrt[n_a-k_a(u)] a_0^dagger,

and the diagonal is sum_a delta_a k_a(u). Pop is its full adjoint.
Proof: each child has one parent; each parent has n_a-k_a possible
same-class labels. Storage and receiver operators respect this
within-class permutation symmetry. No incoherent label average is used.

One class recovers S3's quadratic pump. With multiple classes the
noncommuting last-in/first-out word history remains. With all distinct
detunings the reduction is essentially the full rooted-word space;
this is not a polynomial all-size solver.

## 9. Formation gate remains open

Every binary corner for N=2,3 is evaluated at the frozen observation
times with the original uniform-root weights and a controlled error
relative to physical L_N. Endpoints do not bound all intermediate
patterns: formation is not proved monotone in detuning. No random
high-probability theorem follows from the finite iid sample.

For completeness a weak all-pattern continuity guarantee exists.
Subtract the conserved root's detuning shift in each root component.
Relative to common bar_delta,

    ||H_dis-H_sym|| <= (N-1) w.

Therefore a probability changes by at most 2|t|(N-1)w. S3's proof has
margin p_macro>0.6 throughout its window. Hence

    w <= 1/[160 N^2(N-1)]
      => p_macro >= 1/2 on [4N^2,8N^2].

This is a shrinking O(N^-3) allowance, not the nonshrinking w=1/4
formation theorem. The full all-pattern or distributional large-N
formation question is still open. No negative no-go is inferred from
the failure of the old invariant subspace or of this weak estimate.

## 10. Independent disorder does not restore the real-energy gap

The S3 witness extends directly. For all q_i=1, a word of length N-1,
and the probe on its unique free event, the Q diagonal is

    80 + sum_(i != root) delta_i + dGamma(h_rel).

For N>=128 choose the far packet's one-particle carrier

    e = -[80 + sum_(i != root) delta_i]/(N-1).

Every declared pattern has |e|<=3/4+80/(N-1)<2. The contact annihilator
still vanishes, the bare append still leaves Q, and the S3 residual
bound remains r_N=2pi(N-1)/(32N^2+1). Its P image still has norm
epsilon/sqrt(N). Projecting the witness onto the Q energy window
|E|<=2sqrt(N)r_N retains nonzero B-dagger coupling by the same spectral
triangle inequality. Thus coupled real-energy poles approach the seed
window even with independent detuning. Four direct packet calculations
check signed-ramp assignments at N=128,256; the all-pattern statement
rests on this argument, not that grid. These packet checks are not
large many-body trajectories or a measurement of the prepared overlap.

## Status

S4 establishes preparation-conditioned bulk lifetime and feedback
bounds for the frozen independent storage disorder, and supplies an
exact history-preserving computational reduction. It does not establish
nonshrinking-disorder macroscopic formation, a uniform real-energy gap,
equilibrium spatial phase, selected dimension, charged-matter
universality or a distinctive prediction about nature.
