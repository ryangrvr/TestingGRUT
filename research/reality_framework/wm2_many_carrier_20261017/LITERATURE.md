# Primary-source context and novelty limits

* Nachtergaele, Sims and Young, [Quasi-Locality Bounds for Quantum Lattice
  Systems, Part I](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.02428), J. Math. Phys. 60, 061101
  (2019), Theorems 3.1 and 3.3. Finite-range local interaction strength, rather
  than the extensive total Hamiltonian norm, controls connected propagation.
  On-site energies can be handled separately. D1 uses that established
  mechanism; its additional obligation is the explicit moving-end/receiver
  comparison and the occupation-weighted buffer account. It does not claim
  the invention of a finite-density Lieb-Robinson bound.
* Donoghue, [Quantum General Relativity and Effective Field
  Theory](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.09902v2). Low-energy quantum gravity already
  has an effective-field-theory treatment. D1 neither competes with its
  gravitational calculations nor derives a gravitational sector. Any later
  such claim must be compared to that existing benchmark.

The free channel's Bessel formulas are derived directly from its sine transform
and cross-checked by a rational series and finite matrix evolution. They are
ordinary quantum-transport results with these inputs, not unique GRUT laws.
