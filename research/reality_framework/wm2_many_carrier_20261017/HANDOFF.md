not an external referee report. Start with MODEL.md and PROOF.md; the paper
history need not be reconstructed to identify the load-bearing assumptions.

   obstruction, the unique cumulative field solution, capacity 3N coverage,

4. **Weighted occupation:** check the N_I-block quadratic-form estimate and

5. **Common physical space:** verify prefix matter/tail factorization with actual

   the extensive bulk cancels from the drive, while all cut terms, bare head
   gates and assisted prefix-breaking pops are accounted for. Full H, not the
   reference, occurs on the physical side of Duhamel.
6. **All-size constants:** check P10–P12, support offsets, source variance,
   separate formation/use thresholds, and short-time positivity with extensive

7. **Preparation and energy:** try to compose the protocol without silently
   creating matter from the neutral seed or resetting the receiver. Verify
   the Theta(N^2) flux and Theta(N^3) electric-energy prices. No original-trial
   populated-world lower bound is currently claimed.
8. **Predictivity:** check the half-line image sign, exact rational intervals,
   finite-cut Duhamel factor, units and calibration/product-error allowance.
   The 64-site device proposal tests an effective channel, not the entire D1
   formation architecture, and is not distinctive against identical tight binding.
