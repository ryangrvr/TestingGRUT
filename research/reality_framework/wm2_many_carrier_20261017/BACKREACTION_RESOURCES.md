# Backreaction, capacity, and what has not been prepared

**Author-derived within D1; independent review pending.** Relational locality,
hard-core statistics, root compensation and zero electric energy are inputs.

## Actual coupling to geometry and receiver

For a word of length m>1 the last cut equation is

    E_last=-(n_A,head+2n_B,head).

Hence its zero-flux gate is exactly the empty-head projector. A charged
head blocks both assisted and bare pops. A bare append reads the head's
total occupation 0,1,2, creates no receiver boson and may produce storage
inconsistency; the reverse process remains. Assisted attachment changes
storage and contact occupation together. Matter motion changes the physical
flux on its traversed link and changes whether these end operations can act.
Hopping and geometry are not globally decoupled Hamiltonians.

These statements account for real backreaction, but all graph changes are
at the movable end. D1 has no bulk rewiring or curvature equation. Nothing
here is a gravitational stress-response law.

The full state comparison P7 cancels the **identical** extensive interior
Hamiltonian and controls only the remaining boundary drive. P10 bounds prefix
loss/storage-error probabilities by [exp(-N/5)+3exp(-N/17)]^2 in its O(N)
window. Any bounded receiver observable differs from its specified reflected-
tail reference by at most twice the state bound. The reference retains the
actual conditional receiver and every assisted return. No physical transition
is disabled in H_D1. At small N the archived full calculation has nonzero
storage-error population; the feedback is not assumed to vanish identically.

## Capacity and energy price

For the P9 product background, N=32n, charges 1 at 4q and 2 at 4q+2,
q=0,...,n-1, the first link carries magnitude 3n-1. Direct summation of M1
gives

    sum_edges |E| = 6n^2-2n,
    sum_edges E^2 = 12n^3-6n^2+2n.                             (B1)

The small adjacent source superpositions change these by lower-order terms.
Thus extensive matter requires extensive link capacity, quadratically growing
total flux magnitude, and an extensive root compensating charge. The new
3N capacity covers all declared sectors, rather than optimizing only P9.

Electric energy is zero in D1. If a fixed positive coefficient u_E multiplying
sum E^2 were added, B1 would cost Theta(N^3), with energy per particle of
order N^2. That perturbation is outside the frozen D1/RI class; no claim of
a conventional extensive thermodynamic charged phase under it is established.
This observation does not prove every neutralized/distributed-charge model
fails; none is added here to repair it.

If preparation uses root injection and bounded-range charge-preserving moves,
B1 also gives an Omega(N^2) **operation-count** cost for the flux string: one
primitive changes only boundedly many field values by bounded amounts. This
is not a serial physical time lower bound when gates may run in parallel,
nor an energy bound when the electric Hamiltonian is zero. Arbitrary external
global preparation would need its own resource accounting.

## Formation/preparation/observation sequence

1. Begin with the original matter-vacuum neutral seed. S9's author-derived
   formation bound and resource sequence are inherited exactly, pending review.
2. Record a qualifying word; rejected records are not silently renormalized.
3. Supply the extensive matter/countercharge/flux preparation. Its controller,
   work, duration and success probability have **not** been derived here.
   Retain the actual receiver rather than resetting it.
4. For any successfully prepared state satisfying the support/buffer hypothesis,
   use the full H_D1 for the stated response and observation window.

Only step 4 has the new all-size finite-density guarantee. The whole sequence
does not yet have an original-trial positive probability, because step 3 is
unpriced operationally. [H_D1,N_s]=0 forbids replacing it by an autonomous
claim that the original seed creates this matter. Enlarging a protected band
or a receiver does not circumvent that exact number-conservation statement.

The formation time remains O(N^2), the conditional use window O(N), the
receiver L=64N^2, and g/N stays size-dependent. No useful asymptotic regime
with a fixed receiver budget or no macroscopic buffer has been shown.
