## 1. What is specified

With signature (-,+,+,+) and R=6(Hdot+2H²), use

\[
S=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\,[K(q)R/2-\epsilon(q)]+S_m[g,\psi],
\qquad q=*dA_3.
\]

The four-form and metric equations are

\[
\epsilon'(q)-\tfrac12K'(q)R=\mu,\qquad \partial_\nu\mu=0,
\]
\[
K G_{\mu\nu}=T_{\mu\nu}-(\epsilon-\mu q)g_{\mu\nu}
 -(g_{\mu\nu}\Box-\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu)K.
\]

Minimally coupled matter is conserved. These are Klinkhamer–Volovik
q-theory equations, also expressible on a regular fixed-mu branch as
omega_BD=0 scalar-tensor gravity/f(R). The earlier blind derivation,
source comparison and Bianchi checks remain in `tracks/equations` and
`tracks/literature`. Codex's separate scalar-tensor construction agrees.

For a nonzero equilibrium q0, the additional state condition P_q is

\[
\epsilon_\infty'(q_0)=\mu_0,\qquad
\epsilon_\infty(q_0)-\mu_0q_0=0,
\qquad K(q_0)>0,\quad\epsilon_\infty''(q_0)>0.
\]

Here epsilon_infinity includes the q-independent energy of a specified
final matter vacuum. A final vacuum has to be identified; it cannot mean
all local minima independently. P_q selects a state. It is not a
consequence of the nondissipative evolution.

An additive constant c illustrates the distinction:

\[
\left.{d\mu_0\over dc}\right|_{P_q}={1\over q_0}.
\]

Selecting mu0 from the fully renormalized final potential cancels its
constant term by construction. Keeping the same numerical mu while
changing that constant makes the change gravitate. Saying that all loop
constants cancel under P_q does not establish a mechanism that prepares
mu or a quantum calculation that protects its value across new thresholds.
Reapplying P_q after each transition would violate the conserved-mu law.

The free functions epsilon and K, matter parameters and initial state
are supplied EFT inputs. Neither packet derives them from GRUT closure.
The earlier vacuum-pass synthesis is preserved as a separate survey;
its result is limited to the routes and read levels it actually covered.

## 5. Dissipation: verified result and correction

For the tested KV16a phenomenological law at constant K=M²,

\[
Q={\gamma\over36}|H|R^2,\quad
\dot\rho_V=-Q,\quad
\dot\rho_r+4H\rho_r=Q,
\quad\gamma={1\over32\pi^2}.
\]

The energy receiver is essential. For expanding radiation plus vacuum,
R=4rho_V/M², hence exactly

\[
{d\rho_V\over dN}=-{4\gamma\over9M^4}\rho_V^2,
\qquad
\rho_V(N)={\rho_{Vi}\over1+4\gamma\rho_{Vi}N/(9M^4)}.
\]

Radiation does **not** destroy this mathematical attractor. The issue
is selecting the observed tiny residual during a specified finite
history. With the pinned M and rho_Lambda inputs, reducing an initial
M^4 to that density requires about 9.91e122 e-folds. The reference
entropy-conserving span from T=M to the present temperature is about
72.5 e-folds. In that span an initial M^4 becomes about 0.9074 M^4.
If the solution instead follows the early-start vacuum-dominated
asymptote up to the reference age, its density is about 8.90e81 times
the reference observed density. Those histories fail; this is not a
no-go theorem for arbitrary earlier histories or different source laws.

**Inequality correction.** `9M^4/(4 gamma N)` is an upper envelope
for positive finite initial rho_V, not a universal finite-time lower
floor. Claude's separate half-minimum lower bound is valid. Calling
the envelope a floor in dissipation section 5.1 and the numerical
verifier is incorrect. The exact finite-initial-data result above
preserves the finite-history failure without that claim.

If the self-draining vacuum is already subdominant at BBN, its
fractional self-depletion over the subsequent reference radiation span
is at most about 3.2e-87. Nonconformal traces and phase transitions
add history dependence, including possible negative offsets; they
do not supply the required small positive value in the worked models.
The source is one-sided. A negative-vacuum expanding control turns
around; it is not restored toward zero.

KSV16 uses a different source that vanishes for the entire static
de Sitter family. Such a source does not select Minkowski uniquely.
K22's illustrative H^5 source need not stop at zero vacuum energy
while radiation remains. Its evolving endpoint also need not solve
the original algebraic n equation. The missing K22 table example is
reproduced by a new energy-variable integration. These are distinct
models and must retain their separate assumptions.

The local production law and its use as a q-energy drain remain
