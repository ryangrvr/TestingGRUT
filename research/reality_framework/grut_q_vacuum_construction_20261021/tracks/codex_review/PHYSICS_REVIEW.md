# Physics review

22 October 2026 UTC. Codex, same author as the new verification, after
reading the archived tracks and both completed sets of calculations.
This is not a second-agent review or outside replication.

| Item | Reviewed statement |
|---|---|
| Action and fixed-mu equations | Confirmed by the two earlier derivations; the new integration uses a separate constant-K formulation and checks its constraint |
| Cancellation of additive constants | Conditional on reselecting the fully renormalized final state; not a dynamical consequence. dmu0/dc=1/q0 explicitly retains the state cost |
| Electroweak kick | No constant remnant is derived from the fixed-mu action in these constructions; the phenomenological freezing law is a distinct assumption |
| Gravitating q potential versus inferred dark energy | Use the full metric response including K and its derivatives. The leading dust correction is nine times the potential-only term |
| MeV/millieV notation | Laboratory scalar limit here is approximately 3.65 **meV**, not MeV; it is an unscreened branch limit |
| Screened escape | The published examples studied have the stated de Sitter endpoints; this does not classify every possible nonlinear f(R) potential |
| Multiple vacua | A single mu cannot independently set all unequal matter minima to zero; a present positive false phase is algebraically possible. Its magnitude and actual future exit must be supplied or derived |
| Electroweak absolute stability | Conditional on identifying that phase with the selected true final phase and on the SM-desert assumptions; an extremely near-degenerate false phase is another branch |
| SM equality versus inequality | Absolute stability gives an inequality. A specified positive gap or multiple-point coexistence is a separate equality condition, with different assumptions |
| SM numerical relation | SMDR library reproduced from its official v1.3 archive; new beta-root minimum agrees with the archived mass to 6.4e-8 GeV; direct full matching checks the interpolation near the root |
| SM precision | That agreement checks numerics only. The finite-order RG stability proxy is not an all-order potential calculation or a resolution of a 1e-120 gap. Theory, mass scheme and ultraviolet assumptions remain |
| Dissipative models | Energy receiver required; three source prescriptions kept distinct. Natural-rate finite-history mismatch confirmed, envelope inequality corrected |
| Phase transitions | Scalar kinetic energy and the recipient fluid must be included; a prescribed time-dependent constant with no compensating energy transfer is insufficient |
| Earlier Codex QV1-M | Useful classical accelerating counterexample to an unconditional current-vacuum stability statement, with a supplied gap. Tunneling/lifetime and actual far-future P_q consistency remain open |
| Early high curvature | Natural Planck-scale starts and compressed super-Planckian numerical controls do not establish physical EFT validity |
| Perturbations | The earlier quasi-static response identities have their stated regime. No full perturbation/stability calculation for the dissipative extension is newly supplied |

The construction is usable as a specified classical working candidate
with disclosed state and parameter costs. It does not satisfy the
stronger requirement of selecting the observed total vacuum energy
with quantum stability and an independent empirical discriminator.
The proposed next mechanism must be a new labelled model if it changes
the constant-mu law, current, matter coupling or state rule.
