22 October 2026 UTC. One-author Codex review, not outside replication.

## Statements to use

- The framework is Klinkhamer–Volovik q-theory, with standard
  scalar-tensor/f(R), false-vacuum and SM-stability consequences.
- The specified classical examples are conserved and reproducible.
- Uncalibrated QV1's defined flat late-time benchmark fails existing
  expansion tests. QV1-cal puts the residual in by hand.
- The tested curvature-production law does not prepare that residual
  during the defined finite thermal history. Radiation itself does
  not remove its positive-branch attractor.
- The Higgs/top line tests a named conditional SM/final-vacuum hypothesis.
  It does not test all q-theory, all GRUT models or an arbitrary ultraviolet
  completion. Different top-mass inputs and error assumptions change the
  retrospective discrepancy materially.
- Recording a relation now can define future comparisons. All data used
  in either construction remain exposed. A future central-value forecast
  is not a measured significance.
- The additional review and programs are written by one AI author.
  Separate formulations provide error checks, not outside replication.

## Boundaries retained

This review does not alter Candidate 1, claim new microscopic particle
content, unify quantum theory with gravity, derive consciousness, or
establish which ontology reality has. It does not merge into the canonical
GRUT-RAI program. The main deliverable is the theory construction and
its executable checks; the limitation is the missing physical selection
of state, residual and history.

The earlier vacuum-pass survey is retained with its own source read
levels and corrections. Its failure to find a route passing all criteria
is a result of that survey, not a proof that no possible theory can do so.
