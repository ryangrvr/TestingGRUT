# Sources and scientific status

The user-supplied WM2 charter and the published WM1 handoff at commit
`8741b6e74d571e643e9208187f5d93b5a8e2471c` define this assignment.
The actual repository handoff and source files were read. No unseen
review document is presented as an independently checked source.

The S1 register law, prefix argument, graph enumeration and scoped
formation bound are laid out completely in this packet. These are
project-specific calculations using ordinary quantum mechanics and
matrix analysis, not evidence that GRUT predicts nature or an assertion
that these broad mathematical ideas are unprecedented.

Primary-source context checked for this work:

1. Sergey Bravyi, David P. DiVincenzo and Daniel Loss,
   [Schrieffer-Wolff transformation for quantum many-body systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.0675),
   Annals of Physics 326, 2793–2826 (2011).
   Established context for distinguishing bare and exact low subspaces,
   effective Hamiltonians and controlled connected expansions. Its
   locality results have hypotheses; they do not prove S1 or WM2. S1's
   Schur series is summed with a direct all-walk bound, not a truncated
   many-body SW approximation.

2. Tomasz Konopka, Fotini Markopoulou and Simone Severini,
   [Quantum Graphity: a model of emergent locality](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0861),
   Physical Review D 77, 104029 (2008).
   Dynamical permutation-symmetric graph proposals predate this project.
   S1 does not claim that merely using graph registers and label
   covariance is a distinctive GRUT prediction.

3. Samuel A. Wilkinson and Andrew D. Greentree,
   [Geometrogenesis under Quantum Graphity: problems with the ripening Universe](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.07588),
   Physical Review D 92, 084007 (2015).
   Primary context for separately testing graph formation and connectivity.
   That paper's model-specific findings are not imported as evidence
   against S1, CP-12 or every dynamical graph theory.

No external reviewer or experiment has validated this packet. No derived
fundamental scale, Born rule, three-dimensionality, gravitational limit,
consciousness mechanism or distinctive empirical prediction is claimed.
