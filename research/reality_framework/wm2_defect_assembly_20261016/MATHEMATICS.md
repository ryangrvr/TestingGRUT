# WM2-S1 exact construction and its formation boundary

All proofs concern the full invariant configuration component of the
declared S1 register Hamiltonian, in probe vacuum or the one-probe
sector as stated. They are not claims about every register component,
finite probe density, CP-12 charged matter or all WM2 architectures.

## 1. The metric is defined before the transport calculation

All distinct-label words beginning at the root are generated by the
primitive append/pop operators. P0 is the full-length sector K=0.
Within P0 the primitive event-transition graph for word s consists of
exactly its occupied consecutive bonds. Its shortest-path metric is
d_op^s(i,j); no virtual amplitude is used to choose it.

For every such s the degree is at most two and diameter is N-1. A ball
of radius r centered at position a in that generated ordering contains

`1 + min(r,a) + min(r,N-1-a)`

events. Interior mesoscopic ball growth is exactly linear. The metric
is additive along each segment. This earns a nontrivial event adjacency
from assembly, **not** the choice of a one-dimensional geometry class;
the latter is supplied by the single-end grammar.

## 2. Prefix lemma: include every geometry change

Start at any full word s and end at any full word t. Let
k=N-min_t|w_t| be the maximum end-removal depth during a configuration path.
The prefix of length N-k never changes: only the current end can pop
or append. At least 2k assembly operations occur because both endpoints
are fully assembled.

Let h be the number of probe hops, and let d=d_op^s(i,j), even when the
final word is t rather than s. If both labels stay in the invariant
prefix, h>=d: excursions into its suffix can enter that prefix only at
its final event. If exactly one is in the removed suffix,
d<=h+k-1. If both lie in the removed suffix, d<=k-1.
These cases also cover a free probe which waits for its event to reattach.

For paths with no assembly moves, the usual h>=d applies. For any
virtual excursion from P0 through Q0=1-P0 and back to P0, k>=1, so

\[
\boxed{k_{\rm virtual}(s,i;t,j)\ge d_{\rm op}^s(i,j)+2.}
\]

For both labels in the prefix, use length>=h+2k>=d+2. For exactly one
in the suffix use length>=h+2k>=d+k+1. For both in the suffix,
length>=2k>=2d+2. The same argument gives length>=d for an unrestricted
configuration path with arbitrary visits to P0.

The root is never popped. All prefix permutations and all final full
orders are allowed in this argument. It does not rely on freezing a
bond during a virtual excursion. In particular a word of fixed length
L cannot connect protected event separation greater than L-2 through Q0.

There is also a **derived geometric activation barrier**. If a new bond
joins labels of original separation d>1, at least one endpoint has been
removed from the initial prefix. To append the later label onto the
earlier one requires popping every event after the earlier label; the
opposite orientation requires popping that earlier label too. In either
case the maximum defect count along the activation path is at least d.
Thus every such path visits diagonal assembly energy UK>=Ud. No term
in the primitive Hamiltonian contains d: this barrier follows from the
one-end dynamics and the metric of its generated branch. A complete
minimax graph search checks the barrier through N=7; for each d its
lower bound is achieved by some branch and bond. It is a path-energy
statement, distinct from the complete resolvent bound proved below.

## 3. Pay for all branching

Let A+ be the append part of the Hamiltonian with coefficient
epsilon/sqrt(N), lifted trivially on probe position. Each child has one
parent, and a word of length m has N-m children. Hence

\[
A_+^\dagger A_+|w,p\rangle
=\epsilon^2\frac{N-m}{N}|w,p\rangle,
\quad\|A_+\|\le\epsilon.
\]

Thus the entire assembly kinetic operator has norm <=2 epsilon,
independently of the factorial number of allowed words. The probe
adjacency on each partial assembly has norm <=2J. The nonnegative
off-diagonal matrix T in H=UK-T obeys ||T||_2<=t0=2epsilon+2J.
This is a Hilbert-space norm, not a false claim that every configuration
has a bounded number of available appends. Without the supplied
1/sqrt(N) normalization, the append norm grows as sqrt(N-1).

At the full-length boundary every parent of length N-1 has one child,
and every full child has one parent. Therefore, with B=Q0 H P0,

\[
B^\dagger B=(\epsilon^2/N)P_0,\qquad\|B\|=\epsilon/\sqrt N.
\]

## 4. An actual isolated low band

Let a=2J. PHP lies in [-a,a]. In Q, K>=1, so QHQ>=mI,
m=U-2epsilon-2J. If g=m-a=U-2epsilon-4J>0, interlacing gives exactly
dim(P0) low eigenvalues <=a; all remaining eigenvalues are at least m.
The separating gap is at least g, independent of N.

This is a derived low spectral band Pi, not the supplied bare projector.
Its distance from P0 is at most ||B||/g. To see this, restrict H to its
high spectral space H_high>=m and let X=P0 V_high. The projected
eigenvalue equation is a Sylvester equation with left spectrum <=a
and right spectrum >=m. Its integral solution has norm <=||B||/(m-a).
Equal ranks identify this norm with the largest principal sine between
Pi and P0. Consequently an initially bare P0 state satisfies

\[
\Pr(Q_0,t)\le4\epsilon^2/(Ng^2)
\]

at every time. This allows other full orderings and is not by itself
an original-word retention theorem. For any initially recorded full s,
the cross-boundary norm is epsilon/sqrt(N), so Duhamel also gives

\[
\Pr(\hbox{original word }s,t)\ge
\max(0,1-\epsilon^2t^2/N).
\]

This persistence scaling comes partly from the explicitly supplied
normalization, not from an earned phase transition.

## 5. Complete connected virtual sum

For real E<=a, set D_E=UK-E on Q. D_E>=U-E>t0 and QHQ-E=D_E-T_Q.
The exact Schur correction is

\[
\Sigma(E)=-B^\dagger D_E^{-1}
\sum_{r\ge0}(T_QD_E^{-1})^r B.
\]

Every term is a connected excursion from the specified source back
to the protected boundary. It includes all intermediate geometry
words, repeated walks and their multiplicities. The prefix lemma
implies its matrix element from (s,i) to (t,j) vanishes for r<d_op^s(i,j).
Bounding each remaining term by its Hilbert-space norm yields

\[
\boxed{
|\Sigma(E)_{t,j;s,i}|\le
\frac{\epsilon^2}{N[U-E-t_0]}
\left(\frac{t_0}{U-E}\right)^{d_{\rm op}^s(i,j)}.}
\]

This is a matrix-element-specific distance bound: a norm estimate alone
would omit the crucial first d vanishing terms. It is uniform over all
initial/final full orders and all E<=2J, and pays for the full virtual
branching. No geometry rows have been discarded. In this model all
off-diagonal coefficients are negative, so the walk terms have one
sign; interference is retained and no cancellation is assumed.

There is a stronger **summed receiver / coherent source** version. Let
R_j^P include all full words with probe at event j. Let P_(i,d;j) include
all full words with probe at i and d_op^s(i,j)>=d. The prefix lemma makes
the first d terms vanish as an entire rectangular operator block, hence

\[
\|R_j^P\Sigma(E)P_{i,d;j}\|_2\le
\frac{\epsilon^2}{N[U-E-t_0]}
\left(\frac{t_0}{U-E}\right)^d.
\]

Thus arbitrary normalized coherent packets over those source geometries
and all final-geometry receiver records obey the same bound. No factorial
number of final records is hidden in a pointwise estimate. Finite-matrix
tests check these operator blocks as well as individual matrix elements.

For U=16,epsilon=1,J=1/4 the band gap is >=13 and

\[
|\Sigma(E)_{t,j;s,i}|\le
\frac1{13N}\left(\frac5{31}\right)^{d_{\rm op}^s(i,j)},
\qquad E\le1/2.
\]

The exponential length is 1/log(31/5). For adjacent events in the same
word, PHP=-J and Sigma is nonpositive. Hence the complete effective
coupling has magnitude at least J=1/4. Transport has not been eliminated.
For arbitrary normalized source packets supported on words where i,j are
adjacent, the receiver action is at least
J-epsilon^2/[N(U-E-t0)] by the reverse triangle inequality: the bare
neighbor map is an isometry times -J. This remains strictly positive at
the declared parameters even with arbitrary geometry coherence.
For d>1, the bare matrix element vanishes and the boxed expression is
already the complete effective coupling. A single bound including
d=0,1 can use C=J exp(1/xi)+1/13, independent of N.

This is an energy-dependent Feshbach statement in an explicitly local
microscopic basis, not permission to propagate exp[-it H_eff(0)] as
though it were an exact energy-independent Hamiltonian. Pi and P0 are
kept distinct. A separate all-orders localized canonical dressing for
all possible full orderings is not claimed here.

## 6. Full-H propagation and a nonzero local source

Let ell be full configuration-graph distance from a given source state.
Every primitive off-diagonal move changes ell by at most one, while UK
is diagonal. The norm domination for nonnegative T gives the weighted
Dyson estimate

\[
|\langle c'|e^{-itH}|c\rangle|
\le\exp[t_0e^\mu|t|-\mu\ell(c,c')],\qquad\mu>0.
\]

The prefix argument also gives ell>=d_op for arbitrary final partial
words: at least k pops occur, and the three prefix cases still imply
length>=d. Let R_j now include **all** final geometries, partial or full.
In the interaction picture of UK the r-th Dyson term has norm at most
(t0|t|)^r/r!, and its block R_j (...) P_(i,d;j) vanishes for r<d. Therefore

\[
\|R_j e^{-itH}P_{i,d;j}\|_2\le
\sum_{r\ge d}\frac{(t_0|t|)^r}{r!}
\le\exp[t_0e^\mu|t|-\mu d].
\]

This includes arbitrary coherent source geometry packets and sums all
receiver records without a factorial loss. For a local density phase
whose initial geometry support satisfies that separation, Cauchy-Schwarz
bounds |chi_j| by twice this norm times ||n_i psi||. This is a uniform
conditional propagation bound, not a numerically fitted speed. Its
metric still comes from the supplied single-end grammar and its
population still fails the formation test below.

The small numerical source controls prepare a recorded full s and probe
(|i>+|i+1>)/sqrt(2), with source number variance 1/4. For the bare local
phase exp(-i theta n_i), chi_j=2 Im <U psi,n_j U n_i psi> and
chi_(i+1)(t)=Jt+O(t^2). Number measurements sum over **all** final geometry
configurations. Full-H +/-pi/2 pulse identities check the responses.
This conditional preparation has low-band weight >=1-epsilon^2/(Ng^2),
but its production from the neutral seed is not granted.

## 7. Formation fails in the closed seeded law, at every time

Use probe vacuum. Start with the permutation-invariant bondless seeded
state; the representative fixed-root state has

\[
E_0=U(N-1),\quad
\|(H-E_0)\psi_0\|=\sigma=\epsilon\sqrt{(N-1)/N}.
\]

Both mean energy and this residual norm are conserved. Since PHP=0 in
probe vacuum and ||PHQ||=b=epsilon/sqrt(N), projecting
(H-E0)psi(t) onto P0 gives

\[
E_0\|P_0\psi(t)\|\le\sigma+b,
\qquad
\boxed{p_{\rm full}(t)\le
\frac{\epsilon^2(\sqrt{N-1}+1)^2}
{N U^2(N-1)^2}.}
\]

The bound tends to zero as 1/N^2 and holds at **all** times, not merely
the sampled radial trajectory. To avoid an overly strict full-length
criterion, also let R_L project onto words of length at least L.
There H_R<=U(N-L)+2epsilon, and its boundary norm is
b_L=epsilon sqrt((N-L+1)/N). The same projected residual argument yields

\[
\Pr(m\ge L,t)\le
\left[\frac{\sigma+b_L}{U(L-1)-2\epsilon}\right]^2
\]

when the denominator is positive. For L=ceil(N/2), this also vanishes
as 1/N^2. Therefore the failure is not an artifact of demanding perfect
full assembly: macroscopic partial assemblies are also suppressed.
These statements hold unchanged in the declared product embedding of
the original CP-12 neutral ray, since the auxiliary Hamiltonian evolves
independently. They do not apply automatically if a new energy-exchange
interaction is introduced.

Nor can parameter retuning within this proved protection regime remove
the scaling failure. U>2epsilon+4J implies epsilon/U<1/2, so the full
formation bound is strictly smaller than
(sqrt(N-1)+1)^2/[4N(N-1)^2], which still vanishes. This is a scoped
obstruction for the declared seeded autonomous S1 family in its proved
gap regime, not a theorem excluding every possible S1 parameter regime
or every WM2 formation mechanism.

An exact invariant radial reduction has N states labeled only by
assembly length, diagonal U(N-m) and adjacent-length coupling
-epsilon sqrt((N-m)/N). Its multiplicity at length m, fixed root, is
D_m=(N-1)!/(N-m)!. This reduction retains the complete coherent formation
state and is checked against the full finite Hamiltonian at small N.
Tiny sampled probabilities may be below numerical resolution; the
analytic all-time bounds, not those tiny samples, decide the gate.

## 8. Complete recording and input costs

A complete ideal record dephases every assembly word, including failure
states and all roots. Its mean energy change is
W_rec=-<H_assembly>. Its magnitude is at most 2epsilon. On the symmetric
radial trajectory its fixed-root entropy is

`H({p_m}) + sum_m p_m log2(D_m)`.

Recording the uniform root adds log2(N) bits and zero root-dephasing
work because root sectors do not couple. One particular full word has
probability p_full/(N-1)! at fixed root, or p_full/N! with uniform roots.
No claim about device entropy, heat or automatic observation follows.
The seed energy, root register and injected probe are supplied resources.

## 9. Why finite support alone is insufficient

A different finite-support primitive could rotate the two blocks after
the root of a full path. For s=(0,1,...,N-1), j=floor(N/2), take
t=(0,j,...,N-1,1,...,j-1). It changes two old bonds, two new bonds and
the two head bits: six qubits, with no supplied coordinates. Then
rotation -> probe hop 0 to j -> inverse rotation is a three-step word
across old S1 distance j. S1 excludes this by its actual primitive
end-defect rules, not by an after-the-fact deletion from a calculation.

If that primitive is added, the protected operational metric must be
recomputed; it collapses rather than remaining the old path metric by
definition. Finite support and permutation covariance alone do not
derive spatial locality. A future cooling/assembly interaction must
preserve the proved prefix property or replace it with a new proof.

## Decision

S1 constructs event adjacency without fixed pair affinities and proves
a size-uniform virtual barrier including every partial assembly. It
does not earn the one-dimensional grammar, appreciable formation,
universality or a continuum. The closed S1 candidate fails the WM2
formation gate. This is a scoped result, not a general WM2 no-go.
