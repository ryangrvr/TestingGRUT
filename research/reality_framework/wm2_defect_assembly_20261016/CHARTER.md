# WM2: dynamic geometry and protected transport

Reference: WM1 commit `8741b6e74d571e643e9208187f5d93b5a8e2471c`.
WM2 is a separate construction campaign. Frozen CP-12, Path A and SCOUT-1
are not changed or reopened.

**WM2 asks whether a microscopic quantum law can generate an operational
spatial geometry while making violations of that geometry dynamically
inaccessible.**

## Nonnegotiable gates

1. No vertex coordinates, Euclidean metric, lattice embedding or fixed
   preferred event adjacency in the primitive law.
2. Define the bare protected sector from an energy/constraint operator;
   derive the actual low spectral band, rather than choosing a favorable graph.
3. Define the operational metric from protected primitive transitions
   before evaluating transport. Proving a distance bound in configuration
   distance alone is insufficient: establish its relation to this metric.
4. Include all reachable geometry configurations and every virtual path.
5. Exclude a constant-length activation–transport–restoration word across
   growing operational distance; pay for virtual-word multiplicity.
6. Prove a connected source-to-receiver, distance-weighted bound. A bare
   global norm, large gap or vanishing transport everywhere is insufficient.
7. Preserve nonzero nearby transport, meaningful scaling and explicit
   accounting for probe preparation, geometry recording and universality.
8. Start formation from the original neutral preparation, specifying all
   added registers, seeds, reservoirs and energy. Supplied cooling or
   externally prepared low-energy states cannot be called derived formation.

## First bounded construction: WM2-S1

Test a single movable assembly-end defect. Every unordered choice of
new event is available; only the current end can attach a free event.
No event pair is prescribed as a spatial neighbor. All reachable
partial assemblies are retained, including those that rearrange the
event order during a virtual excursion.

The single-end grammar is an input and favors a one-dimensional class.
Do not call that dimension dynamically selected. Distinguish generated
event adjacency from a fully earned spatial phase.

Deliver: primitive register Hamiltonian; invariant configuration graph;
energy-defined protected sector; its operational metric; exact virtual
word enumeration; all-orders source-to-receiver bound; nonzero adjacent
transport; and a formation feasibility bound from the declared preparation.

Primary parameters, supplied before numerical interpretation:
U=16, epsilon=1, J=1/4 and the permutation-symmetric assembly amplitude
epsilon/sqrt(N). The size normalization is an explicit part of the law.
Check small full configuration graphs through N=7, virtual words through
length 10, Schur complements through N=6, and small full-H spectra.
Use exact symbolic/integer checks where possible. A symmetry-reduced
formation calculation is permitted only after the exact analysis.

Success for the **full WM2 campaign** additionally needs appreciable
formation, stable physical neighborhoods and dimensional scaling not
merely imposed by the grammar, and shared transport for relevant matter.
If a candidate only solves protection or fails formation, preserve its
mathematics and mark the failed gate. Do not weaken these requirements.
