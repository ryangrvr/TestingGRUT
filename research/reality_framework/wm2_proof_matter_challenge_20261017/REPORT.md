## What the receiver analysis resolves

integrable power-law decay, a uniform positive spectral-overlap rate,
and a finite-kernel error with N^2 epsilon_N tending to zero. It derives

An equally large flat receiver fails by an all-time energy inequality.
Thus the exact path bandwidth is not the only possible working choice,
but mode count, vacuum and charge alone do not explain formation.
No receiver-resource reduction or proof of minimality follows.

## Decision and remaining obligations

stops here. Preserve WM2 as a controlled model of formed relational paths
and its chosen probe; prepare S9 for an external proof review. A successor
foundational architecture must justify why every relevant matter sector
obeys the same geometry, including direct terms as well as virtual paths.

Grammar-selected dimension, supplied quantum dynamics/receiver, recording
and injection, original charged-matter universality, continuum/causal
structure, gravity and empirical discrimination remain open. The primary
literature comparison identifies closely related graph and locality work;
no foundational priority or distinctive prediction about nature is claimed.
