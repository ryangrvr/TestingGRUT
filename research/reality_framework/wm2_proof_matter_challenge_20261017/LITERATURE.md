The bounded result offered here is the combination of WM2's existing
formation/use obligations with explicit allowed extra-species counterexamples
and an assumption ledger. No general novelty is claimed for complete-graph
or star quantum walks, Duhamel bounds, moment inequalities, or the logical
fact that unrestricted couplings can violate locality. The next foundational
task would be to derive a common coupling restriction, not rename the
occupied-bond choice as universality.
