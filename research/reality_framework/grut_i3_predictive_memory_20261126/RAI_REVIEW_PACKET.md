## Independent challenges

1. Construct a separate finite implementation from the frozen
   state edges. Compute acquisition, raw retained accuracy, transfer
   accuracy, conditional yields, all scored arms and thermodynamic
   ledgers. Report tail or solver errors, failed limits and
   confidence intervals if using sampling.
2. Verify the no-hit hazard proof with the actual no-bond filtration.
   Try to beat the null ceiling with the allowed history dependence,
   a time-dependent port split, randomized expectations and
   cost-free latching. Identify any missed admissible causal
   signal. Check the weak cue bound for complete cue histories.
3. Reconstruct what the passive, shuffled and inverted controls
   retain in the full accessible state. In particular, $A\oplus C$
   reconstructs the shuffled actuator's delivered record at
   transfer when both can be read. Assess whether fuel status,
   phase duration, pre-binding scattering, loading, or an actuator
   register changes the physical access assumptions.
4. Rebuild the bath entropy, one-molecule conservation, device
   marginal reset accounting, and the common overhead assumptions.
   In a chemical or mechanical realization, measure the energy and
   timescales of barrier modulation, transfer, transport and
   reloading. Report if the prepared ensemble or cap is
   unattainable for that device and which costs drive the result.
5. Seek contrary examples under realistic correlations or altered
   interactions. The main quantitative targets are the primary
   reliability and ceiling, the moderate noise pass, and the
   severe miss. Distinguish violations of frozen assumptions from
   contradictions within them. State exactly where an external
   experiment has greater access or fuel options.
6. Compare the central claim against the strongest conventional
   partially observed stochastic controller and rate-distortion
   result. Identify any residual predicted observable after
   matching resources, or establish the equivalence. Report A, B,
   C or D using the user's I3 definitions.
