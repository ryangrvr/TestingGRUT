# Negative results and scope

| Test or claim | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Stronger nonlinear probes applied only at the original F1 port | Exact equivalence for corresponding preparations and all operations confined to that algebra. |
| Uniform mass doubling, a root pin change, or a second measured oscillator coupled to the symmetric collective displacement | Exact F1-pair equivalence. Additional dynamics can preserve the same hidden symmetry. |
| A radial quartic on the hidden block | Exact symmetry control. A tagged nonlinearity distinguishes through a different operator. |
| Using mass and pin changes as separate constraints at only one matched shift | At $z=1$ equal increments give identical changes to the matrix. At $z=2$ those increments differ in effective strength. |
| Unknown scalar load strength in a single-shift pair comparison | The two possible loss intervals overlap near zero. Hidden support alone gives no guarantee at one shift without strength calibration. |
| Every static singleton loading experiment, even with all shifts and strengths | The matched rooted rook and Shrikhande graphs agree exactly. The worst-case theorem needs access to simultaneous pairs or another class of independent data. |
| F1 massless conformal field in the same tube | The whole retarded map is exactly invariant for either bump. |
| Early window or bump translated after the full window | Exact blindness for all registered field species. The outer causal ambiguity persists at the level of the full joint map. |
| Strong deformation probed by a massless minimally coupled scalar | Inconclusive with the controlled bound. The norm upper certificate is 256, outside the small-norm argument. This neither estimates the actual signal nor rejects the conventional equations. |
| Reconstructing an arbitrary visible conformal bump from the two nontrivial scalar scores | Exact residual ambiguity by dimension and continuity, even for calibrated models and profiles inside causal reach. Richer wave data would pose a different question. |
| Unknown effective potentials, gains or boundary law | Coefficient compensation can preserve the full maps with unknown spacetime-dependent potentials. A single massive score can be gain-degenerate. A free exterior response law can trade geometry for a boundary map. |
| Inferring ambient dimension, an Einstein action, or unification of fields | These are outside what the calibrated graph and prescribed-background scalar equations establish. |

The weak scalar certificates are mathematically separated from
zero with very small lower bounds. A laboratory implementation
needs an independent error and systematics budget. The finite
reconstruction requires all tagged supports, and exact inversion
can be ill-conditioned. Prior failures and the F1 obstruction are
part of the pinned parent evidence.
