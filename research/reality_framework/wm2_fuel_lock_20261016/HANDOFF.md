# Reviewer handoff: WM2-S2

## Decision

Preserve this candidate as a **resonant formation-and-protection module**,
not a completed spatial phase. Do not weaken the robustness or dimension
gates to promote it. Exact resonance permits appreciable macroscopic
assembly; a permitted detuning gives an all-time obstruction. The naive
branching alternative fails its own operational-metric test.

## The positive construction

1. Added per-event storage bits receive released free-event binding
   energy. The seed already has that energy; no implicit extra supply
   is required. The uniform-root seed and its initial charged root bit
   are specified resources. Reverse storage-assisted pops remain.
2. The independent S1 append is replaced in this new law. Unsynchronized
   append/pop is a declared probe interaction. Its vacuum vanishing is
   an operator identity; all storage patterns are restored after probe
   injection. This choice must remain visible in every summary.
3. The protected sector is M=0, storage consistent with attachment,
   across **all assembly stages**. It is not the old K=0 full-length
   sector. The whole small root component is independently checked
   from raw register operations.
4. At delta=0 the exact vacuum radial reduction is a finite oscillator.
   It gives uniform seed eigenweights, a uniform radial time ensemble,
   and an explicit finite observation window T_N=O(N log N).
   Macroscopic formation probability >=3/8 refers to a uniformly sampled
   observation time in that window, not every time or rare ordering.
5. The minimum protected configuration route between originally
   attached events still equals their path distance even with assembly
   allowed. Every Q excursion costs at least d+2 primitive moves.
   The new Schur bound sums all storage/geometry paths and receiver
   records: `(1/680)(6/91)^d` for E<=2.5. The auxiliary band gap is >=42.5.
6. Nearby coherent packets have effective receiver action >=0.248529.
   An injected adjacent two-site probe has variance 1/4, full-H nearby
   number response >0.000795 at t=0.005, and a controlled distant
   response front. These statements hold for formed partial paths.
7. A full-H prefix estimate provides conditional bulk-neighborhood
   stability even for times proportional to N. This uses end access
   and the supplied 1/sqrt(N) coefficient rule, not a one-way fuel lock.

## Claims that must not be carried forward

* The S1 diagonal Ud activation barrier does not survive resonant return.
  Its old constants and proof cannot be copied into S2.
* P0 and the exact spectral band Pi are distinct. The mismatch gap is
  proved in the auxiliary at-most-one-probe component at resonance,
  not in the unbounded original CP-12 product spectrum or finite density.
* Full-N assembly has diagonal-ensemble probability 1/N. The positive
  theorem is a macroscopic attached component, with isolated unused
  events. It does not establish an all-N connected stationary phase.
* Exact resonance is supplied. The allowed perturbation delta sum q_i
  gives an all-time O(N^-2) macroscopic formation bound for every fixed
  nonzero delta. Retuning delta with N cannot be called robustness.
* The new neutral probe is injected and prepared after a geometry
  record. Original CP-12 charged a,b excitations have not been shown
  to obey that metric. The embedding remains a product benchmark.
* Full geometry records have a growing explicit entropy. Zero mean
  projective work at resonance is not zero device cost.
* The single-end grammar supplies its one-dimensional class. A proper
  dimension claim cannot relabel this as selected three-dimensionality.

## Branching result to retain

The separately declared two-child/leaf-removal Hamiltonian allows
delete leaf, attach to distant leaf, probe hop, delete, restore. All
five moves stay M=0 and restore the complete original geometry and
fuel. Exact full fifth coefficients at old distances 6,12,24,48 are
-2 with unit coefficients. The normalized value -2g^4 J/N^2 is a
fixed-size Taylor coefficient, not a uniform finite-time theorem.

The true operational distance between leaves is <=5. Complete binary
trees then have radius-five operational balls containing O(N) leaves.
This rejects uniform finite-dimensional geometry on the allowed naive
branching class. It is not a no-go for all multi-defect laws, and no
formation probability for those trees has been inferred.

## Next bounded target

Before increasing simulation size, ask whether a **declared microscopic
conservation principle or explicit autonomous energy receiver** can
make macroscopic formation survive the allowed storage detuning without
destroying the prefix/weighted-path proof. Such a principle is a new
input unless independently earned. Any additional receiver states must
enter the complete configuration graph and new resolvent calculation.

Keep the branching counterexample as a compulsory primitive-word test
for future multi-end grammars. Do not reopen frozen SCOUT-1, claim an
empirical prediction, or use interpretive observer mechanics to repair
the detuning and branching failures.
