# What a replacement transport mechanism would have to earn

The endpoint experiment tests the frozen law. This document does not
alter it, append a fitted suppression term, or claim a successful new
theory. It specifies the next architecture decision after a negative
candidate-metric result.

## The concrete mismatch

CP-12's existing matter hops on active half-legs. An active neutral chord
therefore remains a transport channel even when the circulating core
assigns its endpoints a large separation. A surviving charged ring is
not sufficient to make its own metric govern those hops. The primary
design problem is this mismatch between the objects defining distances
and the objects carrying propagation.

Two hypotheses should not be conflated:

1. **Use the complete active-host metric.** It already contains these
   shortcuts, so the endpoint response is unsurprising. This abandons
   the claim that circulation alone defines distance. It needs a fresh
   demonstration that original neutral dynamics select persistent,
   low-dimensional active hosts with controlled large-distance fronts.
   Calling the measured chord "local" is not such a demonstration.
2. **Change microscopic transport.** Retain the successful invariant
   algebra and numerical controls, but propose a microscopic reason
   why the propagating low-energy excitations preferentially use the
   same dynamically formed relations that define geometry. This is a
   new law and must be named and tested separately from frozen CP-12.

## Constraints on a constructive candidate

A new Hamiltonian must be specified in primitive variables before any
core is selected. It must preserve its declared gauge constraints and
particle/statistics structure, be Hermitian on a stated domain, and
have a viable original-neutral preparation. No operator may depend on
the observer's selected ring mask, a supplied embedding, a target
dimension, or a receiver's distance after its response is observed.

There is an immediate formation obstruction to the simplest apparent
repair. If **all** off-diagonal terms are multiplied by a circulation
projector that vanishes on the neutral ray, and the remaining diagonal
and rewiring terms leave that ray an eigenstate, then the neutral ray
stays an eigenstate. Charged geometry never forms. Suppressing transport
must not suppress the only mechanism that forms geometry.

One research direction is to distinguish geometry-forming microscopic
degrees of freedom from the collective excitations that serve as
matter. An effective hopping coefficient would then have to be derived
by a controlled low-energy reduction, with its unwanted virtual paths
and approximation errors included. Postulating a separate probe species
or a flux-dependent hopping factor is a changed model, not a derivation
from the frozen one. It also raises universality and matter-content
questions rather than solving them automatically.

## Prospectively required evidence

Before tuning parameters or running the replacement, freeze its law,
preparation family, source, operational metric and receiver pairs. Use
the same gauge-invariant phase-response protocol, including the actual
recording instrument if one is needed. Reserve unseen sizes and waiting
times for validation. Require jointly:

- A declared positive lower formation-probability benchmark across the
  size sequence, with every record class and failure included.
- Persistent geometry and nonzero propagation along its intrinsic
  distances, with no globally blocked source.
- Early-time bounds at growing separation for specified receivers,
  including any active relations that bypass the proposed geometry.
- Robustness to preparation time and source choice without retuning.
- Resource-matched random-host, conventional lattice and other suitable
  control models, using a declared prediction rather than an appearance
  selected after plotting.

Only a candidate that clears this stage should be advanced toward a
continuum, gravity or empirical prediction. No observer/consciousness
postulate substitutes for any failed item.
