GRUT is the objective; CP-12 is one candidate microscopic architecture.
This packet tests candidate circulation distances with source-resolved
matter response. Successful mathematics or reproducible code does not
establish a theory of nature.

## Acceptance decisions

**Milestone A** requires gates 1 and 2 simultaneously, with robustness
over preparation times and conditions and no increasingly rare favorable
selection. A ring survival probability conditional on a rare record,
an average-degree argument, or a decreasing extensive ratio is insufficient.
The current maximum tested ring separation is three; a thermodynamic
propagation front cannot be inferred from it.

**Milestone B** requires recovery of a relativistic continuum, effective
matter physics and universal gravitational coupling. Preserving unitary
microscopic evolution and finding graph back-reaction is not that result.

**Milestone C** requires a distinctive successful empirical prediction
with independent confirmation. No such claim is made here.

The prospective six-site finite-test benchmark and its limitations are
in `TEST_PROTOCOL.md`. Four- and five-site pilots preceded that protocol;
this packet must not be described as an independently preregistered
comparison with random graphs or conventional lattices. A later positive
claim would need those resource-matched alternatives, a declared target
observable and blind held-out sizes/preparations.

## Constructive architecture rule

Do not preserve an unsuccessful core metric by redefining a responding
shortcut as local after inspecting propagation. A broader metric, a
different selected geometry or a different microscopic transport law
is a new hypothesis requiring its own prospectively specified tests.

If full-law endpoint responses retain bypasses, pause claims that a
circulating ring already supplies physical distance. Preserve the signed
gauge-invariant algebra, original-neutral preparation and error-control
methods, then write a separate transport charter: a proposed microscopic
mechanism must suppress its own nongeometric couplings, retain a viable
geometry-formation probability and nonzero core response, and survive
the same source-resolved protocol. A manual core mask is not that mechanism.

Consciousness and observer mechanics remain separate prospective research
questions. They neither repair a propagation failure nor provide evidence
for any of these five gates without a mathematical model and independent
predictions.
