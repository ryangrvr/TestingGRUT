## Known failures and exclusions

- **The old moving-channel bottleneck is not solved.** Candidate 1 explicitly replaces that mechanism. No two-source delivery, moving retention, capacity, positive-electric-energy, D1 density, or B1/B2 theorem transfers into it.
- **Six initial numerical starts failed.** They are retained in the code archive. The successful state followed a documented preparation search; no generic state-finding or formation success probability is inferred.
- **A frozen organization field does not supply historical memory.** The profile tracks the charged source and changes D propagation. It does not establish a learned rule, persistent message, consciousness, intention, or purpose.
- **Binding below free carriers is not every kind of stability.** The package has no all-partition energy minimum, nonlinear lifetime simulation, tunneling calculation or exact quantum eigenstate.
- **The scalar charge is not electric charge.** There is no current loop or positive electromagnetic self-energy calculation here. A laboratory current-loop problem needs carriers, electromagnetic fields, support stresses and preparation in its total stress tensor.
- **The detector field is not a built detector.** The action defines D and its organization coupling. It does not implement a Standard Model source/beam splitter/detector with a costed preparation and persistent record.
- **The packet's unitary norm is not a continuum error bar.** The grid sweep changes delivery and phase. The packet result is quoted at modest precision; monochromatic phase has the stronger numerical check.
- **The finite-box spectrum is not a universal excitation gap.** Symmetry modes approach zero and a continuum threshold remains. No negative grid artifact is dismissed without an identified mode and refinement.
- **The matched finite action is not quantum closed by itself.** Allowed mass, portal, nonminimal and higher operators exist; their zero renormalized matching values are choices, and they run.
- **Small compactness is not all-charge stability.** At large charge the flat thin-wall extrapolation becomes gravitationally strong. Its breakdown is not a computed collapse threshold.
- **Known laws are adopted.** Einstein gravity, quantum probabilities, standard matter and the charge-supported scalar mechanism are not derived or claimed as new GRUT discoveries.

The quantum stability caution is substantive: [Su, Xie and Zhou's FLS calculation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25243) finds that classical and quantum-corrected stability can differ in its approximation. Its potential lacks this candidate's positive X quartic, so neither its instability windows nor its lifetimes apply to this benchmark. Our quantum lifetime remains unknown.

## Bounded next work

The next most decisive calculation is a **finite-charge branch/partition study plus controlled nonlinear perturbation evolution for this exact action**, followed by a renormalized fluctuation calculation. It must use the fixed couplings or explicitly create Candidate 2. A failure should be allowed to reject this operating regime.

If that survives, the next construction bottleneck is a physical reporter apparatus with explicit source, coherent routing and readout couplings. Numerical phase precision alone will not close it. Completing quantum gravity, deriving the Standard Model or proving the entire universe is not a prerequisite for testing this narrower model, and none should be promised as the next routine computation.

## Final decision record

The session supplied one complete leading-law proposal and executed a nontrivial regime connecting supplied interacting matter, altered propagation, ideal observation and gravitational response. It found no contradiction in the inspected leading equations and checks. It did not establish that this is a universal microscopic theory or that it describes nature.

This is the completed Candidate 1 construction milestone. A document set is complete; a theory of reality is not validated or finished by that fact.
