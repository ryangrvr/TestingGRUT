# Challenger audit of GRUT Candidate 1

18 October 2026. Audit target: `GRUT_Candidate_1/GRUT_CANDIDATE_1.md`, read directly after the reporter and EFT matching prescription were added. This is a bounded adversarial audit, not independent quantum validation or an originality assessment.

## Verdict

**Do not reject the displayed leading classical theory as internally contradictory. Do reject its promotion to a demonstrated stable quantum object, a completed quantum theory of gravity, an autonomous memory/formation mechanism, or an empirically established new theory.** With the explicit limitations already present, it can be presented as a specific Einstein-plus-matter EFT construction and a conditional semiclassical worked example. The scalar binding condition is satisfied by the stated couplings. That condition does not certify the particular numerical state, its fission resistance, quantum lifetime, or complete readout apparatus.

The strongest defensible deliverable is: a supplied, charge-bearing scalar preparation; a converged stationary leading solution; its conserved positive matter energy; a controlled short classical evolution or other explicitly limited stability diagnostic; a weak reporter scattering phase; a supplied ideal readout instrument; and a linearized metric response using the full stress tensor. Every stronger item needs its own calculation. A failure to obtain a nontrivial converged profile must be reported as failure, not repaired by choosing a favorable plot, relabeling the vacuum, or importing earlier moving-word theorems.

## Immediate text corrections and qualification checks

1. **Fermions require a chosen spin structure.** Add it to P1 or the ordinary-matter definition. The oriented globally hyperbolic setting admits the usual spin construction, but the choice is still part of the datum.
2. **The Fock construction is a regulated perturbative starting point.** Bare photon/graviton Fock states do not by themselves provide an infrared-complete scattering Hilbert space. Use infrared-safe inclusive probabilities or dressed asymptotic states at the claimed order. Nonperturbative QCD bound states and confinement are not constructed merely by listing quark/gluon Fock modes or taking perturbative BRST cohomology. The adopted Standard Model can supply them as known physical input; the session does not solve them.
3. **The reporter apparatus is not implemented by the displayed scalar phase calculation.** At leading order D couples to phi and gravity. Its source, splitter, recombiner, and an ordinary Standard Model detector do not acquire a calculated usable interaction merely because an ideal CP instrument is postulated. Supply the ideal instrument openly, or calculate an action-compatible apparatus. In particular, replace “the explicitly calculated instrument” by “the calculated reporter phase with an assumed ideal interferometric readout” unless the apparatus interaction is actually supplied and evaluated.
4. **The exact-charge/coherent-state distinction is now stated honestly.** A coherent state can have a large mean Q and small relative fluctuations, approximately Q^(-1/2) in the simple Poisson case. It cannot simultaneously be an exact fixed-Q state with nonzero charged one-point expectation. Charge projection sets <X>=0; invariant densities can retain the leading classical profile. Neither construction is an exact interacting quantum soliton solution by declaration.
5. **The positive potential is a leading scalar statement.** Do not extend it to the all-scale quantum effective potential, the entire Standard Model potential, or all Wilson-coefficient choices. Matching zeros and the finite working domain are assumptions, not radiative protection.
6. **“Structural record” is acceptable only with its present definition.** It means an occupation-correlated field profile maintained while X remains present. It does not demonstrate a separately writable, historical, error-correcting memory or a record that survives removal of the source. The microscopic Hamiltonian is reversible. Persistence over an observation interval still depends on actual dynamical stability and probe backreaction.
7. **The new global Q is not electric charge.** The worked state is stationary and has no spatial X charge current. It demonstrates an organized populated state, not two-source delivery, transport, electromagnetic self-energy, or a solved current loop. No CP-12/B1/B2/MC-E1 theorem transfers to it.

## Equation audit

The displayed leading scalar equations, masses, Einstein equation, ADM gravitational normalization, and complex-scalar Hamiltonian kinetic factor are consistent with the stated (-,+,+,+) action. In particular, `-lambda_X |X|^4` gives `-2 lambda_X |X|^2 X` in the complex equation, and `X=f exp(-i omega t)/sqrt(2)` gives the radial cubic coefficient `lambda_X f^3`, not `2 lambda_X f^3`.

With D=0 and flat background, the radial equations are

\[
f''+2f'/r=(g_X^2\varphi^2-\omega^2)f+\lambda_Xf^3,
\qquad
\varphi''+2\varphi'/r=\lambda_\phi\varphi(\varphi^2-v^2)+g_X^2\varphi f^2.
\]

Regularity and finite energy require `f'(0)=varphi'(0)=0`, `f(infinity)=0`, and `varphi(infinity)=v`. The energy and positive charge convention are

\[
E=4\pi\int_0^\infty r^2dr\,[\tfrac12f'^2+\tfrac12\varphi'^2+
\tfrac12\omega^2f^2+U(\varphi,f/\sqrt2,0)],
\qquad Q=4\pi\omega\int_0^\infty r^2f^2dr.
\]

The displayed covariant current is conserved. With (-,+,+,+), its contravariant time component is negative for the chosen `exp(-i omega t)` convention; the stated hypersurface orientation compensates. This is not an inconsistency if kept consistent, but explicitly writing the positive radial Q formula avoids an easy sign confusion.

The main text does not define its Clifford algebra/Dirac-adjoint sign conventions. Stating the chosen conventional pair would remove an unnecessary ambiguity in the imported fermion action. This does not affect the scalar benchmark.

## Binding exists as a class; the chosen state still needs validation

Let `y=|X|^2`. Minimizing U at fixed y gives

\[
\varphi_{\min}^2=\max(0,v^2-2g_X^2y/\lambda_\phi).
\]

Below the transition, the minimized potential is

\[
U_{\min}(y)=g_X^2v^2y+(\lambda_X-g_X^4/\lambda_\phi)y^2.
\]

The negative effective quartic is an attraction obtained by allowing phi to respond; the full positive two-field potential remains bounded. On the depleted-phi branch,

\[
U(0,y)=\lambda_\phi v^4/4+\lambda_Xy^2.
\]

For the chosen parameters the minimum of `U/y`, equivalently `2U/f^2`, lies at

\[
\varphi_0=0,\qquad f_0^2=v^2\sqrt{\lambda_\phi/\lambda_X},\qquad
\omega_0^2=v^2\sqrt{\lambda_\phi\lambda_X}.
\]

Thus

\[
\omega_0/m_X=(\lambda_\phi\lambda_X/g_X^4)^{1/4}=0.5623413<1.
\]

The bulk condition `g_X^4 > lambda_phi lambda_X` is satisfied. The asymptotically decaying frequency interval is the candidate range `omega_0 < omega < m_X`. This establishes a nonempty binding class; it does not show that every omega in the interval has the desired stable branch, that a solver has found it, or that an arbitrary nodeless profile is a minimum. The real field cannot simply be algebraically eliminated in the wall: its gradients are part of the full solution and energy.

The model is a quartic extension of the two-field Friedberg–Lee–Sirlin construction. The original FLS model without positive X self-quartic has a different large-charge asymptotic structure; do not quote its numerical bounds as this model's results. The historical existence and stability analyses are relevant precedent, not replacement calculations. [FLS 1976; Coleman 1985; Heeck–Sokhashvili 2023 below.]

## Fission, emission, and perturbation stability are separate checks

`E(Q)<m_X Q` blocks complete dissolution into free charge carriers at leading order. It alone does not exclude every charged fragment or all small perturbations. A proper energetic comparison is

\[
E(Q)<\sum_i E_{\min}(Q_i)+m_X Q_{\rm free},\qquad
\sum_iQ_i+Q_{\rm free}=Q,
\]

for every nontrivial allowed partition, with the same physical parameters and vacuum. In particular, finite-charge emission compares `E(Q)` to `E_min(Q-k)+k m_X`. A fixed-Q global energy minimum, if actually established, covers these comparisons; calling a profile nodeless does not establish that fact.

Along a smooth stationary branch,

\[
\frac{dE}{dQ}=\omega,\qquad
\frac{d^2E}{dQ^2}=\frac{d\omega}{dQ}.
\]

Negative `dQ/domega` is a useful branch/concavity diagnostic. Its use as a sufficient perturbative stability test requires the relevant spectral assumptions; a multi-field constrained Hessian can have additional negative modes. Report the actual diagnostic performed, its perturbations and duration, and the symmetry sector tested. A short radial evolution cannot exclude nonradial instabilities or a longer quantum decay.

For large thin-wall balls,

\[
E(Q)=\omega_0 Q+aQ^{2/3}+\cdots,\quad a>0.
\]

The positive surface term favors one large ball over two large thin-wall fragments because `(Q1+Q2)^(2/3)<Q1^(2/3)+Q2^(2/3)`. This is an asymptotic argument, not a computed finite-Q fission boundary. Charge fractions outside the thin-wall branch still require comparison to their actual ground-state or free-particle energies.

## No all-Q or all-gravity stability claim

At the stated coupling point the leading thin-wall estimates are

\[
f_0^2=\sqrt{10}\,v^2,\quad\omega_0=0.05623413v,\quad
Q\simeq0.74488394(vR)^3,
\]

\[
R\simeq1.1031565Q^{1/3}/v,\qquad E\simeq0.05623413vQ.
\]

Using the reduced Planck mass, `G=1/(8 pi M_P^2)`, gives

\[
\frac{2GE}{R}\simeq0.00405651(v/M_P)^2Q^{2/3}.
\]

Compactness grows without bound in the nongravitating thin-wall extrapolation. For `v/M_P=10^-5`, extrapolated compactness becomes order one at `Q~3.9 x 10^18`; this is a breakdown estimate, not a calculated collapse threshold. At much smaller Q the weak-gravity approximation can have a large valid window. Small compactness and subcutoff scales control an approximation; neither is a proof of full nonlinear stability. Strong-gravity branches require solving the coupled Einstein-scalar system and testing their stability.

## Gravitational source must retain stresses

For a stationary weak-field configuration in the standard static gauge, with `g00=-(1+2Phi)`,

\[
\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G(T_{00}+\sum_iT_{ii}).
\]

For the rotating scalar state with static phi and D=0,

\[
\rho=\tfrac12\omega^2f^2+\tfrac12f'^2+\tfrac12\varphi'^2+U,
\qquad \rho+\sum_ip_i=2\omega^2f^2-2U.
\]

The gradient terms cancel in the last local combination but determine the profile, wall stresses, and equilibrium. Replacing that local source by rho alone is generally wrong. For an isolated flat-space stationary solution with sufficient falloff, stress conservation gives the von Laue identity `integral T_ij d^3x=0`, and hence the integrated active source equals E. In an exact static spacetime the corresponding Komar/ADM relation includes lapse and volume factors. The far exterior mass and local source formula must not be conflated.

The scalar energy here is explicitly nonzero: it contains time-rotation, gradient, and potential terms. This avoids relabeling a zero-energy finite-density algebraic state as positive matter energy. It does not establish a nonzero electromagnetic energy: this sector is electromagnetically neutral. For a future current-loop comparison, include electromagnetic fields, carriers, mechanical support stresses, heat, and the state preparation in the total stress. Neither this stationary scalar example nor perturbative gravity alone solves that laboratory system or supplies a novel calibrated current-loop prediction.

## Reporter: conditional scattering, not a full detector construction

Let the reporter time dependence be `exp(-i Omega_D t)` and

\[
m_{D,\infty}^2=m_{D0}^2+h_D^2v^2,\quad k^2=\Omega_D^2-m_{D,\infty}^2>0.
\]

The linear partial-wave equation on the fixed background is

\[
u_\ell''+[k^2-h_D^2(\varphi^2-v^2)-\ell(\ell+1)/r^2]u_\ell=0.
\]

The depleted core is an attractive mass-squared well. No tachyonic D mass is introduced because `m_D0^2+h_D^2 varphi^2` stays positive. With the asymptotic convention

\[
u_\ell\sim\sin(kr-\ell\pi/2+\delta_\ell),
\]

the elastic channel is `S_l=exp(2 i delta_l)`. An interferometer comparing that S-matrix channel to the free channel therefore uses the phase `2 delta_l`, unless a differently named phase is explicitly already the full S phase. For equal ideal arms the output probability has the corresponding cosine of the full relative phase. Selected-mode probabilities require their selection/normalization factor if the scattered wave is not wholly in that channel.

A real D field supports a one-particle wave-packet interpretation of the linear positive-frequency problem; it is not itself an exact conserved-number complex field. Fixed-background elastic scattering omits target recoil, target excitation, reporter-induced deformation, entanglement, and inelastic losses. Large target energy, weak h_D, and low probe occupation can justify leading approximations, but compute or bound the relevant small parameters before calling the measurement nondestructive. “Conditional phase in a test-field channel” is an honest result; an exact finite-mass reporter-target unitary has not been solved.

## Quantum, radiative, and observable limits

The new matching paragraph addresses the central radiative-closure objection: the finite displayed action is not presented as quantum closed, and omitted scalar mass, Higgs/mixed portals, nonminimal curvature terms, and higher operators are admitted with explicit matched values. Those values run. Tree-level exact minimal coupling is not a radiatively protected all-scale assertion. Specifying infinitely many zero matching coefficients is a formal EFT prescription; it does not demonstrate that a consistent UV completion realizes it, nor all-orders convergence.

The low-energy theory can be used order by order with the required counterterms and physical renormalization/matching data. A classical saddle plus a statement that BRST cohomology will be used is not a constructed nonperturbative physical Hilbert space or a solved exact quantum ADM constraint algebra. The main text now makes this limitation explicit. Treat its theory tuple as a perturbative specification, not an exact completed realization of all its entries. [Donoghue 1994 below.]

Local covariant Lagrangian interactions and leading metric characteristics are compatible with a perturbative working description. They do not create an exact algebra of compactly supported commuting physical Dirac observables once gravity is dynamical. Diffeomorphism-invariant readouts require apparatus relations, asymptotic data, or gravitational dressing. The main text's distinction between coordinate representatives and physical observables is appropriate; no stronger exact-locality claim should appear in companion tables. [Donnelly–Giddings 2016 below.]

The October 2026 primary Hartree calculation finds that classical and quantum-corrected stability need not coincide in an FLS model. Its standard potential lacks the positive X self-quartic used here, so its numerical windows do not transfer to this candidate. It establishes no lifetime or instability for this parameter point. It is a concrete reason to leave quantum stability and lifetime open rather than infer them from a classical profile. [Su–Xie–Zhou 2026 below.]

## Calibrated prediction versus a standard baseline

Printed illustrative couplings are not independent measurements. A numerical phase or metric solves the chosen model at those inputs; it is not evidence that this model is realized in nature. Calibration must use quantities independent of the subsequently held-out prediction. Vacuum masses alone do not fix every scalar coupling: scattering amplitudes or other independent observables are needed. Prepared Q and apparatus settings must be determined before the held-out comparison.

The appropriate theoretical baseline is known Einstein gravity and ordinary quantum field theory with or without this specified extra scalar sector, according to the observable being compared. Born probabilities, geodesic motion, ordinary photon light cones, and the existence of Q-balls are adopted known mechanisms. Reproducing them is a consistency check. A claim of empirical discrimination requires new-sector measurements, independent parameter calibration, and predictions separated from generic instrumental systematics. No such empirical dataset is supplied in the current demonstration.
