**GRUT Candidate 1 · 18 October 2026**

## One theory, rather than imported successes

Every worked new-sector quantity uses the same action, couplings, asymptotic vacuum, solved X/phi profile and stress tensor. The reporter potential is `h_D²(phi²−v²)`, not a separately fitted channel. Gravity uses the same scalar kinetic and potential energy. The matched SM sector is present in the proposal but is in its vacuum in the new-sector calculation; no SM many-body detector or atom was simulated.

## Constraint and energy consistency

The classical ADM Hamiltonian and momentum constraints, SM Gauss constraints and scalar equations are specified in the main candidate. The benchmark's flat stationary profile satisfies the scalar equations, and its conserved leading stress can consistently source first-order Einstein equations. The resulting metric includes spatial as well as lapse response; using just a density-dependent clock rate would omit a necessary part of the gravitational calculation.

The fixed-charge Hessian retains the nonlocal positive term arising from eliminating the time-rotation frequency at fixed charge. An unconstrained variation of the local `E−omega Q` functional has a negative radial mode; this is not substituted for the actual fixed-charge test. Translation and U(1) phase symmetry give identified continuum zero modes whose grid errors decrease with refinement. The finite-box lowest positive radial number is not advertised as a universal gap.

The energy comparison `E<m_X Q` blocks complete free-carrier dissolution at leading order. Stability against all fragment charges requires the still-uncomputed comparison `E(Q)<sum E_min(Q_i)+m_X Q_free`. No fission theorem is smuggled in through the phrase “charge-stabilized.” In this package that phrase names the mechanism, not a proven lifetime.

## Quantum and EFT consistency

The scalar action by itself is not closed under quantum corrections. The definition admits all symmetry-allowed operators and specifies matching values, including zero values for otherwise allowed masses, portals and nonminimal couplings. Those zeros run and are construction choices. Loop effects, counterterms and higher operators must be included at the order of any future quantum claim.

The cutoff prescription is a formal low-energy definition, not a demonstrated UV origin. A regulator-dependent perturbative gauge/BRST construction with inclusive or gravitationally dressed physical observables is supplied. An exact nonperturbative physical Hilbert space, QCD bound-state construction and quantum ADM algebra are not supplied. This use of gravity as an EFT follows the scope of the standard low-energy approach; see [Donoghue, 1994](https://arxiv.org/abs/9405057).

The main equations explicitly supply time orientation, a metric, dimension, local kinetic structure, quantum probabilities and Einstein dynamics. None is called emergent. New interacting structure and its propagation/gravitational response are calculated inside that supplied framework.

## Challenge results

The Constructor audited action, field equations, matter content and constraints. The Calculator independently implemented the scalar profile, stress and spectra. The Challenger was authorized to reject the architecture and identified the binding condition, radiative closure, infrared/state-space qualifications, fission and quantum gaps, and the detector/selection limitations. The root worker integrated the law and calculated reporter observables. See `audit/` and `code/results_notes.md` for their actual notes.

Corrections included an explicit spin structure and spinor convention, positive charge orientation, theta matching, EFT counterterms, dressed/inclusive observable scope, a fixed-charge fluctuation test, full stress in gravity and correctly normalized selected packet readout. No second physical architecture or new physical parameter fit was introduced during calculation. Numerical preparation search and added diagnostic refinements are recorded.

These are complementary automated workers sharing one project, tools and assumptions. Their agreement is not independent peer review or empirical validation. No independent software implementation of the entire calculation was executed.

## Final outcome

**A narrower working model rather than the intended universal theory.**

More precisely, the session delivered an explicit leading EFT proposal with a populated classical regime and conditional reporter/weak-gravity calculations. No internal contradiction was found in the equations and checks inspected. This is not a proof of consistency at every order or every state. The universal microscopic state space, quantum stability, complete physical apparatus and empirical discrimination remain outside what was achieved.
