# Execution and interpretation

Original S8 charter `0e42a5d` was public before any implementation checks
or original trajectories. S8-G1 charter `b15fcf0` was public before its
implementation controls or primary trajectories. Original S8's first
per-charge-cap stop motivated the separate G1 rule. It did not change
either physical instance or turn an original stop into a pass.

The original implementation was optimized before its primary workers:
charge-index layouts were cached, zero-block Python overhead reduced and
LAPACK zgesdd called directly. The full controls were rerun on that source;
the small and 576-mode state discrepancies were unchanged. No original
numerical source changed after EXECUTED_SOURCES.json was captured.
G1's source differs at its named rank-allocation rule/default/import only,
checked in global_controls.py and pinned before G1 workers. Gates,
integration and arithmetic routines are otherwise identical.

Original S8 workers ran sequentially. Both stopped on Schmidt-cap/truncation
allowances, before their target, wall and memory limits. G1 controls then
ran, followed by its separately named recovery workers and the immutable
fresh S7 comparison. Any six-event launch requires G1's own recovery gate.
All source, controls, outcomes and rejected targets are retained.

Both original S8 components stopped. G1's first component stopped and its
second completed. Both fresh S7 components completed. The G1 gate therefore
failed and no six-event trajectory was launched. The final summary is
regenerated from those six raw worker records; no incomplete population
or cross-method root mixture is used as a target-time family result.

The resource timers begin before imports and include setup/operator audits
and propagation through result construction, before final JSON serialization
and printing. Peak RSS is the Linux process high-water mark then. Timed
limits are soft physical-step checks; an overshoot is reported. A rank or
error stop can occur within a sweep: completed_steps_time then describes
the last fully completed physical step, not the partially updated stored
state. Such runs have no target-time probability interval.
Their accumulated partial ledger is not a certificate comparing that
partially updated state to physical H evolution at the last whole-step time.

The computational receiver prefix is a mathematically paid approximation
to the original physical chain. It has no irreversible sink and retains all
reverse transitions. Skipped vacuum gates are exactly identity on the stored
state. Schmidt loss, matrix/gate errors, physical splitting, finite-field
factorization, receiver-cut and arithmetic costs are separate.

RANK_ALLOCATION.md's witness is a capacity control, not a dynamically formed
state. SWEEP_BOUND.md and SWEEP_CONTROLS.json are separate analytical/dense
diagnostics. They do not lower a primary ledger or launch a new variant.
In particular, square-summing errors over reversals or all time is explicitly
refuted by the coherent-return control. A future implementation must first
enclose the in-range numerical projection defect.

All primary arithmetic claims are under the declared IEEE/BLAS model.
Passing comparisons, saved checks or a hash manifest is not external
validation of the model, full mathematical proofs or software/hardware stack.
