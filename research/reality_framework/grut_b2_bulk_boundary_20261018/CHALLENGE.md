# Internal challenge and scope checks

One assistant conducted this cycle's derivation and controls, with shared
theory/code ancestry. This is not independent external mathematical review.

* **Does extending to Z^2 change the physical source?** The whole-line
  operator is used only to define its translation-invariant bulk symbol.
  Every missing quadrant path is restored by W. Fuel and -A root remain.
* **Is the boundary kernel assumed finite rank?** No. Its truncated words
  have finite boundary support; the exact remainder is trace class with
  a complete entrywise bound.
* **Is missing-path length 2(n+m+1)?** No. A path can leave and return
  along one constituent while the other stays. The correct lower bound
  is 2max(n,m)+2, used throughout. This avoids a false stronger decay.
* **Is entrywise positivity promoted to operator positivity?** No for
  W as a whole. Only the explicitly displayed W^(2) matrix is proved
  positive definite before replacing its trace norm by its trace.
* **Was the physical root counterterm dropped?** No. It cancels W^(0)
  at the reference energy 2. At other energies beta=s/(13/4-E)-A remains.
* **Is a tiny U enough for a real-axis Neumann inverse?** No. The bulk
  inverse is singular in unweighted L2 on its propagating band. The
  J/500 trace bound is not a probability or a weighted inverse estimate.
* **Are Fourier factors arbitrary scalar surrogates?** No. Their symbol
  is the exact actual whole-line Q-resolvent continued fraction; W and
  fuel restore the actual source. Fixed-z factorization is justified by
  Im kappa>0; no uniform limiting constants are asserted.
* **Does the Toeplitz factor order have the correct sign/order?** With
  entries a_(n-m), T(kappa_- kappa_+)=T(kappa_-)T(kappa_+). Hence the
  inverse is T(1/kappa_+)T(1/kappa_-), giving a finite convolution sum.
  There is no complex conjugation in that analytic formula.
* **Does I_b contain all low source weight?** Not established. It covers
  the known first bulk band. Unknown source atoms outside it remain in
  the original spectral reconstruction and cannot be charged to the tiny
  high tail at 2.5.
* **Is the user's new high-tail bound stronger?** No. It is correct but
  weaker; the existing 36/27613^2 bound remains in the physical ledger.
* **Do six-site/word controls establish propagation?** No. The controls
  verify static closed-channel coefficients and analytic constants only.
  No source trajectory, arithmetic operating certificate or all-L output
  probability is computed.
