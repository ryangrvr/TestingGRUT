The common operational measure is the optimal task value under access
to a record minus that under the stated restricted access, with
physical budget and admissible interventions declared. It can be
positive, zero, or costly to realize. Generating correlations and
having a capability that depends on them are independently testable.
Both can follow ordinary closed dynamics with resources and chosen
partitions. The integrated I1–I3 models use externally specified
preparations, geometries or timing where documented.

The developmental series closes as a route to fundamental novelty.
A genuinely different mechanism and observable comparison would
define a separate proposal. The pillars' results and failures stand
at the scope of their own protocols. Candidate 1 remains an
independent matter-sector hypothesis in the audit.
