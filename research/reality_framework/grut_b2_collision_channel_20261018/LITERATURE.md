G. Calajò, Y.-L. L. Fang, H. U. Baranger, F. Ciccarello,
*Exciting a Bound State in the Continuum through Multiphoton Scattering
Plus Delayed Quantum Feedback*, Physical Review Letters 122, 073601 (2019),
https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.02582,
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.073601.

It studies nonlinear waveguide scattering and delayed-feedback excitation
of dressed bound states. It is a useful reason to keep coherent return and
trapping in the account, not a theorem about B1 or evidence that B1 has such
a state. No result on its particular trapped fraction is imported here.
The other cited waveguide models have not been used as mathematical inputs.

Color ordering, hard-core motion, permutation channels, spectral-variance
inequalities and finite unitary recurrences are conventional quantum
mechanisms. No priority claim or new empirical distinction is made. The
specific contributions here are the matched channel accounting, the frozen
negative finite test, and an explicit local obstruction to extending its
endpoint-order proof to growing geometry.
