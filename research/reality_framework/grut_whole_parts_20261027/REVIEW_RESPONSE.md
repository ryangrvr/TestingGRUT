## Agreement and the whole/parts question

The broad result agrees: no specifically new observational law has been derived from
plain closure. Our new example establishes the user's positive compatibility question:
one stationary quantum whole can have physical internal individuals and changing records.
That construction is standard constrained quantum mechanics; it does not overturn the
closure-null result or select a unique cosmology.

Combining compatible ingredients is possible. Combining every strengthening is not:
the specific eternal-de-Sitter capacity proposal, zero-energy final vacuum and recollapsing
finite-four-volume histories specify different futures. These are choices of model and
boundary conditions. Finite-dimensional quantum mechanics alone does not select one
of those futures. A new state law or operational restriction could add content, as could
new dynamics; the foundation gate does not require that only equations of motion change.
