# From a coherent concept to a working physical theory

The current precise concept is one constrained quantum whole with internal clocks,
objects and records. An individual may itself be a composite whole: the neutral XC
pair in this example has two charged parts and one logical degree of freedom. Quantum
constraints limit which properties can be independently assigned; they do not remove
all individuality. This composition principle is compatible with ordinary physics.

The proposed continuum theory still has the explicit slots

\[
\mathfrak T=(\mathcal K,\{\widehat C_A\},S_{\rm EFT},\vartheta,
\mathcal B,\{E_{a|T=t}\}).
\]

The parts and whole use the same state and dynamics. No extra fundamental reporter
is needed merely to name an observer: ordinary matter can carry records. A complete
theory must nevertheless explain the relevant physical records and their reliability.

These are existing dependencies, not additional screens introduced by this packet.
The work should finish connected claims instead of adding an interaction for every
failure. The same parameters, state and history have to survive each step below.
