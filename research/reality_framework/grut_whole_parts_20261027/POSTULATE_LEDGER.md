## Space and finite information

Compact space forbids noncompact spatial slices in the chosen branch. It does not fix
curvature sign, give a finite-dimensional field Hilbert space, or imply TCC. A finite
physical dimension forbids an exact unlimited canonical oscillator algebra, as the
trace-of-commutator calculation shows. Its relation to observer-accessible entropy
and cosmological horizons needs a specified dictionary. No information-capacity number
or inflation constraint follows just from the word finite in this packet.

For a concrete additional **TCC** test, choose constant-H single-stage inflation,
instantaneous reheating and subsequent entropy conservation. Write M for the reduced
Planck mass, A=(90/(π²g_*r))^(1/4), and

\[
T_r=A\sqrt{MH},\quad {a_e\over a_0}={T_0\over T_r}
\left({g_{s0}\over g_{sr}}\right)^{1/3}.
\]

The present Hubble scale must start inside the inflationary Hubble radius, requiring
N ≥ ln[(a_e/a_0)H/H_0]. The adopted TCC requires N < ln(M/H). Together,

\[
H<M\left[{AH_0\over T_0}\left({g_{sr}\over g_{s0}}\right)^{1/3}\right]^{2/3}.
\]

For the explicitly frozen cosmological constants in TCC_JOINT_GATE.json this is
H < 0.11277 GeV. The supplied heavy point H=1.333496×10¹¹ GeV requires N≥58.4183
but permits N<16.7202: incompatible under these assumptions. No automatic change to
the GRUT point is made. Different reheating, inflation or capacity postulates require
their own calculation. TCC's status as a conjecture and its inflationary application
are explicit in Bedroya et al., [1909.11106](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.11106), abstract read.

## Equilibrium vacuum and Higgs stabilization

In the existing fixed-μ q-theory construction, equilibrium matching to a specified
final reference does not equate the present phase with that reference. An explicit
dimensionless potential is

\[
\epsilon(q,s)=q^2/2+1+V(s),\quad \mu=q_0=\sqrt2,
\quad V(s)={(s^2-1)^2\over4}+{\delta\over4}(2-3s+s^3).
\]

At δ=.01 both s=±1 are local minima. The true reference at s=+1 has
ρ=ε−μq=0, while the false phase at s=−1 has ρ=.01. The selected μ is held
fixed throughout. This is an equilibrium-reference counterexample to an unconditional
present-phase absolute-stability implication. It does not calculate the observed tiny
gap or an eventual transition. Requiring actual final equilibrium adds a future-exit
condition. Requiring the present electroweak phase to be that final ground state is
a separate stronger premise. A conditional SM-desert comparison further needs its
threshold, higher-operator and mass-scheme assumptions. The earlier project report
already records this distinction and its nonunique archived significance.

Hidden-scalar stabilization is published physics, not automatic success for these
couplings: see Gonderinger et al., [0910.3167](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.3167), and
Elias-Miró et al., [1203.0237](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.0237), abstracts read. The latter
uses a heavy-scalar threshold, which the light-only proxy below does not test.

## UV self-consistency

A UV fixed point can reduce parameter freedom without eliminating it. For the example
β_g=g−1, β_y=−(y−2), the general flow is g=1+A exp(t), y=2+B exp(−t).
UV regularity sets A=0 while leaving B arbitrary. Three explicit flows approach the
same fixed point with different low-energy y values. This is a mathematical
counterexample to unique couplings from UV completeness alone, not GRUT beta functions.

Shaposhnikov and Wetterich's conditional 126-GeV Higgs result was published before
discovery, [0912.0208](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.0208). Its abstract explicitly states
gravity-running and spectrum assumptions. Applying such a program to GRUT must include
its actual heavy thresholds and allowed operators and count the remaining relevant
directions. Repeating that historical number supplies no GRUT-specific prediction.

## Pure whole, clocks and symmetry

The exact history in this packet is pure while local states may be mixed. Finite
logical clocks can give exactly unitary conditional probe evolution; coarse readout
gives adjustable apparent loss of coherence. No positive universal floor follows
without a physical clock/resource model. Purity alone is also not a dynamical exclusion
of objective collapse: normalized stochastic pure-state trajectories can be considered.
Exact reversible linear global evolution would be the stronger excluding premise.
Bassi et al., [1204.4325](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.4325), review stochastic nonlinear
collapse modifications; abstract read here, not an updated experimental bound survey.

An internal compensator permits nonzero subsystem charge and zero total charge. If
both the action and the state-selection rule are invariant under a sign-flipping
symmetry, the unconditioned selected ensemble cannot choose a sign. That does not
require an asymmetric input outside the universe. It requires a specified internal
interaction, preparation or conditioning; the present model demonstrates compatibility,
not a cosmological selection mechanism.
