# Independently testable vector production screen

Following the bounded ALP decision and 40 GeV scalar test,
evaluate a fixed free spectator Proca vector of mass 1 meV.
This is a round-number mass choice for a comparator. Use a
constant mass, negligible vector energy during inflation,
the inflationary vacuum, ordinary Einstein tensor fluctuations
and prompt reheating into radiation. Keep the vector stable
and decoupled. Ask for the primordial tensor amplitude
required for all the observed dark matter. A laboratory
mass determination would be separate.

First verify the infrared and ultraviolet longitudinal
radiation-transfer limits against the full mode evolution.
Freeze the transfer and the constant-H tensor prediction
before using a published tensor upper limit for comparison.
Include a second calculation at finer momentum spacing,
wider momentum range and a later adiabatic readout. State
the dependence on inflationary Hubble evolution between
the CMB pivot and the modes that dominate the vector density.
Use no adjustable particle production coupling.
