# Analytic entry gate for a separate reheating mechanism

The logistic realization is closed as a causal radiation gas. Use no
numerical target from that branch. This is an analytic preflight for a
separate microscopic source. The only case screened here is the minimal
instant-preheating realization specified below.

Use the Peebles–Vilenkin example in [Haro and Aresté Saló,
Sec. 4.3 and Eq. 54](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.11843), with crossing
\(H_0/\bar M=1.44\times10^{-6}\), \(g=10^{-5}\) and their illustrative
\(\Gamma/\bar M=10^{-10}\). Use \(\bar M=2.435\times10^{18}\) GeV.
Take a real conformally coupled scalar \(\chi\) with mass
\(g|\phi|\), no bare mass, and an initially adiabatic vacuum. Let
\(\chi\) decay to one initially empty massless Dirac species through
\(-y\chi\bar\psi\psi\), with constant \(y\) chosen so its rest-frame
width is the quoted \(\Gamma\) at \(m_\chi=g\bar M\). In this screen
the width follows \(m_\chi\) as \(\phi\) rolls.
[Instant preheating](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9812289).

Use the source's kinetic limit after the crossing, \(H=H_0 e^{-3N}\)
and \(\phi=\sqrt6\bar M N\), where \(a(0)=1\). Compute the
Landau–Zener creation scale, particle count, decay optical depth
and free relativistic daughter energy. Compute equality from its
own produced radiation and the tree kinetic density. This is a
formal tree-level estimate. For the pivot mass estimate use the
source's tree slow-roll example \(n_s=0.968\),
\(H_p/\bar M=5.48\times10^{-6}\), and its potential on the negative
\(\phi\) branch. The input scalar calibration is solely the source's
example. A thermal-equivalent temperature may use \(g_*=106.75\) to
display an energy scale. The explicit Dirac daughter is freely
streaming in this test.

Apply two control checks before a coupled calculation. A flat-space
burst is a controlled working approximation here only if \(H_0/k_*\)
is below 0.1. Compare the \(\chi\) one-loop potential to the nominal
kinetic density through equality. Use the explicit screening convention
\(\overline{\rm MS}\) with \(\mu=g\bar M\), and the given runaway tree
potential without added compensating field-dependent terms. An
order-one ratio closes this unprotected tree trajectory. The loop
diagnostic is a check of this realization and renormalization choice.
[One-loop effective potential](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0111209).

Report the resource estimate for any remaining calculation. The
large-scale adiabatic transfer may be tested analytically for a
common crossing and local vacuum. The spectrum of independent
production fluctuations and the visible-sector thermalization require
their own calculations. A failed control ends the screen before
a new coupled history or relic calculation.
