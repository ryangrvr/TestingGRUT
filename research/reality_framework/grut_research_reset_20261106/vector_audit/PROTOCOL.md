Retain the [bounded results](../bounded_route/REPORT.md) with
their scopes. The ALP lane is closed as computationally unresolved.

for independent checking. Here the mass remains 1 meV.

Use an Einstein-gravity spectator Abelian Proca field with constant
mass, minimal gravitational coupling, no detection coupling, and the
relativistic inflationary vacuum. Assume a stable vector and all the
dark matter in this field. Audit these assumptions and the size of
the stress at production. Use $A_s=2.1\times10^{-9}$ at
$0.05\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ and $\rho_{\rm DM}/s=0.44$ eV for
calibration, with $g_*=g_{*s}=106.75$ where radiation determines the
transfer. Record the approximation in that thermal input.

## Production and inflation

polarization counting. Compute transfer using the cosmic log-scale-factor
Hamilton equations for the field and canonical momentum. Test both
spectral tails and compare the radiation integral with the earlier
conformal-time result to 0.5%. Check the entropy and absolute
wavenumber conversions.

Use a canonical E-model family
$V=V_0(1-e^{-\sqrt{2/(3\alpha)}\phi/M_{\rm Pl}})^2$.
The $\alpha=1$ and $\alpha=4$ controls fit only $A_s$.
A separate root on $1\le\alpha\le40$ may fit the vector density
and $A_s$ jointly. Keep the scalar tilt, running and CMB tensor
amplitude out of the fit. Derive the pivot exit from the expansion
history and stop inflation at $\epsilon_H=1$. Integrate the scalar
and tensor vacuum modes using the evolving Hubble history. Use
tensor mode transfer as the relativistic longitudinal scalar
transfer during inflation. Integrate over the vector spectrum.
Test the mode solver against the constant-$\epsilon$ solution.
Independently tighten the start, stop, and momentum quadrature
for the fitted points. Require changes below 0.2% in $r$ and
$2\times10^{-4}$ in the scalar tilt.

## Reheating and exclusion assumptions

The primary case converts inflaton energy immediately to radiation.
The early delayed control uses $w=0$ until a radiation temperature
of $10^9$ GeV. Check where that transition occurs relative to the
vector modes. It is a stress test of pivot matching even if the
vector transfer is already radiation-equivalent.

Use a second control outside prompt radiation. Let the post-inflation
component have effective $w=3/5$ until radiation at 200 GeV.
This is an explicit background history, for example a separate
oscillating reheating component, with no direct vector coupling.
Use field and momentum continuity and the entropy of the later bath.
This control tests whether the prompt-radiation tensor lower bound
can fail while the mass and abundance are kept fixed. Check the
finite momentum band's contribution separately from the UV tail and
estimate the relativistic backreaction.

State a lower envelope for the declared decreasing-tensor-power
class and the range found in the selected E-model histories.
Separate the numerical transfer error, inflation history, reheating,
and observational inputs. Freeze the calculations before comparing
the reserved observables with published measurements. Do not scan
the vector mass.
