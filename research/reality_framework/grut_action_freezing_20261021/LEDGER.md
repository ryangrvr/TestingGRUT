## Failures, changes and uncertainty

The local quadratic/linear scratch pilot preceded the initial protocol and
motivated the metric comparison; its numbers are not substituted for positive
production-action results. It was not a preregistration. A stale checkout
failed to fetch, and sympy was unavailable; a fresh clone and hand/numpy/scipy
derivations were used, without new dependency installation.

KP0 fails the named physical compatibility test. KP1 fails the desired rapid
preparation, analytically and by its stalled compressed roll. c=1 gives a low
amplitude; parameters were not optimized to erase it. All runs/controls that
passed remain labelled internal. No production integration failed a numerical
threshold. The late solver was strengthened with an expansion-work integral,
all-six-scenario refinements and halved maximum steps without relaxing gates.

A transient workspace reset interrupted the first publication after orphan
blobs had uploaded but before a completed tree/commit. The first raw files
were lost, not retrospectively certified. The equations/source/thresholds were
restored from this session's record; source was promptly checkpointed publicly
as 988843e0, and every integration and verifier was rerun. The raw archive in
this packet contains the **restored execution**, with its environment and
manifest. Archive compression changes storage only, not the dynamics. Do not
claim bitwise identity of all first-run artifacts or surviving wall-clock times.

Physical limitations: no quantum noise/loops, full thermal action, reheating
receiver, perturbation population, detector fit or cosmological likelihood.
The compressed example is not a physical Planck/TeV limit. Late evolution
assumes successful preparation. False-vacuum decay was deferred, not computed.
Old q/Candidate 1 packets are unchanged; no new target was approved or merged.
