reset during publication. Base 10e66b7. The first results and source were
recorded in the conversation. Source checkpoint 988843e0 is public. Every
published integration is rerun from the restored source. This is neither a
prospective empirical freeze nor a blind reproduction.

the observed residual independently? Select the Dimopoulos--Markkanen mechanism,
with the positive potential/metric hybrid explicitly defined in MATHEMATICS.
Do not silently call it the original q action or the full S1 (3.6).

100 V_H(0),h=1e-6 v,phi=the h=0 minimum, zero velocities,N=0..3. Evolve both

Late conditional states: start a=1e-8,canonical displacement/velocity zero,
b=1,3,10;c=1,2.5. c=2.5 is already dark-energy-calibrated in the literature.
Use DOP853,rtol=1e-10,atol=1e-12,max step .05, then tighten by ten and halve
step for each scenario. Require endpoint relative differences <=1e-6 with
absolute allowance 1e-9 and scalar-work closure <1e-6. Friedmann is algebraic
and does not count as another solver check. The expansion-work integral and
all-scenario step refinements were added after the first exploratory pass;
original gates were not relaxed.

Seven analytic controls: stationary minimum; each metric's positive determinant
and connection against metric variation; portal phase-space normalization;
physical small-field remnant. Twenty-eight numerical gates: eight full
conservation, two full refinement, twelve late work, six late refinement.
These are internal checks, not independent scientific replications.

Report both physical failures, conditional late states and missing vacuum/history
inputs. Compare only the named existing ATLAS bound under its assumptions;
no new detector likelihood or cosmological fit. Do not retire all action-level
freezing based on this finite class. No model is adopted as GRUT's new empirical
target. The false-vacuum decay calculation considered before the latest relay
