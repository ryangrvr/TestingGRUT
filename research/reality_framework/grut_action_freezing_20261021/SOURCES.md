# Primary sources, calibration and exposure

**S1.** K. Dimopoulos and T. Markkanen, *Dark energy as a remnant of inflation
and electroweak symmetry breaking*, JHEP 01 (2019) 029.
[arXiv:1807.04359v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.04359v3),
[publication](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP01(2019)029).
All 12 PDF pages were read before the reset; (3.6)--(3.8) visually inspected.
Use the positive potential (2.3), displacement/remnant (2.4)--(2.7), and the
separate kinetic illustrations in section 3. The paper assumes zero final
vacuum energy and treats the Higgs as a mean field. It already discusses the
collider portal problem. Our hybrid action and c continuation differ from its
literal full (3.6). The mechanism is borrowed; no GRUT origin or priority for
the path-bound derivation is claimed.

**S2.** ATLAS Collaboration, *Combination of searches for invisible decays of
the Higgs boson using 139 fb^-1 of proton-proton collision data at sqrt(s)=13 TeV
collected with the ATLAS experiment*, Phys. Lett. B 842 (2023) 137963.
[arXiv:2301.10731v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10731v2),
[HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.10731v2),
[publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2023.137963).
Abstract, assumptions and results consulted; the observed invisible branching
upper bound .107 at 95% CL assumes SM Higgs production. Our width uses a light
escaping scalar and .0041 GeV as a rounded SM visible-width reference. We do
not re-fit ATLAS or assert this is the latest worldwide 2026 limit.

**S3.** J. E. Camargo-Molina, T. Markkanen and P. Scott, *Dark energy without
fine tuning*, JHEP 10 (2019) 044.
[arXiv:1905.00045v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.00045v2),
[HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.00045v2),
[publication](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP10(2019)044).
Sections 1--5 were read, including the simple action (8), its preparation
assumptions, late pole, and the more complete (19)--(24), with the quantum
caveat after (24). It uses an inflaton-controlled pole and supplies a common
mass, sufficiently long inflation, Higgs stabilization and zero absolute
broken-phase vacuum energy. No numerical validation of this action is claimed.
Normalize fluctuations in the stage where they arise before asserting a tiny
late distribution.
