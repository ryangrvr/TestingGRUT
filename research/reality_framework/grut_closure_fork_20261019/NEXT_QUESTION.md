# A concrete question for a cosmological successor

The next proposed research target is state selection, not another deeply bound
Q-ball energy. This is a research specification; its central result is open.

Choose one dynamical action and matter content. State whether it is ordinary
Einstein/inflaton theory or an explicit global modification such as
sequestering. A no-boundary wavefunction calculated for one action cannot be
combined with another action's global constraints without recalculating it.
Do not silently identify Candidate 1's binding scalar with an inflaton.

Specify a candidate state as an actual amplitude, for example

\[
\Psi[h,\phi]=\int_{\mathcal C}\!Dg\,D\Phi\;\mu[g,\Phi]
\exp(iS[g,\Phi]/\hbar),
\]

including its integration cycle C, boundary conditions, allowed topologies,
measure and perturbative domain. Identify exactly which of those choices the
new closure condition fixes, and which are postulates. The phrase "one closed
whole" does not by itself specify this datum.

The first discriminating task is whether the new condition selects among
otherwise viable cycles/states **without using the desired final spectrum or
curvature to make the choice**. Evaluate the homogeneous saddle and at least
one inhomogeneous scalar/tensor mode. Check the Hamiltonian constraint,
normalizability or the appropriate probability construction, and perturbation
suppression. Compare with the established prescriptions using the same
action and calibrated inputs. A contour chosen by hand remains an adopted
prescription, even if its resulting state is useful.

Only after that choice is explicit should the calculation produce a
distribution for present curvature or another observable. It must include
the potential, duration of inflation, reheating/history and conditioning
relevant to that observable. A sign prediction supplies no finite lower
magnitude bound. A probability distribution can be tested with likelihoods;
neither the observed value nor a desired confidence threshold may be used
to select its prior after the fact.

For the proposed thermal bridge, retain the v11/v14 field, frequency, observer
and midpoint conventions. Compute any closed-geometry corrections or state
the controlled small-curvature limit. Show separately what an actual detector
or other instrument measures. An extension should identify a mathematical
statement that is not already the consequence of the adopted state and QFT.
Priority of the precise finite-separation/minimally coupled lemma is open.

Success is a specified additional state rule with a distinct calculable
consequence, or a precise demonstration that the proposed rule does not
select among the tested alternatives. No universal theory or resolution of
the quantum-cosmology contour dispute is presumed.
