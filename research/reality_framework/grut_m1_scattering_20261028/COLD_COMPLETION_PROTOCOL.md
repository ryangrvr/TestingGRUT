# Conditional post-conversion mass accounting

Before solving any new profiles, fix the following diagnostic. For each measured
5/10/20/40-percent source, suppose every outgoing phi decays once, all resulting
and directly emitted antiparticles later undergo Xbar+Q->X+(Q-2), no additional
particles are created, the emitted positive X do not rejoin a core, and all
kinetic energy redshifts away. These are explicit ideal processing assumptions,
not a computed capture or cooling history.

Then Q_final=Q0-Nplus-Nminus-2Nphi and N_X_free=Q0-Q_final. Re-solve the unchanged
M1 stationary branch at this Q_final; do not hold E(Q)/Q at its Q200000 value.
Compare [E(Q_final)+m_X N_X_free]/E(Q0) with the frozen one-percent mass tolerance.
Do not change the charge yield or any coupling to repair a failed comparison.
If the stationary solution is absent/unbound, report that outcome. This is a
conditional completion for one pulse, not final eta,p,f(Q), physical f_sym or a
demonstration that the processing assumptions occur cosmologically.
