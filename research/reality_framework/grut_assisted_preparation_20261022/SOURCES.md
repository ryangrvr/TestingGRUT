# Primary sources and prior exposure

**S1.** Camargo-Molina, Markkanen and Scott, *Dark energy without fine tuning*,
JHEP 10 (2019) 044, [arXiv:1905.00045v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.00045v2),
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.00045),
[HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.00045v2).
Read sections 1--5; visually inspected PDF pages 8--9 for action (5.2),
subsequent canonical text and preparation discussion. PDF section numbering
differs from HTML equation numbering. This packet uses the literal action's
normalization. Its author-stated small residual and absolute vacuum-zero
assumptions do not supply quantum transport through reheating. Mechanism
credit remains with its authors; no priority claim for the consistency rewrite.

**S2.** Starobinsky and Yokoyama, *Equilibrium state of a self-interacting scalar
field in the de Sitter background*, Phys. Rev. D 50 (1994) 6357,
[arXiv:astro-ph/9407016](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9407016),
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/9407016).
Consulted the assumptions, canonical de Sitter variance and section II's
coarse-grained Langevin/Fokker--Planck equations. This supports the standard
leading light-field stochastic limit, not an exact full multifield/reheating
description. Our canonical-pole map and finite-volume computation are stated
derivations, not a reproduction of every result in that paper.

**S3.** Planck 2018 results VI, [arXiv:1807.06209v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209v4),
Tables 1--2, and X, [arXiv:1807.06211v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06211v2).
Named base-model snapshot: A_s≈2.100e-9 and n_s=.9649±.0042. These are
inferred parameters, not assumption-free raw data. The n_s sensitivity exercise
uses two quoted marginal standard errors, not a joint confidence contour.

**S4.** BICEP/Keck XIII, *Improved Constraints on Primordial Gravitational Waves
using Planck, WMAP, and BICEP/Keck Observations through the 2018 Observing Season*,
[arXiv:2110.00483](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.00483).
Consulted the abstract's likelihood/model assumptions and named 95% r_0.05<.036
bound. It is a dated snapshot, not asserted to be the latest limit. We do not
combine it with Planck marginals to invent a new joint significance.
