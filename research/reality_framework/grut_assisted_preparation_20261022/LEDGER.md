Physical uncertainties exceed numerical errors. The first-order slow-roll
relation needs a full cosmological perturbation calculation and updated data
inference before any observational exclusion probability is quoted. The chosen
rectangular sensitivity range is not a joint confidence region. As was held
at a rounded benchmark; M,v,lambda are not assigned new inferred covariances.
The stochastic truncation is leading in light mass and local/background
approximations; mode agreement checks normalization, not all their errors.
Finite grids bound discretization/domain errors, not reheating evolution.

The fixed-inflaton process is not the closed universe. Its noise comes from
tracing shorter modes in an assumed inflationary state; it is not an unexplained
energy-production law for the full model. We show the remnant energy is small
relative to that background in this local probe, but do not replace the missing
conserved full-history calculation by that check.

No control failed in the executed numerical run. Negative results are retained:
the low conditional amplitude, non-normalizable fixed-H equilibrium and broad
finite-time stochastic limit. A naive linear Gaussian across the pole was
rejected analytically before being used as a physical distribution. Exact
Hankel/local mode powers remain diagnostics rather than such an extrapolation.
No external replication, new dependency installation, higher-order likelihood,
physical reheating simulation, model adoption or canonical merge occurred.

Independent review should:

1. Derive sqrt(3/2)c from the **printed action**, rather than the subsequent
   intermediate canonical expression; audit the factors in the eliminated relation.
2. Check the domain z>1 and calibration assumptions; do not count A_s and rho
   twice or turn the dated rectangular box into a significance.
3. Derive the canonical map across the pole neighborhood, remaining on sigma>0.
4. Audit the asymptotic potential, stochastic drift/diffusion normalization and
   finite-volume generator; verify positivity, volume and refinement controls.
5. Keep the c=10 local probe distinct from a claimed observationally compatible point.
6. Propagate the full distribution and its correlations through a specified
   conserved reheating receiver before deriving a relic density or survival claim.
7. Reproduce the raw files and temporary re-execution; integrity is not independent science.

Checkpoint: the next required calculation cannot be supplied by further grid
refinement of the fixed-inflaton probe. It needs the reheating physics and
joint distribution. That extension must be versioned, with its new parameters
and owner approval before adoption as an empirical project target. Without it,
the outcome remains a conditional inconsistency and a demonstrated missing
prediction dependency, not a finished theory.
