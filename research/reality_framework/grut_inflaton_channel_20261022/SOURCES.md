The new computation derives the vertex, phase-space width, external-pulse
mode equation, Born energy integral and receiver from the stated action
and approximations. The core processes and the entropy relation are
known physics. No novelty search or new-theory priority is asserted.

The inflation normalization uses the already-exposed reference A_s,
N and Higgs parameters from d601fd1. It is not a fit to today's dark
energy, a new inflation likelihood or a current-world constraint.
The absolute vacuum zero and common scalar potential scale remain
model assumptions. No new empirical measurement is used as held out.
