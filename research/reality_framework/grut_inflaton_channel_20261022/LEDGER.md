The constructive advance is a derived partial rate with a conserved
receiver and an observable radiation interface. It closes the narrower
question of whether the displayed metric supplies any receiver at all.
It does not close the joint-state, visible-reheating or vacuum-selection
problems by relabeling produced waves as dark energy.
