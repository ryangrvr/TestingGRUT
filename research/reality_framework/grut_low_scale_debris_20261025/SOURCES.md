  issue #13 [comment 6404819222](https://github.com/ryangrvr/TestingGRUT/issues/13#issuecomment-6404819222).
  The full summary and consolidated critic were read. Per-file code/note
  scopes and immutable hashes are in inputs/PINNED_REVIEW.json. The

  exponential decay correction, case-L benchmark and vector withdrawal

- Codex, [373d37e](https://github.com/ryangrvr/TestingGRUT/commit/373d37eda07860dad836d6be755bfe9763667409).
  Full scalar evolution and DOP853 engine are reused with explicit source
  hashes; only particle observables are extended. All 96 published
  ensemble endpoints are projected. The new thermal integrator,
  independent real-matrix projection, decay transport and Proca solver
  are new Codex code, not an outside-author replication. The optional
  reference replay executes one reviewer benchmark and is labelled as such.

- Graham, Mardon and Rajendran, *Vector Dark Matter from Inflationary
  Fluctuations*, [arXiv:1504.02102](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.02102).
  Official abstract rechecked; primary action/mass-assumption read scope
  is also recorded in 373d37e/SOURCES.md. The standard Proca equations are
  derived in MATHEMATICS.md. The approximate abundance normalization is
  explicitly the review's convention; this packet does not claim the
  paper's fixed-mass result solves a time-dependent Higgs background.

- Sato, Takahashi and Yamada, *Gravitational production of dark photon dark
  matter with mass generated by the Higgs mechanism*,
  [arXiv:2204.11896](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11896).
  Official abstract rechecked. It establishes relevant prior work on
  changing Higgs masses/longitudinal spectra. Its quantitative model is
  not imported into this three-field calculation.

- Solitosynthesis/Q-ball formation: Postma,
  [Phys. Rev. D 65, 085035](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.65.085035),
  official publisher abstract surfaced in search; full page inaccessible
  this turn. The seed/accretion premise is prior art, not a computed
  outcome here. Kusenko–Shaposhnikov and Enqvist–McDonald credits and exact
  earlier read scopes remain in the parent packets.

- Bose equilibrium, Goldstone equivalence, tree decay phase space and
  cannibal reactions are standard physics. The specific conditional
  benchmark/rates come from the pinned reviewer code; no GRUT novelty
  is claimed for these methods. A full lattice/Boltzmann treatment was
  not run, nor were all reviewer tracks replayed.
