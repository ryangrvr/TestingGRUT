# The remaining particle match

The new input is a nonlinear classical field history and its measured covariances. Two reference projections give about2.24 and1.18 for the main nonzero-mode negative-charge number per net current. Their identical charge identity does not choose a physical basis. The coherent zero mode and interaction energy also remain significant.

The next calculation should use the measured medium rather than assume a massless or Hartree gas:

1. **Measure unequal-time response and correlations.** On the actual nonlinear backgrounds, apply small canonical impulses and evolve their tangents. Combine the retarded response with unequal-time statistical X/phi correlators over windows resolving several oscillations. Vary window, momentum and impulse size; include the two real X directions and their mixing with phi. Extract whether narrow, slowly changing poles exist, their dispersions and widths. A fixed vacuum frequency is not that measurement.
2. **Assign occupations only where justified.** Separate the coherent mean and any other persistent coherent modes. Use the measured spectral/statistical relation, with a consistent charge basis, to assign populations in the weak-wave window. Retain broad/nonadiabatic or strongly interacting components as fields when required. Check higher correlations or demonstrate that the kinetic closure erases their influence. The exported covariance lacks the quantum commutator and is not itself a Wigner particle distribution.
3. **Complete quantum and heavy/gauge matching.** The present finite-band Gaussian is a stated reference control, not the complete cosmic state. Quantify IR/UV and preparation dependence, vacuum subtraction/counterterms and quantum collision terms. Verify the reduced scalar stage against the full X/Y/Sigma/gauge evolution over the nonlinear regime, preserving the total gauge charge. The measured1–3% early matching errors and the frozen-heavy estimate are not a bound on the entire later state.
4. **Carry the resulting state through the sources and binding.** Recompute parent production and normalization on the selected reheating history. Include the early Y_H cascade and subsequent timed Y_L/vector daughters without adding a thermal copy of energy already in the fields. Evolve the neutral reservoir, medium masses, actual kinetic reactions, bias and eventual binding transition. Track finite-Q object energies and capture if objects emerge. Keep the common final abundance denominator fixed.

A passing result requires charge/energy and matching-window stability throughout that chain. If plausible preparations or formation rules change the allowance verdict, report that conditional dependence. No measured field statistic in this packet is declared the final antiparticle fraction.
