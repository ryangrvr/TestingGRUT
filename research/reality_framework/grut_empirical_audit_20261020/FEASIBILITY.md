# Empirical-target feasibility, before a new parameter search

Only the stated action and matching are allowed. No search or additional
production campaign is authorized by the scientific dependency analysis below.
Costs are rough requirements, not booked runtime or promised sensitivity.

| Candidate observable | Physical inputs and missing dependencies | Discrimination; calibration vs test | Cost and likely limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nearby maser D_A(z) upper envelope from conserved charge | Fixed old mass/yield point, leading energy bound, standard BBN, flat weak-binding FRW, disk inference and peculiar velocities. Does not need an object population or hidden dust EOS. | Rejects an overenergetic preparation; generic massive charged sectors share it. Calibration: D+3nu LUNA BBN. Test: all six Pesce 2020 Table 1 distances/velocities, separate measurement streams, already exposed to analyst. | Seconds/minutes of analytic/decimal checks, archive validation and quadrature. Dominant limits are stated cosmology, peculiar velocities and disk inference. **Selected.** |
| Published combined maser H0 | Same inputs plus the inference's adopted LambdaCDM distance relation and Omega_m=.315 from Planck | Conditional rejection, not sector identification. Same masers as selected test; cannot be counted again. | Cheap, but cosmography transfer is an avoidable extra assumption. Audit/reference only. |
| CMB omega_c, CMB/structure spectra | Independent mass/yield, hidden thermal history, radiation fraction, EOS, sound speed, interactions, distribution and perturbation transfer | More shape information could distinguish a specified history. BBN calibration and Planck spectra could be distinct data streams, but importing Planck omega_c assumes cold collisionless matter. | Months-scale development of consistent cosmology/likelihood, not just finer 3D core tests. Unknown abundance/history dominates before numerical convergence. Not selected. |
| BBN extra expansion or Delta Neff | Reheating/temperature ratio, radiation/condensate evolution, any RH-neutrino population, nuclear network | Tests an early hidden energy density; not unique to these fields. Existing BBN density used as calibration cannot simultaneously be counted as a new held-out success. A new independent light-element likelihood would be needed. | Moderate homogeneous cosmology + BBN work after initial inputs are fixed. Initial hidden energy upper bound/history currently missing. Not selected. |
| Microlensing / compact-object encounters | Converged cosmological mass/spatial distribution, compact fractions, survival, lens/source geometry and survey response | Lensing is adopted Einstein gravity; compact-object detection alone does not identify a Q-ball potential. Independent survey event data would be needed. | Very high population/evolution burden. The old reference object's mass/radius are converted as an accessibility diagnostic, not a relic distribution. Optical wave parameter is calculated in the audit before any signal claim. Not selected. |
| Ordinary-particle scattering or fifth-force signal | Explicit SM source/coupling, flux, propagation, background and detector response | New scalar/SM portals vanish at leading matching. Gravitational interactions remain; running/loop portals require an order-consistent calculation. There is no supplied ordinary detector signal. | No justified rate yet. Adding a sizable portal would be an approved new model version, not an interpretation of D. Not selected. |
| Ideal D-reporter phase versus momentum | Independently measured scalar masses/couplings, prepared charge/profile, a D source, splitter, propagation, recombination and detector | Could test a correlated new-sector scattering law if physically realized. Existing ideal phases are synthetic calculations, not calibration or test data from nature. | Apparatus interaction chain is missing; no accessible magnitude in an ordinary detector has been derived. Not selected. |
| Lepton-number-violating process (e.g. neutrinoless double-beta decay) | Exact adopted EFT B-L and Dirac neutrinos forbid the process within scope; a physical search needs nuclear and detector inputs | A verified B-L-violating observation could reject an adopted postulate; a null result is shared by ordinary Dirac-neutrino alternatives and does not identify the new scalar sector. | Does not fix hidden scale/yield or provide a new-sector abundance validation. No fresh experimental likelihood is attempted. Not selected. |

Selection: perform only the conservative distance-envelope audit at the already
excluded benchmark and derive necessary product constraints. There is currently
no independently fixed accessible prediction whose success could identify the
proposed scalar mechanism. All six published distances are retained; none is
selected after residuals. The old summary H0 and the distance table share data.
Their agreement is an inference check, not independent replication.

The selected observable needs total conserved charge, not composite survival.
Consequently expanding the current network simulation or extrapolating a core
lifetime cannot supply the missing empirical calibration and is not warranted
for this target. Individual decay/evaporation/merger rates and unassigned core
energy are left open, explicitly, rather than silently supplied by old runs.
