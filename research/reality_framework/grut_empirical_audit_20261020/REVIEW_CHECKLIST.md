# Independent-review checklist

This review is still owed; none of the checks below has been completed by an
independent researcher merely because the author's code passes.

- Check the action's current/sign convention using a canonically normalized
  rotating complex scalar; verify Q=Qbar/gX^2 and E=(v/gX)Ebar.
- Reproduce the potential-ratio minimization in both ab<1 and ab>=1 regimes,
  the pointwise square completion, and its distinction from ADM/quantum energy.
- Check every transition from local proper energy to a cosmological mean,
  charge/entropy conservation, standard-BBN calibration and the flat Friedmann
  source. Identify any unjustified use of a lower bound as an upper bound.
- Verify that the borrowed BBN likelihood has no CMB baryon prior and that
  applying it assumes a standard radiation/reaction history not derived here.
- Read Pesce Eq. 1 and Table 1 directly. Check the Omega_m/Planck qualification,
  distance/velocity conventions, all six transcriptions and disk-model limits.
- Rederive the D_A envelope and peculiar-redshift composition; check the
  monotone low-redshift corner and the exact role of 250/500 km/s allowances.
- Recompute the published-source results in a separate clean environment.
  Do not rewrite the old preflight, freeze or source manifests.
- Challenge the use of extrapolated percentile widths; use raw distance and
  velocity likelihoods before quoting a calibrated exclusion confidence.
- Verify that all inputs were retained, no successful point was selected, and
  conditional product limits were not relabeled an abundance prediction.
- Assess whether loops, strong binding, curvature, dilution or different BBN
  evolution change the explicitly conditional result. Such a change is not
  evidence that the scalar fields exist.
- For any positive empirical claim, demand independent new-sector scale and
  initial-yield provenance, a discriminating forward observable and a reserved
  test. Do not count adopted GR/SM success, synthetic verification, or the same
  masers twice as new mechanism evidence.

Record reviewer, source commit, environment, exact checks, disagreements and
the evidence that decides them. Until that happens, reproduction remains
same-author implementation verification.
