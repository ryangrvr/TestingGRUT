calibration/inference; C = additional cosmological or observational hypothesis.

The standard BBN hypothesis C2 is not independently proved by a small hidden
energy fraction at the initial high temperature. A lower energy floor cannot
upper-bound later hidden radiation or false-vacuum energy. A complete history
would need to establish C2, C4 and C5 dynamically. The audit tests their stated
conditional intersection, not existence of an entire viable cosmological solution.

The numerical three-width construction is an intentionally transparent
sensitivity check. It is not a simultaneous 99.7% confidence region and does
not justify a Gaussian exclusion significance. The 1% baryon conversion
allowance is distinct from uncomputed EFT/cosmological theory uncertainty.
