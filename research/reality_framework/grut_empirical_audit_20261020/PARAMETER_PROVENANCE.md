# What independently fixes scale and charge yield?

Nothing in the committed model or available evidence fixes both. The original
Candidate-1 sections 3 and 7 explicitly list v and couplings as renormalized
inputs and require new-sector calibration. The old empirical protocol chose
v=1000 GeV and r=YQ/YB=1. The old v/MP=1e-5 illustrative regime was another
specified point, not a derivation. No unavailable Claude result is substituted.

| Quantity | Current provenance | Independent constraint actually available here |
|---|---|---|
| MP, ordinary constants | External gravity/SI calibration | Fix ordinary scales; do not fix v/MP. |
| gX=.1, lambda_phi=.01, lambda_X=.001 | Construction choices | No pole-mass or scattering calibration for the new sector. Dimensionless synthetic spectra do not supply one. |
| mX=gX v, v=1000 GeV | Free scalar mass scale and old benchmark | Matching mX0=0 is a relation, not a numerical measurement of v. There is no specified Higgs portal tying v to the electroweak scale at leading order. |
| mD0=100 GeV, hD=.01, lambdaD=.001 | Reporter benchmark choices | No ordinary source or detector calibration; D does not become an electromagnetic detector by naming it a reporter. |
| Net primordial Q, YQ and temperature ratio | Initial-state data | Exact conservation fixes evolution of a supplied charge, not its magnitude. U(1)-symmetric dynamics cannot generate net Q from a globally neutral closed state. |
| omega_b, YB | Standard-BBN deuterium/nuclear inference | Calibrates baryons. YQ=YB is an added hypothesis, not an independent hidden-sector measurement. |
| reheating, inflationary initial distribution, entropy transfers | Not specified | Needed for a gravitational freeze-in or fluctuation production calculation; such processes do not by themselves create a global charge asymmetry. |

At fixed dimensionless scalar couplings, the leading flat equations admit a
continuous change v -> c v, mX -> c mX, lengths/times -> (lengths/times)/c,
physical energies -> c E, with the same dimensionless profiles and charges.
Gravity introduces the additional ratio v/MP, which remains an input. Refining
a dimensionless simulation cannot choose c from nature.

Independent variation of the initial charge/entropy ratio supplies a second
freedom. The conservative abundance floor depends on `mX * abs(YQ/YB)` for
the fixed couplings. A single density or distance constraint can restrict this
product; setting it equal to the desired density is a calibration fit. It is
not an abundance prediction. Equality of a lower bound to a density is not even
a calculation that the actual relic reaches the lower bound.

The exact hidden vacuum X=D=0, phi=v is permitted by the leading action. Its
additional classical stress vanishes. Thus the classical hidden population can
be arbitrarily small without changing the action, and a null gravity test
cannot positively identify this sector. Vacuum/loop effects at higher order
are a separate, uncomputed question; this argument is not a statement that
all quantum effects vanish.

Minimum additions for a predictive relic test are (i) independently measured
new-sector masses/couplings or a specified, separately testable scale-setting
mechanism, (ii) a specified initial-state/reheating/asymmetry mechanism with
its independent inputs, and (iii) the evolution/observable calculation the
selected test actually needs. A positive mechanism claim further needs an
observable relation that competing sectors do not share.

No action extension is adopted here. No new portal, asymmetry interaction,
inflaton coupling or charge-breaking term has been added. Proposing one would
require a separately versioned action, operator list, new parameter provenance,
and owner approval before using it as the project's new empirical target.
The version in this packet is **audit protocol v1**, not Candidate-1 model v2.
