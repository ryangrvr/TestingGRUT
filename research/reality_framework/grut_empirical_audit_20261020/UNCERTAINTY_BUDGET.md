# Numerical and physical uncertainty budget

The primary result is a conditional inequality checked against published
summary distributions. A list of small numerical errors is not a total
physical uncertainty, and no formal exclusion significance is computed.

The notation omega_b=Omega_b*h^2 does not import a measured H0 into calibration:
omega_b=rho_b/(rho_c/h^2), with rho_c/h^2=3*H100^2/(8*pi*G) and the fixed
unit H100=100 km/s/Mpc. BBN plus the adopted photon-temperature history infer
the physical baryon density. No maser H0 is used to obtain it. Equating hidden
yield to that baryon yield remains a separate initial-state hypothesis.

Under the predeclared conservative corners, NGC 5765b gives Dlow=96.90 Mpc
versus Dmax=77.8415 Mpc (250 km/s rms); the 500 km/s stress case gives
Dmax=83.9531 Mpc. Their ratios are 1.24484 and 1.15422. This is an auditable
conditional mismatch. It is not a quantified probability for every unmodeled
physical or observational systematic, or an exclusion of the full quantum EFT.

The old Planck omega_c comparison additionally requires cold, collisionless
late hidden matter. It is not added to the new statistic. The old H0 result
and new distance check share all six masers and must not be multiplied as
independent likelihoods. Standard BBN and maser disk data are distinct empirical
streams but share adopted GR/cosmological/ordinary-physics assumptions.
