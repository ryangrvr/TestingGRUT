# Verdict C — Residual electric-charge freedom

In the frozen four-dimensional spin gauge EFT with one right-handed neutrino per family, anomaly cancellation and the faithful compact quotient admit the infinite family $Y=Y_{\mathrm{SM}}+3k(B-L)$, $k\in\mathbb Z$. Every member admits finite-dimension interactions that remove all connected ordinary internal phase symmetries modulo gauge. The minimum dimension of a local unbroken-phase $B-L$ violator grows at least linearly in $|k|$. Excluding an exact symmetry therefore fails to select one charge assignment even though it constrains operator content. [Proof](DERIVATIONS.md)

The noncanonical members predict a free-neutron monopole of at least $3/4$ of the electron charge magnitude. The frozen neutron benchmark selects $k=0$ within the declared comparison class. The standard charge assignment has the same prediction. Existing charge-dequantization and accidental-symmetry results give the conventional explanation. This packet claims neither scientific priority for the dimension bound nor a quantum-gravity completion of the counterexamples. [Reality test](REALITY_TEST.md) · [Novelty audit](NOVELTY_AUDIT.md)

The mathematical obstruction closes the qualitative symmetry route at its tested strength. An additional hypothesis about measurable low-dimensional symmetry violation, a physical ultraviolet embedding or another independent observable would be needed to restrict the surviving class further.
