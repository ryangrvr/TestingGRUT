## Independent derivation

With Higgs weight $h=3$ and quark-doublet weight $q$, $[SU(2)]^2U(1)$ sets the lepton-doublet weight to $\ell=-3q$. The four Yukawa equations then give $(y_{u^c},y_{d^c},y_{e^c},y_{n^c})=(-q-h,-q+h,-\ell+h,-\ell-h)$. Per family the mixed color coefficient is $2q+y_{u^c}+y_{d^c}=0$. Color and weak multiplicities make the mixed gravitational coefficient zero separately in the quark and lepton sectors. Expanding the cubic coefficient yields $-6h^2(3q+\ell)=0$. The color cubic anomaly cancels between fundamentals and antifundamentals, and the weak doublet has zero perturbative cubic anomaly. The charge on $Q$ under the specified central generator requires $q\equiv1\pmod6$, and all other quotient conditions follow. These equations reconstruct the family using only the representations, Yukawas and anomaly conditions. The canonical weights specify the $k=0$ coordinate on the family.

To test faithfulness, act trivially on $H$ first. The covering-group circle angle must be $m\pi/3$ and the weak factor is $(-1)^m\mathbf 1_2$. Acting trivially on $Q$ then fixes the color factor to $e^{2\pi i m/3}\mathbf 1_3$ for each $q=1+6k$. The other fields are invariant under each such element. The total kernel on the cover is exactly the prescribed central subgroup for every integer $k$. The vanishing spin bordism group $\Omega_5^{\mathrm{Spin}}(BG)$ removes the residual global gauge-anomaly check once the perturbative anomaly has canceled. The familiar four weak doublets per family also pass the lifted $SU(2)$ parity check. The latter check on the covering group alone would leave the general quotient-bundle check unaddressed.
