# Novelty audit

The audit compared the frozen proposition against charge quantization and its failure, the compact Standard Model global forms and their anomaly conditions, baryon/lepton violating operators, and quantum-gravity arguments for absence of global symmetries. It also reviewed EFT and amplitude consistency as candidate selectors. [Established constraints](ESTABLISHED_CONSTRAINTS.md)

| Result or inference | Primary comparison | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A family-universal anomaly-free $B-L$ direction with Dirac Yukawas and singlet neutrinos | Babu and Mohapatra derive this spectral ambiguity under the corresponding hypotheses. Foot, Lew and Volkas review it and additional family-dependent possibilities when flavor assumptions are relaxed. [Babu and Mohapatra](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.41.271) · [Foot, Lew and Volkas](https://doi.org/10.1088/0954-3899/19/3/005) | Established. The frozen generic-flavor class removes the family-dependent possibilities. |
| Integer members for a fixed compact gauge group, with global anomaly control | Tong gives the different possible Standard Model quotients and their operational distinction in line operators. Davighi, Gripaios and Lohitsiri give the representation congruences and global spin bordism calculation. [Tong](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP07(2017)104) · [Davighi et al., Secs. 4.5–4.6](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11277) | Applying established global group constraints gives a discrete family. Compactness and the quotient do not select an element. |
| Low-dimensional $B-L$ violation can fix charge in the declared spectrum | Babu and Mohapatra give the Majorana route. Weinberg identifies low-order operators violating baryon and lepton number. Kobach links operator dimension with $B-L$ in the usual charge assignment, including right-handed neutrinos. [Babu and Mohapatra](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.63.938) · [Weinberg](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.43.1566) · [Kobach](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2016.05.050) | The low-dimension control is conventional. An observed or derived gauge-invariant term would be additional input, so there is no new explanation from this branch. |
| No exact global symmetry as a qualitative constraint | Harlow and Ooguri establish the bulk statement in the AdS/CFT setting with its assumptions. The result supplies no rate or operator dimension for a particular low-energy breaking process. [Harlow and Ooguri](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.191601) | The tested connected ordinary phase condition is a restricted low-energy consequence. No claim is made about all of quantum gravity. |
| High-dimensional operators can violate an accidental phase despite the absence of low-dimensional violations | Fichet and Saraswat give explicit large-integer-charge examples with leading breaking at unbounded dimension. They propose an additional thermal rate bound with cutoff, mass and coefficient conditions. Their paper distinguishes low-cutoff EFTs from the regime in which the proposed bound applies. Discrete gauge symmetries can also appear global in low-energy probes. [Fichet and Saraswat, §§8–9](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP01(2020)088) · [Krauss and Wilczek](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.62.1221) | The qualitative mechanism has a direct prior example. F4 exhibits it in this compact chiral charge family with explicit all-integer bounds. It does not test, derive or falsify the separate rate conjecture. |
| Electric neutrality as a known discriminator | A direct beam-deflection experiment probes free-neutron monopole charge in electron-charge units. Conventional QFT predicts a neutral neutron given the experimentally selected matter representations. [Baumann et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.37.3107) | The exact ratio gap distinguishes the counterexamples empirically. The observation does not make the qualitative criterion select the canonical weights. |

The bounded deliverable is a local counterexample and an engineering-dimension bound for a specified chiral gauge family. All mathematical ingredients are standard. The source review found these precedents and did not establish priority for the particular dressed operator and numerical bounds. There is zero additional empirical displacement from the ordinary model with the observed charge assignment. [Reality test](REALITY_TEST.md)
