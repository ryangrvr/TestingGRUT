# What established consistency supplies

| Requirement | Established consequence in its domain | Inputs and residual freedom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unitarity | Positive-norm physical states and probability conservation imply the optical theorem for a well-defined scattering matrix. For inclusive channel sums the spectral density appearing in the forward relation is nonnegative. | State space, spectrum and interactions are inputs. An equality among amplitudes still depends on the action and matching. Positivity of norm and probability does not identify the species or fix their couplings. |
| Analyticity and scattering positivity | With the appropriate gap or infrared control, analyticity, crossing, high-energy bounds and subtractions, dispersion yields integrals of nonnegative spectral weights for suitable low-energy coefficients. These constrain Wilson-coefficient combinations. | A massless photon or graviton can make the naive forward argument singular. Graviton poles and assumptions on ultraviolet behavior matter. An inequality on combinations of higher interactions does not generically select an independent gauge charge or justify universal effective luminality. No full amplitude positivity audit is implied for the F4 examples. [Adams et al.](https://doi.org/10.1088/1126-6708/2006/10/014) · [Gravitational pole analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP05(2023)076) |
| Gauge and gravitational anomalies | Local gauge and mixed gauge–gravitational anomalies must cancel in a gauge-invariant four-dimensional spin EFT with the supplied spectrum and no additional inflow or compensator. Global gauge anomalies are separate tests, sensitive to global group and spacetime structure. Ordinary global anomalies can instead encode physical violation or matching. | Anomaly equations depend on chosen multiplets and group. They leave the $B-L$ shift in this class. The spin-bordism group used here applies to the fixed quotient. Chiral nonspin models and higher-dimensional pure gravitational anomalies involve different requirements. [Davighi et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11277) · [Babu and Mohapatra](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.41.271) |
| RG and matching | The renormalization group runs couplings from independent boundary data. With suitable separation of scales, integrating out heavy modes gives threshold corrections and suppressed operators, with possible nondecoupling effects in other regimes. Scheme-dependent parameters can represent the same physical amplitudes. | Running does not fix boundary values without an independently selected fixed point, basin or matching law. Gauge selection rules hold for admissible counterterms. In F4, the calculable one-loop coefficient varies with representation and is not a selector by itself. [Decoupling](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.11.2856) · [F4 running](DERIVATIONS.md#4-physical-equivalence-radiative-stability-and-running) |
| Radiative stability | An enhanced exact symmetry in a limit protects the corresponding zero against additive breaking from symmetric interactions. Approximate symmetry can organize small spurions. Power counting and loops set relative orders within an EFT. | Technical naturalness guides the acceptability of small values but supplies no measured number or prior distribution. Thresholds and a symmetry-preserving regulator or matching scheme must be specified. The vacuum volume term is ordinarily allowed, and the cosmological hierarchy remains unresolved. [Weinberg on the cosmological constant](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.61.1) |
| Causal ultraviolet completion | Local relativistic QFT imposes microcausality. Conditional analyticity bounds and UV matching can rule out some proposed low-energy descriptions. Low-frequency group or sound velocities are different observables from microscopic wavefronts. | Perturbative local consistency below a cutoff is weaker than an ultraviolet completion. F3's common gravitational algebra and canonical microscopic comparison explicitly distinguish the collective acoustic response from metric fronts. The F4 construction tests a necessary low-energy symmetry condition at fixed matter content. [F3](../grut_f3_coupled_consistency_20261126/VERDICT.md) |
| Quantum-gravitational consistency | The absence of bulk global internal symmetries, compactness and charge completeness have holographic arguments or theorems under specified AdS/CFT assumptions. | Their scope beyond those hypotheses is conjectural. The weak gravity conjecture links charged states and gravity in proposed UV classes. The swampland symmetry conjecture adds a quantitative rate condition. Neither is a general measured low-energy law, and neither is invoked to select $k$ here. [Harlow and Ooguri](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.191601) · [Weak gravity conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0601001) · [Fichet and Saraswat](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP01(2020)088) |
| Universality and EFT correspondence | Scale separation lets different microscopic systems yield the same infrared operators or universal scaling. After matching, leading long-distance nonanalytic terms can be fixed by fields and symmetries already in the theory. | Agreement at long distances may conceal distinct microphysics and generally leaves local matching coefficients independent. The conventional gravitational EFT supplies this correspondence, including calculable quantum corrections. [Donoghue](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.50.3874) |

The F4 counterexamples satisfy the declared weak-coupling local constraints with exact gauge invariance. Requiring every example to realize a particular quantum-gravity embedding or to share an ultraviolet scale under identical couplings would be a different proposition. Nonperturbative gravitational consistency remains an open filter. [Frozen protocol](FROZEN_PROTOCOL.md)
