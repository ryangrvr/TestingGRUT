The physical distinction is between requiring a conserved quantity to be violated at some scale and requiring a calculable amount of violation in a specific low-energy channel. The holographic result motivates the former, at the scope defined in its proof. The second condition would be additional physics. A small finite family of hand-picked charge assignments could be misleading here. The test solves the compact weight lattice exactly, supplies explicit violations for all its members, bounds the first available operator dimension and uses five registered cases to check the algebra.
