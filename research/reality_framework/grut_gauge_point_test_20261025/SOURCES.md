# Primary sources and actual read scope

The pinned review and its notes were retrieved in full through GitHub.
The pair code was read, not executed; its output is included as a clearly
labeled quoted reference. Our integration uses a different reduction and
the original Codex profile. Full external-review replay is not claimed.

- **Hertzberg (2010), Quantum Radiation of Oscillons**,
  https://arxiv.org/html/1003.3459v4 . Read abstract, introduction and selected
  pair-emission/golden-rule passages in section V around equations 59-61.
  It supplies precedent for quantum pair loss dominating weak classical
  radiation, not our model's numerical rate. Our pair formula is derived
  directly in MATHEMATICS.md.
- **Lee, Stein-Schabes, Watkins and Widrow (1989), Gauged Q balls**,
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.39.1665 . Publisher abstract/metadata read.
  The ordinary Coulomb-size issue is relevant prior art; that abstract
  does not establish stability of our charge-six-Higgs model.
- **Ishihara and Ogawa (2019), Homogeneous Balls in a Spontaneously Broken
  U(1) Gauge Theory**, https://arxiv.org/html/1901.08799v1 . Read abstract,
  introduction and basic-model/current/energy/ansatz passages. It explicitly
  treats Higgs countercharge screening and contrasts it with the unscreened
  size bound. Its charges, exact global symmetry and scalar potential differ
  from ours. Its solutions are not a validation of our object spectrum.
- **Saikawa (2017), A review of gravitational waves from cosmic domain
  walls**, https://arxiv.org/html/1703.02576v2 . Read the scaling-annihilation
  passage and equations 2.27-2.28. The formula is an estimate assuming a
  scaling network, with order-one constants.
- **Babichev et al. (2025), Biased domain walls: faster annihilation,
  weaker gravitational waves**, https://arxiv.org/html/2504.07902v3 . Read
  abstract/introduction and selected scaling passages around equation 27.
  Their simulations find a different tiny-bias scaling in their model.
  This is why our timing estimate is not called a network certificate.
- **Klinkhamer and Volovik (2008), Dynamic vacuum variable and equilibrium
  approach in cosmology**, https://arxiv.org/html/0806.2805v7 . Read the
  introductory near-equilibrium interpretation, section V.1 equilibrium
  chemical-potential discussion and selected concluding limitations. This
  directly supports the credit correction. Its oscillatory model is not
  substituted for the different archived GRUT source-law test.
- **Akers et al. (2025), On observers in holographic maps**,
  https://arxiv.org/html/2503.09681v1 . Read abstract/introduction and the
  compared dimension formulas around equations 1.5-1.6. Different observer
  prescriptions give different assignments. No observer rule for GRUT is
  derived here.

Krauss-Wilczek and Banks-Seiberg credits/read scopes remain in the parent
gauge construction. The review's additional references and quantum-gravity
wormhole estimates are not promoted here into independently checked
coefficient predictions. None of these sources is evidence that the
specified GRUT fields exist.
