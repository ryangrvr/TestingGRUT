## What the calculations say about a conscious universe

The existing finite theorem concerns a maximally mixed state in a supplied unitary history construction. It is not a theorem that every closed universe is featureless unless an agent makes a conscious choice. A symmetric ensemble can have nontrivial individual histories; an average charge of zero does not imply every history contains no structures. Conditional records are already possible in the ordinary stationary history state once its input is specified.

A record-selection functional is a mathematical hypothesis for the boundary law. To become a new physical result it needs a well-defined invariant objective, a demonstrated consequence beyond the assumptions inserted, and a discriminating observational test. Nothing calculated here identifies consciousness, proves a cosmic decision, or derives the M1 couplings, charge phase or vacuum density from record capacity.
