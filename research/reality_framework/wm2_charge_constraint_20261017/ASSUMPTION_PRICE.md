## Rule, enforcement, selection

1. A **universal occupied-bond hopping rule** directly stipulates how
   carriers move. It is a permissible microscopic input, not a deduction.
2. The **hard Gauss plus flux-support constraint** enforces the analogous
   restriction for elementary charged motion and a fixed-range generalization
   for bounded-support operators. This is an explicit operator reason inside
   the new law. It is stronger than a verbal appeal to gauge invariance.
3. A **mechanism selecting that constraint and its physical sector** has
   not been given. In fact the proposed H conserves each constraint value;
   it cannot autonomously heal a wrong-sector preparation. Adding a commuting
   penalty does not demonstrate selection or finite-penalty robustness.

The second achievement does not remove the assumption in C_ij: it
installs the graph/flux relationship in kinematics. It also leaves
internal and other charge-neutral information channels unrestricted.

## Energy, interventions and lifetime

The augmented neutral seed adds no matter, compensation or electric
energy and inherits the original formation energy ledger. With E=0
the constraints make that vacuum extension unique. Recording, carrier/
compensator creation and phase operations are external interventions.
They are not an autonomous observer mechanism.

The nearby charged pair can be prepared at the first bond locally.
The far-B counterexample requires a j-link charged preparation or
sequential transport, with j=O(N). Its acceptance, time and work are
not silently imported from S9. With a positive electric energy its
preparation can also cost O(j) energy. The proof is conditional on
that admissible receiver state and applies to all actual conditional
old receiver fields; it does not reset the old field.

The high-retention bound in the signal experiment is a single-time
guarantee at fixed time pi. It is not permanent locking or a continuously
monitored survival probability. No additional asymptotic phase, dimension,
continuum limit, gravity, original CP-12 matter universality or empirical
prediction is claimed.

## Why the full gate stays failed

Forbidding C3 by adding a connected occupied-bond support rule for
internal interactions would address the exhibited channel, but would
add the missing locality premise. The commutant-product lemma shows
why preservation of the present constraints cannot supply that rule.
The bounded campaign ends with this obstruction; it does not narrow
U to its favorable charged-transport subset after the calculation.
