## Primary literature

1. F. R. Klinkhamer and G. E. Volovik, *Dynamic vacuum variable and
   equilibrium approach in cosmology*, Phys. Rev. D 78, 063528 (2008),
   [arXiv:0806.2805](https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.2805),
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.78.063528).
   Read section II, especially equations (2.6)--(2.9) and the f(R)
   discussion; inspect the opening of section III. Credits the underlying
   q mechanism and its fixed-mu scalar-tensor equivalence. KV use an
   opposite Riemann-sign convention; our convention follows the pinned
   brief, with positive de Sitter R.
2. A. De Felice, T. Kobayashi and S. Tsujikawa, *Effective gravitational
   couplings for cosmological perturbations in the most general
   scalar-tensor theories with second-order field equations*, Phys. Lett.
   B 706, 123--133 (2011), [arXiv:1108.4242](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.4242).
   Read sections IV and V.A, especially (41)--(59). Compared the
   quasi-static growth, slip and lensing expressions after translating the
   spatial-potential sign. Their scalar mass convention in (58) is not
   identical to the constant-curvature trace mass; our derivation states
   the distinction. No full re-derivation of general Horndeski theory.
3. L. Amendola, I. Sawicki, M. Kunz and I. D. Saltas, *Direct detection of
   gravitational waves can measure the time variation of the Planck mass*,
   JCAP 08 (2018) 030,
   [arXiv:1712.08623v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1712.08623v2).
   Read section II, equations (3)--(10), including the source/environment
   qualifications following those equations. Compared the independently
   varied tensor action with the wave equation and endpoint-amplitude law.
   No use of the paper's forecast as a present instrumental sensitivity.

De Felice and Tsujikawa's review [arXiv:1002.4928](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.4928)
was located, but its full 156 pages were not audited; the response comparison

## Numerical inputs and evidence levels

The 4 meV uniform unscreened mass is an **assumed control**, relayed from
Claude's stage-1 ledger. This contribution does not reanalyze a laboratory
dataset, certify that exact limit, or apply it to screened models. Unit
conversion constants and the reference Hubble value are listed explicitly
in INPUTS.json. There is no observational fit or significance calculation.

The code is a different implementation by the Codex author, after reading
the action/specification and the published results' context. It is not a
blind scientific replication, outside review, or new laboratory evidence.
No subagents were used in this contribution.
