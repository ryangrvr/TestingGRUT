# GRUT Construction 1

Frozen specification and decision rules · 6 November 2026

The construction is a finite autonomous quantum model of writing, keeping, and using a record. Standard quantum mechanics, the microscopic access algebras, the clock couplings, and the initial low-entropy resources are supplied. The prior stationary-data obstruction applies to any attempt to infer these concrete access algebras from the bare stationary input alone. [Prior result](https://github.com/ryangrvr/TestingGRUT/blob/b362372b6747e0957d3ece19ed510908dd451720/research/reality_framework/grut_foundational_access_20261106/REPORT.md)

## Microscopic tuple

\[
\mathfrak G=(\mathcal H,H,\rho_0,\mathcal A_{\rm access},
                 \mathcal O_{\rm clock},\mathcal O_{\rm readout}).
\]

**Space and units.** The ordered factors are \(C,S,M_1,M_2,A,D_1,D_2\). \(C\) is an eight-state clock, \(S\) a signal, \(M_1,M_2\) memory qubits, \(A\) an action qubit, and \(D_1,D_2\) two conserved disturbance selectors. The Hilbert dimension is \(8\cdot 2^6=512\). Use \(\hbar=1\), clock frequency \(\Omega=1\), and report time in \(\Omega^{-1}\). \(X,Y,Z\) are Pauli matrices with \(Z|b\rangle=(-1)^b|b\rangle\).

**Dynamics.** The time-independent Hamiltonian has only adjacent clock blocks,
\[
H=\sum_{k=0}^{6}g_k\big(|k+1\rangle\langle k|\otimes U_k+
                                  |k\rangle\langle k+1|\otimes U_k^\dagger\big),
\quad g_k=\tfrac{\Omega}{2}\sqrt{(k+1)(7-k)}.
\]
Write \(P=Z_{M_1}Z_{M_2}\), \(\Pi_{F,\pm}=(I\pm F)/2\) for any Hermitian involution \(F\), and
\(R_x(\theta)=\exp(-i\theta X_{M_2}/2)\). Identities on spectator registers are implicit.

| Link \(k\to k+1\) | \(U_k\) | Function |
|---|---|---|
| \(0\to1\) | \(\Pi_{Z_S,+}+\Pi_{Z_S,-}X_{M_2}\) | Write source bit into parity |
| \(1\to2\) | \(\exp(-i\lambda Z_{M_1}/2)\) | Rotate the memory phase within each parity sector |
| \(2\to3\) | \(\Pi_{Z_{D_1},+}+\Pi_{Z_{D_1},-} R_x(\alpha)\) | First disturbance |
| \(3\to4\) | \(\exp(-i\beta P/2)\) | Advance relative parity phase |
| \(4\to5\) | \(\Pi_{Z_{D_2},+}+\Pi_{Z_{D_2},-} R_x(\alpha)\) | Second disturbance |
| \(5\to6\) | \(\Pi_{P,+}+\Pi_{P,-}X_A\) | Act on the parity record without reading \(S\) |
| \(6\to7\) | \(I\) | Read after the action |

The card fixes \(\lambda=\pi/2,\alpha=\pi/3,\beta=2\pi/3\). Pulse enable bits are part of the initial state of the closed model. Every link is present throughout evolution. There are no independent on-site energy terms. Global energy is conserved and the energy zero is the midpoint of the clock spectrum. \(Z_S,Z_{M_1},Z_{D_1},Z_{D_2}\) commute with \(H\). Clock parity conjugates \(H\) to \(-H\). \(P\) commutes with the hold and action links but can change at the writer or disturbance links. \(Z_A\) changes only at the action link.

**Preparation.** Set \(C=|0\rangle\), \(S=|+\rangle\), \(M_1M_2=|\Phi^+\rangle=(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt{2}\), \(A=|0\rangle\), \(D_1D_2=|00\rangle\). The Bell blank has definite even parity and pure joint state. It supplies a one-bit ready record and a definite phase of \(X_{M_1}X_{M_2}\). Signal coherence, initial clock localization, and pure target and selector bits are supplied. There is no initial signal-memory correlation. The calibration disturbance states vary \(D_1D_2\) through \(00,10,01\), with all other registers prepared identically.

**Access and interventions.** The primitive readout algebras are generated by \(X,Z\) on each qubit and by clock position projectors \(Q_k=|k\rangle\langle k|\) and adjacent Hermitian clock matrix units \(B_k=|k+1\rangle\langle k|+|k\rangle\langle k+1|\). The corresponding projective instruments, and jointly commuting products, define allowed diagnostic interventions. Baseline evolution uses only \(H\). Pre-run state preparation and final diagnostic readouts are supplied operations. A separately labeled diagnostic measures an observable and propagates its nonselective state using the same \(H\). This measurement is an intervention for comparison and is outside the unmeasured autonomous run. The dynamical access of the actuator is exactly its link in the table.

**Clock and readouts.** The clock observable is \(K=\sum_{k=0}^7 kQ_k\). For work effect \(E\), the single joint observation conditioned on \(K=k\) has probability
\[
p(E\mid k)=\operatorname{Tr}[(Q_k\otimes E)\rho]/\operatorname{Tr}[(Q_k\otimes I)\rho]
\]
on nonzero-weight ticks. The registered readout is the complete joint distribution of the commuting signs \(Z_S,P,Z_A\), at every tick and fixed selector value. \(P\) is obtained by multiplying the two memory \(Z\) readouts. Use \(r=\Pr(P=Z_S)\), \(a=\Pr(Z_A=Z_S)\), and \(I(Z_S:P)\) from this distribution. Separate diagnostics read \(X_{M_1}X_{M_2}\), \(Y_{M_1}X_{M_2}\), clock coherence and entropy. These diagnostics are outside the reduced solver's target readout.

## Clock and interaction predictions

Put \(V_0=I\), \(V_{k+1}=U_kV_k\), and \(T=\sum_k Q_k\otimes V_k\). Then
\(H=T(H_C\otimes I)T^\dagger\), with \(H_C=\Omega J_x\) in the spin-\(7/2\) representation ordered by increasing \(J_z\). Starting at tick zero, the joint state has blocks \(c_jc_k^*V_j\rho_W V_k^\dagger\), where \(c=e^{-itH_C}|0\rangle\). Thus the conditional work state is \(V_k\rho_W V_k^\dagger\) even when \(t\) is unknown. The conditional state rule also holds in the deliberately prepared stationary eigenhistory \(T(|J_x=7/2\rangle\otimes|\psi_W\rangle)\). The eigenhistory is a separate clock diagnostic with the conditional history supplied in its preparation.

The clock marginal is binomial with \(p=\sin^2(\Omega t/2)\), mean \(7p\), variance \(7p(1-p)\), and monotone mean on \(0\le\Omega t\le\pi\). The model returns to the initial density at \(2\pi\). Compare the conditional predictions with propagation of the full 512-dimensional matrix at the card's sample times, for ticks of probability at least \(10^{-9}\). Report the binomial spread and the root mean square error of the single-read estimator \(\hat t(k)=2\arcsin\sqrt{k/7}/\Omega\) on the registered grid. Its error concerns lab-time estimation, separately from exact tick-conditioned readouts.

Measure clock disturbance by comparing the final \(K=7\) probability with and without a nonselective measurement of \(K\) at \(\Omega t=\pi/2\). The expected probabilities are \(1\) without the read and \(\binom{14}{7}/4^7\) with it. Compare the full energy distributions in every preparation and Hamiltonian control started at \(K=0\). They are the same eight binomial weights \(\binom{7}{m}/2^7\) at energies \(\Omega(m-7/2)\), with zero mean and variance \(7\Omega^2/4\). This comparison covers total energy in the declared model.

Recover the operational factors by the noncommutation components of the supplied primitive matrices. The clock projectors and adjacent units generate \(M_8\). Each qubit's two primitives generate \(M_2\). Within a joint factor basis, expand the off-diagonal clock blocks of the actual Hamiltonian in work Pauli strings. Group squared coefficients by support and include the clock in each nonzero support. The microscopic support graph must account for the writer, dimer phase, pulses, and actuator, with no edge between signal and actuator. Verify that the sum of support weights squared equals \(\operatorname{Tr}(H^2)/512\).

## Record, action and controls

For the undisturbed \(00\) state, persistence means \(r\ge 1-10^{-10}\) at every tick \(1\) through \(7\). It applies to one traversal of this finite clock. The writer places the source correlation into \(\tau_z=P\). The memory operators \(\tau_x=X_{M_2}\), \(\tau_y=Z_{M_1}Y_{M_2}\), and \(\tau_z\) form a logical qubit algebra. \(Z_{M_1}\) and \(X_{M_1}X_{M_2}\) generate its commuting complement on the two memory qubits. The phase readouts must show that the two-carrier state changes between ticks \(1\) and \(2\) while record parity persists. A memory-only trace-preserving channel with each Kraus operator commuting with \(P\) preserves the source-parity distribution. Test a nonselective \(Z_{M_1}\) read at conditional tick \(1\), continue with \(H\), and evaluate future supported ticks. The \(Z_{M_1}\) read removes the within-parity phase. Report memory entropy and signal-memory correlations, including the supplied blank. The result concerns reversible storage and a specified disturbance class.

Use the following controls with \(D_1D_2=00\).

| Control | Specification and acceptance |
|---|---|
| Writer removed | Replace \(U_0\) by \(I\). Source and parity agree with probability \(1/2\) throughout. |
| Informative | Use the primary model. At ticks \(6,7\) the action agrees with the signal with probability \(1\). |
| Scrambled parity basis | Use the same \(H\). Prepare \(U_0^\dagger H_\tau U_0|\psi_W\rangle\) at tick \(0\) where \(H_\tau=(\tau_x+\tau_z)/\sqrt2\). After writing, the \(Z_S,P\) outcomes are independent and \(\rho_S,\rho_{M_1M_2},\rho_A,\rho_{D_1D_2}\) match the informative case at tick \(5\). Check equal purity and total energy distributions. The full source-memory state retains correlations in a different basis. The same actuator gives agreement \(1/2\). |
| Fixed action | Replace \(U_5\) by \(I\). Keep the informative preparation and verify \(A=0\) with agreement \(1/2\). |

For the same-\(H\) pair, any prediction based solely on the separated pre-action marginals has worst-case absolute error at least \(1/4\) on the action agreement. A product reconstruction gives agreement \(1/2\) in both cases. The preparations differ in joint structure and are both pure. There is no scramble intervention during evolution. The removed-link controls are different, explicitly specified Hamiltonians.

## Effective description and withheld outcome

Before the action, use a block for each pair of source and action signs \(s,a\) and known disturbance program. It carries the probability weight \(w_{s,a}\) and the three unnormalized parity Bloch components \(v_{s,a}^i=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\Pi_{Z_S,s}\Pi_{Z_A,a}\tau_i)\). The writer rotates about \(x\) by \(\pi\) in the \(s=-1\) block. The pulse links rotate about \(x\) by the enabled angle. The parity phase rotates about \(z\) by \(\beta\). The \(Z_{M_1}\) hold is invisible to this algebra. The joint probabilities are \((w_{s,a}+p v^z_{s,a})/2\) for parity sign \(p\). At the action link relabel \(a\to pa\). These updates apply to arbitrary work density matrices within a known program sector for the declared readouts. A mixture of program sectors uses their conditional weights.

Propagate the eight sign projectors backward from every tick. The predicted real ranks for \(00,10,01,11\) are \(8,12,12,16\) including the identity, versus \(256\) Hermitian directions for four unconstrained work qubits at fixed program. Compare the reduced joint probabilities with the full solver using the primary pure state, the record controls, and a fixed-seed rank-three work state per program. The reduced solver must agree within \(10^{-10}\) total variation at each scored tick. This is sufficiency for the declared joint readout family.

The population-only comparator retains \(s,p,a\). It applies the stochastic flip probability \(q=\sin^2(\alpha/2)=1/4\) at each enabled pulse and discards the transverse parity components. It matches the registered \(00,10,01\) readouts of the informative preparation, including single-pulse final agreement \(3/4\). The value of the intermediate phase \(\beta\) is fixed in the Hamiltonian and is invisible to those calibration readouts.

**Withheld prediction.** Use \(11\) with the same informative preparation and both pulses enabled by the initial selectors. Read action agreement at tick \(7\). Immediately after writing the sign-corrected parity Bloch vector points along \(+z\). Successive \(x,z,x\) rotations yield
\[
\langle Z_S P\rangle_{\rm preact}=\cos^2\alpha-\sin^2\alpha\cos\beta=5/8,
\quad
a_{\rm coherent}=13/16,\qquad
a_{\rm population}=(1+\cos^2\alpha)/2=5/8.
\]
A full-state counterfactual that pinches parity at tick \(3\) by \(\rho\mapsto(\rho+P\rho P)/2\) must agree with the population-only prediction. It leaves the immediate registered distribution intact. The primary withheld comparison is rejected if either prediction misses its target by \(10^{-10}\) or more, the coherent-population separation falls below \(0.18\), or the calibration comparator differs by \(10^{-10}\) or more. A failed clock, record, or reduction check must be reported at its own scope. No refit of angles, states, or tolerances follows from these checks.

All quantitative checks use the card's tolerances. Freeze this specification and card before implementing or evaluating the model. Run the calibration and record it before opening \(11\). A complete construction includes the failures if any test misses. Agreement is a finite-model result within standard quantum mechanics.
