# Source credit and actual scope

The specified model, benchmark and review inputs are pinned in SOURCES.json
and ISSUE_CONTEXT.json. The finished low-scale review is f7d641a; the heavy
review's draft gate files are not represented as a completed critic verdict.
Our scalar and mode solvers are extensions of our own earlier implementations.
The relic calculation is explicitly a replay of the pinned reviewer code.

Graham, Mardon and Rajendran,
[Vector Dark Matter from Inflationary Fluctuations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.02102),
arXiv:1504.02102v1: abstract, section III A (equations 3-18), the inflationary
scalar-limit normalization and the abundance formula were read. The paper
supplies the standard Proca longitudinal action and inflationary mechanism;
its fixed-mass abundance fit is not our normalization or a validation of this
Higgs multiplet. The new actual-norm modes and early coupled common-mode
propagation are computed here. Our scalar thermodynamics, A0 elimination
and finite-object work identity are explicit derivations, not new fundamental
GRUT laws. No new observational likelihood is evaluated.
