W1-01: a continuum predictor with actual modes and a uniform error bound
======================================================================

Prospective scope
-----------------
This derivation precedes any W1 physical-source evaluation. The primary
level is fixed at1024 modes;16,64,256 are diagnostic levels only. N22,
L5,g1, the D1 native three-sine probes plus beta witness, six new response
times, and reference counts16384/32768 are fixed in the companion card.
No lattice evolution, history comparison, fitted frequency or adaptive
mode selection is part of W1. The result concerns the adopted massless
continuum model, not the justification of the lattice/continuum dictionary.

Exact spectral model
--------------------
On0<z<L, take Dirichlet modes e_n(z)=sqrt(2/L)sin(n pi z/L) and
omega_n=sqrt((n pi/L)^2+1/pi), with g=1 and Phi=phi/sqrt(pi). For a real
native cell source f_i, b_in=int f_i(z)e_n(z)dz. Its centered vacuum source
response is

  M_ij(t)=sum_{n>=1} b_in b_jn exp(-i omega_n t)/(2 pi omega_n).

Set a_n=b_n/sqrt(2 pi omega_n). Each a_n a_n^T is PSD. For M retained modes,

  M_M(t)=sum_{n<=M} a_n a_n^T exp(-i omega_n t), C_M=M_M(0),
  R_M(t)=C_M^(-1/2)M_M(t)C_M^(-1/2).

Thus R_M(0)=I and ||R_M(t)||<=1 for every real t. This uses the actual
continuum frequencies and their physical positive source weights. Unlike
the R1 three-source Galerkin predictor, it does not replace the spectral
distribution by three first-moment centroid frequencies. Source dimension
and retained dynamical mode count are different quantities.

The identity-valued apparatus edge terms and static vacuum mean cancel in
the centered response. They remain part of the physical source definition;
this cancellation does not authorize changing their controlled detector
phase in a nonlinear or interferometric experiment.

Uniform raw tail
----------------
Let Delta_M=C_infinity-C_M=sum_{n>M} a_n a_n^T>=0. Cauchy-Schwarz in the
discarded mode index gives, for arbitrary unit vectors x,y,

  |x^dagger(M_infinity(t)-M_M(t))y|
  <=sqrt(x^dagger Delta_M x)*sqrt(y^dagger Delta_M y)
  <=||Delta_M||.

Hence the raw operator error is bounded by ||Delta_M|| uniformly in t.
No bound on a second spectral moment or a short-time Taylor expansion is
used. For an individual entry the bound is sqrt(T_i T_j) if the omitted
variance of source i is bounded by T_i.

For a zero-extended real step source of total variation B_i, integration
by parts as a Stieltjes integral gives |b_in|<=sqrt(2/L) B_i/(n pi/L).
Using omega_n>=n pi/L and sum_{n>M}n^-3<=1/(2M^2),

  T_i <= B_i^2 L^2/(2 pi^4 M^2),
  E_M := sum_i T_i >=||Delta_M||.

The sum is over the primary three probes for its matrix bound. Scalar beta
tails are separately reported. Step sources can have divergent higher
spectral moments, which is irrelevant to this finite-variance tail proof.

Canonical normalization with a fixed positive covariance
---------------------------------------------------------
Assume C_M>0, m=lambda_min(C_M), q=lambda_max(C_M), and ||Delta_M||<=E.
The exact full normalized response is R_infinity=C_infinity^(-1/2)
M_infinity C_infinity^(-1/2). Since Delta_M>=0, both covariance lower bounds
are at least m. The resolvent integral for inverse square roots implies

  ||C_infinity^(-1/2)-C_M^(-1/2)|| <= E/(2 m^(3/2)).

For completeness, X^(-1/2)=(1/pi)int_0^infinity s^(-1/2)(X+sI)^(-1)ds;
the resolvent identity bounds the difference by
(E/pi)int_0^infinity s^(-1/2)/(m+s)^2 ds=E/(2m^(3/2)).

Since ||M_M(t)||<=q, add and subtract the two products with one changed
inverse square root. This proves

  ||R_infinity(t)-R_M(t)|| <= E/m + E q/m^2 =: B_M

for every real t. The equality at t0 is exact even though the constant
all-time envelope need not vanish there. Canonical whitening may rotate
with covariance perturbations; the condition-dependent second term covers
that change and is not silently replaced by an aligned-basis estimate.

For each scalar response r_i=M_ii/C_ii, the parallel bound is
|r_i,infinity-r_i,M|<=2T_i/C_ii,M. Every comparison to a finite reference
uses the sum of the predictor and reference tail bounds. The fine reference
is not identified with an exact infinite-mode response.

An a priori rational primary certificate at N22 and M1024
--------------------------------------------------------
This section does not evaluate any physical source coefficients or response
times. It bounds the entire primary family using analytic envelopes.

Let q_cells=N+1=23, a=L/q_cells, and g_i(z)=pi/(2L)sin(i pi z/L), i1,2,3.
The native step f_i equals the average of g_i on each interior cell and
zero on the two outer cells. Therefore f_i=P g_i for the orthogonal L2
projection onto interior-cell constants. The original g_i are orthogonal
and have common squared norm b0^2=pi^2/(8L).

The step total variation does not exceed the smooth zero-extended variation
2i*pi/(2L)=i*pi/L. By the mean value theorem for integrals, every cell
average is g_i(xi_j) for some point xi_j in that cell. These points are
ordered. Appending g_i(0)=g_i(L)=0 makes the step variation a polygonal
variation sum of the original function, bounded by its total variation.
Thus the three-source raw tail obeys

  E_M <= sum_{i=1}^3 (i*pi/L)^2 L^2/(2 pi^4 M^2)
       =7/(pi^2 M^2) <=7/(9M^2).

For the covariance upper bound, orthogonal projection is contractive:
the L2 Gram of f_i is <=b0^2 I. Since omega_n>=pi/L,

  C_infinity <= [b0^2/(2 pi*(pi/L))]I = (1/16)I.

For a covariance lower bound, retain just continuum modes1,2,3. Before
projection their coefficient matrix is b0 I. The interior-cell Poincare
bound and sin(x)<=x at the two edges give

  ||g_i-f_i||_2^2
    <= b0^2 i^2 [q_cells^-2 +4 pi^2/(3 q_cells^3)].

The first term uses the optimal interval zero-mean Poincare constant a/pi;
it also follows by expanding the cell deviation into its Neumann cosine
modes. The second follows from2 int_0^a[pi/(2L)]^2(i pi z/L)^2 dz.
By Bessel's inequality the Frobenius norm of the first-three coefficient
error is bounded by the combined L2 errors. Consequently its least
singular value is at least b0(1-r), where

  r^2=14[q_cells^-2+4pi^2/(3q_cells^3)].

At q_cells23, using pi<22/7 gives r^2<1/16, so r<1/4. All fixed retained
levels exceed3. For L5 and g1,

  b0^2>=9/40,
  omega_3=sqrt((3pi/5)^2+1/pi)
          <=3pi/5+1/sqrt(pi)<=66/35+3/5=87/35,
  2pi omega_3<=2*(22/7)*(87/35).

Hence every fixed C_M has the rigorous lower bound

  m_*=(9/40)*(9/16)/[2*(22/7)*(87/35)]>0,
  q_*=1/16.

The exact-rational envelope

  B_*(M)=[7/(9M^2)]*[1/m_*+q_*/m_*^2]

is therefore valid for the primary canonical response at every real time.
The companion exact Fraction calculation verifies B_*(1024)<1/1000. It
also verifies3<pi<22/7 with alternating rational bounds on Machin's formula
pi=16 atan(1/5)-4 atan(1/239). That formula follows from the tangent
addition formula and the principal-argument ranges, so no measured
constant or empirical fit enters the certificate.

What is proved, checked, and still missing
-----------------------------------------
The uniform bound proves an exact-arithmetic modal-truncation statement
for this specified continuum source model. The synthetic precheck tests
the spectrum, matrix normalization, discarded PSD-tail logic, and rational
certificate independently of physical production. The held-out time
comparisons and frozen R1 implementation comparison test the executable
calculation; agreement alone is not the proof. Floating-point quadratures,
matrix square roots and exponentials are not interval-certified by a
small residual. Their validation remains distinct from the exact-rational
analytic truncation theorem.

Success would construct a working continuum response predictor under the
adopted source dictionary. It would not improve or explain away any
R1 lattice-continuum discrepancy, change conditional backaction, validate
a Hamiltonian realization, prove the full physical dictionary or establish
GRUT empirically. Those remain separate milestones.
