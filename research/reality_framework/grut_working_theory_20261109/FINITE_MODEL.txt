W1: a finite, parameterized Construction 1 realization

Status: mathematical model and deterministic theoretical checks only. No hardware
data, fitted parameters, simulated shots or empirical validation. The model uses
ordinary finite-dimensional quantum mechanics; it is not an additional GRUT law.
It supplies a complete finite operational realization conditional on its stated
Hamiltonians, initial state, channels and readout. A real device must independently
qualify those assumptions and then face held-out tests.

1. Definitions and finite physical schedule

Four work qubits are ordered (S,M1,M2,A), with Z|b>=(-1)^b|b>. Write
P=Z_M1 Z_M2. The initial boundary state is |0000>. Initial reset is a preparation
assumption with an explicit error allowance; it is not derived from the unitary
model. Finite preparation pulses make |+>_S |Phi+>_M1M2 |0>_A.

Use eta in [0,1] for coherence survival. This avoids confusing it with the original
Construction 1 hold phase, historically denoted lambda and fixed to pi/2.

Every pulse below has strictly positive duration. In hbar=1 units, Hamiltonians
are angular frequencies (rad/ns). The illustrative durations are specifications
of an exact finite model, not claims about an available device:

  segment                       duration ns  Hamiltonian
  simultaneous H on S,M1             20     pi/(2t) [(X_S+Z_S)+(X_M1+Z_M1)]/sqrt2
  Bell CNOT(M1,M2)                   100     H_CNOT(M1,M2)
  writer CNOT(S,M2)                  100     H_CNOT(S,M2)
  historical hold                    20     phi Z_M1/(2t), phi=pi/2
  first disturbance                  40     theta1 X_M2/(2t)
  dephasing/identity slot             60     random finite I/P pulse, below
  parity phase                       80     beta P/(2t)
  second disturbance                 40     theta2 X_M2/(2t)
  actuator CNOT(M1,A)                100     H_CNOT(M1,A)
  actuator CNOT(M2,A)                100     H_CNOT(M2,A)

Total duration is 660 ns. A disabled disturbance uses theta=0 for the same 40 ns
slot. There is no instantaneous-pulse approximation. Hadamard phases are global
and disappear from the density matrix. Exactly,

  H_CNOT(c,targ)=pi/(2t) |1><1|_c (I-X_targ),
  exp(-i t H_CNOT)=CNOT(c,targ).

For a pulse with a known fixed axis, an arbitrary bounded envelope gives the same
rotation with area theta=integral Omega(t)dt because its Hamiltonians commute at
different times. The rectangular schedule above is one explicit choice. Unknown
detuning, axis error, overlap, crosstalk or additional dissipation is not absorbed
into theta or eta without derivation; it belongs in a separately bounded channel
remainder or in an explicitly enlarged dynamical model.

The dephasing slot is

  Lambda_eta(rho)=(1+eta)rho/2+(1-eta)P rho P/2.

It preserves both parity blocks and multiplies cross-parity blocks by eta. One
finite realization chooses H=0 with probability (1+eta)/2 and H=pi P/(2t) with
probability (1-eta)/2 for the same positive slot duration. The second unitary is
-iP, which implements P conjugation exactly. The random choice is independent of
the work state and is never used to select or correct primary outcomes. Thus even
eta=0 requires no infinite rate and no zero-duration operation.

Alternatively, a designated Markov dephasing slot obeying

  d rho/dt = Gamma/2 (P rho P-rho)

gives eta=exp(-Gamma t). Exact eta=0 is not attained by finite Gamma,t in that
particular semigroup. It is attained by the finite random-unitary realization.
Noise during the noncommuting X pulses is a different model.

A closed dilation can replace the external random choice: prepare an environment
qubit in sqrt((1+eta)/2)|0>+sqrt((1-eta)/2)|1>, apply controlled-P, and ignore E in
the work readout. A finite generator is pi/(2t)|1><1|_E(I-P). The total evolution
is unitary; only the reduced work state dephases. This adds an environment qubit
and does not claim that four work qubits form an isolated closed whole.

2. Exact observable prediction and reduced derivation

The memory logical Pauli operators are tau_x=X_M2, tau_y=Z_M1 Y_M2, tau_z=P.
For signal sign sigma=(-1)^s, the written memory has logical Bloch vector
(0,0,sigma). After the first X rotation it is

  (0,-sigma sin(theta1),sigma cos(theta1)).

Lambda_eta multiplies the first two coordinates by eta. The parity phase is a
logical Z rotation by beta. Its y component becomes
-sigma eta sin(theta1)cos(beta). The second X rotation therefore gives

  <P>_s = sigma [cos(theta1)cos(theta2)
                 -eta sin(theta1)sin(theta2)cos(beta)].

The two actuator CNOTs copy parity to the initially blank action bit. Hence

  p_agree(theta1,theta2,beta,eta)
    = [1+cos(theta1)cos(theta2)-eta sin(theta1)sin(theta2)cos(beta)]/2.

The fixed hold phase commutes with every relevant logical operation and the final
Z readout, so the formula is independent of it. The model retains its physical
pulse; it does not silently remove it.

For theta1=theta2=pi/3 and beta=2pi/3,

  p_agree = 5/8 + 3 eta/16.

Thus coherent eta=1 gives 13/16, complete parity dephasing gives 5/8, and the
contrast is 3/16. This is a continuous parameter family, not a single fitted point.

With the declared plus/Bell preparation, the full final computational distribution
has A=M1 xor M2. Each allowed bitstring has probability p_agree/4 when A=S and
(1-p_agree)/4 otherwise. The code checks this entire 16-outcome distribution.

Immediately after the dephasing slot, A=0 and

  Pr(S=s,parity=p,A=0) = (1/2)[1-q1 if p=s, q1 otherwise],
  q1=sin(theta1/2)^2.

All computational-basis populations are identical before and after Lambda_eta.
Equality of that immediate distribution does not determine the channel.

3. Restricted comparator and the identifiability counterexamples

Independent parity flips with q_i=sin(theta_i/2)^2 predict

  p_flip=(1-q1)(1-q2)+q1q2
        =[1+cos(theta1)cos(theta2)]/2.

This equals the eta=0 model for all beta. It is a memoryless population comparator,
not every classical model or a device-independent classical bound.

For all beta and eta, calibration programs 00,10,01 give

  p00=1, p10=[1+cos(theta1)]/2, p01=[1+cos(theta2)]/2.

They do not identify beta, eta or even the signed angle branch. At theta1=theta2
=pi/3, they always give (1,3/4,3/4), while coherent held-out program11 gives

  beta=0:1/4; beta=pi/2:5/8; beta=2pi/3:13/16; beta=pi:1.

At beta=2pi/3, eta=0,0.4,1 instead gives 5/8,0.7,13/16. Every one of these is an
explicit finite-pulse model with identical zero/one-program calibration and
immediate registered populations. Those calibrations cannot select the desired
held-out prediction. Independent phase/coherence calibration is necessary.

There is a stronger channel counterexample. Replacing P conjugation by Z_M2
conjugation gives the same final agreement formula and immediate populations in
this protocol, for every eta. Yet the correct parity channel preserves |Phi+>
within the even sector, while the local Z_M2 channel gives Bell XX expectation
eta rather than 1. Agreement curves alone therefore do not certify parity-sector
preservation. Local Z_M1 dephasing is another wrong channel: it leaves the target
coherent contrast intact instead of implementing the declared eta dependence.

4. Parameter calibration and confidence sets

Use separate preparations/readouts, not the held-out program11 outcomes, to fix:

  - theta_i: prepare the driven qubit in |0>; measure Z=cos(theta_i) and
    Y=-sin(theta_i). The two quadratures fix the signed angle modulo2pi.
  - beta: prepare M2 in |+>, fix M1=b; after the parity-phase pulse measure
    X=cos(beta), Y=(-1)^b sin(beta). Check both b values, which tests the coupling
    sign and conditional dependence rather than only one unconditional phase.
  - eta: prepare a cross-parity superposition, e.g. M1=0,M2=+; the declared slot
    gives X=eta,Y=0. Check Y and within-parity Bell coherence as well. These are
    model checks, not a complete process certificate for arbitrary hidden errors.

For K independently sampled +/-1 expectations, each with n trials, Hoeffding and
a union bound give simultaneous radius

  r=sqrt[2 log(2K/alpha)/n]

with family failure probability at most alpha. Bounded independent samples are an
assumption; undocumented drift or state-dependent selection is not repaired by
this formula. Readout corrections must themselves have calibrated uncertainty.

For angle quadratures with observed vector u=(xhat,yhat), confidence radii rx,ry,
let R=|u| and d=sqrt(rx^2+ry^2). The enclosing disk intersects the unit circle only
if |R-1|<=d. If compatible and R>0, an angular confidence radius is

  epsilon_angle=acos(clamp[(1+R^2-d^2)/(2R),-1,1]).

The central angle is atan2(yhat,xhat), with its branch fixed independently. If the
disk is inconsistent, reject this parameter model; if R=0, it identifies no angle.
The disk bound is conservative relative to the rectangular quadrature region.
For eta=X under the qualified real-dephasing model, intersect [Xhat-r,Xhat+r]
with [0,1]. A nonzero phase rotation or other channel changes the model rather
than automatically becoming a real eta.

For rectangular physical pulses theta=Omega t, independent interval bounds imply

  epsilon_theta <= |t_hat|epsilon_Omega + |Omega_hat|epsilon_t
                   +epsilon_Omega epsilon_t.

For the random-unitary slot eta=1-2q, epsilon_eta=2epsilon_q. For a Markov slot,
positive rate/time intervals map monotonically through eta=exp(-Gamma t).

5. A rigorous finite-model probability allowance

Let absolute parameter radii be e1,e2,eb,ee for theta1,theta2,beta,eta. Put
eta_max=min(1,eta_hat+ee) and B=|sin(theta1_hat)sin(theta2_hat)|. A telescoping
path, using |partial p/partial theta_i|<=1/2, gives

  |p_actual_parameters-p_nominal|
    <= (e1+e2+eta_max B eb+B|cos(beta_hat)|ee)/2.

The theta derivative bound follows from Cauchy-Schwarz and eta<=1, not from adding
two separately maximized sine terms. The beta step uses fixed nominal theta and
the actual eta; the eta step then uses nominal theta,beta.

Let preparation trace distance be <=epsilon_prep, final readout effect error in
operator norm <=epsilon_ro, and each residual channel have half-diamond distance
<=epsilon_j from the parameterized model. Contractivity and channel telescoping
then add epsilon_prep+epsilon_ro+sum_j epsilon_j to the above probability bound.
Do not count an angle error twice: epsilon_j describes only the unmodeled channel
remainder after the explicit parameter uncertainties have been removed.

For a residual Hamiltonian delta H(t), Duhamel gives the sufficient gate bound

  (1/2)||U_actual(.)U_actual^dagger-U_model(.)U_model^dagger||_diamond
       <= ||U_actual-U_model|| <= integral ||delta H(t)||dt.

Known fixed-axis pulse areas are exact; arbitrary drift requires this residual
budget or explicit evolution. The bound may be conservative. Preparation/readout
and full-channel certificates are additional empirical work. The listed simple
Pauli probes alone do not establish a diamond-norm certificate.

When coherent and pinched arms share calibrated theta1,theta2,beta, their contrast
is D=-(eta_C-eta_P)sin(theta1)sin(theta2)cos(beta)/2. A joint confidence box permits
a tighter bound than adding two independent probability allowances. Maximize the
absolute trigonometric factors over each angle interval and multiply them by the
corresponding partial derivatives; the code implements this interval Lipschitz
bound. Any differential drift goes in the sum of the two residual channel budgets.
No independence between parameter errors is needed once the joint box is valid.

An illustrative design in the output uses12 Pauli expectations with80000 trials
each (960000 structured-parameter calibration attempts). This is NOT acquired
data and is NOT a complete apparatus qualification within EX1's total cap. Its
separate residual preparation/readout/channel certificates are hypothetical.
No existing frozen hardware protocol or acquisition budget has been changed.

6. Implemented checks and honest scope

work/W1_finite_model.py evolves full16x16 density matrices via scipy matrix
exponentials of the finite Hamiltonians. The logical formula is evaluated only
afterward. The latest output is work/W1_finite_model_results_v2.json.

The prespecified product grid has500 cases, with theta1 in
{-0.71,0,0.43,pi/3,2.27}, theta2 in {-1.13,0,0.82,pi/3,2.91}, beta in
{-2.3,0,0.37,2pi/3,pi}, eta in {0,0.17,0.63,1}. Checks include complete final
populations, trace, positivity, Hermiticity, immediate-population invariance,
conditional phase calibration, and eta=0 agreement with the flip comparator.
Additional checks vary the historical hold phase; construct the indistinguishable
countermodels; reject the wrong local M1 dephaser; test16 uncertainty corners with
explicit preparation/readout noise; test32 shared-contrast parameter corners;
and evolve noncommuting pulse detunings under their residual-channel allowance.

Maximum agreement error was3.33e-16 and maximum full-population error3.05e-16.
The finite uncertainty-corner example had maximum probability shift0.0259683
inside its derived allowance0.0581217. These test the code and the specified model;
they are not physical observations or an empirical pass.

What now works mathematically is a finite-duration, bounded, predictive quantum
operational model with an explicit parameter dictionary and quantified allowance.
It is falsifiable after independent calibration because program11 was not used
to infer theta1,theta2,beta,eta. What remains open is an actual apparatus realizing
the qualified model and any new empirical law distinctive of GRUT. Neither the
finite construction nor its clock embedding establishes the Schwinger continuum
dictionary, a massive transfer, P1 closure, gravity or a universal theory.
