M1 physical milestone: prospective acceptance contract and EX1 interval audit
Prepared 9 November 2026. DRAFT SUCCESSOR CONTRACT; NOT A HARDWARE AUTHORIZATION.

This note reviews work/R1-delivery/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md without modifying it.
No device, apparatus calibration, experimental dataset or booking is supplied.
The proposed rules must be versioned and frozen before any new calibration.
They cannot be attributed retrospectively to EX1-01, R1, D1, B2 or N22.

1. TWO REQUIRED DELIVERABLES, WITH SEPARATE VERDICTS

M1-P: one calibrated physical realization of the four-qubit Construction 1
work protocol, with prospective predictions for raw held-out outcomes and an
explicit, supported uncertainty budget. The quantum and population comparator
must use the same physical readout convention and independently calibrated
nuisance information. Outcomes must include attempted-shot erasures.

M1-C: a separately justified continuum dictionary/boundary statement for the
Schwinger detector experiment. It must identify the source, normalization,
offset, source interpolation, endpoint placement, prepared/reached state class,
finite switching and regulator-dependent corrections, then delimit which are
proved, numerically supported or still assumed. Four-qubit hardware cannot
supply this field-theory result. Conversely, a continuum numerical match is not
a laboratory observation. Joint milestone status is the conjunction of the
two declared deliverables; report each independently.

M1-P success supports a calibrated, finite, externally scheduled operational
realization under standard quantum mechanics, on the named device/configuration
and acquired schedule. It does not establish the autonomous 512-dimensional
clock model, a novel GRUT law, microscopic access/state selection, a general
continuum theorem or GR/Standard Model recovery. A successful event prediction
does not identify every internal channel uniquely.

2. WHAT MUST BE FROZEN BEFORE CALIBRATION

Freeze hardware identity/configuration; four-qubit assignment; initialization;
compiled gates including phase/sign conventions, routing, finite durations and
matched idles; parity-dephasing implementation; all readout basis rotations;
the complete attempt/erasure definition; circuit and shot allocation; schedule
randomization; drift model/sentinels; stopping and failure rules; analysis code;
admissible model class and constraints; all confidence constructions; numerical
enclosure method; error-accounting ledger; comparator; calibration exclusions.
Hash immutable artifacts and record timestamps. Empty template fields block this
stage. A schema validator cannot certify the truth of an apparatus assumption.

Retain the EX1 resource profile unless explicitly replaced before data:
1,000,000 attempted calibration/characterization/sentinel shots maximum, followed
by 20,000 C11, 20,000 P11, 50,000 I_C and 50,000 I_P held-out attempts. No optional
stopping, unseen postselection, outcome-based rerouting or threshold rescue.

Define the model class operationally: preparation(s), finite-duration gate/idle
channels, dephaser and physical output POVM, including leakage/erasure. A 16x17
classical response matrix is valid only with a justified diagonal-in-Z readout
assumption or a separately bounded departure. It does not represent an arbitrary
four-qubit POVM. State-preparation errors and SPAM identifiability must be carried
through; computational-basis preparation labels are not certified input states.

The four-qubit pulse and phase model needs phase-sensitive calibration independent
of C11/P11. Zero/one-pulse event frequencies alone do not determine beta. Require
independent parity-phase and Rx-axis/sign probes, within-parity coherence checks
of the dephaser, leakage, crosstalk, composition and drift coverage. A fitted local
depolarizing channel or one Rabi fringe is insufficient evidence for a universal
channel-distance or pulse-error bound.

3. A CONCRETE FINITE-SAMPLE CALIBRATION COVERAGE CONSTRUCTION

Retain alpha_control=0.0025 for the five EX1 baseline control intervals,
alpha_parameter=0.0025 for all other characterization/stability objects together,
and alpha_validation=0.005. Total family failure allocation is at most 0.01.

One conservative construction available now is calibration-probability inversion:
predeclare J characterization circuits, n_j attempts for each, K_j exhaustive
categories including erasure, and M=sum_j K_j. Under the frozen independent-shot
observation model, let f_jb be each empirical frequency and define

  h_j = sqrt(log(2*M/alpha_parameter)/(2*n_j)).

Intersect the admissible physical model class Theta_0 with ALL constraints

  |q_jb(theta)-f_jb| <= h_j + eta_jb_cal,

where eta_jb_cal is an independently supported calibration-model remainder fixed
before observations, zero only when justified. The same construction may include
preallocated sentinel observations; otherwise reserve their alpha separately.
Coverage follows by Hoeffding for each bounded category indicator and a union
bound. Dependencies among category frequencies do not invalidate that union
bound; dependence across shots would require a different predeclared argument.
For time-dependent devices, define which scheduled-average probabilities each
q_jb represents and justify transfer to the validation schedule using the frozen
drift envelope. Interleaving alone is not such a justification.

This gives a joint confidence set Theta(D) with declared frequentist coverage
CONDITIONAL ON the observation and physical-model/remainder assumptions. It does
not prove the physical model class from finite data. Its identifiability test is
whether the desired prediction projections are narrow, not whether a local
optimizer found a visually convincing best fit. Empty Theta rejects the model
class/qualification. Unbounded or broad predictions leave it unresolved. No
relaxation of constraints or model-class addition after looking at the holdout.

Construct certified outer bounds on predictions over the entire Theta(D):
L_a <= inf_theta p_a(theta), U_a >= sup_theta p_a(theta), for a=C11,P11.
A local optimum, posterior percentile or sampling cloud is not automatically an
outer bound. Use analytic inequalities, validated interval methods or another
documented enclosure; include numerical slack explicitly. Compute the paired
contrast and immediate-distribution TV over a COMMON theta rather than fitting
each arm separately. If the declared calibration cap cannot bound these
quantities tightly enough, qualification remains unresolved. This is an honest
possible outcome; the protocol does not guarantee feasibility on an unknown
device.

An alternative inference method is allowed only if its joint coverage and
assumptions are specified and justified before calibration. Confidence coverage,
model adequacy and computational enclosure are three different obligations.

4. EXACT DEFINITIONS PREVENT DOUBLE COUNTING

For each primary arm a, define:
  p0_C=13/16, p0_P=5/8: ideal reference values, not fitted to data.
  J_a=[L_a,U_a]: outer projection of the calibrated forward model, including only
                uncertainty represented inside Theta(D).
  b_a: summed probability-error bounds for effects NOT represented in Theta(D).
  B_a=[max(0,L_a-b_a), min(1,U_a+b_a)]: complete physical prediction band.
  c_a=(L_a+U_a)/2: the fixed midpoint prediction, computed before validation.
  u_a=(U_a-L_a)/2+b_a: its conservative physical uncertainty.

For every effect (preparation, readout, pulse duration, crosstalk, leakage,
dephaser imbalance, drift, approximation and numerical enclosure), the ledger
must specify either 'inside Theta' OR 'external residual', its observable/state
scope, units, evidence and statistical failure allocation. Do not add the same
error twice. In particular, finite-duration pulse uncertainty represented by
the forward channel is not also a full external switching allowance. A certified
outer optimization already containing numerical slack must not acquire that
same slack again. Shared physical effects can enter multiple predictions; the
ban is on double-counting one effect within the same prediction budget.

For immediate distributions, use Q_IC(theta), Q_IP(theta) and per-arm external
TV bounds b_IC,b_IP. A sufficient comparison bound is

  D_I = sup_theta TV(Q_IC(theta),Q_IP(theta)) + b_IC + b_IP.

If a tighter direct joint bound is derived, record it instead of adding per-arm
bounds again. Clarify that a total immediate residual budget 0.01 means
b_IC+b_IP<=0.01, not an unspecified 0.01 independently added at each stage.

Use the original EX1 qualification limits as explicit checks, not as power
guarantees: width(J_a)<=0.04; b_a<=0.01; B_a inside p0_a+/-0.04; D_I<=0.02.
The template records these for continuity. Any tightening, added gate or change
of confidence method is a new prospective contract version.

5. AUDIT OF THE OLD INTERVAL GATE

EX1-01 states max(|phat-L|,|phat-U|)+r+b<=0.06. If L,U are the UNEXPANDED
model interval and b has not been included, this is a sufficient condition for
agreement within 0.06 with EVERY allowed expanded physical prediction. It is
mathematically valid but stronger than ordinary point-prediction equivalence.
If L,U already include b, adding b again is double counting. Clipping B to [0,1]
can make the old expression additionally conservative near a boundary.

The phrase 'model discrepancy is excluded' is too broad: one coarse event gate
cannot validate all microscopic channels or even prove the calibrated prediction
band contains the true event probability. Example: phat=0.750, r=0.001 and
B=[0.770,0.780]. All values are within 0.06 of the observation, so uniform coarse
equivalence passes; the observational CI [0.749,0.751] is nevertheless disjoint
from B and contradicts that tighter predictive budget.

Qualification also does not guarantee the old gate has uniform statistical
headroom. A band of width 0.06 can pass qualification, but a true probability
near its endpoint gives an old uniform metric near 0.06+r. Qualification leaves
room near the center, not for every member of the band. This is a power issue,
not failure of confidence coverage.

6. RECOMMENDED SUCCESSOR DECISION, FROZEN BEFORE DATA

Keep the four original RAW validation gates as separate ideal-realization tests:
  |phat_C-13/16|+r<=0.06;
  |phat_P-5/8|+r<=0.06;
  phat_C-phat_P-2r>=0.10;
  TV(f_IC,f_IP)+2r_TV<=0.05.

Here r=0.013581015157406196 at n=20,000 and alpha_object=0.00125;
r_TV=0.011667120310490607 at n=50,000, K=9 and the same alpha_object.
The four confidence objects collectively use alpha_validation=0.005.
Reuse of their confidence sets in additional gates adds no union penalty.
All empirical frequencies use attempted shots; erasure never disappears through
postselection or readout inversion.

Clarify the model sentence in a SUCCESSOR VERSION by three explicit decisions,
all mandatory for the claimed physical predictive milestone. Retain EX1's strong
EVERY-admissible-prediction gate; there is no need to weaken it to a midpoint test.

  (P1) Robust uniform event-prediction equivalence, for both arms:
       |phat_a-c_a| + (U_a-L_a)/2 + r + b_a <= 0.06.
       This is exactly the original endpoint formula for the UNEXPANDED model
       interval. It certifies the actual scheduled event probability is within
       0.06 of EVERY admitted expanded physical prediction on the validation
       coverage event. SPAM/other parameter uncertainty already in [L,U] enters
       only through its halfwidth; external residuals enter only once through b.
       Numerical error must be either enclosed in [L,U] or included in b, not both.
       Keeping the unclipped formula is a conservative prospective convention.

  (P2) Calibrated-budget non-contradiction: the observational confidence interval
       O_a=[max(0,phat_a-r),min(1,phat_a+r)] must intersect B_a, for both arms.
       Disjointness rejects the registered prediction budget at joint declared
       coverage. Overlap is non-contradiction, not proof of the model.

  (P3) Common-model consistency: exhibit or certify existence of one admissible
       theta whose external-residual-expanded predictions intersect ALL four
       validation confidence objects simultaneously. Coordinatewise overlap can
       mix incompatible theta values; it is necessary but not sufficient.
       The immediate confidence objects are TV balls around f_IC,f_IP. If joint
       feasibility cannot be established, verdict unresolved; a verified witness
       is enough for feasibility, whereas exclusion requires a certified global
       argument. This check is not a fit that updates predictions or Theta.

For clarity only, |phat-c|+r<=0.06 would be a weaker MIDPOINT-ONLY variant: it
certifies accuracy relative to one frozen point, not every admissible prediction.
It is not recommended here and does not replace P1. Adopting it would require a
separately labeled prospective change and a corresponding narrower claim.

Before validation, publish a power/headroom calculation over the calibrated
prediction set. Simple conservative sufficient conditions for acceptance whenever
the validation confidence event holds are:
  robust uniform gate: (U_a-L_a)+2*b_a+2r<=0.06;
  ideal-reference gate: sup_{q in B_a}|q-p0_a|+2r<=0.06;
  contrast gate: calibrated lower contrast -4r>=0.10;
  immediate gate: D_I+4r_TV<=0.05.
These are sufficient worst-case conditions, not necessary power criteria and not
new empirical evidence. They show exactly where EX1's original qualification can
be weak: .04+2r=.06716 exceeds .06, and .02+4r_TV=.06667 exceeds .05. A proper
predata power calculation may instead use the frozen categorical prediction
sets. Disclose inadequate power before acquisition; revise resources only in a
new predata version. Do not label all calibration-qualified configurations likely
to pass by construction.

Population-only comparator rejection is a SEPARATE discrimination statement.
Use its frozen physical prediction set, including the same nuisance conventions
and one-counted residuals. Reject it only if it has no admissible member consistent
with the validation confidence objects (a disjoint event interval suffices).
Otherwise record 'comparator not discriminated'. A contrast alone is not a
universal rejection of classical descriptions. If discriminator success is made
part of the milestone, specify that conjunction now; it cannot be appended after
seeing a favorable outcome.

Overall M1-P requires valid adapter/coverage, independent calibration qualification,
completed held-out collection, supported sampling assumptions, stable frozen
drift envelope, four raw gates and P1-P3. An overall pass does not follow merely
from a file validator, numerical solver, plausible best fit or statistical scorer.
Distinguish empirical failure at the declared accuracy from incomplete collection,
unsupported model adequacy, undecidable numerical feasibility and missing input.

7. EVIDENCE AND ACCEPTANCE RECORD

Pre-calibration record: adapter identity, circuit/schedule manifests, model class,
coverage proof, calibration allocation, ledger, comparator and code hashes.
Post-calibration/pre-validation record: immutable calibration data, model-confidence
set, certified prediction projections, c/u/B values, joint prediction geometry,
qualification decisions, power analysis, prediction-freeze hash and timestamp.
Post-validation record: raw attempted-shot logs/settings/timestamps/erasures,
confidence objects and all gates, model-feasibility witness or exclusion proof,
sentinels, failures/aborts, unchanged freeze hashes, and an exact scoped claim.

Suggested success wording:
'On device [ID/configuration], the frozen calibrated four-qubit work protocol
predicted the held-out raw event probabilities within [reported bounds]. All
registered calibration, validation and model-consistency conditions passed under
[explicit assumptions]. This supports this finite operational GRUT realization
under standard quantum mechanics. The continuum dictionary verdict is [separate
M1-C status].'
