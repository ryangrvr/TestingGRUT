W1: a closed, solvable operational core for GRUT
Source audit at commit 4300c1836c4967a268fe3b028fa190e0f9886153
Prepared 9 November 2026. No repository edits, new physical data or production run.

The repository already contains enough structure for a working finite theory of
writing a record, retaining its relevant coherence and using it to control an
action. Its positive mathematical content can be presented as one explicit
closed parent and an exact effective description, rather than another checklist.
The theory is conditional quantum dynamics with specified operational access.
It is not a new fundamental law selecting the dynamics or physical constants.

1. THE PHYSICAL PRIMITIVE AND THE LAW

The primitive is not 'information' alone. It is an operational quantum tuple

  G = (A, omega, H, I_access),
  I_access = ({A_i}, {K_R}, {M_R}).

A is the observable algebra; omega is a positive normalized preparation; H is
the Hamiltonian; A_i identify operational sites; K_R specify permitted couplings
on regions R; M_R specify permitted instruments. A region or subsystem has
physical meaning only with these specified operations and readouts. H and a
stationary state do not themselves select the concrete operational factors.

For the finite model, adopt the Born rule and unitary evolution

  rho(t) = exp(-iHt) rho(0) exp(iHt),  hbar=1.

An allowed instrument has Kraus operators K_xb satisfying
sum_xb K_xb^dagger K_xb = I. Its state branch is
I_x(rho)=sum_b K_xb rho K_xb^dagger. For specified successive times,

  p(x_1,...,x_n)
   = Tr[I_xn o U_dt_n o ... o I_x1 o U_t1(rho_0)].

Conditioning divides a branch by its positive trace. Ignoring a measurement
outcome retains the nonselective channel; it is different from omitting the
operation. Thus state, dynamics, disturbance and readout form one probability
law. This is operationally and mathematically closed once the tuple is given.

2. ONE FULLY SPECIFIED AUTONOMOUS PARENT

Space and observables:
  H_total = C^8_C tensor C^2_S tensor C^2_M1 tensor C^2_M2
            tensor C^2_A tensor C^2_D1 tensor C^2_D2;
  A = B(H_total), dimension(H_total)=512.

C is an eight-level clock, S the source/signal, M1,M2 the memory carriers,
A the actuator, and D1,D2 conserved selector registers. Here 'source' means the
physical signal register S and its writer coupling. It is not the continuum
electric-field source of the separate Schwinger study.

Use Z|b>=(-1)^b|b>, P=Z_M1 Z_M2 and Pi_F,+/-=(I+/-F)/2.
Define Rx(theta)=exp(-i theta X_M2/2). The seven work links are

  U0 = Pi_ZS,+ + Pi_ZS,- X_M2;                    writer
  U1 = exp(-i lambda Z_M1/2);                     within-parity hold
  U2 = Pi_ZD1,+ + Pi_ZD1,- Rx(theta);             first disturbance
  U3 = exp(-i beta P/2);                          parity phase
  U4 = Pi_ZD2,+ + Pi_ZD2,- Rx(theta);             second disturbance
  U5 = Pi_P,+ + Pi_P,- X_A;                       actuator
  U6 = I.                                       final clock link

The time-independent Hamiltonian is

  H = sum_{k=0}^6 g_k (|k+1><k| tensor U_k
                       + |k><k+1| tensor U_k^dagger),
  g_k = (Omega/2) sqrt((k+1)(7-k)).

The instance fixes Omega=1 in replay units, lambda=pi/2, theta=pi/3,
beta=2pi/3. All links are present throughout the unmeasured evolution. No
externally switched pulse schedule is required in this specified parent.

Preparation, for selector program d=(d1,d2):

  |Psi_d(0)> = |0>_C |+>_S |Phi+>_M1M2 |0>_A |d1 d2>_D,
  |Phi+>=(|00>+|11>)/sqrt(2),  rho_d=|Psi_d><Psi_d|.

The state supplies clock localization, a coherent signal, the Bell blank and
ready actuator/selectors. These resources and their orientation are inputs.
The clean program is d=00; d=10,01,11 change only the initial selectors.

Access is explicit: X,Z on each qubit, clock projectors Q_k and adjacent
Hermitian clock units, and the specified link couplings on their supports.
Elementary diagnostic instruments are the spectral Luders instruments for
these operators, K=sum_k kQ_k, P, X_M1 X_M2 and Y_M1 X_M2. Include joint readout
of the commuting K,Z_S,P,Z_A. The P instrument has only Pi_P,+ and Pi_P,-,
not projectors onto separate M1,M2 outcomes.

The registered work effect at a supported clock tick is

  E_s,p,a = Pi_ZS,s Pi_P,p Pi_ZA,a,
  p(s,p,a | k) = Tr[(Q_k tensor E_s,p,a)rho(t)]/Tr[Q_k rho(t)].

The primary response is action agreement Pr(a=s). Memory agreement is Pr(p=s).
The parity-dephasing diagnostic is D_P(rho)=(rho+P rho P)/2. This changes later
predictions without changing the immediate Z_S,P,Z_A distribution.

The writer and actuator are autonomous links of the 512-dimensional parent.
The diagnostic measurements are explicitly inserted instruments, not additional
terms silently included in that H. A completely unitary account of a diagnostic
requires an enlarged parent with a ready detector and specified coupling. The
finite instrument admits a unitary dilation mathematically; that does not make
the detector a physically calibrated part of the existing 512-dimensional H.

3. WHY THE PARENT CAN BE SOLVED, NOT JUST NAMED

Set V0=I, V_{k+1}=U_k V_k, T=sum_k Q_k tensor V_k and H_C=Omega J_x for spin
7/2. Direct block multiplication gives

  H = T(H_C tensor I)T^dagger.

For a tick-zero preparation with any work density rho_W,

  rho_jk(t)=c_j(t)c_k(t)^* V_j rho_W V_k^dagger,
  c_k(t)=<k|exp(-itH_C)|0>.

Consequently the supported tick-k work state is exactly V_k rho_W V_k^dagger.
There is no fitted clock-to-work dictionary in this finite identity.
The clock probabilities are binomial:

  Pr(k)=C(7,k) p^k(1-p)^(7-k),  p=sin^2(Omega t/2).

At t=pi/Omega the final tick has probability one in the ideal model. A clock
position measurement with its outcome ignored at the midpoint reduces the
final-tick probability to C(14,7)/4^7 = 0.20947265625. This is an explicit
backaction prediction, not an assumption that an internal clock is perfect.
The clock is cyclic; rho returns after 2pi/Omega. No irreversible eternal
record or universal arrow of time is derived.

Unmeasured total evolution is unitary, so the total density spectrum, entropy
and total energy distribution are conserved. Subsystem entropies and energies
can change. The prescribed energy eigenvalues are Omega(m-7/2), m=0,...,7,
with degeneracy from the work space. These consequences follow from the given
H, rather than supplying an independently selected Hamiltonian.

4. AN EXACT SMALL LOGICAL CORE

This is an explicit simplification derived from the repository's own memory
operators. Change the memory basis from (m1,m2) to (g,p)=(m1,m1 xor m2).
Then

  tau_x=X_M2, tau_y=Z_M1 Y_M2, tau_z=P

act as a Pauli algebra on parity p, while Z_M1 and X_M1 X_M2 generate its
commuting complement g. The Bell blank becomes |+>_g |0>_p. The writer and
actuator are CNOT(S,p) and CNOT(p,A); disturbances are Rx on p; the parity phase
is Rz on p; the lambda hold acts only on g.

For a fixed selector program and a clock-conditioned work protocol, tracing g
therefore yields an EXACT THREE-QUBIT WORK THEORY on S,p,A:

  rho_logical,0 = |+><+|_S tensor |0><0|_p tensor |0><0|_A;
  U_logical sequence = CNOT(S,p), I, Rx_p(d1*theta), Rz_p(beta),
                       Rx_p(d2*theta), CNOT(p,A), I;
  instruments = declared logical operations, including the parity Z instrument;
  readouts = Z_S,Z_p,Z_A, with the ordinary Born rule.

More generally rho_logical=Tr_g(rho_W) evolves by the same logical unitary
sequence for arbitrary work preparations, because each work link factors into
a logical operation and an operation on g. This is an exact closed prediction
law for these logical operations; it has no approximation parameter. It is a
direct algebraic reduction here, not a new numerical production result.

Crucial scope: this three-qubit reduction does not reproduce arbitrary clock
coherences or measurements of the discarded complementary phase. The full
autonomous clock Hamiltonian contains the g-dependent hold link; tracing g out
of its unconditioned clock state need not give autonomous reduced dynamics.
The full 512-dimensional parent remains the closed model for that larger
instrument family. Replacing the clock by scheduled laboratory gates implements
the work theory and does not establish the autonomous clock claim.

For ONLY the registered Z_S,P,Z_A readouts, an even smaller exact effective
state before the actuator is the four blocks

  w_sa=Tr(rho Pi_ZS,s Pi_ZA,a),
  v_sa^j=Tr(rho Pi_ZS,s Pi_ZA,a tau_j), j=x,y,z.

They obey w_sa>=0, |v_sa|<=w_sa, sum_sa w_sa=1. The writer rotates v by pi
about x in the s=-1 blocks. Enabled disturbances rotate about x by theta,
the parity phase about z by beta, and the hold leaves these variables unchanged.
At any pre-actuator tick,

  Pr(s,p,a) = (w_sa+p v_sa^z)/2.

The actuator maps the action label a_old to a_new=p*a_old. This terminal rule
gives every later registered probability, because the remaining link is I.
Do not extrapolate this terminal classical relabeling to arbitrary subsequent
quantum operations: new readouts/interventions can require additional retained
operators. For the fixed two-disturbance readout family, 15 independent linear
expectations after normalization suffice; the backward-effect rank is 16.

This gives the most useful finite meaning of 'emergence': the memory relevant
to the actuator is the logical parity and the coherence components that its
future couplings can turn into observable differences, rather than every degree
of freedom in the carriers.

5. THE MODEL'S MAIN PREDICTION IN CLOSED FORM

After writing, the sign-corrected parity Bloch vector points along +z. The
successive x,z,x rotations give, allowing separate fixed pulse angles,

  z_final = cos(theta1)cos(theta2)
            - sin(theta1)sin(theta2)cos(beta),
  Pr(a=s) = (1+z_final)/2.

For theta1=theta2=pi/3 and beta=2pi/3,
  z_final=5/8,  Pr(a=s)=13/16.

Parity dephasing after the first disturbance removes the transverse vector:
  z_final,dephased=cos(theta1)cos(theta2)=1/4,
  Pr(a=s | dephased)=5/8.

The coherent contribution to action probability is therefore 3/16. Zero-pulse
and either single-pulse programs give 1 and 3/4, respectively. The independent
population-flip comparator q=sin^2(theta/2)=1/4 reproduces those calibrations
but predicts 5/8 with two pulses. It misses a particular sufficient-state
requirement, not a new conservation law. The general difference is

  a_coherent-a_dephased = -(1/2)sin(theta1)sin(theta2)cos(beta).

The beta dependence is an additional family of calculable predictions, not a
new parameter inferred from the one displayed two-pulse outcome. The archived
numerical checks in the source are historical evidence for the implementation;
this audit does not rerun them or turn them into laboratory observations.

The common-parent principle prevents contradictory reductions once one parent,

state's readiness, time orientation and operational architecture remain physical

7. PARAMETER MEANING AND MEASURABILITY

Omega sets the physical time/energy unit. In the abstract replay Omega=1 is a
unit choice, not a measurement of a cosmic constant. An implementation could
calibrate its clock probabilities/spectrum against physical timing/energy units.

theta is a rotation angle. A single-pulse parity population gives cos(theta),
but not by itself the axis/sign or coherent error. Independent phase-sensitive
operation characterization supplies those identifications.

beta is a relative parity phase. It is invisible to the declared zero/single-
pulse population calibration. Its value must be independently specified and
calibrated if the two-pulse response is to be a prediction rather than a fit.

lambda rotates the complementary memory phase. It is invisible to the logical
parity/action readout family, but is observable through X_M1 X_M2 and
Y_M1 X_M2. Do not claim its measurement from the action probabilities.

The link strengths g_k and their sqrt((k+1)(7-k)) ratios specify a chosen clock
implementation. Source/readout axes, qubit labels, preparation fidelity and
instrument disturbance are apparatus identifications, not universal constants.
Hamiltonians with different work links here are unitarily equivalent and have
the same total spectrum; spectroscopy alone cannot identify all work gates.
This shows why specifying only 'the Hamiltonian spectrum' would not close the
physical theory.

The values pi/2, pi/3 and 2pi/3 are designed, falsifiable experiment settings.
They are not derived coupling constants of nature. All quantities appearing
in the finite predictions have an operational meaning in the stipulated access
model; whether a real apparatus realizes them is a separate empirical fact.

8. THE HONEST WORKING-THEORY STATEMENT

'GRUT has a closed finite operational candidate: a specified quantum parent
supports a source, internal clock, parity record and actuator, and an exact
coherence-aware effective state predicts its registered outcomes and intervention
responses. In this domain the equations are executable and give nontrivial
quantitative predictions, including 13/16 versus 5/8 in the two-disturbance test.'

This is stronger than a slogan or an uninstantiated framework. It is weaker than
a completed physical theory of the universe. The new empirical restriction
needed to distinguish GRUT from standard quantum mechanics is absent: this
candidate accepts the standard quantum law and supplies its model/access data.
An additional rule selecting a restricted family of H, omega or I_access, or
changing their dynamics/probabilities, would be a different candidate and must
make its own independent quantitative predictions. No such rule is invented by
this audit.

The separately adopted semiclassical-GR/Standard-Model action is a target, not
a derived extension of this finite clock/record model. The Schwinger source
dictionary and boundary analysis address another stated approximation problem.
Neither can be supplied merely by relabeling S or the parity memory as spacetime
or a continuum field.
