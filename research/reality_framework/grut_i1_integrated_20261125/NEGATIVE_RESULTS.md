|---|---|

| Unscheduled local formation of a durable record | The uniform closed XY ring disperses an injected defect rather than retaining it in a fixed local window at large N. The source bit remains in a global conservation law. This setup does not show formation of a new durable record or an adaptive organization law. |

| Thermodynamic or laboratory closure | The collision order and clean cells are supplied. Fixed-rate refinement needs more cells, and has no unlimited finite-capacity realization here. The chosen pilot does not have resolution for the thermal gain, and no hardware test was performed. |
