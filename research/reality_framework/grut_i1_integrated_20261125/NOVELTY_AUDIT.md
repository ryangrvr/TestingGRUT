# Novelty audit and falsification targets

The exploratory scientific objective is a quantitative result
that cannot be recovered by the resource-matched standard theory.
Every successful I1 test below has a conventional derivation and
no numerical departure from it. The audit compares primary literature and
task definitions. It is not a claim about publication priority of
every special-case equation in the archive.

## Closest established comparators

| Question | Conventional comparison and consequence |
|---|---|
| Does an exposed record improve an optimal task? | Value of information and statistical comparisons optimize task payoffs over a specified experiment. Quantum sufficiency uses simulation of an experiment. A complete comparison can require ancillary systems. Binary equal-prior discrimination gives the trace-norm expression, and conditional min-entropy has the operational guessing interpretation for classical labels. Here $R_T$ is one task-value difference. [Buscemi](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.3794) · [König, Renner and Schaffner](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.1338) |
| Is access a useful resource? | Quantum resource theories relate restricted operations to performance gaps in discrimination. Quantum combs formalize causally ordered multi-slot operations. These frameworks explain monotonicity, allowed policy composition and the boundary dependence of $R_T$. They do not equate one score difference with a new conserved resource. [Takagi and Regula](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.08127) · [Chiribella et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.4483) |
| Can an estimate of a noise source enhance a quantum channel? | Environment-assisted recovery and optimal phase feedback apply. D7's Bell fidelity is a standard channel score. The I1 trace-norm formula extends the explicit calculation to common population damping and uses only standard Stinespring optimization and polar decomposition. It agrees with direct chronological quantum propagation. [Schumacher](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9604023) · [Gregoratti and Werner](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0403092) |
| Do repeated finite interactions justify effective noise? | Collision limits give continuous CP evolution under stipulated fresh, scaled contacts. Random telegraph phase averaging is a standard decoherence comparator. Hot–cold swaps give familiar excitation transfer and ordinary heat conduction. Reuse must retain correlations. [Attal and Pautrat](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0311002) · [Galperin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.74.024509) · [Barsky-Giles et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18300) |
| Does energy/entropy accounting impose a new general memory law? | Gibbs contacts obey exact entropy identities in terms of correlations and disturbances of the bath. Landauer bounds depend on the process and bath. Bounds on dissipation from nonpredictive memory adopt dynamical assumptions. I1's $R_T$ is not a direct entropy balance. Zero bare net work in a closed switching episode with prepared resources does not imply free preparation or disposal. [Esposito et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.1125) · [Reeb and Wolf](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.4352) · [Still et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.3271) |
| Does protection select a causal cone or spacetime? | Lieb–Robinson propagation estimates assume an interaction metric and decay or range bounds. Specific holographic quantum error-correcting codes reconstruct operators within supplied boundary/bulk constructions. They do not give a general inverse theorem from successful repair or a score to spatial dimension. The supplied ring and non-geometric controls are explicit counterchecks. [Lieb and Robinson](https://www-thphys.physics.ox.ac.uk/talks/CMTjournalclub/sources/LR.pdf) · [Pastawski et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.06237) |

## Future conditioning and closure

The fundamental test uses ordinary forward quantum probabilities.
An offline tilted trajectory ensemble is a statistic on histories.
A driven or Doob-transformed process can realize a conditioned
law under mathematical assumptions. Its implementability, Markov
state space and locality are additional questions. Conditioning
and no-signalling must be checked at the physical instrument, and
generic future-score reweighting supplies no justified fundamental
dynamics here. Finite closed recurrence limits sustained
irreversibility under applicable hypotheses and does not destroy a
stationary memory. [Chetrite and Touchette](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.5157)

I1 leaves the Gemini roadmap's broad hypotheses as hypotheses.
A nonzero phase-recovery gain or device implementation is a
test of a specified effective process. Neither supplies a
GRUT-specific signal with the present comparator.
