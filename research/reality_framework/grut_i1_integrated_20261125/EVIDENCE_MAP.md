**Evidence classes used here**

- **Adopted law** means an external physical premise used to calculate.
- **Proven** means an exact mathematical result for its stated inputs.
- **Conditional theorem** specifies an architecture, preparation,
  access class or regime before drawing a conclusion.
- **Numerical evidence** concerns a particular run, convergence test
  or held-out control. Its scope does not extend to nature by replay.
- **Hypothesis** is an unestablished input or physical extension.
- **Failed** records a negative test or a closed route at its stated
  scope. An uncomputed or underdetermined route remains marked as such.
