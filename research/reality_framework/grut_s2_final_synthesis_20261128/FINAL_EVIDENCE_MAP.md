## Evidence classes

| Class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Adopted | External physical law, mathematical formalism, or independent model/preparation input, with its applicable physical scope. |
| Derived | Exact mathematical statement within stated assumptions. |
| Certified | Validated continuum enclosure or inequality with a controlled numerical proof and its specified arithmetic assumptions. |
| Perturbative numerical | Calculated at a fixed order and matching, with recorded numerical allowances and explicit omissions. |
| Conditional prediction | Observable calculated for a supplied state, forward model and operational interface. The physical availability of that interface is a separate claim. |
| Unsupported mechanism | A needed physical process or connection lacks a demonstrated implementation, rate or population. |
| Negative | Scoped counterexample, failed registered gate, withdrawn claim or matched degeneracy. The controlling class is part of the result. |
| Unresolved | Hypothesis or target without adjudicating evidence, including frozen tests that never ran. |
