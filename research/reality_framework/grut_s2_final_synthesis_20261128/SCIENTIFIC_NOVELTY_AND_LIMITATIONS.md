## Consistency methods and conjectures

Gauge and anomaly cancellation enforce established conditions in specified representations. Positive residues, unitarity, dispersion relations and forward positivity can bound EFT coefficients under the needed spectral, locality/analyticity and subtraction assumptions. These do not establish a unique UV theory or apply automatically to gravity with long-range poles. Renormalization constrains allowed counterterms and scale dependence. Radiative stability is an independent demand on the proposed parameter hierarchy and matching. EFT universality relates infrared predictions to common low-energy data, leaving microscopic realization potentially nonunique. Holographic no-global-symmetry results have precise AdS/CFT premises. Their extrapolation to a general quantum-gravity completion is an additional conjectural premise. Neither the F4 criterion nor Candidate 1's low-energy global charge resolves that question.
