# Open physical questions

This register records what would have to be known for a stronger answer. It marks questions at the end of S2. Existing scoped theorems, numerical results and negative findings remain available for independent audit. [Foundational limits](FOUNDATIONAL_OBSTRUCTIONS.md) · [Closure verdict](FINAL_PROGRAM_VERDICT.md)

| Question | Current limit | Additional independent premise or evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why these state and probability rules? | The classical floor has nonunique lifts. Quantum state structure and probabilities are adopted in all quantum models. Operational records and decoherence establish joint statistics under those inputs. | A physically motivated restriction or empirical anomaly that distinguishes a specified state or outcome theory from standard quantum instruments. Include a matched preparation and measurement calculation. |
| Why a particular state or thermodynamic direction? | Closure, stationarity and modular KMS allow many seeds or bath conditions. No selected early-universe state or unique arrow follows. | An independently specified state/boundary mechanism, with probabilities, clock calibration and a discriminating cosmological or local coarse-grained prediction. |
| Why these parts, locality, dimension and signature? | Operational algebras and continuum metrics are supplied where used. F1 and CP-12 show underdetermination or failed propagation in their domains. F2 reconstructs with stronger access. | A calibrated physical net or derivable continuum correspondence identifying the same causal and geometric observables, including off-network and causal-window controls. |
| Why Einstein gravity and these characteristic cones? | Coupling, geometry and dynamics are adopted. Homogeneous stress and first-class constraints leave the effective matter cone free. | Independent gravitational degrees-of-freedom and kinetic-response evidence, or a justified microscopic restriction with covariance and a conventional comparator. |
| Why these charges, matter content, masses and interactions? | Anomaly and symmetry constraints leave independent matching data. F4 gives concrete distinct charge options. The observed neutron charge selects the known one within that class. No extra parameter relation is demonstrated. | Independent operator evidence, a UV premise with a controlled low-energy implication, or a new measured relation separating calibrated viable alternatives. |
| Why the measured vacuum component? | The fixed-metric shift equivalence has no power to select a gravitating coupling. The measured vacuum value and radiative stability remain unresolved. | A covariant predictive matching with threshold and scheme checks and an observable fixed independently of the measured value used for testing. |
| What law or boundary makes internal clock agreement selective? | F5 covers arbitrary finite laws and supplied seeds in its class. Clock comparison relates the same effects when transported. | A calibrated boundary, interaction or state restriction that cannot be absorbed into the compensating history. It must predict a difference for common physical events. |
| Can organization constrain fundamental dynamics? | Reversible finite models realize several organizational functions within specified rules. There is no validated bridge from those successes to unique microscopic dynamics. | A physical scale and operator map supported independently, plus a quantitative relationship that removes a conventional matched alternative. |
| How do internal observation and subjective experience relate? | Physical access, records, reporting and limited feedback are modeled. Subjective experience has no established measurement or explanatory bridge in the archive. | An independently motivated operational criterion and held-out observations capable of distinguishing an explicit account from alternative internal-observer descriptions. |
| Does an original Candidate 1 object form and persist in nature? | The prepared leading configuration, some channel energies and local continuum stability are supported. General fission, nonlinear evolution, full quantum and gravitating stability, physical lifetime, source and abundance remain open. | Independent evidence of a production environment and conserved-charge/energy accounting, a yield and lifetime model validated where used, and empirical access. The unchanged investigation is closed. |
| Can Candidate 1's structure be identified empirically? | C3 gives a theoretical field scattering law and C4 has ordinary gravity plus a weak material tidal bound. Matched conventional models reproduce the observables. | A physically characterized target and readout, a calibrated accessible difference from matched alternatives, and uncertainties small enough for a rejection criterion. |
| Are any archived alternative cosmologies physically realized? | Conditional transfer and narrow parameter results coexist with failed microscopic handoffs. No cosmological or laboratory identification follows. | Independent source dynamics and a verified transfer of stress, work, spectra and perturbations with a prediction compared against established cosmology. |
