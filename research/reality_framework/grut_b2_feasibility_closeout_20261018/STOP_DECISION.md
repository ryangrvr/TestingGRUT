## Quantitative reasons

J=1/3840 and T=640L. At the formal fixed-path minimum L=2016, the raw
positive crossing certificate is already 40,320, versus a sufficient
information amplitude target about 0.00632456. Even clipping the certificate
to the unitary difference maximum two leaves no 1% guarantee.

For the INHERITED whole-word preparation, N>=2^28 and L>=N/2. The smallest
L divisible by 24 is actually 134,217,744; T=85,899,356,160. The crossing
certificate is then 2,684,354,880. A uniform crossing-population majorant
used in this positive-access method would need to be at most
1/180144028044495360000000 (about 5.55e-24). No such prepared-state bound
is available. This tiny number is sufficient for this particular method,
not a necessary condition for successful coherent operation.

The available off-diagonal current norm is at most 1411/640. Its absolute
integral at that same T is 189,381,236,784, so probability bounds reduce
to [0,1]. These large numbers are failed ERROR CERTIFICATES, not lower
bounds on actual error or probabilities of failure. FEASIBILITY.md gives
the bookkeeping and identifies the missing signed quantities.

The physical receiver has at least 2^62 modes. A dense complex128 vector
in even its one-boson sector needs 2^66 bytes (64 EiB), 2^36 times the
1-GiB ceiling. No dense array was allocated. An analytical or certified
compressed representation could avoid this cost; none used here supplies
the required moving-window estimates. More compute alone is not prescribed.
