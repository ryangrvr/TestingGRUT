# Core/environment audit protocol

26 October 2026, America/Chicago. Written before this audit's production outputs.

This is post-processing of the existing seven cold classical histories at8159556. No new history, cooling, physical heat source, object mass subtraction or particle preparation is supplied. Accept870a2ab's correction: the static elimination has a finite limiting coefficient, but its validity condition vanishes at the critical endpoint. The older sealed packets remain intact.

The audit asks whether the measured candidates already resemble single-frequency stationary cores and whether a locally conserved energy/charge ledger can support a later core/wave match. It does not identify a stationary solution from one successful diagnostic.

1. Use the exact fourth-order lattice operator, with every spatial edge's energy shared equally between its endpoints. Keep the charged Hamiltonian (including X–phi interaction) and the neutral Hamiltonian separate, with their explicit reversible exchange term. Their sum is the unchanged total Hamiltonian. A subsystem mass is not inferred from this convention.
2. For every threshold4 final candidate, determine the best common instantaneous angular velocity by a least-squares fit to its actual momenta. Decompose charged kinetic energy into common rotation, radial motion and angular-velocity dispersion. Do not remove any of those energies from the history. Evaluate the stationary rotating-field equation residual and neutral motion as necessary diagnostics, not sufficient binding tests.
3. Around the three largest compact final candidates use fixed spheres of radii2,4,6,8,10 in m_X^-1 units. Calculate energy, charge, exchange and outward flux from antisymmetric lattice bonds. A fixed-sphere flux is an instantaneous transport rate, not an escaping-particle spectrum or a moving object's loss rate.
4. Record robust positive/negative neutral signs in shells from r90+1 to r90+3. Gaussian smoothing length1/m_X and sign cut|phi_smooth|>v/2 are fixed here. This is a two-sign neighborhood diagnostic, not a topological or persistent domain-wall certificate. Waves and finite boxes can produce both signs.
5. Retain every final state, and threshold4 masks in the main mixed and mostly one-sign histories for all saved times at or after40/m_X. Use the existing candidate masks/centers. Do not interpret their snapshots as a tracked isolated population.
6. Verify the kinetic identity/minimum, the local charge/energy equations, an independent boundary-bond sum, homogeneous rotating controls, energy-allocation equivalence, and conjugation/parity behavior. Then check all production reconstruction identities. These are same-author checks. Claude reviewed the prior equations and growth code but did not repeat the long3D histories or review this audit.

All physical abundance, particle spectrum and bound-population outputs stay null unless a separately justified calculation supplies them. No numerical cut on a residual will be tuned into a binding verdict.
