## 0. Bottom line

- **The track reproduces exactly.** Its three scripts, rerun from a scratch copy, give byte-identical JSON.
- **The headline survives.** No postulate in this family gives GRUT content that is both distinctive and testable.
- **No postulate is refuted outright.** Several claims are overstated, two numbers are wrong as stated, two verdicts
  use a label the brief does not allow, and one postulate is missing.
- **The TCC exclusion of the heavy window is the strongest result. It needs a premise the track does not state:**
  inflation at (or ending at) H_i is what makes our Hubble volume homogeneous and seeds its perturbations.
  - With that premise, I find a stronger form than the track's [D]. Under the composite (all-interval) form of
    TCC, **any** history in which our Hubble volume descends from one H_i Hubble patch is excluded, whatever
    stages follow.
  - Without that premise, a short episode at H_i is TCC-allowed: N ≤ 16.7, or ≤ 8.4 after a Planck-density
    radiation era. The large-scale homogeneity and perturbations must then be supplied by B or come from a
    non-inflationary mechanism. GRUT's architecture leaves B supplied ("The low-entropy initial condition remains in
    B", 04c53f5 THEORY.md §5), so this escape is open in principle. It is expensive.
- **The exclusion is also a published class result.** Tenkanen (arXiv:1910.00521) already shows that TCC "has
  serious consequences on scenarios where the dark matter density was generated by amplification of quantum
  fluctuations during inflation" [Q abstract]. The GRUT instance should be credited as an application of that result.

## 2. Postulate by postulate

### P1-S3 (spatial slices S³). Not refuted. The verdict should be STANDARD, not MIXED.

- **"MIXED" is not a verdict the brief allows.** The brief allows DISTINCTIVE-TESTABLE, STANDARD, ALREADY EXCLUDED,
  UNTESTABLE and INCONSISTENT WITH GRUT BRANCH. The only testable content is the curvature sign of standard closed
  FLRW. It is not GRUT-native, so the verdict is STANDARD.
- **Correction: the PR4 likelihood does not "remove" the DESI deviation.** Chudaykin–Ivanov–Philcox
  (arXiv:2511.20757 v2, full HTML read) report:
  - 10³Ω_K = 2.4 ± 1.2 for CMB+BAO (1.9σ) and 2.7 ± 1.2 for CMB+FS+BAO (2.3σ);
  - **2.2 ± 1.1 for CMB-PR4+FS+BAO (1.9σ)**: "decreases to below 2σ";
  - CMB-only with PR4: −8.7 +6.2/−5.6.

  P(Ω_K < 0) is still 0.023 [C]. The deviation is reduced, not removed.
- **Missed data point.** An exponential-quintessence fit with free curvature (arXiv:2504.04226, abstract) finds
  **Ω_K = 0.003 ± 0.001**, "∼3σ preference for a positive Ω_K". That gives P(Ω_K < 0) = 0.0013 [C].
  - The track's S³ failure condition ("Ω_K > 0 at ≥ 3σ …") is therefore **already met in one dark-energy model
    class** (retrospective, with SNe). It is not met in ΛCDM (2.1σ) or w0wa (0.5–0.8σ).
  - The condition does not say which dark-energy model applies. Until it does, the sign test has no fixed
    discriminator (Codex gate item 4). This strengthens the track's own "no fixed discriminator".
- **Minor.** With the asymmetric error used correctly, Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE gives P(Ω_K < 0) = 0.993 (upper error
  0.018), not 0.996.

### P1-T3 (Codex's T³ branch). Not refuted. The verdict should be STANDARD, not UNTESTABLE.

- The track lists an operational failure condition: a robust Ω_K ≠ 0 above the floor, or a detected non-T³
  topology. A postulate with an operational failure condition is not UNTESTABLE.
- That test is shared with flat ΛCDM and with the standard topology searches. It is STANDARD content, not GRUT
  content.
- "Ω_K = 0 exactly" is a statement about the background. Locally measured curvature has a floor of about 10⁻⁵ (the
  track already notes this).

### P1-Gauss. Confirmed, STANDARD. The argument can be widened.

- The track's argument is "homogeneous ⇒ div E = 0 ⇒ ρ = 0". Codex's sentence (THEORY.md §5), though, concerns
  charge transfer into localized objects.
- A wider argument covers that case [D]. In the Higgs phase, any finite-energy localized configuration on R³ has
  zero total gauge charge, because its flux falls off exponentially. So Codex's statement holds on R³ for objects
  too.
- Only the global Z6 residue needs compactness. Discrete gauge charge as an Aharonov–Bohm observable is standard:
  Krauss–Wilczek, PRL 62, 1221 (1989) [R]. Credit it.

### P1-QG. Confirmed, UNTESTABLE.

- HUZ present the one-dimensional Hilbert space as "fairly compelling evidence" from earlier work, not as
  established.
- Credit the baby-universe origin: Marolf–Maxfield, arXiv:2002.08950 [R].

### P2-BF. Not refuted. Two claims are overstated, and the verdict should be STANDARD, not MIXED.

- **The e-fold cap is not forced by finite capacity as such [Q/C].**
  - BF state that their principle "cannot be derived from the covariant entropy bound". They also concede that
    "models with a number of e-foldings violating our bound do not violate the covariant entropy bound"
    (astro-ph/0307459 v3, PDF pp. 3 and 11).
  - The cap needs two further inputs: **purity** of the causal-patch state, and **"local field theory valid over the
    entire inflationary patch"**. Under those, the observer's patch is entangled with radiation far outside its
    causal diamond.
  - My computation [C]: an eternal comoving observer's causal diamond holds at most S_rad ≤ S_H0 (χ_EH + χ_P)³ =
    3.16×10⁸⁸ × (1.150 + 3.179)³ = **2.6×10⁹⁰ = 7.7×10⁻³³ S_Λ**, for any N. A covariant (Bousso N-bound) reading
    of "finite capacity" therefore caps nothing.
  - The track's [D] for "N − N_min ≤ 26.1" should be [D given BF's purity plus field-theory-everywhere premise].
  - Credit the published critique: Lowe–Marolf, hep-th/0402162 (BF ref. [35]).
- **"Supplies the vacuum-sign premise" is not distinctive.**
  - The q-vacuum ledger already made the same move with P_q: "The H₀-exclusion premise ρ_vac,tot ≥ −(2.28 meV)⁴ then
    follows from P_q" (`grut_q_vacuum_construction_20261021/tracks/ledger/NOTES.md` A4).
  - Any postulate that fixes Λ_∞ ≥ 0, combined with the [A] "monotone dark sector" link, does the same.
  - A sibling strengthening (P1-time, §3) reverses it.
- **The failure condition is not operational.** "Dark energy decays to ρ_DE ≤ 0 or ends in a crunch" concerns the
  asymptotic state. Finite-time data reach it only through model extrapolation. Λ > 0 is a satisfied retrodiction.
- **Verdict should be STANDARD:** a relabelled BF / N-bound, with the cap untestable. The conflict with the q-vacuum
  P_q is real, but P_q is an unreviewed draft, not one of the brief's GRUT branches.

### P2-TCC. Verdict INCONSISTENT WITH GRUT BRANCH stands, conditionally. One number is wrong as stated.

1. **Unstated premise.** "Within GRUT the exclusion is logical" holds only if the H_i stage is the inflation that
   puts our Hubble volume inside one Hubble patch.
   - Without that premise, TCC allows N ≤ ln(M_P/H_i) = 16.7 at H_i, and ≤ ½ ln(M_P/H_i) = 8.4 if a radiation era
     from Planck density precedes it [C].
   - The heavy-window mechanism itself seems to need only O(5–10) e-folds [E]. The attractor is reached in 1–3
     e-folds (REVIEW_HEAVYWINDOW), and tachyonic growth at −10H² is about e^{2N}.
   - The escape is to supply homogeneity in B, or use a non-inflationary structure source, as the TCC literature
     itself suggests. That price should be stated, not hidden.
2. **The two-stage "dilution ≥ 10^{54.3}" is not a lower bound, and its history is TCC-forbidden anyway.**
   - Composite TCC, a_f/a_i < M_P/H_f on every interval, is equivalent to max_t a(t)H(t) < M_P a_start.
   - Our patch descending from one H_i patch means a_start H_i ≤ a₀H₀. Together these require e^{N_min(last stage)}
     < M_P/H_i [D].
   - N_min of any final stage and any w ≥ −1/3 history is at least 19.38 (aH frozen until reheating at 5 MeV). That
     exceeds 16.72, so **every** such multi-stage history is excluded.
   - Random scan [C]: 0 of the scanned two-stage histories (stage 1 at H_i, horizon solved from the stage-1 patch)
     pass composite TCC. The best margin is −3.5 e-folds; the analytic best is −2.66.
   - The track's D5 history is therefore not a "rescue that dilutes". It is forbidden outright.
   - If instead stage 2 alone solves the horizon problem (TCC-allowed), the minimum dilution is **10^{25.2}**, not
     10^{54.3}. That happens at T_rh2 = 5 MeV with N₂ = 19.4.
   - Recovering the yield through Y ∝ H^{3/2} would still need H ≈ 9×10²⁷ GeV > M_P, so it remains excluded. Our
     Hubble volume would then hold about 10³⁰ stage-1 patches [C].
3. **Robustness of the "most permissive" exclusion.**
   - Its margin is only **2.66 e-folds** with the reduced Planck mass, and **1.05 e-folds (×2.8 in H)** with the
     non-reduced one. That is the size of the O(1) normalisation freedom of a conjecture.
   - With the non-reduced convention, the scaling-family yield floor (3.70×10¹⁰ GeV) lies **below** the relaxed bound
     (4.68×10¹⁰ GeV) [C]. There the family exclusion rests only on the window's fast-reheating requirement, which
     the w = −1/3-to-BBN history violates.
   - The standard-history exclusion (≈ 40–42 e-folds) is robust.
4. **Credit.** Tenkanen 1910.00521 (above). The 41.7 e-fold deficit is a GRUT instance of a known incompatibility.
5. **Late-time TCC does face data.** The lifetime bound is untestable, but "TCC forbids eternal acceleration" has
   already been combined with DESI DR2 to constrain w(z) parameterisations: Li–Wang–Zhang–Saridakis–Cai,
   arXiv:2504.07791 [Q abstract]. This is not GRUT-native.
6. **Framing.** TCC is "related in spirit" to capacity, at best. Bedroya (arXiv:2010.09760, abstract) relates TCC
   and de Sitter complementarity through a scrambling-time coincidence, from TCC towards dS, not the reverse. The
   digest's "the postulate that does force something new" should read "an imported swampland conjecture forces…".

## 3. Missed postulates and new issues

1. **P1-time (finite four-volume: the universe recollapses).** FORMAL_CORE §4 lists "finite four-volume" as SCOPE,
   but the track never puts it through the gate.
   - It forces Λ_∞ < 0, or dark energy that turns negative.
   - It contradicts P2-BF and is compatible with TCC.
   - It relabels published work: Andrei–Ijjas–Steinhardt arXiv:2201.07704 [R], and Luu–Qiu–Tye arXiv:2506.24011
     [Q abstract and HTML]. Luu–Qiu–Tye find Ω_Λ with mean −0.69 +0.70/−0.20 and best fit −1.61, a total lifespan of
     33.3 Gyr, and "a high probability that Λ < 0".
   - For Candidate 1 [C]: the best-fit constant is −6.0×10⁻⁴⁷ GeV⁴, below the H₀-exclusion premise −2.72×10⁻⁴⁷ GeV⁴
     = −(2.28 meV)⁴. The mean, −2.58×10⁻⁴⁷, is just above it. This applies if the premise refers to the constant
     term rather than the total present dark energy.
   - So the "vacuum premise" flips sign between two closure strengthenings.
   - **Verdict: STANDARD**, model-dependent and retrospective, not GRUT-native.
2. **Closed-universe QG ⇒ no exact global symmetries** (Marolf–Maxfield 2002.08950; McNamara–Vafa 1909.10355;
   Harlow–Ooguri 1810.05337 [R]).
   - It is inert for GRUT, because dark-matter stability rests on the **gauged** Z6, which is allowed.
   - It only forbids promoting the approximate global X number to exact.
   - **Verdict: STANDARD / UNTESTABLE.**
3. **The verdict labels "MIXED" (P1-S3, P2-BF) are outside the brief's five categories.** Both should be STANDARD.
4. **The TCC exclusion's premise** (inflationary origin of homogeneity) must appear in the verdict. So must the
   Tenkanen credit.
5. **Composite TCC is the correct multi-stage statement.** It replaces the two-stage dilution argument.
6. **The BF e-fold cap needs BF's purity and field-theory-everywhere assumption.** The covariant bound gives no cap.
   Credit Lowe–Marolf.
7. **The S³ failure condition must name the dark-energy model.** It is already met at 3σ (retrospective) in the
   exponential-quintessence class.
8. **[A] The gauge point is probably not an independent comparison.** Its H = 4.7×10¹³ GeV equals the BK18-derived
   H_max (4.702×10¹³) to 0.04%. That suggests it was chosen at the tensor bound, so "r = 0.0360 at the BK18 limit"
   is probably by construction, not an independent retrospective comparison. Not checked against the gauge-point
   source.
9. **Missing credits:** Krauss–Wilczek (discrete gauge charge), Marolf–Maxfield (one-dimensional closed-universe ℋ),
   Lowe–Marolf (BF critique), Tenkanen (TCC and inflationary dark matter).
