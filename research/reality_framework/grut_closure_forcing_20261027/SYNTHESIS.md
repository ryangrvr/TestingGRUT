## Two findings that matter for the dark-matter branch, whatever happens with closure

1. **Higgs stability during high-scale inflation (standard physics).** The heavy window's H_i = 1.33×10¹¹ GeV is about
   half the Higgs barrier height h_max = 2.7×10¹¹ GeV at the central top mass. A massless Higgs random-walks to about
   1.6×10¹¹ GeV over 60 e-folds. The window therefore needs a stabilising Higgs–curvature coupling ξ_H between about
   0.05–0.07 and O(1), or an equivalent mechanism. This obligation is new and not yet recorded in the branch.
2. **The reference couplings are not perturbative to M_P.** λ_Y = λ_Σ = 1 reaches 4π at 5.7×10¹⁵ GeV. In the band
   0.24–0.57 the κ stability bound holds and the release still works. The spot point should move into that band.

## Additional notes

- **The P_q Σ corner.** P_q can be satisfied inside GRUT only with:
  - λ_Σ ≲ 3×10⁻⁵ (M_Σ ≲ 4×10¹⁰ GeV);
  - a Higgs portal λ_HΣ between 2×10⁻¹¹ and 6×10⁻³;
  - a new coupling λ_ΣY ≳ 0.05, needed to keep Y from condensing.

  There is no measurable Higgs mixing, and two tunings are reintroduced. The visible bath at T_RH then controls Σ, which
  undoes hw_gates gate 3's premise in that corner only. The critic suggests re-solving the release there, with a stated
  failure condition (tracks/cf_critic/NOTES.md).
- **Stabilisation through φ or X** needs a Higgs portal b ≥ 0.117. That is 23× the invisible-width bound.

## What this means for GRUT

The closure idea, as stated, has no independent physical content, and none of its natural strengthenings provides
any. That is a result, not a failure of effort. If GRUT is to be more than a framework name over standard physics plus
a hidden dark sector, it needs a genuinely new dynamical postulate: a new law, stated explicitly and tested directly,
not inferred from "closed".

The dark-matter branch can still stand on its own as a model. Two practical outcomes of this pass apply to it: the Higgs
stability obligation and the perturbative coupling band.
