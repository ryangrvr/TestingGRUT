The literature's MeV-scale constraint is contextual. Its formation-scale
labels must not automatically be identified with the cube root of our
canonically normalized tension. In particular, the 2015 paper prints both
an SI surface density and a MeV formation scale; their convention/conversion
has not been reconciled here. We use neither as a precision bound. The
4.2e16 warning in this response comes from the separately stated dimensional
estimate G sigma/H0, not from a claimed reproduction of its CMB analysis.

Independent constraints on total vacuum energy, curvature, a hidden thermal
history, a chemical potential and a wall population have not been added.
Known published observations and prior review findings were available during
analysis, so retrospective exposure is explicit throughout.
