# Additional nonlinear excitation ladder

The five-percent spherical result passes the radius/time controls and emits net
negative charge while remaining bound. Before running additional amplitudes,
fix radial amplitudes .10,.20,.40 with the same Gaussian shape, exact Q200000,
dr=.2,dt=.025,T1000,R200, absorber start100. Repeat amplitude.40 at dr=.1,
dt=.0125. Keep the original five-percent result as primary.

This is a finite-excitation response curve, not a change to the potential, a
selected formation distribution or an early hot-release prediction. Report the
core charge/energy and explicit receiver, including any loss of the core.
Negative absorbed charge in a weak vacuum exterior is a lower bound on emitted
negative-charge quanta; do not equate its magnitude with a complete pair count
when positive waves may also be emitted. The frozen cosmological mass criterion
is unchanged, and subsequent capture/chemical reactions are not performed here.
