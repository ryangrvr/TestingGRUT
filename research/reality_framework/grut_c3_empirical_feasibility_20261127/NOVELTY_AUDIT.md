# C3 conventional baselines and novelty

| Claim or comparator | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Charge-supported scalar soliton | Friedberg, Lee and Sirlin established the conventional nontopological scalar mechanism. Candidate 1 uses a quartic extension and specified matching values. Local linear stability at three exact charges is a benchmark-specific result. [Friedberg, Lee and Sirlin](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.13.2739) · [C2 certificate](../grut_c2_continuum_stability_20261127/REPORT.md) |
| Scattering off a stationary or rotating scalar configuration | Scattering about Q-ball backgrounds has a substantial conventional antecedent. For example, coupled perturbations of a rotating charged field can have superradiant amplitudes. The C3 reporter is a spectator at linear order and obeys a separate real static radial equation. [Saffin, Xie and Zhou](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03269) |
| Quantum corrections | Renormalized fluctuation methods for Q-ball energies are established. C1 addresses two benchmark-specific energy comparisons. Their determinant gives a soliton energy, while the C3 loop problem would require a reporter two-point response in the inhomogeneous state. [Graham](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0105009) · [C1 calculation](../grut_c1_quantum_channels_20261127/REPORT.md) |
| Matched conventional scalar well | Equal vacuum-normalized potential and flux imply identical linear mode equations, boundary conditions and phases. The extended angular pattern provides no difference against this matched competitor. An identical matched quartic FLS action also has identical higher-order observables for identical prepared states and measurements. |
| Pointlike matter or other scalar matter | The restricted pure S-wave point model differs in angular moments at $k=5$. A different extended scalar state can duplicate the reporter potential. The far-field gravitational monopole of the benchmark is reproduced by an ordinary same-mass source. |
| Gravitational access or cosmological production | Planck-suppressed production in an early universe is a known model-dependent possibility with supplied expansion and matter histories. That literature does not produce this charged target, its compensated charge, or a calibrated reporter population under the frozen benchmark. [Garny, Sandora and Sloth](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.03278) |

The specific momentum, profile-sensitivity, and angular numbers characterize the original illustrative benchmark. An independent physical phenomenon beyond known scalar EFT or a novelty priority claim is unsupported. [Feasibility report](REPORT.md)
