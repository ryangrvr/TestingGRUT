# B2-SA: a controlled analytical route, not a scaling success

**Decision:** the unchanged B2 law has a concrete route for studying a
growing-distance two-source window. The operating probability and retention
claim remain mathematically unresolved. No B3, replacement endpoint, or
new trajectory was launched. B2's eight-event operating gate remains failed.

## What this assessment adds

1. **An exact low-band construction for every host length.** In the complete
   two-resource matter/fuel sector, a common spectral contour yields a
   convergent expansion with ratio 1206/2867<0.421, uniformly in length.
   Every virtual charged configuration, conversion and coherent return is
   retained. The exact band evolution avoids the old fixed-order error
   allowance growing linearly with observation time. Input and observables
   must be transformed with it; it is not a shortcut pair-only law.
2. **A uniform preparation-weight bound.** The double-fuel seed has high-band
   spectral weight <=32/37027225. Normalizing its exact low-band component
   changes any event probability by less than 0.001, at every time and
   length. Recurrence inside this low band remains possible.
3. **A genuinely mobile exact bulk channel.** For the isolated one-composite
   infinite bulk, the lowest neutral band has velocity
   `(16928/8825) J sin(k) < e'(k) <= (1128/529) J sin(k)` for 0<k<pi.
   These bounds retain all charged separations and do not accumulate a
   long-time perturbative error. A bare bulk co-pair has band weight at
   least 1058/1059. Neither statement establishes emission from fuel.
4. **The endpoint information question is separate.** On every fixed path,
   ordered color carriers cannot pass. The successful two-qubit channel is
   exactly `p_R rho`, including entangled inputs; all other outputs are
   erasures. Its still-unknown probability cannot be replaced by this
   conditional channel quality or a different collective encoding.

These are author-derived results with independent mathematical review
pending, not an empirical test or a demonstrated theory of reality.

## The size-scaling task is explicit

The prospective family uses nominal B2 parameters, two excited endpoint
fuels, and prepared paths with L divisible by 24. At times
`Jt in [L/8,L/6]`, receivers lie between L/8 and 3L/8 from each source.
The proposed target is joint outgoing probability >=0.1 throughout that
window, arbitrary-two-qubit error <=0.01, and two-matter probability >=0.25
from `Jt=2` through its end. Failure time is the first subsequent drop below
the matter threshold after that preparation deadline. No earlier measurement, reset or fuel decoder
is included. These criteria were frozen before controls; they were NOT
tested or declared successful. No size threshold for their validity is known.

The velocity margin makes a **pre-encounter** route plausible to analyze.
It would establish dilute composability, not a collision operation or an
extensive medium. The preparation has two resources and O(1) energy;
electric-field expectation remains <4.11. Path registers cost O(L).
Fuel loading and the fixed geometry remain supplied resources.
Energies and times use the supplied microscopic units with hbar=1; no
conversion to experimentally calibrated units has been established.

## The missing result is now specific

The exact half-line fuel resolvent is

    G_f(z) = 1/[z-Omega-J^2 <0,0|(z-H_ch)^(-1)|0,0>].

First, its spectral measure must put enough of the actual fuel preparation
into useful outgoing velocities, while bounding trapped and return terms.
Second, a complete two-source comparison must control contacts, fields,
boundary effects and dressed observables throughout the growing window.
The convergent projector series and bulk velocity do not prove that
comparison. A crude constituent-speed bound is too weak for this purpose.

That is a tractable, sharply specified analytical program; closure of its
inequalities is not guaranteed. The next useful proof challenge is the
boundary source measure, rather than another solver or parameter campaign.
The present cycle stops here.

## Verification and limits

Exact-fraction checks passed for the stated scalar constants. Matrix
controls at L=4,6,8 confirmed low-band counts 12,23,38 and exact ordered-color
matrix factorization. The replay's data were byte-identical. Combined
workers took 1.96 seconds, peak RSS 65.14 MiB; no propagation or new output
probabilities were computed. These controls share B1/B2 code ancestry and
use ordinary double precision, without outward-rounded matrix certification.

Nothing here restores the full WM2 receiver's real-energy consistency gap,
proves source-disorder robustness, handles interior-fuel decoding, or
composes this scaling family with actual growing geometry. Formation,
recording, resource preparation, supplied locality, dimensional selection,
finite-density operation and empirical discrimination retain their prior
status. A useful long path is still not a derived spacetime.
