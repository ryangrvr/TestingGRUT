## Result in one paragraph

A free X hitting bulk Q-matter is absorbed with high probability [C]. The linear (Bogoliubov) scattering problem on
the planar M1 interface is exact at tree level and has only two open channels for an X with T < 1.55: reflected X, and
a phonon in the bulk. It gives s = 0.680 at T = 0.02, 0.927 at 0.1, 0.9915 at 0.3 and 0.9993 at 1 (normal incidence),
with s ≈ 7.9√T at threshold. Flux-weighted thermal averages are s̄ = 0.864 / 0.948 / 0.966 at T_h = 0.1 / 0.3 / 0.5.
An X̄ is destroyed with probability 0.91 / 0.965 / 0.977 at the same T_h. Neutral (φ) emission is exactly zero at
linear order for X below T = 2.23 and ≤ 8×10⁻⁴ for X̄.

Time-domain classical lattice runs of the full X–φ theory reproduce these fractions to 0.03–0.3% in 1D and quasi-1D.
An independent finite-object solver reproduces sk_xcore (332/m_X²) to its digits. Large spheres with a thermalising
interior absorb πR²·s̄ to 0.5–14%.

Evolving the object population with this sticking (ripening by the sticking-limited exchange, Brownian mergers,
capture freeze-out) from the spinodal lumps gives ⟨Q⟩ ≈ 0.5–3×10²⁰ [C on E models], set by Ostwald ripening and
independent of the first-lump size. The vapour fraction at freeze-out is f_v = 4–12% for S/Q = 1.5–3.2, T_f ≈ 0.009 m_X
(T_vis ≈ 0.4–0.7 MeV) and ζ ≈ 1×10⁻⁷. With spontaneous (Beliaev) phonon decay as the only trapping mechanism at
freeze-out [E], freeze-out comes earlier: f_v = 6–15%, T_vis ≈ 6 MeV, ζ ≈ 6×10⁻⁹.

The relic mass per charge is M/m_X = 0.784–0.810. That gives H_i = 1.49–1.52×10¹¹ GeV, consistent with the
WORKING_THEORY value, now with a computed ±1% spread. ζ ≪ 10⁻⁵ and λ_fs ≈ 0.04–0.6 kpc, so this branch is CDM-like on
Lyman-α and satellite scales [E, via fx_observables' map]. The WORKING_THEORY scenario "⟨Q⟩ ~ 10¹², s ~ 1, O(10%)
deficit at k ≈ 10–50/Mpc" does not occur.

## 1. Sticking and X̄ destruction on Q-matter

### 1a. Interface and linear problem [C/D]
- **Planar interface at coexistence.** Ω − Ω_b = 1.1×10⁻¹³. Surface tension σ_planar = 99.04 m_X³ (equipartition) /
  99.04 (direct); the E(Q) fit gives 98.8. The Gibbs dividing surface for charge lies at x = −1.02.
- **Linearisation** in the rotating frame, with ψ = e^{−iΩt}(F + u + iv) and (u, v, s) = (U, iV, S_x)e^{−iwt}:
  - Y″ = M(x; w, k⊥) Y, with M real symmetric [D];
  - rotated to (U ∓ V)/√2 it is the canonical operator of bf8ab3b / sk_xcore.
- **Conserved flux.** The Wronskian Im(Y†Y′) is the rotating-frame energy flux divided by 2w. Every quantum (X at
  energy Ω + w, X̄ at w − Ω, φ at w, bulk modes) carries rotating energy w, so this is the quantum-number flux. The
  norm sign of every channel was checked: all are positive.
- **Channels for an incoming X** (w = E − Ω_b) [D]:
  - outside, only the reflected X is open (X̄ needs T > 1.55, φ needs T > 2.23);
  - inside, only the phonon (gaps 1.740 and 2.134 m_X), with c_s = 0.5467 from both the EOS and the dispersion [C].
  - Hence s = 1 − R exactly, and neutral emission is zero at linear order.
- **Channels for an incoming X̄** (w = E + Ω_b): reflected X̄, X at E + 2Ω_b (conversion), φ for T > 0.673, the
  gapped bulk mode(s) and the phonon.

### 1f. What classical fields at occupation < 1 cost, and trapping [A/E]
- **Exact at tree level.** The linear S-matrix of 1a–1c is the one-quasiparticle result at tree level (corrections
  O(g²) ≈ 1%). The lattice reproduces it at small amplitude.
- **Nonlinear corrections are classical artefacts here.** They appear only for coherent multi-quantum packets: −1.8%
  in R at ε = 0.1 and −18% at 0.3, with φ and X̄ emission. A physical vapour has amplitude ε ≈ 0.007 at T = 0.3,
  occupation 0.1–0.6 per mode, so these corrections are ≲ 10⁻⁴ there.
- **Where the classical picture does fail:**
  - (i) Thermal averages need Bose, not Rayleigh–Jeans, weights. They were done with Bose weights.
  - (ii) Multi-phonon and loop channels.
  - (iii) **Interior trapping.** The transmitted phonon must decay below the 1 − Ω_b = 0.224 re-emission threshold
    before it re-crosses the object. Otherwise the object only delays an elastic scattering. Near freeze-out this is
    spontaneous Beliaev decay, a quantum effect.
- **Beliaev estimate** [E] (universal Γ = (1+u)²k⁵/(480π(e+P)), u = 0.170, e + P = 348.6 m_X⁴):
  - mean free path 2×10⁷ (T = 0.005), 2.5×10⁶ (0.1), 2.3×10⁵ (0.3), 5.8×10³ (1);
  - Landau damping is about 40× smaller at T_f, and thermal capillary roughness (h ≈ 0.01) is negligible.
- **Trapping probability.** With the escape-cone geometry (reciprocity keeps the phonon inside the cone, about 2
  chords), P_trap = 1 − e^{−8R/3ℓ} → 1 above T ≈ 0.3 for R ≳ 10⁵, but ≈ 0.1 at T_f for Q ~ 10²⁰.
- This is the largest uncertainty in f_v (item 3).

- **Gibbs–Thomson.** n_sat(T, Q) = n_vap(Ω(Q), T), from the Bessel-series vapour and Ω(Q) of family.json.
  - The ratio to bulk, n_sat(Q)/n_sat(∞), is e^{(Ω(Q)−Ω_b)/T} in the non-relativistic limit.

1. "⟨Q⟩ = 10¹²–10²⁰" narrows to **0.5–3×10²⁰** with the computed sticking (up to 1.6×10²¹ with Beliaev trapping).
   It is set by Ostwald ripening plus re-condensation after the evaporation dip, not by the first lumps or by mergers.
2. "A few-percent warm vapour, 3–11%" becomes **4–12% (perfect trapping) / 6–15% (Beliaev)**, rising with S/Q. It is
   warm at freeze-out (v ≈ 0.16–0.19c) but freezes out at T_vis ≈ 0.4–7 MeV, so it is cold today (λ_fs ≤ 0.6 kpc).
3. "ζ near Lyman-α reach for ⟨Q⟩ ~ 10¹², s ~ 1" does not occur: **ζ ≈ 0.6–1.2×10⁻⁷ (or 6×10⁻⁹)**. The Q-matter branch
   is CDM-like on all scales probed by the linear power spectrum. Its distinctive physics is in its composition, not in
   P(k).
4. Mass per charge **0.784–0.810 m_X**, H_i = **1.49–1.52×10¹¹ GeV**, consistent with WORKING_THEORY.
5. S/Q = 1.5 is not reachable by e in M1r: the e → ∞ floor from M1r's own mediator heat is S/Q ≈ 1.84.

## Limits

- **Sticking.** It uses the T = 0 planar profile. Interface thermal broadening at T_h ≳ 1 is not included; s → 1 there
  anyway. Curvature corrections are O(1/kR), and the objects have R ≥ 10⁴.
- **Trapping.** Interior trapping is an order-of-magnitude [E] (universal Beliaev coefficient; k ≈ 0.4–1 is near the
  edge of the phonon regime). It brackets f_v.
- **History.** The kinetics run on fx_formation's equilibrium T(a) history (one-loop liquid thermal pressure [E]) up
  to freeze-out. Variant (b) brackets what follows. Off-coexistence fluxes use exponential μ scaling, with Bose
  corrections of a few % [E].
- **Early stage.** The early dense stage (volume fraction 0.53) is treated only through the hydrodynamic-start variant
  [E]. The Fuchs–Epstein kernel is [E].
- **Inputs not computed here.** e(S/Q) is dq_entropy's reading B [Q/E]; dq_heat supersedes it. g*(T) is approximate
  [R]. The transfer-function verdict uses fx_observables' map, not a new calculation.
- **No 3D runs** were made. The lattice is 1D and quasi-1D.
