## What will be tested

1. Trace the H55 source columns to their time levels, units, injection
   terms and redshift laws. Use the existing two-helicity Majorana phase
   space, with physical energy including rest mass. Quantify which
   source approximations can change the obstruction. A number rescaling
   requires a derivation from the source and the kinetic dictionary.
2. Test a replacement with explicit comoving momentum occupations
   and energy exchange. Derive number, energy and pressure as moments
   of the same occupations. A calculation with a supplied background
   or a reduced production model will be identified by that scope.
3. Carry the HFa spectator and dark-radiation daughters in the energy
   and expansion ledger. Use the inherited 0.05 eV common seesaw inputs
   to keep the comparison matched. Use the inherited HFa scale rule
   `Gamma_s/H_i = 1.3e-6 (H_i / 1.30e11 GeV)` at H55.
4. Check energy transfer, physical occupations, momentum and time
   refinement, and the interface budget. Preserve code, results and a
   restart point on this branch.

A numerical history must show a full initial splice error at most
`1e-6`, a maximum scalar budget error at most 1%, and late `K=a^2 H`
agreement at the 1% level in tested refinements before use in release.
Any preparation or production approximation must also have a control
in its claimed regime. A converged conditional network alone leaves
the cosmological acceptance decision false. Release, object formation,
and abundance comparisons require a physical source that passes these
checks. The legacy results and excluded extrapolations stay archived.

The continued construction uses the existing M2 field content and H55
scale. Its free-particle matching follows the two-helicity mode
description of Majorana production. See [Adshead and Sfakianakis
(2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.00891) and [Greene and Kofman
(2000)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.62.123516).
