## Initial data and coupled spatial control

Reconstruct the H55 scalar initial fields from the archived X2
configuration, vacuum generator, and seed. Compare their first
diagnostics to the archive and quantify the fluctuation and
gradient power in one-axis projections. Record the limitations
of the reduction as a separate production uncertainty.

Couple X8's unitary one-axis mass operator to X9's signed
lepton–Higgs vacuum spectrum in each momentum and helicity
sector. Begin with the negative-energy projector of the whole
coupled Hamiltonian at constant phase and scale. With the bath
coupling present, drive a smooth spatial scalar profile from
time -1.5 to time zero. Release the scalar into reciprocal
field, sterile and bath evolution with an averaged FRW metric.
The preparation fixes all the joint covariances.

Use the X8 physical mass 0.7, phase scale 1.15 and scalar mass
2.6 in the finite control. Take a first-harmonic initial field
of amplitude 0.15 and speed 0.9 at release. Use the dimensionless
vacuum coupling 0.012, a baseline conformal bath cutoff 5.5,
eight positive and eight negative bins per chiral sector,
and evolve to time 4.5. Independently vary bath spacing and
cutoff, longitudinal cutoff, transverse quadrature and timestep.
Compare the volume derivative pressure, reciprocal scalar work,
momentum and total energy. The target for the budget is 1e-5.
The slab is a finite one-axis control with an averaged metric.

## Handoff

Test boundaries at 1.25, 2.25 and 3.25. Compare mass-axis
occupations including channel populations, energy-eigenmode
occupations, a separation of sterile and bath blocks, and a
hierarchy retaining the sterile coherence plus explicit bath
directions generated by the coupling and bath Hamiltonian.
Use positive covariance pinching and leave the scalar state
unchanged. Re-evolve the same dynamics so the test isolates
information discarded at the boundary.

Require immediate jumps in total energy, pressure, momentum
and scalar work below 1e-6 in units of the reference total
energy. Require terminal bulk count, bath energy, and each
daughter count within 1% of the coupled reference at every
boundary, with the predeclared floor of 1e-3 of the peak
reference daughter count for a nearly empty channel. Check
the results at moved boundaries and on refined runs. Record
any stricter or weaker observational agreement separately.
This is a sufficiency test for the carried state. A Markov
transport equation and a spatially resolved H55 relic yield
have separate tests.
