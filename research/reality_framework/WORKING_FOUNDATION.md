# Reality first: a physical foundation and two quantitative bridges

2026-10-11. Owner instruction: GRUT is whatever the investigation finds; it is
not an established theory to defend. This document develops the conventional
working foundation and reassesses CP-06. It is not CP-07, a new frozen law,
a claimed microscopic completion, or a Stage-3 execution.

## Correct the objective

A working theory predicts observations from independently specified inputs
within a stated domain. It need not uniquely choose every constant, initial
state, particle inventory or mathematical representation. Explaining those
inputs is a further, legitimate objective. Our repeated selection tests must
not be substituted for the entire physical task.

Conversely, supplying a potential and calculating its consequence is genuine
conditional physics, but is not a derivation of that potential's origin.
Both distinctions matter. No present construction earns a complete theory of
reality, and this document does not relabel established physics as a discovery.

## What CP-06 really showed

Its invertible classical Legendre map produces Einstein-scalar equations on
regular branches. This is stronger than an isolated spin-2 pole and weaker
than a new physical mechanism distinguishing the theory from Einstein-scalar
gravity. With mapped initial data, boundary conditions and probes, the two
classical descriptions make the same predictions. Merely renaming the
connection microscopic and the metric emergent changes no experiment.

The coexistence of Euclidean and Lorentzian classical solutions establishes
nonselection by the classical action. It does NOT prove that a Lorentzian
quantum sector cannot exist. A quantum state, integration contour and physical
observable construction could select a sector, but CP-06 specifies none.
We should not declare the existence of Euclidean saddles a universal defect.

Likewise, its two potentials establish allowed classical interaction freedom,
not failure of quantum consistency or evidence that all acceptable physical
theories must determine scalar self-couplings uniquely. A real quantum matching
problem would compare matched renormalized quantities, not just bare tree data.

The numerical check tests diagonal algebra only. It does not independently
verify the full constraint algebra, quantum measure or astrophysical fitness.
The original report remains preserved; these are interpretive qualifications.

Bibliographic correction to CP-06: Phys. Rev. D 29, 2746 (1984) is by
M. Martellini, not "Kijowski and collaborators." Its abstract credits the
earlier Kijowski-Tulczyjew result. The publisher abstract was inspected;
the subscription-restricted full article was not independently audited here.

## An explicit working physical foundation

In natural units, a conventional low-energy action can be organized as

    S_eff = integral sqrt(-g) [M_P^2 R/2 - rho_Lambda + L_SM
                              + L_neutrino + L_dark
                              + c1 R^2 + c2 R_ab R^ab
                              + sum_i C_i O_i / Lambda_UV^(d_i-4)].

This is an EFT family, not a fully calibrated single model. M_P is the reduced
Planck mass; Lambda_UV is a cutoff/matching scale, not the cosmological constant.
L_SM contains the SU(3)xSU(2)xU(1) gauge, chiral fermion, Higgs and Yukawa
sectors. Neutrino masses require specified extra data/operators beyond the
minimal renormalizable Standard Model. L_dark is explicitly unresolved, not
a hidden claim that the dark sector has been explained. The operator sum
means a complete basis at a specified order, including allowed matter and
mixed curvature terms after removing redundancies. It is not permission to
refit a held-out result without accounting.

The quantum specification also needs a state/preparation, positive physical
state space, amplitudes or density matrices, measurement probabilities,
renormalization/matching and constraint-compatible boundary data. An action
alone does not provide an observed universe. This foundation supplies those
structures openly and claims validity only where the expansion and quantum
description are controlled. Higher derivatives are treated perturbatively;
resumming a finite EFT truncation into additional exact poles can create
spurious ghosts and is not automatically a physical completion.

Metric variation gives, at leading derivative order,

    M_P^2 G_ab + rho_Lambda g_ab = T_ab.

Diffeomorphism invariance and the matter equations give nabla^a T_ab=0.
Gauge constraints remove unphysical polarizations. Ordinary local Lorentzian
evolution supplies causal propagation in its domain, not a derivation of the
origin of causality. Quantizing fluctuations produces low-energy graviton
effects; nonrenormalizability does not make every low-energy calculation
inconsistent. Local unknown coefficients and infrared nonanalytic effects
must be separated. The latter depend on physical light states and couplings.

This conventional framework is an actual calculational foundation. Calling
it GRUT would not make it a new explanation. Its uncalibrated dark sector,
extreme-curvature regime, fundamental quantum-gravity dynamics and full
measurement interpretation remain open here.

## Bridge 1: an observable interaction can be restricted without being selected

Consider a stable massive scalar, no gravity or other massless particles,
with an analytic crossing-symmetric unitary scattering amplitude satisfying
the high-energy boundedness needed for a twice-subtracted dispersion relation.
Let nu=s-2m^2 at t=0. Subtract isolated exchange poles consistently and call
the crossing-even amplitude B(nu). The first multiparticle cut starts at
nu_0=2m^2 for a two-scalar threshold, if there are no lower states.

Cauchy's relation plus crossing gives

    B(nu) = B(0) + (2 nu^2/pi) integral_(nu0)^infinity
              dnu' Im A(nu'+2m^2,0) / [nu'(nu'^2-nu^2)].

Expanding below threshold,

    a2 := B''(0)/2
        = (2/pi) integral dnu' Im A(nu'+2m^2,0) / nu'^3 >= 0.

The optical theorem supplies the nonnegative absorptive part; strict positivity
requires nonzero spectral weight. This constrains an on-shell interaction, not
an arbitrary field-coordinate coefficient. Subtracting a known low-energy cut
defines an improved bound on the remaining ultraviolet contribution.

There is no uniqueness claim. Different positive spectra give different a2.
There is nevertheless real physical exclusion under the stated assumptions.
Our earlier conclusion that general consistency did not select particular
couplings must not be expanded into the false statement that consistency
cannot restrict interactions.

Gravity changes the problem: its massless t-channel exchange behaves as
nu^2/(M_P^2 t), making the forward limit singular. Deleting the pole by hand
does not establish the above positive sum rule. In the published gravitational
analysis, assumed Regge behavior cancels the divergent pieces but can leave
finite negative terms. Their size depends on additional high-energy data.
Consequently, this document derives no universal gravitational coefficient
bound and no exclusion for CP-05. It identifies the amplitude and ultraviolet
assumptions a real selection calculation must supply.

## Bridge 2: information and curvature have a quantitative conditional relation

For a small four-dimensional geodesic ball of radius ell, assume a locally
maximally symmetric Lorentzian vacuum, conformal quantum matter and the
local modular Hamiltonian. Let alpha be entropy per area and use k_B=1.
At first order and fixed ball volume,

    delta A |_V = -(4 pi ell^4/15) delta G_00,
    delta S_IR = (8 pi^2 ell^4/(15 hbar)) delta<T_00>.

Entanglement equilibrium, alpha delta A + delta S_IR=0, gives

    delta G_00 = [2 pi/(alpha hbar)] delta<T_00>.

Requiring the relation in all local timelike frames fixes the tensor relation
up to a metric term. Conservation makes that term an integration constant.
The Einstein coefficient is recovered when alpha=1/(4 G hbar).

This is Jacobson's established conditional bridge, not a GRUT theorem. It
relates information, geometry and universal stress response. But it supplies
Lorentzian geometry, the entropy-area coefficient and equilibrium; it does
not create a causal spacetime from an unspecified substrate. Nonconformal
matter needs additional control. An independently derived entropy coefficient
and causal quantum system could add explanatory content; assuming them is
not such a derivation.

The bridge's value is not that it completes gravity. It distinguishes a
necessary macroscopic relation within stated premises from an arbitrary
choice of scalar potential. Quantum correlations are pertinent, but a bath,
rest frame, relaxation kernel or consciousness variable does not follow.

## Physical research conclusion

There are already working physical theories in limited domains. We do not
have a verified complete fundamental theory. Those are different statements.
The present work earns a conventional operational foundation and identifies
two quantitative bridges; it does not earn a new microscopic mechanism.

CP-06 should not be followed automatically by another familiar action with
a different label. The scientifically productive construction would have to
specify a physical quantum system and calculate a measurable interaction or
entropy/response coefficient that its conventional comparator leaves free,
or recover known behavior with fewer independently supplied structures.
That need not select every coefficient uniquely or begin with a theory of
everything. No such construction has been completed in this document.
