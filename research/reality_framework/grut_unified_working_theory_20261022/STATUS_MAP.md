historical calculation. Primary packet reads and inherited review scopes

The new relation is a prediction of this proposed scalar action at a stated
order. A conventional model with the identical action predicts it too. It
does not distinguish GRUT's framework from standard quantum EFT. What it
does is remove freedom to refit mass and leading probe response separately
within this specified candidate after their common couplings are fixed.

No old constant, spectral match or consciousness source is promoted to an
earned universal parameter. The names "closed", "response" and "observer"
add no unspoken evolution equation.
