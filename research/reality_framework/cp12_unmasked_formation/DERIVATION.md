# Unmasked formation: preparation, diagnostics, and numerical error

## Preparation and its supplied resources

Let M=N(N−1)/2 and D=binom(M,Q). Use the neutral sea at every graph:

    ψ₀ = D^(−1/2) Σ_{|G|=Q} |G, sea, E=0⟩.

No graph classifier, spatial embedding, marked-edge mask, or relative graph phase enters this definition. It is permutation invariant with the same fermionic convention as the parent signed quotient. This ray is a supplied boundary condition, not a derived state-selection law.

The neutral rewiring Johnson Laplacian annihilates ψ₀. Each of its Q occupied edges has two orthogonal one-hop outputs, so for the frozen parameters

    ⟨H⟩₀=Q/2,     Var₀(H)=2Q.

The state is pure: H∞=S=0, its neutral-basis coherence and reference min-entropy deficit are log₂D. At Q=νN this is νN log₂N+O(N). It evades the earlier preparation obstruction using a substantial supplied purity resource. Zero rewiring energy is not zero total energy, and does not imply stationarity under the coupled law.

The invariance of ψ₀ and H implies ⟨n_e⟩=Q/M for every potential edge at all times. The mean adjacency matrix therefore cannot diagnose intrinsic spatial dimension: it is proportional to the complete-graph adjacency even if a superposition were supported on spatial graph samples. Measure intrinsic balls within individual graph configurations instead.

An abstract preparation resource accounting is possible. Sequentially process the M graph bits with a remaining-occupation counter k. At step j rotate the next bit with conditional probability k/(M−j), then decrement k if the bit is one. Every final weight-Q string has squared amplitude Q!(M−Q)!/M!=1/D; the counter ends at zero. There are at most Q(M−Q) nontrivial counter-controlled rotation angles, plus counter operations and deterministic assignments. Thus an abstract circuit uses O(MQ) such rotations and O(log Q) counter qubits, before elementary-gate decomposition and finite-precision costs. This is a construction, not an optimality or physical cost lower bound. Its graph-bit operations can change Q during preparation. That controller, its pure input, its clock, its control work, and its interactions are not generated by frozen CP-12. We have not implemented this circuit as an autonomous process in that law. A compact boundary-state formula does not solve that missing mechanism.

## Exact finite-size saturation

For a simple N-vertex graph, if u and v are nonadjacent and have no common neighbor, at least N−1 potential edges are absent: uv and at least one of uw,vw for each other vertex w. A disconnected graph likewise needs at least N−1 missing edges. Therefore

    M−Q < N−1  ⇒ every Q-edge graph is connected and has diameter ≤2.

At Q=2N, N=5 has M=Q, hence only K₅ exists; N=6 has M−Q=3<5, hence every graph has diameter two. Seven is the first admissible size in this density family where this saturation argument ceases to apply. It is not the first size large enough to infer a spatial dimension.

Define the root-averaged intrinsic ball

    B_G(r) = (1/N) Σ_a |{b : d_G(a,b)≤r}|.

Exactly, B_G(0)=1 and B_G(1)=1+2Q/N=5 in this density family, independent of shape and quantum state. Thus B(1) cannot be an evolving locality diagnostic. At N=6, B(r)=6 for r≥2, again exactly for every state. No fit to these one- or two-step saturated curves can establish three-dimensional growth.

## Same-preparation observables

K₄(G) is the maximum number of vertex-disjoint cycles with length 3 or 4. The code enumerates those cycles exactly and solves this tiny packing problem exhaustively. C is the projector onto K₄≥floor(N/3), a declared finite-size diagnostic, not an asymptotic choice of α.

At waiting time w, loop persistence is the sequential projective probability

    p_joint = || C U(τ) C U(w) ψ₀ ||²,
    p_survival = p_joint / ||C U(w)ψ₀||².

This is a measured-event survival probability, not an undisturbed classical trajectory or proof that the same named loop persists. At K₅ the event is certain from the outset and its persistence is trivial. General histories of individual cycles would need additional diagnostics.

The gauge-invariant source is vertex occupation n₀; the intervention is exp(−iηn₀) at w. For r≥1 define

    A_r(G,b) = (1/(N−1)) Σ_{j≠0} 1[d_G(0,j)=r] n_j(b).

The disconnected-distance bin is r=N. Both n₀ and A_r commute with Gauss generators. With v=U(w)ψ₀ the exact linear susceptibility is

    χ_r = 2 Im ⟨U(τ)v | A_r | U(τ)n₀v⟩.

This follows by differentiating the kick, not by finite-difference fitting. Detectors use the FINAL graph distance; the response can include graph redistribution. It does not isolate matter transfer through a fixed geometry, identify a photon, or establish a causal propagation cone. Source-free formation and probe measurements refer to separate copies of the same state. The diagnostic probe is an externally imposed intervention, not the autonomous preparation mechanism being sought.

## Full-law projection and leakage bound

Retain physical states reachable from the neutral preparation under the projected Hamiltonian with total integer electric square Σ_l E_l²≤b. Close this set under EVERY full-law transition whose destination satisfies that budget. The cutoff bounds the total electric square, not each leg independently. It is solely a numerical projection P; it is not the frozen physical law. All full rewiring degrees remain on the diagonal even at the boundary.

Use the exact signed S_N quotient for formation and the exact signed stabilizer S_(N−1) fixing vertex zero for the source. Orbit conflicts cancel the corresponding invariant vector, just as in the parent quotient. The smaller source symmetry is necessary: evolving only within S_N after applying n₀ would incorrectly discard the source response.

Construct

    H_P = PHP,       B=(1−P)HP.

Every outgoing full-law destination is included in B, including arbitrarily large electric fields allowed by a single transition from the finite retained space. Its target space is finite. No boundary hopping destination or rewiring diagonal is silently removed from the parent law. H_P and B are bounded finite matrices; unretained evolution itself need not be bounded.

For any initial retained vector x, Duhamel gives

    || U_H(t)x − U_HP(t)x || ≤ ∫₀ᵗ ||B U_HP(s)x|| ds.

The state x may be subnormalized. For normalized states, graph measurement total variation is at most this state norm error (and at most one). Unbounded integer rotors do not invalidate the integral identity on these finite-support vectors under the parent self-adjoint Hamiltonian.

The implementation shifts H_P by Q/2, which changes only a common phase. Let A=H_P−Q/2, mesh width h, and x_j=e^(−iA s_j)x at interval midpoints. Taylor's formula for the LEAKAGE VECTOR gives the valid upper sum

    ∫₀ᵗ ||B e^(−iAs)x|| ds
      ≤ Σ_j [ h||Bx_j|| + h²||BAx_j||/4
               + h³ ||B|| ||A²x||/24 ].

Use ||B||≤sqrt(||B||₁||B||∞). A commutes with its unitary, so ||A²x|| is time independent. The h²/4 and h³/24 constants are the exact integrals of |s−s_j| and (s−s_j)²/2. This avoids treating norm or energy conservation as evidence that omitted electric states are negligible.

These bounds are evaluated in ordinary floating point; they are not interval-arithmetic certificates. Complete-matrix checks assess numerical consistency. The displayed response uncertainty is conservative: if ε_w bounds the waiting-state error and ε_0, ε_1 bound the two subsequent propagated-column errors, put e_i=ε_w+ε_i. As ||A_r||≤1 and all relevant unprojected vectors have norm at most one,

    |χ_r(full)−χ_r(projected)| ≤ 2(e_0+e_1+e_0 e_1).

The positive product is harmless slack. For sequential loop probabilities, an absolute error bound is min(1,2(ε_w+ε_C)), with ε_C the propagation error of C v. Ratios need separate denominator control; raw conditional survivals are not independently certified merely by quoting a joint-probability error.

## Interpretation

The integrated gate needs three independent controls: a preparation not carrying the desired topology, meaningful intrinsic length and size ranges, and bounded numerical errors below the measured effect. This packet implements the first as a declared pure boundary state and instruments the remaining two. It does not derive an autonomous preparation procedure. Its small graphs and current higher-density electric budgets do not pass the macroscopic formation gate.
