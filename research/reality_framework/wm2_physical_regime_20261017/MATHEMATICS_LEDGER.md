# Mathematical-analysis ledger

* Complete-word append identity, weighted-word operator norm, exact retarded
  receiver equation and formation current: derived and checked in small
  independent full tensor matrices.
* Finite-correlation comparison on the physical N^2 horizon: all-N Taylor
  closed-walk estimate. Tiny physical tails are below double resolution;
  floating kernel comparisons are not their proof.
* Retardation error: retains both creation and absorption commutators.
  An explicit noncommuting-history example rules out dropping the former.
* Uniform formation theorem: equations (14)-(16) in FORMATION_ANALYSIS.md,
  with explicit N_0=2^28. Exact rational checks cover the final constants;
  no independent mathematical review is claimed.
* Sweep theorem: orthogonality within nested monotone ranges, direct
  in-range residual and numerical prefix-Gram correction implemented.
  Returns require linear addition across batches, demonstrated by the
  preserved counterexample.
* Growing-distance injection transport: complete Dyson comparison to an
  independently evolving half-line probe, with the reference restrictions
  paid by two full norm tails. A boundary-acceleration/Paley-Zygmund argument
  supplies its nonzero arrival lower bound. Support enumeration and finite
  reference walks check the construction; the all-size result is analytic.

The current estimate controls a first moment and its macroscopic probability
consequence. It is not a full reduced-state Markov approximation or a
distributional law for independent attachment times.
