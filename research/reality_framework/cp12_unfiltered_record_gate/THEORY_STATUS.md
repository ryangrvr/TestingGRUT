## What is specified

The model supplies canonical quantum mechanics, external evolution parameter T, N vertex fermion modes, one edge fermion and two compact-U(1) rotor legs per potential connection, fixed graph-edge number Q, a fixed total fermion number M=N(N-1)/2, and Gauss constraints. It also supplies the couplings and an initial state. These inputs have not themselves been derived from a more fundamental physical principle.

For e=(i<j), the frozen generator is

\[
H=\kappa Q+2m\sum_i n_i+\frac{\epsilon_E}{2}\sum_{ie}E_{ie}^2
+t\sum_{e=(i<j)}n_e^{graph}
\big[(a_i^\dagger U_{ie}-a_j^\dagger U_{je})b_e+\mathrm{h.c.}\big]+R.
\]

Here kappa=1/2, m=t=1, epsilon_E=1/4 and Gamma=1/10. R is the positive neutral-rewiring Laplacian with a constant Gamma/M coefficient for replacing any removable present edge by any absent edge. It contains no intrinsic-distance suppression.

The main initial ray is the equal coherent superposition of all labelled Q-edge graphs, with vertex vacuum, occupied edge modes and zero fields. It has no preferred spatial graph or topology mask, but it is a special supplied coherent state. Its mean energy is Q/2, variance 2Q and global entropy zero. Its basis support has binom(M,Q) configurations. This support count and a short mathematical description do not derive its physical preparation cost or explain autonomous selection of the ray.

## The integrated formation criterion

The next decisive target is one **unfiltered physical state family**, with a stated preparation and energy/resource scaling, meeting all of the following in the same regime:

1. **A geometry with appreciable physical weight.** A connected relational network covers a nonvanishing fraction of the system, with enough independent cycles for the proposed geometry. Record the event's original probability. Renormalizing a vanishingly rare branch is insufficient without a physical outcome-selection account.
2. **A persistent usable metric.** The same relational structures, rather than only their population counts, retain metric correlations over an increasing physical time window. Quantify offdiagonal history terms or explicitly model any recording operation and its work. A frozen arbitrary graph does not meet the organization criterion.
3. **Three-dimensional intrinsic growth.** For typical roots, ball volumes approach a consistent r^3 regime over a growing range 1 << r << L_N, with L_N increasing with size. Do not fit a dimension to diameter-two graphs or a few saturating radii. Compare against graph/resource/energy controls whose distinctions are stated.
4. **Operational local response.** On that same state and geometry, gauge-invariant source-detector responses depend on intrinsic separation and time. Compare pre-source distance with final distance, and keep geometry-bin redistribution separate from density or conserved-number response. Initial-distance readout must have a valid quantum history or an explicit record; it cannot silently insert a cost-free classical metric.
5. **A controlled size and truncation limit.** Fix physical density and energy scaling, enlarge size and the accessible metric range, and prove residual bounds on the actual detector responses. Account for neutral connections outside the protected network, since hopping can still use them.

These are joint conditions. Positive tests on unrelated finite preparations do not combine into one emergent spacetime state.

## What a valid next numerical family must admit

Q=N spanning circulation can only be a ring. A richer fixed-density family, for example Q approximately 2N as a candidate rather than an imposed host, is needed to test an extensively cyclic network. Its input must still range over all Q-edge graphs, without a spatial seed or phase controller.

The electric-square budget must grow with the network capacity. The necessary condition for connected spanning circulation of cycle rank beta is

\[
B\ge2(N-1+\beta)+n_v+2h_C.
\]

If all Q edges circulate, B>=2Q+3n_v. A degree-four example at Q=2N therefore requires B>=4N even in vacuum, and additional capacity for matter. A calculation below the required budget cannot reject that physical phase. Increasing the budget only to the vacuum threshold can also make a density-phase probe inert in the desired selected sector.

The all-to-all average detector used in this packet has a size-diluted short-lag coefficient. The next propagation test must resolve intrinsic separation with an operationally justified record, rather than seeking a nonzero all-system average as its locality criterion.

## How to decide after that test

If loops and flux grow but the network remains disconnected or random, the result is protection without spatial assembly. If a network spans but distances collapse and source response remains effectively all-to-all, emergent locality fails under those conditions. If a stable metric forms but only after external graph selection, the state-selection problem remains. If the integrated gate succeeds with controlled size dependence, derive its low-energy equations, effective clocks and signal scales before claiming relativistic spacetime or gravity.

The present contribution is concrete: the microscopic model is executable, its loop-response mechanism no longer requires an initial diagnostic filter, and the resource and record assumptions for a larger test are explicit. It still requires an autonomous geometry-selection mechanism and empirical predictions before it can function as a theory of reality.
