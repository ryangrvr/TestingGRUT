# Required formation-to-kinetics export

The existing no-formation runs can serve as regression controls. They cannot choose the field-to-particle or field-to-object map. The following is a calculation interface, not a result already obtained.

1. Fix the release/reheating point, gauge coupling, phase preparation and removal history. A vector source from the older prompt history must not silently be combined with a newer reheating trajectory.
2. Keep the binding field dynamical until its response hierarchy is checked at the relevant momenta and frequencies. A sign change of its effective curvature is a dynamical event, not an externally timed switch. Include the explicit bias if present.
3. Partition energy among coherent fields, hard injected vectors/daughters, scalar radiation, thermal/quasiparticle excitations and candidate cores. Compute medium masses from the actual distributions. Counting the entire release both as a coherent background and as a fully thermal gas is not a matched initial state.
4. State the initial covariance/seed spectrum and its regulator. A selected classical seed is a control. If quantum vacuum fluctuations seed a classical lattice, the approximation and vacuum subtraction need their own validation.
5. Evolve until actual linear-mode backreaction and spatial charge redistribution can be tested. Measure mode spectra, charge correlations, core candidates and energy transfer with grid/volume/seed controls. A fastest linear wavelength and an order-one shape factor do not select the final Q distribution.
6. Identify durable cores by profiles, energy, persistence and stability criteria, not Q alone. Separate local stationary existence from E/Q<m_X. Determine growth/merger/dissolution rates if those processes occur; conserve E(Q) with object-number and binding work.
7. Export outgoing weak-field modes only where a free/quasiparticle basis is valid: time-resolved X, Xbar and scalar distributions, covariance assumptions and a charge/energy ledger. Remove bound/coherent components before assigning particle counts; do not count their energy twice. Track the hard X emitted by core conversion as a nonthermal source.
8. Feed these distributions and the measured or explicitly bracketed formation kernel to kinetics. Preserve the fixed4.4e-10GeV/visible-entropy allowance denominator. Report sensitivity to remaining source/formation alternatives before quoting a physical threshold.

The current packet checks limited vacuum onset problems and two supplied seed histories. It has not executed this full interface or produced f_sym.
