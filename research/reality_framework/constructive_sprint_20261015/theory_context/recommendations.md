# Recommendations for the integrated sprint manuscript

1. Lead with constructive achievements: exact N6 actual-ray charged access and
   N7 topology-dependent formation, persistent matter transport in a supplied
   protected host, newly controlled virtual gauge interactions, and the
   full-law/local-compression bridge. These form one mechanism with explicit
   state and resource requirements, rather than a disconnected list of audits.
2. The new host theorem is stronger than final graph-survival probability. For a
   single host, the exact residual Gram identity gives C_N=Γ/M sqrt(Q(M−Q)) and
   a state-vector error ≤C_N T, so source–detector response differs by at most
   4||O||||B||C_N T. This requires no electric cutoff or flux assumption. The
   true compression includes a=Γ(M−Q)/M times each removable-edge projector.
3. Explicitly attribute host stability to both the graph kinetic normalization
   and, where present, extra flux protection. Every supplied bounded-degree
   host inherits the coarse bound, including wrong-dimensional and random ones.
   The d>2 crossing-time regime is a sufficient bound, not dimension selection.
4. Do not average single-host rms errors over the coherent neutral ray. Multiple
   branches can reach common destination graphs. Either retain the coherent
   residual or model an actual physical record and account for its disturbance,
   energy and preparation. Previous pinching controls already show that this
   distinction changes the response.
5. The continuous description should be called a conditional matching program.
   CP03's supplied Lorentzian composite pole is not a module that can be inserted
   into CP12. No map between their physical state spaces, measures or constraints
   exists. CP01/02 scalar/gravity EFTs are also separately supplied constructions.
6. In the executive verdict say that a coherent quantum graph–gauge working
   model with tested mechanisms was built, while a unified empirical theory of
   reality was not. Quantum rules, external time, one gauge group, fermionic
   inventory and initial preparation remain inputs. No new observational result
   excludes established quantum physics: a simulator implements that physics.
7. Prefer one next integrated finite-time Q=2N unfiltered state test over another isolated
   positive small signal: network weight, persistence, volume/heat growth, and
   density propagation with initial-vs-final distance adversarial comparison.
   Random, thin, fragmented and differing-dimensional controls are essential.
8. Keep successor permission active but do not introduce a new law just because
   the formation question is hard. A local adjacency-mediated graph-move law
   would address all-pairs kinetic locality but imposes a new hypothesis and can
   trap disconnected sectors. It neither solves dimension nor explains quantum
   rules. No successor is frozen by this synthesis.
