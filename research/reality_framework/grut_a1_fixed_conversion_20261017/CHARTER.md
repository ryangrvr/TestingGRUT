## One question and competing mechanisms

Does the minimal fixed-Hamiltonian successor to P1 sustain extensive matter
after it first forms, with conversion and recombination always present?

* Proposed success: nonzero charged hopping and inter-cell interactions
  delocalize/dephase the converted matter enough to prevent collective fuel
  return, while local information propagation survives.
* Plausible failure: a local Rabi return remains accessible even in an
  extensive interacting medium. Coupling more cells does not necessarily
  supply a sink or an autonomous retention mechanism.
* Conventional baseline: reversible finite-dimensional conversion plus a
  confining lattice gauge medium, not a new law of particle creation.
