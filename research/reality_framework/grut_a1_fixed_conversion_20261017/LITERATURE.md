# Primary literature and conventional baseline

* B. Nachtergaele, R. Sims, A. Young, *Quasi-Locality Bounds for Quantum
  Lattice Systems. Part I*, J. Math. Phys. 60, 061101 (2019),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.02428v2 . Local interaction and perturbation
  estimates are established tools. A1 adds a model-specific moving-geometry
  comparison and fuel-count obstruction; a propagation bound is not relativity.
* V. Sharma, K. R. A. Hazzard, *Meson dynamics from locally exciting a
  particle-conserving Z2 lattice gauge theory*, Quantum 9, 1872 (2025),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02791v2 and
  https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2025-10-01-1872 . This already studies moving
  confined bound objects, internal size dynamics and dependence on excitation
  energy. P1 has different statistics/link algebra; it is not identified with
  that model, QCD or observed particles. Bound composites are not a novel
  GRUT phenomenon merely because they occur in this supplied Hamiltonian.

The primary records were consulted during construction. They do not validate
the new proofs or imply priority. All predicted numerical values are internal
consequences of conventional quantum evolution with the declared couplings;
no distinctive empirical GRUT prediction or experimental observation is claimed.
