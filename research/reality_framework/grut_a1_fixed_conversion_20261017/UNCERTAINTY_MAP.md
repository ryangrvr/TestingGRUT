The highest-value discriminant is whether extensive coupling actually removes
the local fuel return. A local isolation estimate, if valid, can answer this
without solving an exponentially large state. Independent neutral-sector
analysis determines what P1's charged hopping means for composite matter,
and prevents interpreting nonzero charged response as a free charged gas.

Challenger obligations before accepting the proposed argument:

1. A global O(N) Hamiltonian norm cannot be used as a small perturbation.
2. Removing five local hoppings in a reference must leave the density terms,
   conversion adjoints and every physical field sector present in the law.
3. The four-site guard must stay empty in that reference, even while the
   outside follows its complete interacting dynamics.
4. An ordinary hard-core hopping norm may bound the physical kinetic block,
   but cannot turn E^2 into a local matter-only interaction for propagation.
5. The measured extensive event must follow from exact resource conservation,
   without assuming independent cells in the physical evolution.
6. A one-pair bound-state band does not establish a many-pair gas, scattering
   theory, low-band preparation, relativistic matter or an autonomous phase.
