## Attempted analytic access to (22)

    G_f,res(z)=1/[z-Omega-J^2 m(z)], Im z>0.           (23)

Its measure nu_f gives G_f(t)=integral exp(-itE)dnu_f(E). Source poles,
singular components and branch thresholds must all be included. The

no positive measure or receiver-overlap estimate for where it does go.

For example, an informative sufficient retention estimate would be

of the uniform half-line hopping J with diagonal Omega:

    dnu_0/dE=sqrt(4J^2-(E-Omega)^2)/(2pi J^2)

time and including any trapped mass. **That measure estimate has not been

by themselves prove it.

Even this measure estimate alone would not supply A_L: physical co-pair
receiver weight needs outgoing generalized eigenfunctions, their velocity
distribution, and a uniform finite-time remainder on I_L. A broad mobile
band and its group-velocity bounds cannot replace that calculation.
