# Path B: a concrete transport design and its activation obstruction

This is a **new microscopic proposal**, not a modification of any Path A
run or a claimed derivation from frozen CP-12. It develops one candidate
far enough to expose a precise obligation before a larger simulation.

Introduce a neutral fermion c_i and let

\[
H_{\rm new}=H_{\rm CP12}\otimes I+
J\sum_{e=(i,j)}g_e F_e(E)
(c_i^\dagger c_j+c_j^\dagger c_i),\quad
F_e=\mathbf1_{E_{ei}E_{ej}<0}.
\]

F_e uses only the primitive fields of that edge, not a selected ring,
embedding, dimension or receiver distance. It is a bounded gauge-invariant
projector. Since it commutes with the new species and the hopping is
Hermitian, the coupling is Hermitian, preserves c-number, and preserves
the original Gauss constraints. At finite N its norm is at most |J|Q,
so it is a bounded perturbation of the original electric-energy operator.

In probe vacuum the new term vanishes exactly. Original neutral geometry
formation is therefore retained rather than blocked by multiplying the
only formation hopping by a projector that kills the neutral ray. The
coupling and H_CP12 generally do not commute: there is genuine coupled
dynamics, although this is not evidence for gravitational universality.

**Inputs remain explicit:** the extra matter species, its neutrality,
the rule F_e and J are new assumptions. Original charged a,b excitations
still propagate by the old law. Treating them solely as geometry-forming
variables would require a low-energy decoupling derivation; relabelling
them does not establish universal matter locality.

## Exact obstruction: the gate is not protection

Consider a ring with circulation flux l>=1 and an active chord 0-j,
j=floor(N/2). The allowed charged configuration has chord fields (1,0),
a particle at 0, and a particle at j balanced by a hole on its outgoing
ring edge with fields (l+1,-l). All other ring fields have magnitude l.
It satisfies the original Gauss and total-fermion constraints. The
chord is not through-flow, so F_chord=0 initially.

A single original hop a_j -> b_chord changes its fields to (1,-1).
F_chord becomes 1. One new c-hop then crosses the chord. For a probe
initially at 0, a specified changed-geometry/receiver-j state has

`|<target|Hnew^2|initial>|=|J|`,

and transfer probability `J^2 T^4/4+O(T^5)` at fixed N. This coefficient
does not decrease with the ring distance. The geometry activation's
diagonal-energy change is **-15/8**, independent of ring flux and N;
simply increasing the circulating flux does not create a detuning barrier.
Exact rational controls check N=4,6,8,12 and l=1,3,8, alongside gauge
preservation, probe-vacuum identity and a nonzero coupling commutator.

These are supplied-state algebraic transfer controls, not a new formation
success or phase-source experiment. No uniform fixed-time large-N
conclusion follows from a small-time coefficient without a remainder
bound. They nevertheless prevent this simple gate from being advertised
as an automatic suppression mechanism.

## What the next design must add and prove

A successful proposal needs a derived, stable low-energy subspace in
which nongeometric channels stay suppressed under geometry formation
and matter back-reaction. A supplied energy gap alone is insufficient:
virtual amplitudes ~v^2/Delta can remain nonzero across growing intrinsic
distance, and there may be extensively many such channels. The required
bound is source-to-receiver and uniform in size, not a shrinking global
operator ratio or instantaneous F_e=0.

Before a large new-model run, require: a declared primitive Hamiltonian;
Gauss preservation and formation identity; its protected spectrum and
leakage/virtual-path bounds; a viable original-neutral spatial phase;
nonzero local source variance; universal coupling of all low-energy
matter; and the same complete-record, full-law receiver tests. Parameters,
sectors, states and operational choices stay in the input ledger.

This design work supplies no earned quotient value and no empirical
prediction. It narrows the construction problem rather than relaxing the
failed ring benchmark or reopening frozen SCOUT-1 under a new name.
