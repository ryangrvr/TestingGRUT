## Method

- **Clock [D].** The radiation background gives a(s) = a_m + C s (a″ = 0) and dτ = a ds, so τ = 20 + a_m s + C s²/2.
  The inverse s(τ) = 2(τ − 20)/(a_m + √(a_m² + 2C(τ − 20))) has no cancellation.
  - C = da/ds = a² d ln a/dτ = a² H.
  - From MATCH_L12 `background` at τ = 20: a = 8.869218738806 and H = 0.025244336. Then a²H = 1.9857962750634526,
    identical to C_match.
  - The implied radiation origin is τ₀ = 20 − 1/(2H) = 0.194.
  - My τ(s) reproduces Codex's (η, τ) columns to 0 (bitwise).
- **Self-tests [C]** (`results/selftest.json`):
  - The spectral operator equals the explicit 5-point FD4 stencil to 4.9×10⁻¹⁶.
  - The force equals −∂H/∂q by finite differences to 4×10⁻⁷ (limited by the finite-difference step).
  - Observed orders: 4.07/4.02 for BM6, 2.0 for velocity Verlet and 4.3–4.5 for RK4.
  - Global U(1) charge is conserved to 2×10⁻¹⁵.
- **Runs** (N = 48, L = 12, about 1.1 s per 10 steps on 1–2 FFT threads):
  - `bm6_h004`: h₀ = 0.004, τ 20 → 800. Records every Δτ = 0.05 through τ = 150, then Δτ = 0.25. The actual maximum
    step is 0.0040 (0.0035 after τ 150).
  - `bm6_h002`: h₀ = 0.002, the same history.
  - `twin_eps1e-6`: a twin test (Lyapunov-type). It restarts from the own τ = 100 field with a random white-noise
    perturbation of 10⁻⁶ × each field's fluctuation RMS in q, and runs to 800.
  - `lastcycle`: a bitwise restart of `bm6_h004` from τ = 400, with snapshots every 4 τ over the last orbit cycle,
    752–800.
- **Diagnostics.** I implemented these from MATHEMATICS.md and analyze.py's definitions, without importing that code.
  - E₀ is H evaluated on the spatial means.
  - The quantity "outside mean" is 1 − E₀/E.
  - Q₀/Q = (χ̄₁π̄₂ − χ̄₂π̄₁)/⟨j⟩.
  - The charge-contrast RMS is std(j)/|⟨j⟩|.
  - The X variance fraction is (⟨|χ_X|²⟩ − |χ̄_X|²)/⟨|χ_X|²⟩.
  - E_φ = K_φ + G_φ + U_φ. The "φ + interaction" diagnostic adds U_mix.
  - First-crossing times are given both as Codex's "first sample ≥ level" and linearly interpolated.

### N1: pointwise agreement is never lost (N48, through τ = 800) [C]

- **Diagnostics, own versus Codex N48 on the same τ grid.** The maximum relative differences over the whole run are:

  | Diagnostic | Max relative difference |
  |---|---|
  | Charge contrast | 2.1×10⁻⁷ |
  | X variance fraction | 2.5×10⁻⁶ |
  | Outside mean | 6.5×10⁻⁷ |
  | Q₀/Q | 6.2×10⁻⁸ |
  | E_φ | 6.9×10⁻⁶ |
  | φ + interaction | 6.9×10⁻⁶ |

  - None exceeds 10⁻⁴ at any time.
  - The difference saturates by τ ≈ 23–35 and is flat afterwards (segment maxima are equal in 20–70, 70–150, 150–400
    and 400–800).
  - My h₀ = 0.004 and 0.002 runs agree to 10⁻⁹ or better. So the residual is Codex's Yoshida truncation error,
    accumulated while its step was 0.004. My values sit closer to Codex's own half-step values (E_φ: 4×10⁻⁷;
    outside mean: 1×10⁻⁸).
- **Fields** (`results/field_comparison.json`). ‖q_own − q_Codex‖/‖q − q̄‖ is:

  | τ | 40 | 60 | 100 | 200 | 400 | 800 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Relative q difference | 1.3×10⁻⁶ | 1.2×10⁻⁶ | 6.9×10⁻⁷ | 7.2×10⁻⁷ | 6.6×10⁻⁷ | 5.3×10⁻⁷ |

  - Momenta: 1.2–7.9×10⁻⁶.
  - φ field: 0.9–1.8×10⁻⁵.
  - The local current density j: ≤ 2.5×10⁻⁷ of its fluctuation RMS.
- **Twin.** A 10⁻⁶ white-noise perturbation at τ = 100 is 7.8×10⁻⁷, 8.4×10⁻⁷ and 7.2×10⁻⁷ in q at τ = 200, 400 and
  800. In φ it grows to 2.2×10⁻⁶ by 800. Twin differences in the diagnostics grow at most about 5× (to 2–7×10⁻⁸).
- **Conclusion [E].** No exponential growth is resolved over Δs = 18.4 of conformal time. So late statistics are
  compared pointwise here, not only statistically.
  - **Caveat:** a few weakly unstable directions with a small projection of white noise could stay hidden. This is
    not a proof that the system is non-chaotic, and I checked only N48.

- **Period.** The coherent zero mode's orbit modulates every diagnostic with period Δs = 0.894. That is
  Δτ ≈ 31 at τ 300 and ≈ 48 at τ 700.

## Limits

- I checked only the main N48 L = 12 seed-1 history, plus an own time-step control and a twin.
- The N32/N64/seed2/cut10/box16 controls, the linear φ control (2.09×), the matching errors, the spectral export
  binning and the full-model scope are not checked by this track.
- Agreement of two codes for the same reduced Hamiltonian says nothing about the omitted Y/Σ/gauge dynamics, the
  quantum matching or a physical particle count.
  - The classical-statistical approximation and its UV caveats are those of Khlebnikov–Tkachev, Micha–Tkachev and
    Epelbaum–Gelis–Wu (arXiv:1402.0115).
  - The reference projections remain non-physical.
- No validation, exclusion or new-theory claim is made.
