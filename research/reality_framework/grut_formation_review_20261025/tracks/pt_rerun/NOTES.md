## Audit (a): infrared width prescription [D/C, own code]

**Eigenvalues and split.**
- The initial Hessian eigenvalues are −2.832376 and −1.999649, giving obstruction momenta 1.682967 and 1.414090.
  CONFIRMED.
- Unstable oscillators: L12 has 9 + 6 = 15 below k = 1.656 and 1.283. L24 has 35 + 25 = 60.
- The smallest stable-side Ω is 0.428 in L12 (k = 1.737) and 0.215 in L24 (k = 1.697).

**Purity and normalisation.**
- In the Hessian eigenbasis, C_qq = ½ diag(1/Ω) and C_pp = ½ diag(Ω), with zero cross-covariance.
- Williamson symplectic eigenvalues of the 12×12 initial covariance are 1/2 to 2.9×10⁻¹⁵. The per-direction
  determinant is 1/4 to 1.6×10⁻¹⁴. The state is pure.
- Normalisation: q = U diag(1/√(2Ω)) ζ, with ζ = (n₁ + i n₂)/√2, so E|ζ|² = 1 and Eζ² = 0. The coefficients are
  c_n = Fζ/√V with c₋ₙ = c_n*, followed by `ifftn(norm='forward')`. This gives ⟨|c_k|²⟩ = 1/(2ΩV), which is the
  continuum ∫d³k/(2π)³ 1/(2Ω), equivalent to ⟨Q₁²⟩ = 1/(2Ω) per real quadrature of each ±k pair. There is no
  factor-2 or volume error.

**Transfer and continuity.**
- The light transfer rows recovered from the width-1 and width-3 factors agree to 4×10⁻¹⁶. The width enters only
  through S, and the φ columns are identical.
- At equal k, the L24 factors (all recomputed by the new code path) reproduce the L12 factors:
  - the old seeded band k ≥ 1.8 to 4.4×10⁻¹²;
  - the new band to 1.1×10⁻¹².
- So at k = 1.8 the prescription is continuous by construction: the same rule applies on both sides.

**Where the discontinuity actually is (NEW).** The prescription is not discontinuous at 1.8. It is discontinuous at

- The (1.683, 1.8) interval holds about 11% of the continuum X variance. L12 samples it with one shell and L24 with
  five, which explains the L12 → L24 → continuum trend.

## Audit (c): Hann statistic [D/C, own code]

**Normalisation.** P(ω_n) = |Σhψe^{−iωs}|²/(MΣh²) sums over n to the h²-weighted mean |ψ|² by Parseval.

**Synthetic free tones** (M = 251, Δs = 0.04, 400 random A, B per mass). The estimator Σ_{ω<0}|ω|P returns m|B|² to:
- 1.3×10⁻⁶ at m = 0.63;
- 1.5×10⁻³ at m = 1, from the A–B beat;
- 1.3×10⁻⁴ at m = 1.5;
- ≤ 7×10⁻⁶ for m ≥ 2;
- but 5% at m = 0.4 and 53% at m = 0.2, from leakage across ω = 0 (resolution 0.626, Hann main lobe ±1.25).

The factors are correct.

**Exact homogeneous orbit** through the same 10-unit window at 109 phases: 1.737785–1.737794, against the exact
1.737788 (5-unit windows: ±7×10⁻⁵; 20-unit: ±2×10⁻⁷). A rectangular window gives 1.708–1.902, so the Hann taper is
needed.

**Rerun data.**
- Wave power within ±2 bins of ω = 0 is 0.11–0.14% of the total. Its negative-frequency action is 2.3–2.9×10⁻⁴ per net
  charge.
- The Blackman taper moves the total by 2×10⁻⁵ / 5×10⁻⁷, but shifts the split by 0.0034 in s 14–24, because the
  coherent amplitude evolves inside the window.
- Hann windows of 5 units give 1.7517, 1.7491, 1.7442, 1.7388 (s 14–34). A 20-unit window gives 1.7465.

**k ≤ 8 truncation (NEW arithmetic on published numbers).** The separately reported k > 8 tail is 0.0013–0.0038
(s 14–24) and 0.0051–0.0100 (s 24–34). It is reference-insensitive: 0.00336–0.00339 for m² from 0 to 26. Adding it
changes the picture:
- the eight totals become **1.7530–1.7546** (s 14–24) and **1.7501–1.7513** (s 24–34);
- the window-to-window decline shrinks from −0.36 to −0.53% to **−0.14 to −0.17%**;
- the L12 coarse/fine difference shrinks from 0.052%/0.160% to 0.048%/0.056%.

Most of the late decline of the k ≤ 8 statistic is cascade into k > 8. The statistic and its normalisation are
CONFIRMED.
