[E] estimate, [A] argued.

## 1. Result in brief

The far-zone outflow is linear and purely outgoing at r = 240 to 960. It decomposes into free quanta. The charge
and energy sum rules close to 4e-5 and 8e-5 at r = 960 with a 48000-unit window, and to 2e-4 at r = 480 with a
24000-unit window.

**Certified (window, radius, method and grid independent):**
- the antiparticle (X̄) number and spectrum: N_X̄ = 256.1 ± 0.4 (0.657% of Q0), with ⟨ω⟩ = 1.70 m_X;
- the neutral (s) quanta: N_s = 109.3 ± 1.1, E_s = 354 ± 4;
- the particle (X) spectrum at p/m_X ≥ 0.03, to within 1.6% per bin (T = 48000; it was p/m_X ≥ 0.05 at T = 24000);
- the X bin at p/m_X 0.02–0.03, to within 5%.

**Not certified, at T = 48000:**
- The momentum distribution of the 1530 X below p/m_X = 0.02 that have passed r = 240 (3.9% of Q0, 6.0% of N_X).
  Their number is fixed by the charge flux; only their spectrum is open.
- The 143 charge units of slow halo still inside r = 240.
- The continuing core emission: lines at ω_c + nν, plus a slow continuum, about 1e2 to 2.5e2 more X [E].

These are sized in section 6.

One further point is outside the classical calculation: the neutral quanta can decay to X X̄ (m_s = √6 m_X > 2 m_X),
which could add up to 109 X̄ (section 7).

## 2. Method

**Independent solver [C].** `kg_radial.c` integrates the same equations as Codex's MATHEMATICS.md, with
U = r psi and Z = r(S-1):

    U_tt = U_rr - (S^2 + 0.1|psi|^2) U,      Z_tt = Z_rr - r S [3(S^2 - 1) + |psi|^2].

The scheme differs from Codex's and from the `independent_solver` track's:
- **space**: 6th-order centred finite differences;
- **time**: 4th-order Yoshida-composed velocity Verlet. This is symplectic, and the Noether charge is conserved
  exactly by every drift and kick. Measured |dQ/Q| ≤ 5e-14.
- **boundary**: none. A hard wall at L, with 2L - max(R_ext) > T, so nothing reflected reaches an extraction radius
  inside the run.

The static neutral profile is solved by Newton iteration on the solver's own grid (residual 2e-13). My
Q0 = 38969.38665302701 and E0 = 38951.47049696 agree with Codex's recorded values to 11 digits.

Each run records three things:
- time series of U, U_t, U_r, Z, Z_t, Z_r at r = 5 to 960, every 0.25;
- the charge and energy inside r = 30 to 960;
- full snapshots.

**Classical-to-quanta map [D].** Positive frequency is a particle, with charge +1 since Qbar = -∫Im(psi* psi_t).
For an outgoing free s-wave, U(R,t) = ∫dω/2π A(ω) e^{-iωt} with k = sign(ω)·sqrt(ω²-1). Then:
- dN_X/dω = 2k|A_out(ω)|² for ω > 1;
- dN_X̄/dω = 2|k||A_out(-ω)|²;
- dE = |ω| dN.

The outgoing and incoming parts at R are separated with U_r: A_out = (ikÃ + Ã_r)/(2ik). For the real neutral
field, dN_s/dω = 4k|Z_out|² for ω > √6. Physical quanta are these values divided by g_X² (g_X = 0.1 [Q], from
bb211d1).

A second, independent route projects a snapshot exterior to R_w onto the box sine modes. These are the exact normal
modes of the grid operator. It gives α_n and β_n, with N_X(n) = 4πh ω_n|α_n|² and N_X̄(n) = 4πh ω_n|β_n|².

**Pipeline validation [C]** (`code/validate_free.py`, `results/pipeline_validation_free_packets.json`). This is a
linear run with exactly known content: N_X = 0.464773, N_X̄ = 0.010170, N_s = 0.0051659.
- The spatial projection recovers all three to 1e-12.
- Fixed-radius extraction recovers N_X̄ to 4e-6 and N_s to 1e-4 at every radius that the packets have fully passed.
- A slow (k = 0.06) packet that has not fully passed a radius shows up as a deficit, exactly as Q_inside(R,T) predicts.

**Certification criteria (stated before use):**
- C1, linearity at R: the slow mass shift δm²_slow = ⟨S²-1+0.1|psi|²⟩₄₀ obeys 10·δm²_slow < k² for the quanta
  counted, and the accumulated phase beyond R is small.
- C2, purely outgoing: incoming number < 1e-3 of outgoing.
- C3, sum rules: N_X - N_X̄ equals the tapered charge flux, and E_X + E_X̄ + E_s equals the energy flux.
- C4, independence of extraction radius and of method: R = 240, 480, 960, and spatial R_w = 120 to 360.
- C5, independence of time window: T = 12000, 18000, 24000 (48000).
- C6, grid and time-step convergence: F1, F2dt and F3h, and Codex's own arrays.

**A channel the classical evolution omits [D/A].** In this action m_s = √6 m_X > 2 m_X, so each neutral quantum can
decay quantum-mechanically to X X̄. The trilinear term is 2 g_X m_X σ|X|², which gives:
- Γ(s→XX̄) = g_X² m_X β/(4π√6), with β = 3^{-1/2}. That is 0.0188 g_X² m_X.
- At g_X = 0.1 this is Γ ≈ 1.9e-4 m_X, a lifetime τ ≈ 5.3e3 in these units, which is shorter than the run windows.

A classical field does not decay spontaneously, and this decay is absent from every run here. The measured X̄ number
is constant from r = 240 to 960 (to 0.13%), so classical stimulated conversion in the far zone is negligible.

The packets' own framework makes s → XX̄ the leading channel [A]:
- bb211d1 treats loss to Standard Model states through Higgs mixing at order κ².
- 59a1a71 uses s → XX̄ as its daughter source: an "instantaneous scalar-at-rest daughter", e0 = √1.5 [Q].

In that case the s quanta add up to 109 X̄ and 109 X, with ⟨ω_X̄⟩ ≈ 1.6. The escaping X̄ number would rise to about
365 (0.94% of Q0), 43% more than the direct classical value. This is conditional on the channel assumption [A]. The
conversion problem should carry both numbers.

## 9. Limits

- **One model, supplied fields.** This is one prepared spherical condensate in one dimensionless model. It is not
  evidence that the fields exist, and it is not a cosmological or 3D source.
- **Classical field.** The quanta map is the coherent-state, large-occupation leading order.
- **Window.** T ≤ 24000 (48000 in F4long). The near-threshold X and the asymptotic core state stay open, as does
  general hot-core leakage.

  absorbers (Codex's receiver, not used here): Berenger; Kuzuoglu–Mittra; Roden–Gedney.

    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/drive.py F1 --h 0.25 --dt 0.05 --T 24000 --L 13000 --rundir $SCR/runs --binary $SCR/kg_radial
    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/drive.py F2dt --h 0.25 --dt 0.025 --T 12000 --L 6700 --snaps 3000,6000,12000 --rundir $SCR/runs --binary $SCR/kg_radial
    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/drive.py F3h --h 0.125 --dt 0.025 --T 12000 --L 6700 --snaps 3000,6000,12000 --rundir $SCR/runs --binary $SCR/kg_radial
    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/drive.py F4long --h 0.25 --dt 0.05 --T 48000 --L 24600 --rext 5,30,60,120,240,480,960 --snaps 12000,24000,36000,48000 --rundir $SCR/runs --binary $SCR/kg_radial
    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/analyze_formation.py $SCR/runs F1 tracks/spectrum/results/F1_analysis.json   # likewise F2dt, F3h, F4long
    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/analyze_codex_trace.py <reproduce data dir> <ff49e5d packet dir> tracks/spectrum/results/codex_trace_spectra_REPRODUCTION_DATA.json
    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/occupation.py $SCR/runs/F1_snap12000.bin 120 tracks/spectrum/results/F1_occupation_T12000.json
    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/summary.py

Free-packet validation and halo diagnostics:

    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/prep.py packet --L 4000 --h 0.25 --out $SCR/runs/pk.bin
    $SCR/kg_radial $SCR/runs/pk.bin $SCR/runs/pk 0.05 6000 0 0.25 300,600,1200 3000,6000 1.0 600 60,300,600,1200
    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/validate_free.py $SCR/runs tracks/spectrum/results/pipeline_validation_free_packets.json
    python3 tracks/spectrum/code/halo.py $SCR/runs/F4long_snap48000.bin 45 240 tracks/spectrum/results/F4long_halo_T48000.json

Run times on one shared core: F1 about 20 min, F2dt about 12 min, F3h about 25 min, F4long about 65 min. Raw binary
outputs (about 0.5 GB) stay in the scratchpad (review/spectrum/runs/). They are not copied into the repository.
