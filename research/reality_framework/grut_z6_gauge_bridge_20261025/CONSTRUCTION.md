# Explicit gauge/mediator construction

This is an additional **tree-level effective model**, not a replacement of
the pinned Candidate 1 action and not a completed theory of quantum gravity.
It makes the proposed protection/charge-source link concrete enough to test.

## Action and supplied point

Retain Einstein gravity, the Standard Model, and Candidate 1's real binding
field phi, reporter D and complex X. Replace the global X symmetry in this
new branch by a gauge U(1), with gauge coupling **e**, distinct from the
existing binding coupling g_X=0.1. Add complex Y and Sigma with charges

| Field | Gauge charge | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| X | 1 | Light populated field |
| Y | 3 | Heavy mediator |
| Sigma | 6 | Higgs field leaving a residual gauge Z6 |

Use canonical covariant kinetic terms and -F_mu_nu F^mu_nu/4. The potential is

\[
V=V_{C1}+\lambda_\Sigma(|\Sigma|^2-h^2/2)^2
 +M_Y^2|Y|^2+\lambda_Y|Y|^4
 +[\kappa Y^\dagger X^3+\mu\Sigma^\dagger Y^2+\mathrm{h.c.}].
\]

All terms are renormalizable. In this construction there is no independent
bare Sigma-dagger X6 operator; the leading X6 coefficient is generated by Y.
Additional gauge-allowed portal couplings are set to zero at tree level as
an explicit matching choice. Quantum corrections and quantum-gravity-scale
operators have not been calculated or shown to preserve those choices.

Neither these inputs nor the old scale/yield are selected by closure.
The reference vector mass is 6 e h=60000 GeV. The heavy scalar masses are
positive. Standard Model fields are uncharged under the new U(1); this
bosonic extension introduces no chiral gauge anomaly. Its perturbative
threshold matching and light-scalar hierarchy remain unsolved.

## Stability of the full tree potential

Let x=|X|, y=|Y| and s=|Sigma|. A sufficient Young inequality is

\[
2|\kappa|yx^3\le {\lambda_X\over2}x^4
                         +{\lambda_Y\over2}y^4,
\qquad |\kappa|^4\le {\lambda_X^3\lambda_Y\over27}.
\]

The supplied kappa is below this sufficient bound. The remaining Y mass is
M_Y^2-2|mu|s. For s/M_Y<1 it is nonnegative. Above 1, minimize the remaining
Y quartic to obtain, in M_Y units,

\[
V\ge(s^2-.005)^2-\tfrac12(s-1)^2
 \ge .490025\,s^2>0.
\]

The other pieces of V_C1 are nonnegative at the supplied tree point. Thus
this **full tree potential** is bounded and has a zero-energy reference
vacuum at X=Y=0, |Sigma|=h/sqrt(2), phi=+/-v (and D=0). The vacuum offset
here is the same adopted calibration issue; this is no solution of Lambda.
The multiple phi signs and the reporter's symmetry are not UV-protected by
this X-sector construction.

## Matching: one torque term and a compulsory companion

For fixed Sigma and sufficiently small fields/derivatives, define

\[
J=\kappa X^3,\qquad
\mathcal D=M_Y^4-4|\mu\Sigma|^2>0.
\]

Ignoring lambda_Y|Y|4 only during the quadratic elimination gives

\[
Y_*=-{M_Y^2J-2\mu^*\Sigma J^*\over\mathcal D},\qquad
\Delta V=-{M_Y^2|\kappa|^2\over\mathcal D}|X|^6
 +\left[{\mu\kappa^2\Sigma^*\over\mathcal D}X^6+
 \mathrm{h.c.}\right].
\]

In unitary gauge Sigma=h/sqrt(2), write Delta V=beta6 |X|6+
(alpha6 X6+h.c.). The **same** mediator parameters fix both coefficients.
One cannot add the angular torque while dropping its radial companion
without checking that companion's effect. At the reference point,
2|alpha6|/|beta6|=sqrt(2)|mu|h/M_Y^2<1. The negative sextic in this
truncation does not imply that the full bounded potential is unbounded.
The truncation fails before such an inference would be legitimate.

Tests compare the formula to an independent real 2-by-2 matrix solve,
Fourier extraction of both operators, complex coupling/gauge choices and
the nonlinear mediator equation including lambda_Y. Integrating out Y also
generates derivative operators. It requires frequencies/momenta below its
mass and |kappa||X|^2/M_Y^2 small. The charge-six Higgs must remain in its
broken phase. These requirements must be checked anew in an early condensate.

The old ground-profile X6 loss calculation can be rescaled by
|alpha6 M_red^2|^2 **conditionally**, after matching the light parameters
and neglecting the extremely small heavy-sector profile corrections at
GeV core amplitudes. That rescaling is not a full modified-action soliton
or hot-core calculation. Other allowed channels and loop operators remain
to be tested. A residual charge modulo six alone does not prove the
absolute stability of a large-charge object.
