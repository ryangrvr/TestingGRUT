# Credits and actual read scope

This construction uses established gauge-Higgs, tree-level matching,
Noether and Affleck-Dine ideas. No novelty claim is made for these methods.

- **Krauss and Wilczek (1989)**, *Discrete gauge symmetry in continuum
  theories*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 62, 1221,
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.62.1221 . Read the publisher abstract;
  full text was behind the displayed access wall. It credits the distinction
  between discrete gauge charge and a merely postulated global discrete
  symmetry, and the associated black-hole/Aharonov-Bohm argument.
- **Banks and Seiberg (2011)**, *Symmetries and Strings in Field Theory and
  Gravity*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.5120v2 . Read the abstract and the
  PDF's relevant charge-p Abelian-Higgs discussion, especially section 2.3,
  equations 2.15 and its neighboring paragraph. This gives the explicit
  continuous-gauge-to-Zp mechanism. Their no-global-symmetry discussion is
  an argument/conjecture with the stated quantum-gravity setting, not a
  calculation of our X-number Wilson coefficient.
- **Harlow and Ooguri (2019)**, *Constraints on Symmetries from Holography*,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05337 . The previous C* packet read its primary
  text; this packet relies on that scoped audit and makes no new extension
  of its AdS/CFT result to a theorem for every compact cosmology.
- **Affleck and Dine (1985)**, *A new mechanism for baryogenesis*,
  Nucl. Phys. B249, 361-380,
  https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(85)90021-5 . Bibliography and publisher
  abstract/search excerpt verified. The full publisher page/PDF could not
  be retrieved. This credits early coherent-scalar charge generation; no
  claim of independently reproducing that original full model is made.
- **Kusenko and Shaposhnikov (1998)**, *Supersymmetric Q-balls as dark
  matter*, https://arxiv.org/html/hep-ph/9709492v3 . Read the introduction
  and section 3's condensate/instability setup. It connects early charge
  generation with subsequent evolution into an approximately conserved
  regime and possible fragmentation. Its supersymmetric potentials and
  cosmological initial conditions are not our scalar model's inputs.
- **Enqvist and McDonald (1998)**, *Q-Balls and Baryogenesis in the MSSM*,
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9711514 . Read the abstract/introduction,
  the potential in equation 1 and the early-history/A-term passage around
  PDF page 10. Its stabilizing higher-power term and chosen cosmological
  preparation are explicit; importing the mechanism's name does not import
  those structures into Candidate 1. The arXiv version stamp is March 1998;
  a later typesetting date printed in the PDF is not its publication date.

The present mediator algebra and compact phase reduction are derived in
the companion files. The conditional phase histories are newly executed
author calculations. No independent Claude rerun or completed literature
review by Claude is claimed.
