Builder task: specify an explicit bosonic gauge completion of the proposed
X6 deformation; derive its correlated operators and the homogeneous compact
Gauss constraint; test the matching and the effective phase dynamics. Keep
the original scalar action and results intact. This is an additional model,
not a silent alteration of Candidate 1 or a quantum-gravity completion.

Use charges X=1, Y=3, Sigma=6 and a renormalizable mediator interaction.
Distinguish a bounded full tree-level potential from a truncated effective
potential. State every new coupling/scale. Distinguish charge transferred
between fields from net gauge charge creation. State the derivative and
field-amplitude conditions for integrating out heavy modes.

A radiation-era phase diagnostic may prescribe f proportional to a^-1,
fixed Higgs amplitude and H proportional to a^-2. Label that preparation;
it is not a solved radial history, inflation model, fragmentation simulation
or cosmological charge prediction. An initial phase, amplitude and entropy
remain inputs even when the same operator enters genesis and late leakage.
