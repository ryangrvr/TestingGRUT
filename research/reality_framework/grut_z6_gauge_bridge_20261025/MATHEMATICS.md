# Charge transfer, not net gauge-charge creation

## Compact Gauss law and the physical phase

In the full theory, integrate Gauss's law on a compact boundaryless slice:

\[
Q_X+3Q_Y+6Q_\Sigma=0.
\]

The two interaction vertices have charges -3+3=0 and -6+2*3=0.
Generating approximate X number therefore transfers charge to other
internal fields. It never creates net gauge charge. A massive vector does
not remove this accounting requirement; its Higgs sector participates.

At leading derivative order after removing Y, use canonical amplitudes

\[
X={f\over\sqrt2}e^{-i\theta},\quad
\Sigma={h\over\sqrt2}e^{-i\sigma},\quad
D_t=\partial_t+ieqA_0.
\]

For a homogeneous mode, Gauss's nondynamical equation gives

\[
eA_0={f^2\dot\theta+6h^2\dot\sigma\over f^2+36h^2}.
\]

The gauge-invariant relative phase is delta=6 theta-sigma, with a constant
coefficient phase absorbed into its definition. Substitution gives

\[
L_{phase}=a^3\left[{I\over2}\dot\delta^2-U_6\cos\delta\right],\qquad
I={f^2h^2\over f^2+36h^2},\quad U_6={|\alpha_6|f^6\over4}.
\]

Here alpha6 is locally constant only if h and the heavy matching remain
constant. Individual charge densities satisfy

\[
n_X=6I\dot\delta,\qquad n_\Sigma=-I\dot\delta,\qquad
\dot n_X+3Hn_X=6U_6\sin\delta.
\]

The omitted Y derivative operators correct the leading individual-current
dictionary. The full three-field gauge identity remains exact.
With P=a^3 I delta_dot,

\[
\dot P=a^3U_6\sin\delta,\qquad a^3(n_X+6n_\Sigma)=0.
\]

The phase energy at fixed n_X is

\[
\rho_{phase}={n_X^2\over2f^2}+{n_X^2\over72h^2}.
\]

The second term is the compensating Higgs-sector energy. The ordinary
single-field phase equation is recovered for f/(6h) much less than one.
Its inertia factor in unitary gauge is Z=36h^2/(f^2+36h^2): Z=1/2 at
f=6h and about 0.0385 at f=30h. The homogeneous constraint reduction does
not require a slow A0, but a local single-field theory for inhomogeneous
modes additionally requires energies/momenta below the vector mass.

## The conditional radiation-era diagnostic

For illustration only, prescribe f=f_i/a, h constant, H=H_i/a^2, a_i=1
and zero initial charge. This is the adiabatic quartic-amplitude law used
as an input, not a solved radial history. The full model's correlated
radial sextic and possible Higgs motion must be checked before applying it.
Write N=ln(a), r=f_i/h, p=P/(H_i f_i^2) and
eta=|alpha6| f_i^4/(4 H_i^2). Then

\[
{d\delta\over dN}=p(36e^N+r^2e^{-N}),\qquad
{dp\over dN}=\eta e^{-N}\sin\delta.
\]

The source is integrable. Independently of phase evolution,

\[
|p(N)|\le |\eta|(1-e^{-N}),\qquad
|p(\infty)-p(N)|\le |\eta|e^{-N}.
\]

The second bound does not extrapolate a fixed instantaneous torque to a
Hubble time. It follows from the explicitly supplied amplitude history.
The numerical diagnostic tests 36 histories, zero-phase and CP-conjugate
preparations, a tighter tolerance, the source quadrature and the two-field
phase reconstruction. Large-r runs test the kinematic approximation;
they are not presented as points certified within the illustrative UV EFT.

If visible entropy is conserved and s_i is supplied, the generated yield is

\[
Y_X={6H_i f_i^2p_\infty\over s_i}
 ={3|\alpha_6|f_i^6\over2H_i s_i}\mathcal J,\qquad
\mathcal J=\int_0^\infty e^{-N}\sin\delta(N)\,dN.
\]

At delta_i=0 and zero initial momentum, charge stays zero. Opposite initial
phases give opposite charge. The new action's constant interaction phases
can be removed by field rephasings; a physical initial relative phase
remains. No initial phase ensemble, inflationary preparation or unique
cosmic sign has been selected here.

The same alpha6 enters the old conditional ground-state response:

\[
\Gamma_Q=\Gamma_{Q,6}^{ref}|\alpha_6 M_{red}^2|^2.
\]

Once f_i, H_i, s_i and the phase history are fixed independently, this is
indeed one coupled genesis/survival problem. With those inputs free, using
the observed abundance to choose them is a fit, not a prediction. The
Higgs/mediator thresholds, accompanying radial term, symmetric energy,
fragmentation and actual daughter source are further physical obligations.

## Two useful cautions about the hand estimates

If |c5|=exp(-S) with unit prefactor, the old survival benchmark requires
S about 9.01. This is a conditional translation; no instanton action,
prefactor or quantum-gravity coefficient has been computed.

M_red/sqrt(Gamma5/Gamma6) is about 0.389 GeV. It is an overlap scale from
two different sourced scattering problems. The actual reference central
canonical amplitude is f0 about 2.42 GeV, and |X0| about 1.71 GeV. Neither
value is inferred from that rate ratio. The ratio is a dimensional sanity
check, not a new amplitude measurement.

Nor is there a universal f^(2d) formation-loss law. At a frozen frequency
and fixed response operator the local X^d force is proportional to
f^(d-1), and its radiative power is quadratic in that force. Fractional
charge loss also divides by the core charge. Changing a real forming core
changes its size, frequencies, Green function and charge together. A hot
history needs its own source calculation, as the running review proposes.
