# Primary-source context and novelty boundary

1. T. Pichler, M. Dalmonte, E. Rico, P. Zoller, S. Montangero,
   *Real-time Dynamics in U(1) Lattice Gauge Theories with Tensor Networks*,
   Phys. Rev. X 6, 011023 (2016),
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04440v2 and
   https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.6.011023.
   This already studies real-time charge production, electric strings,
   confinement and correlations in one-dimensional gauge models. Pair
   conversion and positive string energy are not a new GRUT phenomenon.
   P1 has commuting hard-core statistics, finite partial link shifts,
   controlled local fuel and generated recorded adjacency; it is not asserted
   to be the Schwinger model, relativistic QED, or a model of observed matter.

2. B. Nachtergaele, R. Sims, A. Young,
   *Quasi-Locality Bounds for Quantum Lattice Systems. Part I*,
   J. Math. Phys. 60, 061101 (2019),
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.02428v2 and
   https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5095769.
   The fixed-prefix connected propagation argument is standard finite-range
   local-interaction control. P1 must additionally pay for moving geometry
   and actual preparation; this does not turn a supplied generator locality
   rule into emergent universal locality or a relativistic causal cone.
