# Reviewer handoff — challenge the construction

No independent mathematical reviewer or experiment is claimed. Passing the
diagnostics only tests the implementations at their declared finite sizes.
The priority is a proof challenge, not another solver branch.

## Load-bearing questions

1. **Physical kernel and primitives.** Check signs of G_i and U, both field
   boundaries, conversion and negative hopping. Check the new M_i constraint
   and empty-head/child gates on co-located neutral pairs. Check invariance
   on ambient valid-word registers, not only a compressed matrix.
2. **Preparation factorization.** Verify that K selected disjoint-bond
   three-state sectors really factor in the common-space reflected reference,
   for an arbitrary actual conditional receiver state, with the original
   finite receiver and all storage states retained.
3. **Complete moving-end comparison.** Re-derive e_p=tau_(a+1)(1.1pi), including
   time-dependent loading, both conversion directions, fuel coherences,
   head/parent bare gates and the reference's orthogonal complement. A missing
   boundary drive would invalidate the main preparation theorem.
4. **Uniform probability.** Check (7), the exact product event distribution,
   the Bernoulli tail, conversion of full-state error to probability, original
   root averaging, and the S9-dependent joint-event formula. Do not replace
   the operational pure state by a classical Bernoulli mixture.
5. **Energy and controller.** Check the rotating-frame load work, detuned
   conversion-switch work, local density activation work and nonselective
   recording energy. Pointer purity, feed-forward, fixed capacity and the
   switch that conserves matter number must remain supplied. No autonomous
   reservoir, cyclic controller or irreversible creation is proved.
6. **Positive-energy locality.** Verify the ambient site-plus-link bound,
   all boundary terms in (21), absence of cut electric drive on the reference,
   treatment of passive fuel/mass, and composition with the real prepared
   state's support error. D1's matter-only isometry must not be substituted.
7. **Nonzero created-matter response.** Independently evaluate the bond-source
   variance and short-time coefficient for arbitrary allowed parameters,
   including surviving pair coherences. Check the Taylor constant and
   actual-preparation correction. It is not a one-site phase-source claim.
8. **Inherited formation.** Separately challenge S9 at 82bcab3; P1's original-
   trial corollary is conditional on its validity. P1 diagnostics do not
   validate S9 or lower its threshold.

## Scope if an estimate fails

Keep exact conversion algebra and diagnostic results; downgrade the affected
theorem and dependent corollaries. Do not repair a failed preparation bound
by postselecting a buffer, retiming pulses, changing fuel detunings after
measurement, discarding adjoints or restarting the closed formation solver.
An autonomous controller or always-on pair-production architecture must be a
separately named successor with a new preparation and energy proof.
