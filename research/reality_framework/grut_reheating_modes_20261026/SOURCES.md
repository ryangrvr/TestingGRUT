## Primary literature and methods

- [Figueroa and Loayza: Geometric reheating of the Universe](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02689v1): Nonminimal curvature reheating context; not validation of our model or numbers.
- [Lozanov and Amin: The charged inflaton and its gauge fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.05663v3): Primary gauge/longitudinal perturbation, Gauss, linear-to-nonlinear and initial-state context.
- [Sfakianakis and van de Vis: Preheating after Higgs inflation](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.99.083519): Self-resonance and gauge-mode literature; our spectator model does not import its metric or Higgs-inflation results.
- [Page and Wootters: Evolution without evolution](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.27.2885): Credit for internal-clock conditional-observation framework; not a GRUT state-selection result.
- [Klauder: Quantization of Constrained Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0003297): Projection-operator/coherent-state method credit for the finite preparation context; our explicit integer-charge group average is supplied.
- [SciPy DOP853](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.integrate.DOP853.html): Installed coefficient tables and numerical-method attribution; Radau used as a distinct method.

The equations, finite-time transfer outputs and matched-orbit diagnostic are computed here. Coherent-state projection, relational clocks, gauge constraints, Floquet analysis and nonminimal reheating are established methods. The toy conjugation argument is standard symmetry reasoning applied to the stated preparation conditions. No source is presented as validating GRUT or selecting its cosmic state.
