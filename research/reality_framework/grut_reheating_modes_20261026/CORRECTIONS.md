2. **The toy symmetry is antiunitary conjugation, not physical C or CP.**
   The finite mode truncation has no complete set of particle/antiparticle

   a nonzero torque sign. A K-invariant density matrix has zero expected
   torque. In the coherent family, `E=E0+.226274 cos(delta)` and
   `torque=6 dE/d delta`; energy minimization selects a torque-free phase.
   This is the standard antiunitary symmetry argument applied to the toy,
   not a new fundamental principle or a continuum cosmological theorem.

   Zero hidden/SM mutual information was imposed by taking a product
   boundary state. The numerical zero only checks that construction; it
   was not an independent physical test of cosmic preparation. Correlated
   conditional preparations need their own density kernel.

   The theorem does not force a new CP-violating operator into the action.
   A supplied asymmetric state, a holder with physical invariant phases,

   can give opposite signs in individual patches while its ensemble mean
   vanishes. None of this derives a preferred sign or our cosmic phase.
   A complex coefficient removable by field rephasing is not automatically
