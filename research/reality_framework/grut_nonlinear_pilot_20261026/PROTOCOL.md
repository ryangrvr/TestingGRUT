Main inputs: the cold case-L orbit at the critical-amplitude crossing, nQ/m^3=23.943819 and rho/m^4=142.5. Give the charged amplitude/phase low-momentum fluctuations of relative RMS1e-6. Begin with a performance/accuracy pilot. Follow the nonlinear stage on at least two spatial grids, a finer time step, a larger-box or second-seed control, and a third spatial grid if resolution or candidate-core claims require it. Report finite-volume and seed sensitivity. Diagnose ultraviolet transfer; do not interpret classical cutoff equipartition as a quantum relic abundance.

Record full-field energy/charge, neutral vacuum/domain fractions, charge contrasts and spectra. Identify only labelled candidate cores, examine expanded masks and persistence, and distinguish local charge reversals from free antiparticle counts. A vacuum-reference particle projection must carry its interaction-residual diagnostic. No final f_sym or physical kinetic threshold may be inferred from this cold pilot, which has no vector heat, quantum collision network, selected cosmic state, heavy/gauge field dynamics or expansion over this short interval.

If cold nonlinear evolution generates persistent structure, quantify it and provide reproducible snapshots. If it disperses or remains cutoff/seed-dependent, report that result. The purpose is to derive one part of the formation input rather than insert an object population by hand. No AI subagents are used.
