# Main checkpoint and public data

[GRUT_3D_mixed_N96_checkpoint_20261026.npz](https://chatgpt.com/library?file_id=libfile_8910cd588d4081919d389ac7162d9897&account_id=personal) contains the complete double-precision main96³ final state at m_X t=240. Access follows the owner account. It includes q,p,N,L,time and the supplied preparation metadata. DATASET.json retains the exact identifiers, hash and engine hash.

The checkpoint is a cold classical field configuration, not a particle catalog or a cosmological result. Public reproduction uses the code and exact Fourier parameters. Intermediate raw files are regenerable and their hashes are in SNAPSHOT_MANIFEST.json. VISUALIZATION_FIELDS.npz contains block-averaged charge, phi and energy densities for the two main final states; those reduced arrays are for viewing and are not used for the catalogs or energy tests.
