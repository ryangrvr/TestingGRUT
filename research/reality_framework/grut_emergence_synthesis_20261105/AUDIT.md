## What remains untested

three-dimensional fermion evolution, kinetic splice, object mass distribution,
portal likelihood, vacuum mechanism or empirical consciousness test was run.

The actual cosmic state and unique physical subsystem selection are not derived.

The new symmetry qualification is a logical scope distinction, not a re-run of
the older toy-model conjugation calculations. The map is a schematic dependency
diagram, not a measured causal graph or scaled cosmic clock.
