# GRUT — Integrated working theory of reality

**Scientific synthesis · 27 November 2026**

Physical systems can retain traces of earlier interactions. When a later interaction can access a trace, it can use information about an earlier state or an unobserved condition to change what happens next. The GRUT program has shown this explicitly for repair, construction, prediction and preservation of quantum information. The common framework is a global physical dynamics with specified subsystems, internal records, admissible operations and finite resources. Its successful examples operate within established physics. The current program verdict is **B/C**. The effective framework survives, and the fundamental laws and cosmological state remain underdetermined.

This manuscript consolidates the research recorded at [the F5 head](https://github.com/ryangrvr/TestingGRUT/tree/d3dd471a0ad69a95cd6f0dc1eba23fc0695a39cd), including its controlling corrections. It gives an account of the current framework and its limitations. The companion [foundational evidence report](COMPLETE_EVIDENCE_MAP.md) carries the claim-by-claim warrant. Earlier publications, versioned theories and the TestingGRUT campaigns have distinct evidence boundaries. The synthesis adopts the governing result of each relevant campaign in its own domain.

## 1. One interacting whole

In the surviving working account, all degrees of freedom needed for a closed calculation are included in the model. An observer is able to interact with only a physically accessible set of degrees of freedom. A local physical state is the restriction of an encompassing state to its observable algebra. This can lose predictive information. Two equally prepared local marginals can predict different future local observations when their correlations with the rest differ. A reduced subsystem can acquire irreversible-looking dynamics while the combined model obeys reversible equations.

The common objects are a positive normalized state $\omega$, a physical observable algebra $\mathfrak A$, a microscopic dynamical specification, and a declared access and measurement structure. In finite quantum models dynamics may be a local Hamiltonian and a supplied calculation parameter. In constrained examples it may be a physical state space obtained from a gauge constraint, with evolution expressed through conditionals relative to an internal clock. In an effective field theory the metric, fields, action, matching conditions, regime and state preparation must be given. The alternatives share the logic of states, interactions and observed probabilities. A map from one alternative to another requires its own derivation. [Mathematical foundations](MATHEMATICAL_FOUNDATIONS.md) · [Assumption ledger](FUNDAMENTAL_ASSUMPTIONS.md)

An internal record is a physical degree of freedom whose statistics depend on an earlier state, event or condition. In a gauge formulation the event and conditioning effects must be physical effects. If an internal reference can describe the whole retained state, a change of reference transports the state and the effect together. F5 constructs two such clocks with finite readout precision and matter-dependent response. Their probability is $2/3$ for one matched joint clock-and-record procedure. The same numeral on the two ideal clocks yields $1$ and $1/2$ for different procedures. If the writer is removed the matched probability is zero and the frames still agree. These comparisons separate agreement about a physical event from selection of the physics that produces it. [F5 proof](../grut_f5_closure_consistency_20261127/DERIVATIONS.md)

One physical whole can have many admissible preparations. In finite models even an exactly stationary constrained state can show conditional recording and erasure. Maximum mixing on the specified history subspace gives a different conditional account. Older stationary equivariance and observer-algebra results likewise leave a choice of state and access. A low-entropy preparation, an inflating geometry, a signal source and blank information carriers are physical inputs whenever they are used. Closure in this program gives no independently selected cosmological initial condition. [Prior closure evidence](../grut_f5_closure_consistency_20261127/EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md)

## 2. Interaction, records and causation

A record can be correlated with a physical outcome without being used. The developmental pillar measures a task under specified access and resources, then compares the best setting with access to the record against a matched setting that lacks it. One operational value is
\[
{\cal R}_{\mathcal T}=V_{\mathcal T}(\text{record accessible})-
V_{\mathcal T}(\text{no record access}).
\]
Here a task determines the outcome set, score, admissible controls, preparation, work and deadline. When the record-access agent can reproduce every no-access policy under the same accounting, the value is nonnegative. Building or maintaining a memory can cost energy, time and resources. Under comparable resource limits those costs can eliminate a persistence advantage. The observable dependence, admissible menu and resource account give the quantity its meaning.

The D series supplies concrete mechanisms. A reversible write transfers information to a record and can restore a prepared system state. A structured reservoir absorbs defects and records their count. An accessible historical record selects which equally organized configuration to restore. Redundancy then allows detection and correction without a fault flag within a specified fault class. In D5, a simple local rule and an acquired seed record support construction and completion of a previously unbuilt output in the declared ordered history. In D6, an internal assay learns a sign and reuses that information for two tasks. In D7, the sign record guides compensation on a new entangled payload. Its optimum is a trace-norm formula for a dephasing channel and a payload-independent accessible record. These results are causal claims within named models, with clock, memory, information and fuel resources exposed. [Developmental effective theory](DEVELOPMENTAL_EFFECTIVE_THEORY.md)

The I series specifies where these mechanisms join. I1 exhibits a microscopic contact model with acquired-record-conditioned quantum recovery and a controlled fresh-bath effective limit. Reusing the bath can invalidate that limit. I2 finds useful passive and fuel-driven organization under a fixed physical policy menu, and the best cost for the registered task uses no sensor. I3 gives a different task where a previous sample of an inaccessible changing condition carries enough predictive value to beat every screened no-record controller under the stated restrictions. A perfect present cue would remove that need. This establishes task-relative physical necessity of *some usable information* when current access and time preclude an adequate substitute. It does not establish a universal persistence benefit of memory.

Historical identity in these experiments denotes retention or recovery of a specified physically accessible invariant or organization. It has no additional metaphysical variable. If two histories leave the same available state, the system cannot distinguish them beyond their prior probabilities. A record or copy can also be wrong, and symmetry alone cannot decide which of two conflicting copies is historically authoritative. Measurement, recording, preservation and adaptive use are therefore distinct operational accomplishments.

## 3. Spacetime and the laws of matter

The program has derived several ways in which calibrated observations reveal structure. It has also found explicit physical freedoms that survive the tested data and consistency conditions. The operations matter. Port responses can identify a minimal reciprocal model while leaving a physical spring graph ambiguous. A calibrated complementary port or interaction can resolve specified ambiguities. Prescribed-background probes can distinguish some metric changes; finite causal access and conformal covariance give exact obstructions in other protocols. None of these results constructs dynamical spacetime from bare observations.

A separate nonuniqueness survives in matter coupled to a dynamical metric. Two Einstein–matter examples have the same complete homogeneous metric and stress history with valid classical constraints, but their effective scalar propagation differs. They share the conventional gravitational law. Gravitational consistency at this level imposes no universal effective sound speed. In the electric-charge problem, gauge structure and anomalies allow a family even when continuous global phases can be explicitly broken. The physical free-neutron charge eliminates the noncanonical members of that family. The symmetry conditions themselves do not select the empirical member. [Foundational obstructions](FOUNDATIONAL_OBSTRUCTIONS.md)

F5 adds a different test. If a cycle can close by undoing the accumulated matter unitary, any finite list of unitaries can fit inside it. The full F5 construction supplies interacting clock references, exact comparisons between frames, material records and independent imperfect readout effects. The freedom survives all of those additions, under the supplied cyclic boundary and access assumptions. An independently justified boundary or interaction law could have consequences that are absent in this class. The archive therefore closes the specific universal-closure route to law selection. It leaves the broader question of deeper necessity open.

## 4. Effective fields and cosmology

Local quantum field theory, general relativity and their effective correspondence enter the working account as established inputs. Where field content, couplings, symmetries and a preparation are stated, conservation laws, responses and thresholds can be computed. In some earlier campaigns a prepared local microscopic model generates a continuum kernel or reduced effective memory. These are conditional limits. The archive leaves the physical origin of a preferred local algebra, Newton's coupling, the complete gravitational quantum constraint theory, particle parameters and the observed vacuum density open. Similar macroscopic response forms do not identify their underlying microscopic sources.

Candidate 1 is a compact, mathematically specified matter-sector EFT with a useful prepared charge-bound solution. Its numerical free-carrier binding survives the declared one-loop comparison. This does not give a formation history, complete quantum stability or an observable population. Later matter-sector variants add interactions and cosmological hypotheses. Their favorable benchmarks, failures and changes of matching remain attached to those versions. The H55 production-to-kinetic handoff fails stress and work checks even where number and energy are reproduced. No observed dark matter population is attributed to Candidate 1 by this synthesis. [Candidate 1 scientific assessment](CANDIDATE_1_STATUS.md)

The vacuum density term in a covariant EFT is distinct from shifting the zero of a fixed-background Hamiltonian. The project has not derived the measured acceleration scale or a new initial-state probability measure. Conditional fits and proposal-dependent failures remain evidence about those filters. They give no selection of the actual origin or future of the universe.

## 5. Place of observers and consciousness

A physical observer model specifies an accessible algebra, an interaction, an instrument, a record and a feasible inference or action. Clock correlations, measurement records and predictive records support such models. They show how internal systems can gain and use limited information about other degrees of freedom. The examples take the quantum probability rule as input. They do not give an observer-independent solution to outcome selection, or a mathematical or empirical bridge from records and control to subjective experience. Internal observation is an operational concept in this manuscript.

## 6. What is established and what would change the verdict

The strongest conclusions are bounded. Closed quantum and effective classical systems can acquire usable records, act on them and achieve measurable task differences in precisely described regimes. Known consistency relations constrain channels and geometry when their physical inputs are supplied. F1–F5 locate several independent obstructions to inferring a unique law from those relations. They do not establish one universal impossibility result or a common countermodel to every possible assumption.

A deeper GRUT law would require an independently motivated hypothesis or a surviving measurable distinction beyond the strongest conventional explanation, with the relevant preparation, dynamics and errors fixed beforehand. No such principle is established in the pinned evidence. The path to further work is therefore by specific scientific questions with reliable boundaries. Candidate 1 can be evaluated as a matter EFT. Its next discriminating calculation would concern the binding and survival of physical objects, with any cosmological programme requiring admissible production and matching. The effective framework and its negative results stand without these future calculations.
