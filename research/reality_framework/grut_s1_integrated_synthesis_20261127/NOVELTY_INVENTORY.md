A mathematical advancement can be publishable while its dynamics and probabilities are standard. This audit identifies known mechanisms and near precedents. Priority for a particular formula or counterexample requires a separate mathematical literature investigation.

The legacy audit also distinguishes uncalculated physical hypotheses from hypotheses that a specific counterexample actually falsifies. The surviving response framework, EFT calculations and negative results do not restore those claims.
