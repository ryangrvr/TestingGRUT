The uniform-walk reference nu_0 is absolutely continuous. Their total
variation in the convention used here is EXACTLY 2: on the finite support
of nu_K their masses are 1 and 0; on its complement they are 0 and 1.

directly establish ||nu-nu_0||_variation<=1/6 for the infinite source.
One would need an infinite-source limiting-absorption/scattering estimate,
or another explicitly controlled argument. Finite artificial far boundaries
also prevent labelling their eigenvectors as infinite-source traps.
