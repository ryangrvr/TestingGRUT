## Dynamics, preparation and operational parts

In the bosonic finite reference, the field Hilbert space is the span of occupation tuples below the registered free-energy cutoff. Its algebra is the full matrix algebra on that span, H is the projected interacting cosine Hamiltonian, and omega is its ground state. The mode basis, interval, boundary charges, quantum structure, preparation and access rule are supplied. Compression does not preserve exact spatial commutation merely because the uncut field is local.

The actual interaction and readout are specified by the Weyl observable W and its Kraus operators. For the compressed parent,

$$
K_\pm=\frac{I\pm W}{2},\qquad
K_o=\sqrt{\frac{I-W^\dagger W}{2}},\qquad
\sum_{r=+,-,o}K_r^\dagger K_r=I.
$$

The positive square root exists because W is a contraction. The overflow marks failure of this cutoff to retain a branch; it is not a new physical particle or adjustable energy reservoir. Ordinary histories are precisely

\quad U(t)=e^{-iHt}.

This is the integrated theory's instrument rule applied to a specific interacting realization. It includes measurement disturbance. Gaussian two-point reconstruction and energy-population propagation are additional reductions, whose sufficiency must be tested rather than assumed.

## One closed construction containing clocks and records

The probabilities above can be embedded in a finite closed quantum model without treating the detector outcomes as interventions from outside that enlarged model. This is an existence construction under supplied access permissions, not a microscopic laboratory realization.

Give each detector a ready register and orthogonal outcome states. The map

$$
V:\ |\psi\rangle|\mathrm{ready}\rangle\longmapsto
\sum_r K_r|\psi\rangle|r\rangle
$$

is an isometry by Kraus normalization and extends to a unitary on a sufficiently large finite apparatus. Keep the outcome registers. Once an overflow is recorded, later detector links act as identity on that branch and write a skip label. This realizes the absorbing record convention without deleting the field state or erasing information. The final readout may aggregate all records containing an overflow.

Let U_j be the work unitaries comprising the prescribed propagation intervals and detector dilations, including these conditional skips. A propagation clock with positions 0,...,J has the time-independent Hamiltonian

$$
H_{\rm closed}=\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}g_j\left(|j+1\rangle\langle j|\otimes U_j+
|j\rangle\langle j+1|\otimes U_j^\dagger\right),
\quad g_j=\frac{\Omega}{2}\sqrt{(j+1)(J-j)}.
$$

Writing V_0=I, V_{j+1}=U_jV_j and T=sum_j |j><j| tensor V_j gives

$$
H_{\rm closed}=T(\Omega J_x\otimes I)T^\dagger.
$$

Starting at clock zero, the work state conditioned on supported tick j is V_j rho V_j^dagger. At the transfer time pi/Omega the last tick is reached with probability one in this ideal clock. Reading its stored outcomes therefore reproduces the calculated field histories exactly within the finite parent. The joint state evolves unitarily; total entropy and the distribution of this supplied total Hamiltonian are conserved. Field energy alone need not be conserved during detector coupling.

This embeds interacting dynamics, internal clock readings and persistent outcome registers in the same formal construction used by Construction 1. At conditional clock ticks beyond a write, the subsequent work links retain that register label. Reverse clock traversal can undo the writing, so this is not an irreversible record or an unlimited lifetime. An actuator conditioned on a stored label can be added using the already declared record-control architecture. That would be information-dependent action under an explicitly supplied interaction, not a derivation of agency or consciousness.

The propagation-clock Hamiltonian encodes the parent dynamics in its work unitaries; it is not an independently derived local field-plus-apparatus Hamiltonian. The ready registers, pure clock preparation, clock couplings and detector dilations are resources and assumptions. The construction does not prove that these operations are local in a relativistic field theory, energetically economical, robust under arbitrary disturbance, or selected by closure. This analytic embedding was derived here; a full enlarged-matrix numerical execution was not performed. The field-history and reduced-history calculations themselves were executed.

## Effective descriptions: what actually survives

An effective state must predict a declared experiment family. A local marginal, occupation count or energy distribution is not sufficient merely because it has a familiar physical interpretation. The exact backward-effect span of the integrated theory gives a formal sufficiency criterion; numerical reductions also need controlled approximation errors.

D1-01 supplies a direct example in the new interacting parent. Dephasing in the energy eigenbasis preserves the immediate energy populations and first-outcome calibration, yet changes later history probabilities by 0.0221 and 0.0375. Retaining coherent amplitudes in 32 low interacting eigenstates reduces the discrepancies to 0.00240 and 0.00427, but misses its fixed 1e-5 target. The derived branchwise projection bound is valid within the finite parent and too loose to certify that target. This is an unsuccessful compression with a successful diagnosis of missing information.

The result is compatible with the finite parity-record construction: an initial calibration can hide correlations that later interventions reveal. The field example extends that statement to this adopted interacting Hamiltonian. Neither example proves that all coarse descriptions fail or that classical physics cannot emerge in an appropriate regime.
