# D5 resource ledger

The target family has four words at each registered N. The rule and
seed phase select among them using two classical bits. For the fair
ensemble the final displayed tape has entropy two bits, irrespective
of its length. The record starts blank and acquires one bit of
mutual information with h at the assay. This is a correlation with
an existing variable. The independent coin contributes a third bit
to global ensemble entropy. Each fixed selector pair, with fair h,G
and coin, starts with three bits of von Neumann entropy. The full
unitary dynamics and the reversible pulses preserve it.

The route requires L+1=256N+1 sites. Each site has a vacuum plus a head
with 18,144 internal states. Only one head is occupied. The Hilbert
dimension of that physical sector, including tape, is
`(L+1) * 18144 * 3^(3N)`. There are L local exchange terms, at
most three sites per term. There are `2N+N/2+4` declared update
links. Each link is either a repeated local transition, one of four
junctions, or an identity on work. Head support is localized at the
start. The phase bit, blank memory, backups, known selectors and
vacant route are preparation resources. The site count and the number
of weights to realize grow linearly with N, even though the couplings
have one compact formula. Choosing N also chooses the layout. The
model does not price fabrication or precision of these couplings.

Each of the N rebuild operations deposits into an initially blank B,
then swaps. Each of the N maintenance operations does the same using C.
The old symbol, including damage, is acquired in the archive. The
two pure blank banks have total capacity `2N log2(3)` bits. No external
sink or resetting is in the model. Exact reversible overwriting of an
arbitrary N-symbol qutrit word while producing the same output requires
at least 3^N orthogonal environment outputs, by preservation of inner
products. This bound concerns arbitrary input words, rather than the
smaller ensemble of D5 disturbances. No archive reuse or saturation
cycling was demonstrated.

The one-head spectrum is that of omega times a spin-L/2 x component,
with omega=4J/(L+1). Its mean energy at preparation is zero in the
chosen convention, its variance is `4 J^2 L/(L+1)^2`, and its maximal
absolute eigenenergy is `2 J L/(L+1)`. Each energy distribution gives
weight `binomial(L,k)/2^L` to energy `omega(k-L/2)`. This equality is
exact for each head and work preparation at position 0. Label, coin,
fault and intervention selectors, and the null share the law. No
control changes the couplings in the internal trials. A conserved
head-energy offset merely shifts these values. Transfer time
`pi(L+1)/(4J)` grows linearly with N at fixed maximum bond energy J.

The external physical X is a real permutation. Just before its
application the adjacent clock amplitudes differ in phase by -i, and
the history-basis matrix elements remain real after the permutation.
The adjacent contributions to mean H are consequently purely imaginary
before taking their real parts. Both mean energies across the pulse
are zero. Its energy probability law can still change. The trace
distance estimate against an ideal stage insertion bounds the change
in total variation by twice the square root of the stage-tail bound.
An external actuator and its timing were supplied. Zero mean work in
this modeled Hamiltonian does not price the apparatus or the
low-entropy preparation of its data and clock.

The resulting scaling is expansion from a compact description,
not extensive new information. Prepared blank capacity, physical
couplings, site count and runtime all grow with the output length.
