This decision uses the pinned foundational evidence in F1
and the F2 separation between a compatible set of response
maps and the dynamics generating those maps. The developmental
work is an effective-theory pillar. Candidate 1 is a distinct
matter EFT with its own tests. The five entries below summarize
the strongest inherited result, the earlier boundary or failure,
and a bounded question. Only universal metric coupling receives
a detailed construction in F3.

suggested by the archive. In a shared metric theory, the
normal deformation algebra compares changes of slices.
Different collective propagation cones can be covariant
within that geometry. A stable counterexample with fully
matched matter–metric histories and an explicit exact
conditional restriction can locate the missing assumption.
