# Analytical feasibility and conventional gate

F1 showed that exact operational response can leave geometry
underdetermined. F2 supplied extra calibrated access sufficient
for specific identification tasks while retaining gauge and
causal ambiguities. Neither result selects the dynamics of a
metric. F3 starts with a genuinely varied metric in a standard
coupled action and asks what its physical consistency can force.

· [F2 joint limits](../grut_f2_complementary_interactions_20261126/JOINT_CONSISTENCY.md)

The candidate inference separates three mathematical objects.
The gravitational deformation algebra encodes how hypersurfaces
compose. The metric variation gives the common stress coupling.
The matter principal symbol governs its propagation. The ADM
and hypersurface-deformation results and the soft-graviton
argument already state conditional constraints in different
domains. Covariant first-derivative matter is a broad allowed
class. Its acoustic principal symbols can differ from the
null metric even on regular positive branches.
[ADM](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0405109)
· [Teitelboim](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(73)90096-1)
· [Weinberg](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.135.B1049)
· [Garriga and Mukhanov](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9904176)

The bounded mathematical test makes the obstruction more
specific. Match the entire homogeneous stress and evolving
metric between the first-derivative noncanonical model and a
canonical scalar. Derive the same constraint algebra off shell
and compare their physical characteristic symbols and quadratic
kinetics. Derive separately the necessary affine kinetic form
if every scalar polarization is imposed on the metric-null
cone across a rank-two branch. Parallel backgrounds can conceal
an interaction. Exact first-derivative and Gram-rank algebra can
settle the comparison with no parameter sweep. Existing acoustic
and multifield results are the conventional comparators.
[Babichev, Mukhanov and Vikman](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.0561)
· [Langlois et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.78.063523)

A relativistic finite-density scalar supplies an important
conventional control. Its light phonon has an acoustic speed
although the microscopic canonical matter equations have
metric-null fronts. The matter integration and any quantum
interpretation are confined to their own declared regimes.
[Relativistic scalar effective theory](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP09(2022)066)
· [Gravity EFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9405057)

The feasibility gate supports an exact obstruction calculation
and a conditional classification. The mechanism is already
contained in established generally covariant matter and
gravity. The test seeks precision about what additional
assumptions purchase the common propagation result, not a
new adjustable gravitational interaction. There is no basis
for exploratory production beyond the frozen exact checks.
