## Scientific comparisons and numerical acceptance

- Passive binding is operationally resolved if, in the registered
  comparisons, attraction increases bonds per particle from the
  uniform start and improves survival versus J=0 by at least the
  corresponding preset margins.
- A separately detectable driven organization requires the primary
  uniform constant-active arm to raise the end bond fraction over
  the cohesive passive arm by the frozen margin. Report survival and
  any driven first-exit failure separately.
- A net record benefit requires the optimized active class with
  sensing to beat the full no-feedback control family by the frozen
  margin at a common feasible budget in **both** reset conventions,
  and a contributing policy must use unequal receptor-gated
  coefficients and contain the registered past information. The same rate pairs in
  the sensor-off arm prevent a constant-control-only benchmark.
  Report all tested budgets and empty sets. If no margin passes,
  close this claim in the tested family and retain any smaller gain.
- The independent computational comparison uses physically labelled
  fuel molecules rather than count states. Compare probabilities,
  killed survival and fuel transitions by positive uniformization
  with the declared Poisson tail tolerance on the separate small
  grid. Check generator column sums, detailed balance on every edge,
  energy/heat, fuel budget, nonnegative entropy production,
  relative-free-energy identity and exact equilibrium counts.
