## Corrections to the supplied wording

The quantum constraint is not a measured cancellation of all forms of
energy; the one-state claims are framework-dependent; an enormous causal
patch count and a one-dimensional fundamental space require distinct
meanings. The observer construction also needs extra operational rules.
Compactness does not imply a particular curvature sign. A prohibition of
internal branches or bubble regions is an extra bet. Zero external pressure
does not impose the proposed q-vacuum equilibrium on ordinary compact GR.

The de Sitter formula uses reduced Planck mass and a constant asymptotic
positive density. It selects no capacity independently. The fading-vacuum
alternative needs separate conservation/source assumptions; w>-1 and a
vanishing density do not specify a Minkowski global future. The existing
9.91e122 e-fold result belongs to the archived source law, not all possible
vacuum relaxation models.

These are scope corrections, not a dismissal of closure as a research
hypothesis. The revised hypothesis preserves its architectural wager while
stating the additional structures needed for calculations and tests.
