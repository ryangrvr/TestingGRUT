# C*: a tagged closure hypothesis, with its state spaces specified

24 October 2026 · AInstein / Codex · provisional hypothesis audit

**C* is a research postulate: every physical clock, environment and observer
belongs to one dynamical whole.** This does not, by itself, select the action,
state, topology, quantum-gravity inner product or vacuum density. The scalar
candidate and numerical work remain effective models within this proposal.

Tags retain the supplied meaning: **[known]** is established physics or a
stated mathematical consequence; **[conjecture]** is a published unproven
proposal or an extrapolation beyond its demonstrated models; **[bet]** is
an additional GRUT assumption. A conditional theorem keeps its conditions.

## Three spaces must stay distinct

1. The canonical/kinematic effective description can contain a clock,
   fields and record systems. Constraints select physical predictions.
2. A factorizing holographic fundamental description can instead quotient
   many apparent states into one state. This is an optional research branch
   here, not a theorem already implemented by the current scalar action.
3. An effective observer or causal-patch description can have many useful
   operational states. Their dimension and accuracy require a dictionary.

On an ordinary one-dimensional Hilbert space every linear operator is a
scalar and there cannot be two distinct nonzero orthogonal record projectors.
Thus simply adding the words “internal correlations” does not construct
ordinary quantum records on that space. Non-isometric coding and observer
prescriptions are substantive additional structure. Conversely, a Hamiltonian
constraint alone need not make the physical Hilbert space one-dimensional:
the supplied finite-clock counterexample has two constrained states and
nontrivial relational correlations. These elementary facts do not refute
the published observer proposals; they identify the dictionary C* must supply.

The unique fundamental vector also does not select a unique EFT history
without the encoding map and observer probability rule. GRUT's requirement
that record-keeping matter arise internally remains a construction obligation;
it is not discharged by citing a framework that already assumes an observer.

## What follows conditionally

**[known, Maxwell sector on compact boundaryless space]**
Integrating Gauss's law gives total Abelian gauge charge zero:

\[
Q_{gauge}=\int_\Sigma\sqrt h\,D_i E^i=0.
\]

All charged sectors must be counted. This is not a species-by-species
neutrality rule, a constraint on an ungauged scalar Noether charge, or a
measurement of charge outside our observable patch. Candidate 1 presently
has global U(1)_X and no X gauge field; this particular Gauss constraint
does not forbid its supplied X asymmetry. Its quantum-gravity protection
is nevertheless missing; [the charge audit](GLOBAL_CHARGE.md) makes that
problem quantitative.

**[known, GR observable construction]** Gauge-invariant statements need
appropriate relational or dressed definitions. This does not erase the
gravitational effect of a constant vacuum term. Changing that constant
changes the metric equations even though local nongravitational forces
are unchanged.

**[bet, additional]** Excluding eternal inflation, internal bubble regions
or Everett branches is not a consequence of “one whole.” Such regions or
branches can all lie within that whole. A prohibition would require a
separate dynamical or interpretive postulate with an operational consequence.

## The two vacuum pictures are alternatives

### A future de Sitter capacity

For four-dimensional Einstein gravity, reduced Planck mass M and a constant
positive density dominating the asymptotic future,

\[
S_{dS}=\frac{8\pi^2 M^2}{H_\Lambda^2},\quad
\rho_\Lambda=3M^2H_\Lambda^2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\rho_\Lambda=\frac{24\pi^2M^4}{S_{dS}}.
\]

**[known, conditional semiclassical identity]** This is the supplied formula
with its Planck convention and horizon specified. The reference H0 and
OmegaLambda used in our earlier calculations give S_dS approximately
3.31e122 nats; these are adopted cosmological inputs, not a prediction.
Interpreting exp(S_dS) as the dimension of the entire universe's fundamental
Hilbert space is a further conjecture.

**[bet, missing]** An independent capacity-selection law could make the
identity predictive. “Capacity equals all eventual records” is not yet
such a law: records, coarse-graining, erasure, duplicated information and
the infinite-future limit need definitions. Fixed finite capacity plus
the saturated identity implies a fixed positive density, not decay to zero.

### A relaxing vacuum

**[bet]** A specific vacuum sector might relax toward zero. For a separately
conserved positive dark-energy component in an expanding universe,

\[
\dot\rho=-3H(1+w)\rho,
\]

so decreasing density implies w>-1. If a Lorentz-invariant vacuum instead
transfers energy to another sector, its intrinsic pressure can remain
p=-rho while its effective evolution mimics w>-1. The receiver must enter
the total conserved stress tensor.

Neither decreasing density nor w>-1 fixes the global future. The explicit
counterexample a(t) proportional to t^2 has w=-2/3 and rho proportional to
t^-2, yet retains a future event horizon. A Minkowski limit, termination of
acceleration or recollapse requires more dynamical information.

This branch cannot simultaneously assume a fixed finite entropy selected
by a future de Sitter horizon and an asymptotically zero density through
that same saturated relation. A different information assumption is needed.

The archived q-relaxation comparator needed about 9.91e122 e-folds for its
specific natural coefficient. That is a failure of that worked mechanism,
not a theorem that every possible relaxation law takes that long. The
DESI situation also remains unsettled: its July 2026 Ly-alpha collaboration
update reports agreement with the standard model and explicitly keeps
several interpretations open. Existing hints are retrospective motivation,
not evidence specifically identifying GRUT disequilibrium or a future fate.

## An operational picture under the hypothesis

**[bet]** There is one physical dynamics, with no physical external agent.
**[known within the adopted EFT]** Its fields can support persistent bound
patterns, and those patterns can become correlated with other internal
systems. **[construction required]** Some must realize robust records and
usable clocks under a stated probability rule. Gravity couples to their
stress tensor and to the vacuum coefficient. **[bet]** A future completion
may identify dark energy as a nonequilibrium residue. The low-entropy
beginning, exact couplings and state-selection rule remain inputs today.

This picture is an interpretation of a research program with prior art.
The present numerical tests establish scoped field-theory constructions;
they do not establish this cosmology or an original fundamental principle.

## Reachable decisions

- Specify an observer/encoding rule before using the one-dimensional
  global-space branch to claim records, clocks or a predicted state count.
- Specify a vacuum action and source law before predicting w(z), a capacity,
  the measured density or the far future. Fix its parameters independently
  of the claimed prediction and test a named likelihood.
- Test compact topology separately from curvature. Omega_k<0 means positive
  FLRW spatial curvature; compact flat tori have Omega_k=0 and compact
  hyperbolic spaces can have Omega_k>0. Missing matching circles constrain
  accessible sizes/topologies, not all compact spaces.
- Supply UV protection or a quantitative approximate-symmetry lifetime
  for X. A residual gauge symmetry is a candidate repair, not an already
  constructed completion.
