# Established physical baseline

| Framework | What it establishes | What still has to be supplied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page–Wootters relational dynamics | Suitable stationary joint states can describe evolution as conditional statistics relative to a clock. In additive deparametrizable examples the remaining state follows Schrödinger evolution with an internal label. [Page and Wootters](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.27.2885) | The joint constraint, clock POVM, Hamiltonian, state and probability rule. A conditional snapshot calculation alone does not implement arbitrary multi-time measurements. |
| Constrained Hamiltonian systems and relational observables | Gauge-invariant complete observables can encode the value of one variable relative to another. For suitable quantum constraints, group averaging constructs or selects physical states and invariant effects. In a common physical quantization, compatible reductions can describe those observables in different coordinates. [Rovelli](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0110035) · [Dittrich](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0411013) · [Höhn, Smith and Lock](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.00033) | The actual constraint and its anomaly and inner-product analysis, boundary conditions, suitable frame domains and accessible relational effects. A projector formula for a compact gauge group does not extend without checking domain and normalization for noncompact or gravitational constraints. |
| Quantum reference frames and interacting clocks | Conditional states and physical observables can transform together under frame changes. Clock–system interactions can modify conditional dynamics. Recent interacting-reference constructions derive unitary frame changes in suitable conventions even when the gauge action does not factor, with the physical inner product treated explicitly. [Clock changes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04153) · [Interacting clock dynamics](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2019-07-08-160/) · [Interacting reference frames preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28732) | The operational identification of the same effect, the degree of correlation, measurement precision and accessibility. Global reversibility of a change of ideal coordinates is compatible with lost information after a partial trace. |
| Realistic and periodic clocks | Noisy clock readings can mix conditional states. Periodic clocks require care about repeated crossings and which observables are invariant over a full cycle. [Gambini, Porto and Pullin](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/6/1/045) · [Chataignier et al.](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ae46d0) | A particular resolution kernel, detector model, or regime for approximate dynamics. F5's reported error is a finite conditional POVM, with no assumed universal decoherence law. Its finite cyclic matters are periodic by construction. |
| Quantum cosmology and the multiple-choice problem | Different choices before quantization can yield inequivalent reduced theories. Changing frames inside a single physical theory is a separate operation. The no-boundary proposal gives a state prescription under its geometric and dynamical hypotheses. It is additional to posing a constraint. [Isham](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9210011) · [Kuchař](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218271811019347) · [Małkiewicz](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/32/13/135004) · [Hartle and Hawking](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.28.2960) | Common physical states and effects, or an explicit mapping between separately quantized theories if it exists. The global state of our universe, the physical clock at cosmological scales and tests of possible state prescriptions remain independent problems. |

## Operational comparisons in F5

1. **Frame covariance.** A unitary map between complete ideal charts represents a common Dirac state and effect. Each chart is an allowed description, and its anchor is a coordinate choice.
2. **Imperfect clocks.** The report POVM and its likelihood are properties of the physical measurement. Two equal reported numbers can describe different events even when the clocks have been accurately calibrated.
3. **Same event.** Hold fixed the joint experiment and its conditional filter. Transform their effects and the physical state to compare Born probabilities. Approximate identification has the norm bound in the derivation.
4. **Restricted access.** If the observer discards B or cannot interrogate correlations, it can lose distinctions the full A and B charts preserve. The reduction is a channel with its own domain, not a reference-frame symmetry.
5. **Inequivalent physical descriptions.** A B slice with a stopped branch cannot access the full physical Hilbert space. A genuinely different separately quantized theory need not admit any common event map. Conversely, swapping the writer with identity at fixed readout is a physical change of the model with measurable consequences while each theory remains internally consistent.

The adopted framework already explains the equality of the matched event. The nontrivial feasibility question was whether any sampled dynamics or states would be ruled out when both references, their coupling and the finite errors are included. The obstruction is achieved by an explicit embedding with controlled boundary and access assumptions, not by treating arbitrary pairs of clock marginals as equivalent.
