# Novelty review

The feasibility gate asked whether imposing agreement of genuinely matched events between two interacting finite physical references leaves all finite matter propagators available. It selected this constructive obstruction because equality of expectation values by itself could not decide whether the requirement of two good physical clocks would narrow the admissible model class. The conventional comparison includes both reference systems and their measurement access.

| F5 ingredient | Established comparison and result boundary |
| --- | --- |
| A stationary physical state has changing clock-conditioned states | Page and Wootters give the original construction. Poulin explicitly studies translation to a fully relational toy model without an exterior frame. The finite pure history with blank memory in F5 is a specified state. [Page and Wootters](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.27.2885) · [Poulin](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0505081) |
| A fixed operator can encode a gate table | Feynman clock dynamics and circuit history constructions already encode supplied unitary evolutions in fixed operators. F5 uses a compact cyclic shift with a compensating wrap and gives a second exact clock chart on the same physical state space. [Feynman](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02650179) · [Aharonov et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0405098) |
| Matched relational observables and changes of reference | The Dirac, conditional Schrödinger and relational Heisenberg descriptions are equivalent in the scope of the relational dynamics trinity. Quantum reference-frame studies implement physical clock switches and transform states and operations. This is the explanation of F5's exact matched-event agreement. [Höhn, Smith and Lock](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.00033) · [Höhn and Vanrietvelde](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04153) · [Castro-Ruiz et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-16013-1) |
| Nonfactorizing clock interactions | Smith and Ahmadi derive interacting-clock conditional equations. Chataignier and Smith give group-averaged interacting reference frames, with a gauge-fixing measure and unitary frame changes under their assumptions. F5 uses transversality of the explicit discrete slices, for which the ideal gauge-averaged reading effect is $I/N$ and the measure is a scalar. The special normalization does not hold for all interacting clocks. [Smith and Ahmadi](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2019-07-08-160/) · [Chataignier and Smith, preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28732) |
| Imperfect clocks, access and periodicity | Mixing conditional states for uncertain clock readings and contraction of distinguishability under partial trace are standard consequences of the measurement and access model. Periodic-clock analyses already require care about quantities that fail to repeat. F5 exactly checks one discarded-reference control and one controlled wrap. It does not posit universal gravitational clock noise. [Gambini, Porto and Pullin](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/6/1/045) · [Chataignier et al.](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ae46d0) |
| Covariance within one theory versus different quantizations | The standard multiple-choice problem concerns choices of time and quantization with no assured shared physical Hilbert space. The F5 comparison starts within one constrained quantization and resolves no general equivalence between separately quantized models. [Isham](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9210011) · [Kuchař](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218271811019347) |

The explicit family, phase-sensitive probe and access controls give a scoped theorem with a checkable counterexample to selection. The audit establishes direct precedents for its components and no distinctive effect beyond standard physics. Scientific priority for the precise finite construction is unestablished. The conditional-probability estimate uses ordinary operator-norm continuity and the converse uses convex mixtures of states. Neither changes the dynamics or the probability rule.
