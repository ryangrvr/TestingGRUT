# Physical comparison

There is no distinctive physical effect to test after the analytical gate. The following quantitative predictions are a conventional laboratory comparison for the admitted finite constraint, state and operations. A laboratory would externally prepare and calibrate an encoding of the history state. It could test the stated internal correlations with that encoding, with no inference from the laboratory's own exterior to a cosmological one. Photon experiments already illustrate the difference between conditional evolution and stationarity of a joint state under specific operations. [Moreva et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.89.052122)

| Physical procedure | Frozen fixture | Standard Dirac and relational comparator |
| --- | ---: | ---: |

| Same joint procedure with writer omitted and inverse wrap updated | $0$ | $0$ |
| Joint phase probe, correct transport in B versus erasing the sign | $1$ versus $0$ | $1$ versus $0$ |

A finite-dimensional experiment could encode two five-level registers and three qubits, prepare $J|+\!,0,00\rangle$ and implement the fixed readout. The single-shot diagonal comparison measures the two clock labels and the record, with the known one-step error kernel or its calibrated pointer realization. The phase test requires a coherent joint measurement on the other clock and matter in the chosen chart. Implementing both charts in independent preparations additionally requires phase and state calibration. A laboratory circuit can realize the relevant isometry with supplied controls. It would not implement a cosmological constraint or demonstrate an origin of time.

With $M>0$ independent filter hits and the ideal calibration, the number of positive records is binomial with parameters $(M,2/3)$. Its conditional standard error as a fraction is $\sqrt{2/(9M)}$. State preparation and readout error add to that budget. With matched states and certified operator-norm effect errors, the [calibration inequality](DERIVATIONS.md#2-a-quantitative-same-event-criterion) bounds the allowed probability discrepancy. An observed departure outside a declared statistical and calibration budget would reject that realization of the model, or the matching of its instruments. It would supply no GRUT-specific discriminator because the gridded predictions agree exactly with the conventional calculation. No laboratory data are claimed here.
