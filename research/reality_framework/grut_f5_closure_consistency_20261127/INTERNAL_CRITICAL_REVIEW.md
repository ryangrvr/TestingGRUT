# F5 internal critical review

I reviewed the frozen model and ran a separate exact calculation. The proposition survives as an obstruction in the declared cyclic, programmed constraint class. Exact agreement for a transported effect is an identity of ordinary quantum theory. The construction is needed to show that genuinely different conditional matter records can coexist with that identity and correlated finite clocks. No restriction on arbitrary finite unitary prefixes or on their seed density operators follows.

## Algebra and assumptions

In the $a,r,s$ labeling the shift is conjugate to the bare cyclic advance by the block diagonal unitary with blocks $V_{s,r,a}$. Each block preserves $r,s$ and the transition factors telescope, including the wrap. The fixed vectors are precisely
\[
J|s,r,\psi\rangle=N^{-1/2}\sum_a |a,r+\nu_s a,s\rangle V_{s,r,a}|\psi\rangle.
\]
Their physical inner product agrees with that of the seed space. Pick the seed by taking $\sqrt N$ times the $a=0$ component of any fixed vector. Its other components then follow from the recurrence, which proves surjectivity of $J$ onto the zero sector. The spectral logarithm has integer eigenvalues, and the finite cyclic group and the continuous unitary generated by this logarithm project onto the same invariant subspace. The gauge parameter carries no observable elapsed time or physical energy premise.

An A chart extracts $\sqrt N$ times the $a$ component and keeps $b,s,\mathcal K$. A B chart extracts $\sqrt N$ times the $b$ component and keeps $a,s,\mathcal K$. The B chart visits the unique $a=\nu_s^{-1}(b-r)$ for each $s,r$. At each ideal anchor the two maps $W_A,W_B$ are unitary, since $r$ gives distinct complementary clock labels after fixing $s$ and the selected reading, and each block acts unitarily on $\mathcal K$. The reduction probability before the normalization is $1/N$ for every seed and every reading. The change of chart is $W_BW_A^\dagger$. The chart anchor is a coordinate for the full physical state. Treating it as a further filter in the joint measurement changes the experiment.

For a kinematic effect $E$ the compressed effect is $J^\dagger E J$ and also equals $J^\dagger\overline E J$ for the gauge average $\overline E$. Frame independence transforms the state and every effect, including a state-dependent conditioning effect $F$. The numerator and denominator agree separately. If $0\le E_i\le F_i\le I$, approximate pulled-back effects on the same state give
\[
\left|\frac{\langle E_A\rangle}{\langle F_A\rangle}
      -\frac{\langle E_B\rangle}{\langle F_B\rangle}\right|
\le \min\left(1,\frac{\|E_A-E_B\|+\|F_A-F_B\|}{p_*}\right),
\qquad \min_i\langle F_i\rangle\ge p_*>0.
\]
This follows by adding and subtracting a term with the same denominator and using $\langle E_i\rangle/\langle F_i\rangle\le1$. Its calibration and probability assumptions are ordinary statistical ones.

## Scope of the freedom claim

For any finite gate list choose a large enough odd prime $N\ge5$ and assign the cumulative products to the first available $r=0$ prefixes. Spare relative sectors support the independent clock phase. Applying the inverse total prefix at the wrap guarantees a cyclic shift. This operation is a supplied boundary and the gate table is supplied to the constraint. The theorem gives exact freedom for arbitrary finite unitary histories and states at the declared resolution. Its isometry also extends to a separable infinite-dimensional $\mathcal K$ because the cyclic sum remains finite and all prefixes are bounded unitaries. Information capacity under energy, time and access constraints would require more premises. The theorem gives no exact autonomous continuum limit for the subsystem, no unbounded recorded cycle count and no derivation of local dynamics, energy constraints, or an independently selected clock Hamiltonian. Distinct choices of regulator, physical inner product or constraint quantization are also outside the equivalence theorem.

The conventional comparator is Page–Wootters conditioning and Dirac group averaging. Transporting quantum reference frames and covariant POVMs already implements joint state and effect changes. Periodic clocks introduce intrinsic cycle and winding restrictions. History-state constructions already encode gate lists into a fixed operator. The interacting-clock reference-frame preprint by Chataignier and Smith also treats generalized group-averaged effects and frame-dependent reduction measures. The benchmark supplies joint probabilities of simultaneous readouts and stored records. It does not derive arbitrary sequential instruments or settle the multiple-choice problem in independently quantized theories. The result combines these ingredients in a specific finite counterexample and does not claim methodological priority or a laboratory discriminator against standard quantum mechanics.
