## What is independent of what

| Proposed statement | Status and consequences |
| --- | --- |
| No physical exterior | The system includes all the modeled degrees of freedom. Its complement for a subsystem depends on the specified algebra or tensor split. This inclusion supplies neither a bath state, bath reset nor Markov approximation. The fact that we describe one whole is compatible with many interiors. |
| No external physical time | An ordinary closed finite system can still have a mathematically supplied evolution parameter. Promoting an internal subsystem to a clock, or implementing time reparametrization as a constraint, are additional structures. Constrained gauge evolution and internal clock readout have different meanings. |
| No external physical observer | All apparatus and records can reside inside the model. Statistics still require a probability rule and a specified operational effect. No measurement-free selection of a unique outcome is derived. |
| A single global quantum state or a compatible family of local restrictions | The existence of a single encompassing state imposes ordinary marginal compatibility when the algebras and observations are given. A state assignment, its purity, its normalizability and the allowed overlapping access are independent assumptions. |
| Existence and agreement of internal reference descriptions | An extra regularity condition is needed for a chosen reference to give an invertible description. Matched probabilities of one event then follow from the representation of one state and effect. Different instruments or access restrictions may give different predictions. |
| Finite information capacity | A finite-dimensional factor has a finite quantum information capacity for a declared access model. Finiteness of all physical information is a separate hypothesis. Compact spatial topology does not by itself bound infinitely many field modes. The F5 lift supports a separable infinite-dimensional matter sector with the same finite references. |
| Maximum entropy or low entropy | Both are state constraints. Even when a finite-dimensional physical projector is specified, its normalized form is only one state among many. Neither a Gibbs assumption nor a low-entropy clock and blank record follow from physical closure. |
| Compact space, zero total energy, or cosmological boundary selection | These require additional geometric, constraint, asymptotic or state hypotheses. $C|\Psi\rangle=0$ selects a gauge sector in a specified constrained theory. It does not assert a universal value of global energy. A compact spatial slice is also distinct from a no-boundary path-integral prescription. |
