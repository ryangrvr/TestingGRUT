# One architectural verdict

**Retain spatial escape as a conditional transport mechanism; do not infer
that B2 proves an intrinsically one-composite device or requires a different
preparation principle. Reliable scalable matter operation is still unproved.**

The unchanged B2 failure is quantitatively associated with resource returning
across endpoint bonds and low-band coherent rephasing into the fuels on a
short closed host. It is not adequately described as matter simply failing
to form, or as high-energy trapped modes absorbing the initial preparation.
At the failed endpoint, creation and outward source currents are already
positive again. This is a poor finite operating endpoint within recurrent
dynamics, not a demonstrated permanent fuel trap.

That verdict is deliberately limited. The diagnosis does not prove that
more space, a different source layout, a time average, decoherence, a new
detuning or an altered readout would pass. None was tried as a replacement
protocol. It does not select a good scalable regime, derive a bound on
returns at increasing host size, or produce an extensive medium. B2 remains
failed at its original event/time. The pinched and averaged comparisons
explain interference; their favorable numbers are not new successes.

Interior fuel permits exchange but does not automatically erase information.
The actual conditional fixture has a nearly unitary internal channel, and
the collective triplet algebra is exactly preserved by the declared law.
For arbitrary two-qubit inputs, fuel/position correlations can nevertheless
make restricted receiver decoding imperfect. Global inverse evolution or
additional recorded feed-forward can recover information with explicitly
different access/resources; neither was part of the original operating task.

The architecture therefore remains a conditional device/research model,
not evidence of a self-sustaining matter-bearing phase. This cycle has not
earned a scalable mechanism to build. It closes with the diagnosis and a
review packet, without opening B3, changing B2, or reopening A1.

Independent review of B1's formation/use composition and the load-bearing
spectral/channel/continuity arguments is still pending. A reproduced script
would improve execution confidence, not independently validate the all-size
mathematics or a theory of nature. No distinct empirical prediction follows.
