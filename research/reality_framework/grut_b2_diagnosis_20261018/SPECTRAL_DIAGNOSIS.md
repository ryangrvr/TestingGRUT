## Propagating, trapped, recurrent: what is known

Spatial propagation is present: source-region resources leave and later
return, and outgoing configurations occur. The source current and its
integral provide that evidence without labelling individual trajectories.
The low cluster carries nearly the entire prepared state's norm and
supports the large coherent return at the failed endpoint.

Every finite matrix has discrete stationary modes. A source-heavy mode in
an eight-event host is not, by itself, an infinite-volume trapped state.
No permanent trapped/propagating spectral partition is established. Source
currents are already outward again at theta=6, excluding the inference that
the observed 70.46% marginal fuel population is permanently immobilized.

The defensible explanation is strong low-band coherent recurrence in a
short closed host, combined with real spatial return to source regions.
This does not prove that every larger host will work, that all return is
caused by a simple wall reflection, or that a new preparation principle is
necessary. Establishing a scalable escape/return regime remains a separate,
unproved physical statement, not an inference from this finite spectrum.
