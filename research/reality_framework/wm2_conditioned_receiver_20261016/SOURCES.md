second quantization. Duhamel, Taylor remainders, unitary telescoping,
SVD and complex two-mode rotations are standard tools. THEORY.md gives
the project-specific derivation so that its exact scope can be checked
without importing an unproved variational approximation.

No exhaustive novelty search, external review, outward-rounded arithmetic
or empirical validation is represented by these sources or computations.
