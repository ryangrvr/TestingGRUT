## Deliberate construction decision

Close the **constraint-commutant-only** route to universal matter locality under
the stated independent-local-algebra hypotheses. Do not add another conservation
constraint just to eliminate the latest product. It would need to remove a
theorem hypothesis, not merely be named 'gauge'.

The defensible immediate modeling option is to **book universal relational
generator locality as a primitive**. RI is an explicit sufficient comparison
class for that option. This packet does not adopt another architecture or
claim that its primitive has emerged. The stronger selection-mechanism route
remains open and would need a separately declared Hamiltonian or encoding.

Choosing the first option can support a controlled relational-geometry model.
It cannot, by itself, explain why every fundamental matter sector in nature
shares space. Reusing the word 'universal' must mean 'all generators and species
in the declared restricted class', not every imaginable physical extension.

## What could reject or change these conclusions?

* Obstruction: exhibit failure of the independent-qubit representation, product
  admissibility, operational preparation, or exact response identity. Without
  one of those failures, constraints commuting with both factors cannot reject
  their product.
* RI theorem: exhibit a permitted interval term whose restricted site-algebra
  support is nonlocal; a shorter omitted-action word; failure of the stated
  norm caps; or a cross-boundary term active outside the counted buffers.
* Derivation of locality: supply an independent microscopic selection mechanism
  that excludes the actual forbidden products and all their complete virtual
  realizations. Merely rewriting RI as a constraint does not pay its price.
