1. Give explicit physical tensor-factor, preparation, source/readout, strength
   and distance hypotheses. The obstruction says that the broad class contains
   a signaling Hamiltonian, not that every member signals nonlocally.
2. Distinguish the full constraint commutant, the algebra preserving its kernel,
   and a generator family. Observable multiplication is not an authorization to
   use every product as a primitive interaction.
3. Include diagonal internal couplings and spectator-dependent coefficients.
   Neither may be hidden in an unrestricted 'configuration-preserving' part.
4. State a sufficient finite-range relational generator class on UC1's generated
   word, with all species and internal operations covered, a fixed range R, a
   size-uniform few-carrier changing norm, and explicit receiver/head restrictions.
   This is a new restriction on admissible laws, not a consequence of Gauss.
5. Prove a recorded-interior response bound by a common-Hilbert-space comparison
   and the complete Dyson/nested-commutator sums. Include all physical geometry,
   storage, receiver and return paths. No real-energy gap claim.
6. Exhibit a nonempty restricted class with both original mobile charged species,
   a nontrivial internal interaction, exact vacuum formation inheritance, and
   nonzero nearby transport. Do not claim that every permitted coefficient choice
   forces transport or equal speeds.
