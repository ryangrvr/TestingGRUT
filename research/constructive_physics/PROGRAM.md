# Constructive physics

**Mission active. Stage 3 inactive. Card 3 unopened.**

The first construction has a finite scientific scope: derive and test a
covariant scalar-curvature interaction mechanism, including its tree-level
nonlinear prediction and whether its proposed parameter economy survives
quantization. It is not a theory of everything and is not the frozen Stage-3
challenge under a different label.

## Minimum foundation supplied in this cycle

1. A differentiable four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime and a metric
   variational principle, with constraint-compatible Cauchy data.
2. Local matter fields, their inventory, masses and interaction parameters.
3. A positive scalar curvature susceptibility F and its specified potential.
4. For the perturbative quantum interpretation, ordinary quantum states,
   preparations, the Born rule and local regulated field quantization.
5. Boundary conditions, apparatus choices and initial states.

None is credited as emergent. A thermodynamic state, consciousness variable,
preferred observer, detector map or state-dependent Hamiltonian is not a
primitive of the proposed action.

## Scientific accounting

The design log includes rejected analytic drafts, not just the selected one.
Freeze LAW.md and its scope before computation. Preserve its text after an
obstruction; do not append an unpriced term to obtain a passing result.

Distinguish:

- classical/local existence and stability;
- ordinary perturbative quantum effective-field interpretation;
- a quantum theory specified by the proposed finite parameter family;
- independent experimental support.

A result in one category does not establish the others. A finite test suite
does not prove global existence, quantum gravity or experimental validity.
