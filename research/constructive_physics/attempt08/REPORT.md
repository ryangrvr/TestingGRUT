For a fixed bipartition into a lower layer of size a and upper layer of size b, every subset of the ab directed cross-relations is a valid partial order. On this configuration hypercube all transport generators Z_i X_j commute: a lower index is never also an upper index. Therefore the matter connection is flat on this entire layered subspace, even though it has nontrivial holonomy on other cycles.

Construct the normalized trial state

    |trial> = 2^(-ab/2) sum_B |C_B> tensor product_(e in B) U_e |psi>.
