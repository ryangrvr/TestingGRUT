## Fixed model and test inventory, before computation

1. Mixed-record locality control: arbitrary pairs of conditional E states on
   an open N-qubit chain; common nearest-neighbour depth-d circuits. N=4,6;
   d=0,1,2,3. Four disclosed initial pairs: product records with Bloch length
   0.8; records mixed with common entangled GHZ noise at weight 0.3; identical
   maximally mixed states; and independently seeded Wishart states. Seed
   20261010. Every initial site, its truncated radius-d block and transported
   Helstrom measurement are checked, without assuming initial independence.
2. Interaction control: standard central-spin dephasing Hamiltonian
   H=Omega*Z_S/2 + sum_i(h_i*Z_i + g_i*Z_S*X_i), with supplied finite tensor
   factors. h_i>0, g_i>=0; initial memories (I-r_i*Z_i)/2, 0<=r_i<=1,
   initially independent of S and one another. If thermal, r_i=tanh(beta_i*h_i);
   this Gibbs preparation and its temperature are **supplied**. No equilibrium
   assumption for the interacting total system. Pointer algebra is computed
   from this given H, not claimed to generate the subsystem or coupling axis.
3. Numerical units hbar=E0=1; Omega=0.3. One-memory pairs (h,g)=(1,1),
   (0.5,0.7),(1,0.3),(1,0); r=0,0.4,0.8,1; times 0,0.2,pi/(2*sqrt(2)),1.7,
   pi/sqrt(2). Two-memory profiles and all matrix-unit checks are disclosed
   in audit.py/results.json. Exact formulae cover the whole stated family.
4. Hostile control: same writing Hamiltonian and initial memories, h_F=h_R=
   g_F=g_R=1, writing time pi/(2*sqrt(2)). Both interactions are turned off
   by the **same accounted quench**; then admit one F-R controlled-phase pulse
   exp(-i*phi*|11><11|), phi=0,pi/3,pi/2,pi. Same pulse duration tau=1,
   local control norm at most pi, unchanged memory size/labels/access F.
   Main two-memory geometry has the three fixed edges S-F, S-R and F-R;
   this declared triangle is distinct from the locality control's open chain.
   Bare drift continues during the pulse. Common total norm ceiling is 8
   in these units; both writing and readout stages satisfy it. No added
   ancillas, feedback, discarded degrees of freedom or changed preparation.
   The phase pulse conserves each bare local memory energy, not just their
   sum. Its ideal net switching work is zero; controller implementation
   costs/native synthesis are not derived. The common quench work is recorded.
5. Noise control: exact reduced channels are CPTP, with a finite recurrence
   witness. A positive time-local GKSL rate is not silently assumed. The
   Recon01 positive-rate discriminator is not applied outside its scope.
   An explicitly outside-product-preparation control compares a Bell state
   with I/4 at the same local thermal marginals and same Hamiltonian. General
   phase-formula controls cover all disclosed two-memory profiles and times.
