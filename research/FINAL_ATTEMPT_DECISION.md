## Exact failure boundary

**Passive covariance** transforms the description together: state, operator
coordinates, Hamiltonian, preparation and readout. For example,
\(\operatorname{Tr}(\rho Q)=\operatorname{Tr}(W\rho W^\dagger WQW^\dagger)\).
It preserves predictions and does not imply that changing the physical state
alone leaves the energy functional unchanged.

**Active invariance** holds the supplied physical structure fixed and requires
\(F(W\rho W^\dagger)=F(\rho)\) when the state is physically rotated. Card 2's
all-axis correlation norm has this stronger symmetry for every independent
local unitary.

The exact boundary is broader than this one quadratic scalar. On a finite
tensor-product Hilbert space, let a differentiable real functional \(F\) define
\(H_F=\delta F/\delta\rho\) and intrinsic evolution
\(\dot\rho=-i[H_F,\rho]\). If \(F\) is invariant under every independent
local unitary, then for every Hermitian local \(A_i\),

\[
0=\delta F(-i[A_i,\rho])
=i\operatorname{Tr}(A_i[H_F,\rho])
=-\operatorname{Tr}(A_i\dot\rho).
\]

Since local Hermitian operators span the local observable algebra,
\(\boxed{\dot\rho_i=0}\). This excludes intrinsic local-state response for
that specified class of Hamiltonian-gradient flows. With supplied local drives,
only \(-i[V_i,\rho_i]\) remains. **Global correlations can still evolve.**
The argument does not exclude arbitrary emergent interactions, passive-covariant
laws, stochastic/dissipative laws, or all nonlinear quantum theories.

The product-block obstruction has additional, Card-2-specific assumptions:
pair-connected correlations are the only interaction source; the scalar is
quadratic; there is no independent cross-block Hamiltonian or entangling control.
For \(\rho=\bigotimes_B\rho_B\), cross-block correlations vanish, so
\(H_C=\sum_B H_C(\rho_B)\otimes I_{\bar B}\). Uniqueness keeps this product
manifold invariant. This stronger block claim is not inferred from active
invariance alone. It is not a theorem that the universe began in a product state.

## Lessons from both cards

Card 1 decoded spectral projectors already encoded in its supplied operator;
deleting dissipation left that identity decoder intact. Card 2 decoded
organization from supplied factors/correlations without generating an observation
interface. **Deriving properties of an input is not deriving the physical input.**

Both attempts moved to a simple conditional rate relation before establishing
the protected generated chain or an experimental lock. Card 1's ensemble
equation already occurs in an energy-driven stochastic reduction model: averaging
Adler's Eq. (2), with \(H=A\) and \(\sigma^2=4\tau\), gives its density drift
[L1]. Ordinary finite dilations also reproduce its measured channels. Card 2's
conditional phase formula is mathematically true, but neither jointly links
generated identity to response nor supplies valid conventional operational
mixture dynamics. A conventional comparator violating a relation is necessary;
it is insufficient when the candidate's own generation or admissibility fails.

The reported partial prices are 2,735 bits and 9,315.46 bits, respectively, with
zero credited joint compression and uncertified full prices. These are not
approved exact totals or a proof that every future compact law is too expensive.
The methodological error was treating a short symbolic equation as a cheap
physical commitment while its algebra, domain, inputs and operational maps
remained costly or missing.

Interaction-Origin 01 supplies a separate inference limit: the complete declared
vacuum/one-particle dynamics is identical across
\(H_u=5N+uN(N-1)/2\), while two-particle spectroscopy measures \(\chi=u\).
The restricted data cannot identify that completion. This does not make \(u\)
unexplained in a fully specified microscopic theory, require one value across
different materials, or prove that no deeper physical principle exists.

## Feasibility of the remaining attempt

The charter's **limited target** is a law-generated identity/carrier/readout/
interface chain and one forced, nondefinitional, nonstandard joint response
restriction, with positive information compression and an experimentally feasible
LT-1 lock (§§1–5). It does not require Stage 3 to select a unique universe or
derive every constant. Stage 4 independently verifies generation; Stage 5 uses
sealed real data; the unchanged-law quantum/thermodynamic/gravitational extension
belongs to Stage 6 (§15.10–12). Passing Stage 3 would not establish the North
Star's theory-of-reality endpoint.

The framework is a meaningful **conditional test** for a proposal that supplies
those objects. Its mathematical satisfiability and practical attainability have
not been demonstrated by either card. Appendix A's ST-10 is explicitly a paper
estimate for compression only: roughly 720–1,220 priced bits against up to
1,000/2,000 coupling bits in its one/two-codimension illustration, before family
and lock-fiber caps. It supplies no physical mechanism or demonstration that
generation, non-standardness and measurability can be achieved together. Conversely,
two failed proposals do not prove that the charter is impossible to satisfy.

The current record has no independently motivated mechanism that generates the
chain, no qualifying joint restriction surviving the complete conventional
comparison, and no corresponding calibrated experimental lock. The interaction
audit's free completion parameter is an inference obstruction, not that target;
the frozen Approach-B experiment tests a conventional supplied Hamiltonian.
External kit clearance would make the judge more trustworthy, but would not
provide the missing physics. **There is therefore no evidence-supported,
experimentally meaningful Card-3 target to recommend now.**
