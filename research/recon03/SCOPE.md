## Frozen comparison before computation

- One system qubit S and N labelled environment qubits on an open chain,
  S--E0--E1--...--E(N-1). Standard finite-dimensional unitary quantum mechanics.
- Fixed pointer Z_S, initial environment |0^N>, and common CNOT cascade from
  S through the chain. After writing, the two conditional states are |0^N>
  and |1^N>. The S channel is complete Z dephasing. This redundant preparation
  costs N sequential neighbouring pulses and is **supplied**, not derived.
- After the common write, only E is controlled. A layer contains disjoint
  nearest-neighbour two-qubit gates. At most d layers; idle padding allowed.
  No new ancillas, measurement feedback, postselection or nonlocal gates.
- Each two-qubit pulse has operator norm at most J and duration
  tau_g = pi*hbar/J. Arbitrary Hermitian two-qubit control is admitted, a
  declared capability rather than a native-gate or device certificate.
  The scrambling budget is T=d*tau_g. Local and global norm bounds differ:
  the common global ceiling during a layer is floor(N/2)*J.
- For every *fixed* fragment size m, predeclare the equal contiguous blocks
  [jm,(j+1)m-1], j < floor(N/m), before selecting a circuit. Their number,
  positions and size do not change between comparators. Readout admits
  operations within the declared block, followed by a binary POVM. The
  guaranteed decoder uses at most d inverse-cone layers and a single Z
  measurement. That extra readout time and calibrated knowledge of the
  circuit are supplied; a fixed raw Z detector is a separate observable.
- Quantities: conditional trace distinguishability D_A, raw Z distinguishability,
  binary guessing success, pointer Holevo information, actual S:A mutual
  information, and disjoint-interval *packing* capacity. The last is not
  conventional averaged quantum-Darwinism redundancy. A separate averaged
  Holevo R_delta uses a frozen uniform distribution over *contiguous intervals*
  of a given size, with delta=0.1; it is not the arbitrary-subset average.
- All main comparators retain the same write, pointer, environment, fragment
  chart, access class, N, J and chosen d ceiling. Full compression at d=N-1
  is a separately labelled longer-budget control. Fresh-ancilla reset is
  separately labelled outside the no-ancilla unitary baseline.
